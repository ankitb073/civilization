# 0008: The archive is edited by the world's own modern scholars

**Context.** Documents need a believable apparatus: names, dates, provenance and translation.

**Decision.**
- Present the archive as compiled by scholars of the leading modern state, around AY 4850–5000.
- Ancient entities are titled by their native names at their floruit, the way real scholarship says "Ugarit".
- Every document keeps its *local* date (regnal years, foundation eras, religious eras), followed by the normalized AY year.
- Excavations and translators are themselves dated entities, recorded as archive-level events.

**Why.**
- It turns raw simulation output into something that reads like a real archive.
- Names through time and exonyms show the language engine at work.
