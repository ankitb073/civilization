# 0004: Procedural documents, plus agent-written flagships

**Context.** The brief asks for thousands of primary sources. LLM-written prose reads richer, but costs money and drifts from the facts.

**Decision.**
- A deterministic generator writes every document from simulation state. It uses era- and culture-specific formulae: Bronze Age oaths, article-numbered treaties, ledgers and glossed inscriptions.
- A few hundred flagship documents are written by AI agents from database dossiers. They are then validated by the same checker.

**Why.**
- Consistency is guaranteed by construction.
- The build is free and reproducible.
- Agents add literary depth where it matters most.

The owner approved this approach on 2026-10-15.
