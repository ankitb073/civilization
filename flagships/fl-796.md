*[Obverse. The song is set out in lines. The refrain is written once and marked to be repeated.]*

We are the mothers. We sit at the gate of [[set:169|Kagün]].  
We sit at the gate and grind, and look down the road.  
Hear us, [[dei:31|Kumbi]], moon, mother of the night, who counts the sleepers.  
Hear us, [[dei:32|Nijinandi]], lady of war, who took our sons by the hand.  

In the sixteenth year of [[per:9479|Navingandu]], the queen, the lioness of [[pol:379|Yif]],  
the criers went through the streets: Yif goes out against Yish.  
That was the [[evt:9391|First Yif–Yizhe War]]. Every house gives a son.  
My house gave a son. Her house gave a son. That house gave two.  
We baked the bread for the road. We sewed the barley in the hems.  
We said: come back. They said: we will come back with the tribute of Yish.  

*Where is my son? My son is at Yizhi.*  
*Where is Yizhi? Yizhi is down the road.*  
*How long is the road? As long as a mother's night.*  

In the seventeenth year of Navingandu, this year, the word came up the road.  
They fought at Yizhi. That was the [[evt:9407|Battle of Yizhi]].  
Our sons were many, more than ten for every one of the men of [[pol:399|Yish]].  
Our sons were many, and the men of Yish were few, and yet our sons fell.  
Five hundred of our sons and more, the runners said. Five hundred and more,  
for a town held by a few. Nijinandi, is that the price of a few?  

And the queen. Hear it, mothers. The queen went first, and the queen fell.  
Navingandu, who sat on the seat of Yif sixteen years and more,  
who stood at Yizhi before, when we were young, and lost there,  
she went back to Yizhi to win it, and she won it, and she lay down there.  
The runners said she fell at the front. The runners said she did not turn.  
We weep for her with our sons. We put her name with our sons' names.  

*Where is my son? My son is at Yizhi.*  
*Where is Yizhi? Yizhi is down the road.*  
*How long is the road? As long as a mother's night.*  

*[Reverse]*

Then our sons went in at the gate of [[set:87|Yish]]. That was the [[evt:9408|Sack of Yizhi]].  
They took the bronze. They took the cattle. They took the grain from the storehouse.  
The runners say: Yif has won. The runners say: Yish will pay.  
The war is not ended. The criers have not said it is ended.  
The sons who live are still on the road. The sons who live are still in the field.  

A measure of barley at the gate of Kagün is one [[cur:199|Vazhüf]] and a little.  
We sold the copper bowls to buy it. We will sell the loom-weights next.  
Our husbands are old. Our daughters work the fields. Who will cut the barley?  

Kumbi, moon, keep watch over the camp at Yizhi.  
Count our sons where they sleep. Count them twice. Let none be missing.  
Nijinandi, lady of war, you have had five hundred. Let that be enough.  
Ugash, sun, light the road home. Let the dust on the road be our sons.  

*Where is my son? My son is at Yizhi.*  
*Where is Yizhi? Yizhi is down the road.*  
*When will he come? When the road is short.*
