*Sung by those who came back, in the 1st year of [[per:9608|Nish]].*

Weep, [[set:74|Tlaiy]], weep, city of the barley-land,  
weep, Tlaiy, city of the hundred doors,  
your doors are ash, your doors are ash,  
weep, Tlaiy, the fifth time weep.  

Our mothers' mothers sang this song, and the mothers before them.  
Four times they sang it, four times the city burned.  
Four times they sang it, four times the city stood.  
We sing it the fifth time. We sing it, and we stand.  

In the year that is gone the men of [[pol:312|Man'a]] came,  
they sat before the walls, they ate our fields,  
the [[evt:9319|siege of Tliyiya]], the old men call it;  
the winter came, and we said: now they will go home.  

In this year the men of Man'a did not go home.  
More came at barley harvest, more, like locusts.  
At the [[evt:9338|battle of Tliyiya]] our men went out,  
one man of ours against eight of theirs, one against eight;  
one fell, and eight came on, and eight came on.  

The gate of the wells broke. The gate of the potters broke.  
Fire in the street of the weavers, fire in the street of the smiths,  
fire on the steps of the house of [[dei:97|Olchi]], but the tablets were kept,  
fire on the roof of the granary. The barley burned.  
The barley burned, and the smoke was sweet, and we wept.  

Where is the bronze-smith? He lies by his anvil.  
Where is the bronze-smith's son? He lies by his father.  
Where is the bride of the street of the dyers?  
She is gone with the men of Man'a, she is gone on the long road.  

The old queen went to [[dei:101|Ami]] in the spring,  
the old queen went down to the house below.  
Her son, the young king Nish, sat on her seat.  
The young king set his seal on the tablet of Man'a.  
"We are their servants," say the old men. "We give them silver,  
we give them silver every year, as the tablet says."  
The [[evt:9340|Treaty of Tliyiya]], the scribes call it. We call it bread.  

Bread? A measure of barley for three [[cur:202|Iw]] and more,  
three Iw and more, and the granary burned.  
The mother sells her bracelet. The father sells his ox.  
The child does not ask. The child has learned.  

[[dei:102|Nilyi]] of the river, carry the ash away.  
[[dei:98|Udhash]] of the sea, take it, take it far.  
Muvas of the earth, cover our dead.  
Ami of the house below, open your door to them gently.  
Olchi of wisdom, teach our hands to build.  

We carry the stones from the fields.  
We mix the mud with straw. We set the beam.  
The swallow comes back to the burned eave.  
The swallow builds. We build.  

Weep, Tlaiy, weep, city of the barley-land,  
weep, and stand, the fifth time stand.  
Our mothers sang it four times, and we sing it the fifth.  
Let no one sing it a sixth time. Let no one sing it again.
