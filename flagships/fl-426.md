*[Obverse. A foundation tablet, found face-down beneath a threshold. The surface is well preserved.]*

I am [[per:378|Tatrelkutnakin]].  
I am the lord of [[pol:12|Chinontonu]], the lord of the river-land, the lord of the barley-land.  
I sit on the seat in [[set:6|Chinontonu]]. I sit on it by the will of the gods.  
Hear me, you who come after. Hear me, and fear.  

In the days of [[per:229|Lalmilin]] the old lord, the land was good.  
Forty summers and four he sat on the seat. I honour him. I poured beer for him.  
But Lalmilin grew old. His hand shook on the staff. His eyes did not see the gate.  
The storehouses grew thin. The elders quarrelled in the gate.  
The men of the river-clan said one thing; the men of the hill-clan said another thing.  
There was no word in the gate. There was no hand on the staff.  

Then Lalmilin went down to [[dei:59|Winba]]. He lay down in his house and went down.  
And the seat was empty, and the elders quarrelled over it like dogs over a bone.  
Who would hold the land? Who would fill the storehouse? Who would shut the gate?  
I stood up. I, Tatrelkutnakin, stood up. I took the staff into my hand.  
I did not wait for the elders. The elders would have waited until the enemy came.  
Some say: he seized it. Let them say it. I seized it from the dogs, not from the lord.  
The scribes write: the [[evt:322|Usurpation of Tatrelkutnakin]]. Let them write it.  
I say: the gods put the staff in my hand, and my hand did not shake.  

Three harvests I have sat on the seat. Three harvests the storehouses have filled.  
Is that not the sign of the gods? Prosperity is the favour of the gods, say the priests of [[mov:8|Ligay]].  
The gods are pleased. The land is quiet. The gate has a word in it.  

*[Reverse. The curse.]*

Now hear it, you who come after.  
Whoever takes the seat from me, whoever takes the staff from my hand,  
whoever takes the seat from my seed after me,  
whoever breaks this tablet, whoever turns it to the light, whoever scrapes my name from it,  
whoever says to the elders, "Come, let us put another on the seat,"  
let the gods do to him as I say.  

May [[dei:55|Dumamnul]] of the sky shut the sky over him. May no rain fall on his field.  
May [[dei:56|Nabuthin]] of the river turn away from his canal. May his canal be dust.  
May [[dei:57|Bubin]] of the moon hide her face from him. May he wander in the dark and not find his door.  
May [[dei:58|Chithal]] of the spear break his spear in his hand. May he turn his back in the field.  
May Winba of the earth shut her mouth to his barley. May his seed rot in the ground.  
May [[dei:60|Shineyta]] of the sea take his ships and his sons. May the water close over them.  
May his name be cut from the tablets. May no one pour beer for him.  
May he go down to Winba with no son behind him.  
May the wheel turn, and turn, and never bring him back.  

This is my word. I have set it under the threshold.  
Whoever steps over it, let him know that Tatrelkutnakin is lord.  

*[Lower edge.]*

> Laid under the threshold of the lord's house in Chinontonu, in the 3rd year of Tatrelkutnakin.
