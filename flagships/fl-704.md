title: Song of the Empty Granary in Sanelu

This is the granary. Count the stones.  
Forty stones in the wall, one door, one bar.  
My grandfather cut the bar. My father greased it every spring.  
I lift it now with one hand.  
There is nothing behind it to keep.  

*Sweep the floor, sweep the floor.*  
*There is nothing on the floor.*  

We kept the tally on the doorpost.  
One notch for every basket in.  
One cut across for every basket out.  
Look at the doorpost now. It is all cuts.  
The last notch was made before the barley failed.  
The last cut was made at the new moon.  

The rain has been short for nineteen years.  
It was short before my daughter was born.  
She is grown, she is married, she has a child at the breast,  
and the rain is still short.  
The traders who come over the hills say it is not ours alone,  
that it is [[evt:13311|the Great Drought in Daadüdhih]]  
and we are only the edge of it.  
I have never seen Daadüdhih. I have seen my field.  

*Sweep the floor, sweep the floor.*  
*There is nothing on the floor.*  

This spring we sowed twelve rows.  
Four rows came up.  
The grasshoppers took one row. The hot wind took one row.  
We cut two rows, and that was the harvest.  
Two rows do not fill a granary.  
Two rows do not fill a pot.  

First we gave each person one cup of meal a day.  
Then one cup for two persons.  
Then the old ones said: give our cup to the children.  
We did it. We were ashamed. We did it.  
My husband's mother went to sleep in the heat of the day  
and did not wake for the evening.  
We carried her to the hill. The ground was too hard to dig deep.  

At the river crossing there was a man with barley.  
He wanted the good cooking pot for one sack.  
The next week he wanted the pot and the goat.  
The week after that he did not come.  
People walk past on the road with bundles. They do not say where.  
They do not need to say. They are going where the rain is.  

There is no king in [[reg:31|Sanelu]] to open a storehouse.  
There is no clerk to write us on a list.  
No one is coming with carts. We know that. We keep our own list.  
The neighbours count their children every morning.  
I count mine. I count them twice.  

In the market at the ford they say it plainly now:  
[[evt:13435|Famine in Anel]].  
Say it plainly, then. Saying it plainly does not fill the bowl.  
The headman says: eat the seed corn and there is no next year.  
Keep the seed corn and there is no this year.  
He says it, and then he sits down, because there is nothing after it to say.  

*Sweep the floor, sweep the floor.*  
*There is nothing on the floor.*  

I am not singing the end of this.  
I do not know the end of this.  
The sky over [[reg:31|Sanelu]] is white every morning and white every night.  
The well rope is longer than it was. The water is lower than it was.  
The children have stopped asking for bread.  
That is the worst thing. I wish they would ask.  

If you hear this song in a full year,  
when the doorpost has notches again,  
sweep your floor, and leave one basket by the door  
for whoever comes up the road with nothing.  
Someone once would have left one for us.  

*Sweep the floor, sweep the floor.*  
*There is nothing on the floor.*
