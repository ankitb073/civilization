*Sung by the mothers at the gate of [[pol:449|Kvile]], in the 23rd year of [[per:11083|Pyivünin]].*

Mothers of Kvile, come to the gate.  
Mothers of Kvile, bring the barley-cake and the jar.  
Come to the gate where the road goes out.  
The road went out. Our sons went out on it.  

Thirteen summers ago they went out on the road,  
in the [[evt:11245|Second Kvile–Kivuna War]], the scribes say.  
The king was a boy then, a boy on the seat.  
Our sons were boys then. Boys on the road.  

The first summer they went to Unushti.  
At the [[evt:11257|battle of Unushti]] the captain led them,  
[[per:11441|Manlomun]] led them, the captain with the red plume.  
The men of [[pol:489|Kivunga]] were few, few as fingers.  
Yet our sons lay down in the barley.  
Mother of the potter's son, where is your son?  
In the barley at Unushti. Woe.  

The second summer they went to Unushti.  
At the [[evt:11283|Second Battle of Unushti]] Manlomun led them.  
The men of Kivunga were few, few as fingers.  
Yet our sons lay down in the barley.  
Mother of the smith's son, where is your son?  
In the barley at Unushti. Woe.  

The third summer they went to Unushti.  
At the [[evt:11285|battle of Unushti]], the third time, they went.  
The men of Kivunga were few, few as fingers.  
Yet our sons lay down in the barley, more than before.  
Mother of the shepherd's son, where is your son?  
Mother of the miller's twins, where are your sons?  
In the barley at Unushti. Woe, woe.  

Then the city of Kivunga was shut in, and opened its gate.  
Then the king set his seal at the [[evt:11287|Treaty of Kivunge]].  
Now Kivunga sends silver every year, every year the silver comes.  
The silver comes up the road where our sons went down.  
Does the silver have my son's face? Does it have his voice?  
I took a Shüvi of it in the market. It did not speak.  

[[dei:64|Nidi]] of war, you were fed at Unushti.  
Nidi, you ate three summers. Nidi, are you full?  
[[dei:66|Shyidartsi]] of the house below, you have our sons.  
Give them a fire in your house. Give them barley-cake.  
Tono of the moon, you saw the barley at Unushti at night.  
Tono, you saw them lying. Did you cover them with your light?  

Mothers of Kvile, come to the gate again.  
The road goes out again. The drum is beating again.  
Now Kvile fights Kvile, brother against brother,  
in the [[evt:11382|First Kvile Civil War]], the scribes say.  
They take the sons of the sons. They take the young ones.  
The grandson of the potter went out this spring.  
The nephew of the smith went out this spring.  

Mothers of Kvile, hold the barley-cake.  
Mothers of Kvile, hold the jar.  
Stand at the gate where the road goes out,  
and sing, so the road hears, so the drum hears:  
we gave three summers. We gave enough.  
Send them back up the road. Send them back.
