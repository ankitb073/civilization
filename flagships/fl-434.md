*[Obverse. A song tablet with the heading "Song of the king's daughter, which she made herself". Lines for the women's chorus are marked; they are printed here in italics.]*

I am [[per:9183|Panornenath]], daughter of the king, daughter of [[pol:370|Yennga]].  
I am the daughter of [[per:9025|Shadhjevan]], who took the seat when the seat was falling,  
who held the staff when the staff was slipping, in the year of the [[evt:8680|Usurpation of Shadhjevan]].  
I was a small girl then. I hid behind the loom. I saw my father take the staff.  
Now I am a woman. Now I go out from the loom.  
*She goes out. The daughter of the king goes out.*

Comb my hair, my sisters. Comb it with the bone comb.  
Put the red thread in it, put the bronze pins in it.  
Wash my feet in the water of [[set:155|Yennga]], the sweet water of my father's well.  
Tomorrow I drink other water. Tomorrow my feet walk another floor.  
*Comb her hair. Wash her feet.*

Who is the husband? Who comes to the gate with gifts?  
It is [[per:9189|Vakotnakhen]], third of the name, son of the house of [[pol:374|Toböjenga]].  
He is young. His cheek is smooth like the cheek of a girl.  
He stands at the gate and does not know where to put his hands.  
I will teach him where to put his hands. Laugh, my sisters!  
*Laugh, sisters! She will teach him.*

He brings the bride-gift. He brings silver, [[cur:96|Bado]] upon Bado in a basket.  
He brings oil in jars, he brings cloth on donkeys, he brings goats from the hill.  
My father weighs the silver. My father counts the goats. My father is pleased.  
The house of Yennga and the house of Toböjenga: two houses, one roof.  
*Two houses, one roof.*

[[dei:18|Eva]] of the hearth, light the new fire. Let it not go out.  
[[dei:15|Venba]] of the earth, open my body. Give me sons for two houses.  
[[dei:17|Nedhekhte]] of the grain, fill the storehouse of the new house.  
[[dei:19|Yenan]] of the moon, watch over the bed. Count the nights for me.  
*Light the fire. Fill the storehouse.*

Hear me, Vakotnakhen, son of Toböjenga. Hear me, boy who will be a man.  
I am the daughter of a king. Do not forget it at the table.  
My father's spears are many. Do not forget it in the gate.  
If you are good to me, I am good to you. If you are a wall to me, I am a wall to you.  
I swear by [[dei:16|Domamnol]] of the sky; I swear by Venba of the earth.  
*She swears. The king's daughter swears.*

*[Reverse.]*

My mother is not here. My mother went down to Venba when I was small.  
Pour beer on the ground for her, sisters, that she may see me from below.  
My father does not weep. A king does not weep at the gate.  
But I saw his hand on the doorpost. I saw it. His hand was slow to let go.  
*Pour for her mother. Pour on the ground.*

Now the priests of [[mov:2|Jenro]] pour the oil. Now they break the bread.  
Now the boy takes my hand, and his hand is cold, and my hand is warm.  
Let it be so. The wheel turns. What ends will begin again.  
The daughter ends. The wife begins.  
This is the [[evt:8860|Marriage of Panornenath and Vakotnakhen III]].  
Let the scribes write it. Let the singers sing it.  
*The daughter ends. The wife begins.*

Sing it, sisters, when I am gone from the loom.  
Sing it at the well. Sing it when you comb your daughters' hair.  
Say: Panornenath made this song. She went out from Yennga with her head up.  
*She went out with her head up.*

*[Lower edge. Colophon.]*

> The song of the king's daughter, set down in the 14th year of Shadhjevan, king in Yennga, on the day of the bride's going-out.
