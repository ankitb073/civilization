*[Obverse.]*

Weep, O [[set:3|Shue'ethie]], city of the river, weep.  
Weep, O house of the king, the lamp of your house is out.  
Weep, O mothers, weep, O fathers, weep, O children at the gate,  
the young king has gone down, the young king has gone down to the earth.

O [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky, you saw him go.  
O [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the broad earth, you took him in.  
O [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river, you carried our tears away.  
O [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] of the moon, you counted his days, and they were few.

Hear, O city, how it was. Hear it, and remember.

[[per:0|Siensayo]], the great lady, she who built the walls,  
she who laid the first brick in the [[evt:34|Founding of Shue'ethie]],  
she who set the gods each in their place and gave them their due,  
fifteen years she sat in the house, and the city was glad.  
Then the great lady went down to the earth.  
The city wept, and the wheel turned.

Then came the [[evt:80|Accession of Ma'she Lieze]].  
He was her son. He was a child of eight summers.  
His feet did not reach the floor from the high seat.  
His hand could not hold the staff of the king.  
So [[per:17|Tlamfen Tlue'e]] stood beside him, the regent, the faithful one,  
he held the staff for him, he spoke the words for him,  
he poured to the gods for him, until the child should grow.

O [[per:16|Ma'she Lieze]], son of the great lady!  
In the morning you ran on the wall, and the guards laughed.  
In the evening you sat with the priests, and you learned the names of the gods.  
You learned the name of Atlie, you learned the name of [[dei:1|Uelti]] the wise,  
you learned the name of [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] who carries the spear.  
You said: "When I am a man I will pour for them myself."

*[Reverse.]*

Six years he sat in the house. Six harvests were cut in his name.  
When he had seen fourteen summers the fever came.  
The fever came up from the river in the hot month.  
The priests burned cedar. The priests read the liver.  
His young wife tore her garment. The regent sat at the door.  
The fever did not go out. The fever did not go out.  
On the seventh day he lay still.

Who will hold the staff that his hand was growing into?  
Who will pour to the gods the cup that he promised?  
Who will run on the wall in the morning?  
The wall is empty. The cup is empty. The high seat is empty.

The high seat did not stay empty.  
In the [[evt:98|Usurpation of Shuesh'anieme]] another man sat down in it.  
Now [[per:35|Shuesh'anieme]] holds the staff, and the city bows.  
Let the gods judge the house of the king. The poet sings only of the dead.

O Muevas'e, keep him gently. He was small; do not let the earth be heavy on him.  
O Niele'i, water the place where he lies.  
O Lashla'e, shine on him at night, for he was afraid of the dark.

Let the priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] pour for him at the new moon.  
Let the women of the city cut their hair for him.  
Let the young men of [[pol:0|Shue'ethie]] carry his name at the feast.  
Let no one say: he did nothing. He was the son of the city, and the city loved him.

What ends will begin again. The wheel turns.  
Weep, O Shue'ethie, and then be still.

*[Colophon.]* Written in the 3rd year of Shuesh'anieme.
