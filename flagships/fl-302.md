*[Obverse.]*

To the keeper of my house in [[set:0|Tallin]], say: thus says [[per:115|Ngamultun]], king of [[pol:1|Tallin]], beloved of [[dei:14|Vulme]], your lord.

With me it is well. With my body it is well. May it be well with you. May it be well with the house, with the storerooms, with the herds and with the women of the house. May [[dei:16|Dumamnul]] of the high sky keep you. May [[dei:15|Vinba]] of the black earth keep you. May Eva of the hearth keep the fire of my house.

Hear what I say, and say it to the elders at the gate.

The men of [[pol:10|Lenunushal]] shut the road of [[reg:9|Tupujinga]]. They shut the road of the donkeys. They shut the road of the boats. The tin did not come down to my city. The wool did not go out from my city. My merchants sat in the dust by the shut road. They tore their cloaks. They came to me weeping. Was it right? It was not right.

Who shut the road? [[per:147|Lomitathrelin]] shut the road. Lomitathrelin, who sits on a stool that was not given to him. Lomitathrelin, who took the house of his lord with his own hands. The gods did not give him the stool. The priests did not give him the stool. He took it as a thief takes a goat.

Last year I went down against him. This year I went down against him again. I took the spears of Tallin, I took the bowmen, I took the young men of the villages. I gave the bull to Vulme at the going-out. I gave the first sheaf to [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]]. I set my face toward Lenunushal.

At [[evt:197|Taulin]] the usurper came against me. Hear how he came. He did not come out on the open ground like a man. He hid in the ditches. He hid behind the banks of earth. He struck from the reeds like a jackal that bites the heel. The dust went up to Dumamnul. The sun was hidden. My men went into the dust and did not see the ditches.

I do not hide it from you: the field of Taulin was not mine that day. Many of my men lay down. Their mothers will wail in the villages. Let the wailing be done in order, as the priests teach.

But hear this also, and say it at the gate. The men of Lenunushal lay down in heaps. They lay down like sheaves in the month of cutting. All night they carried away their dead. All night their wailing went up from their camp. I heard it, my captains heard it. Let no man in my city say that Lomitathrelin laughed at Taulin. He did not laugh. He counted his dead.

What ends will begin again; the wheel turns. So say the priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]]. In the days before my days the spears of Tallin went into [[evt:159|Poynge]], and the gods gave Poynge into our hands. The gods have not forgotten us. The wheel has not stopped.

*[Reverse.]*

Therefore do this. Open the storerooms. Send me grain, send me oil, send me beer for the men. Send me bronze from the house of the smiths: spearheads, arrowheads, blades. Send me the young men who have reached the age of the spear. Send them before the new moon. Do not hold them back. Do not let the elders say, "The harvest, the harvest." The harvest will wait. The road will not wait.

Let the priests give a white ram to Vulme. Let them give a black ewe to Vinba. Let them not let the lamp of [[dei:19|Yenan]] go out in the moon-house. Let the fire of Eva burn day and night until I come home.

When the road of Tupujinga is open, the tin will come down again. The donkeys will come down again. The boats will come down again. Then I will fill the house of Vulme with the spoil of Lenunushal, and the usurper will sit in the dust by the shut road, as my merchants sat.

Send a word back to me with the runner. Let me hear that it is well with my house.

*[Left edge.]*

> Tablet of the king, written in the camp, in the 20th year of Ngamultun.
