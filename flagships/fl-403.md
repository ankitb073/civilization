*[Obverse]*

To the king, to our lord [[per:6480|Naliyun]], king of [[pol:262|Mankha]], the great one, the shepherd who feeds, the strong bull of the land, say: thus say your servants, the headmen of the villages of the low canals, the widows of the villages, the orphans of the villages. We kiss the ground before the feet of our lord. Seven times and seven times we kiss the ground.

May [[dei:20|Nothol]] of the fire warm the house of our lord. May [[dei:24|Navbuthen]] of the river bring water to the fields of our lord. May [[dei:22|Vinba]] of the earth open her womb for our lord. May [[dei:25|Novegu]] of wisdom give our lord a hearing ear.

Hear us, our lord. Hear us.

The rain has not come. The river has not come. From the days of the fathers of our fathers the sky has been bronze over [[reg:19|Mankha]]; the old men call it [[evt:5818|the Great Drought in Nivi]], and they say it has no end. Now it has become worse. Now it is a hunger.

In the twelfth year of our lord the barley did not come up. In the thirteenth year the barley came up and burned. In the fourteenth year we ate the seed corn. In the fifteenth year we ate the oxen that pull the plough. In the sixteenth year, in this year, we eat grass. We eat the leaves of the tamarisk. We eat the bark of the willow.

One measure of barley costs five [[cur:140|Nakhtse]] in the market, and more than five. Who has five? The headman does not have five. The priest does not have five. The widow has a cooking pot, and she has sold it.

*[Reverse]*

Hear us, our lord. Of every five houses in the villages of the low canals, one house is empty, or nearly one. The father died. The mother died. The children died, or went away. They went away on the road to Mikhukhizhi, in a long line, with the old men on the asses and the small children on the backs of their sisters. The scribes of our lord write it on their tablets: the [[evt:6232|Migration of the Ryethti into Mikhukhizhi]]. They do not come back. Their fields are dust. Their doorposts are fallen. The jackal sits on the threshold.

We who are left, we are your servants. We did not go. We stayed by the canals of our lord. We stayed by the altars of [[mov:3|Ryethki]]. We gave the priests the last of the oil. We gave [[dei:21|Duminu]] of the storm a black kid, the last kid, so that he would send rain. Duminu did not send rain. The priests say: the gods are angry; prosperity is a sign of favour, and we have no prosperity. Then what are we to do? We cannot buy the favour of the gods with grass.

Our lord has storehouses in [[set:13|Kivinkuthakhu]]. Our lord has granaries by the palace gate, sealed with the seal of our lord. Let our lord open one granary. Let our lord open one door of one granary. Let the barley come down the canal road in boats, and let the headmen measure it out, a measure to every mouth, and we will write the name of every mouth on the tablet for our lord.

Let our lord remit the barley-tax of this year, and of the year before. There is no barley to tax. Let the tax-gatherer not come with his staff to the empty house.

If our lord does this, we will sow again when the rain comes. We will dig the canals again. We will send our sons to the host of our lord. We will pour beer for our lord at every feast as long as we live.

If our lord does not do this, then let our lord send a scribe to count the dead, for we will not be able to count them ourselves.

*[Left edge]*

Written in the sixteenth year of Naliyun, in the month of the empty threshing floor. The seal of the headmen. The thumbprints of the widows.
