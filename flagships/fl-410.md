*[Obverse. A small, thick tablet. The signs are crowded, as if the scribe wrote quickly at dictation; the seal is the old lord's.]*

To [[per:26|Tyettkupantset]], my wife, the mother of my son, the lady of the house, say:

Thus says [[per:7|Luemitattpen]], lord of [[pol:7|Buneynga]], your husband.

With me it is not well. I will not write to you that it is well. The fever sits on my chest like a stone. The priests have poured to [[dei:136|Duemino]] of the storm and he has not lifted it. They have poured to [[dei:138|Tuel]] of the moon and she has not lifted it. I know where the road goes. It goes down to [[dei:137|Shyedartsi]], lord below. I am not afraid. I have done my duty. I have made the sacrifices. I go.

Hear me, wife. Hear me, mother of my son. Hear me, and keep this tablet in the box with my seal.

I set the first post of [[set:11|Buneynga]] with my own hands. That was the [[evt:53|Founding of Buneynga]], when the people came up from the river and I said: here. I set the post. I dug the ditch. I built the house of the gods. Eighteen years I have sat on the seat. Eighteen years the barley has come up. [[dei:139|Nidiktti]] has been kind; [[dei:141|Nabüdin]] of the river has been kind. The storehouses are full. That is the sign that the gods are pleased with Buneynga.

Now hear what I command.

When I go down, [[per:28|Rudan]] my son sits on the seat. Rudan, my son, the son of your body, he is the lord of Buneynga. No other. Not any kinsman of mine, not the elder of the river-clan, not the elder of the hill-clan. Rudan. Let the priests of [[mov:21|Kunelmu]] pour the oil on his head. Let them put the staff in his hand. Let the people see it.

But Rudan is a child. He is six summers old. He cannot hold the staff; it is taller than he is. Therefore you hold it. You, Tyettkupantset, you hold the staff for him. You sit beside the seat. You speak for him in the gate. You judge for him in the gate. You open the storehouse and you shut the storehouse. Until he is a man, the word of the mother is the word of the lord.

If the elders say, "A woman does not hold the staff," say to them: Luemitattpen gave it to her. Show them this tablet. Show them my seal. If they say it again, say to them: Shyedartsi hears oaths, and I am going to Shyedartsi, and I will tell him who broke faith with my son.

Teach Rudan the sacrifices. Teach him the barley-count. Teach him to sit still in the gate and listen before he speaks. Teach him that the lord feeds the people in the bad year, so that the people feed the lord in the good year. Do not let him be proud. I was proud. It is a fault.

Do not weep long. Pour beer for me at the new moon. Put a bowl of barley by the door at harvest. That is enough. [[dei:140|Winba]] of the earth will hold me.

I have spoken. My hand is on the seal.

*[Reverse. In a different, neater hand.]*

> The lord Luemitattpen went down to Shyedartsi three days after these words.  
> The oil was poured on the head of Rudan his son, and the staff was given into the hand of the lady Tyettkupantset, his mother, as the old lord commanded.  
> That was the [[evt:84|Accession of Rudan]].  
> Sealed and set in the box, in the 1st year of Rudan, lord of Buneynga.
