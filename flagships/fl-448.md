**MINUTES of the Privy Council of the Kingdom of [[pol:1786|Zhinontunu]], held in the Long Chamber of the palace at [[set:6|Zhinontunu]], on the 16th day of Vithni, in the year 2449 of the Vishithmikh.**

*Present:* His Majesty King [[per:44540|Khuldun]], presiding; the Lord Chancellor; the Lord Treasurer; the Archpriest of the royal chapel; the Keeper of the Seal; the Master of Requests. *Absent, excused by reason of illness:* the Marshal. *Minutes kept by the undersigned, secretary to the Council.*

**I.** The Council having assembled after the morning office, His Majesty opened the sitting by observing that it was the first at which he presided upon matters of religion, and that he desired the Council to remember his late brother of blessed memory, under whose authority the synod had been summoned. The Councillors stood in silence for the space of a short prayer.

**II. Report upon the synod of last year.** The Archpriest, being called upon, read his report upon the [[evt:45545|Council of Zhinontunu]], held in this city the year past under the protection of the Crown. He stated, in substance:

1. That the doctors and bishops of the [[mov:58|Vishithmikh]] had sat for the full term appointed, and had heard the delegates of [[pol:1656|Anakhi]] at length upon every article in dispute.

2. That the chief article concerned the ordering of the priesthood, and whether the authority of ordination descends solely through the established succession or may be conferred by the assent of a congregation.

3. That the delegates of Anakhi would not subscribe to the form proposed, notwithstanding three successive revisions, each milder than the last; and that they departed without taking leave of the presiding bishop.

4. That, in his judgement, the synod had therefore failed of its principal object, which was the reconciliation of Anakhi to the common communion.

The Lord Chancellor asked whether the failure was owing to the obstinacy of the Anakhi or to the drafting of the articles. The Archpriest replied that he would not presume to apportion blame before God, who alone judges at the end of all things; but that he had himself read every draft, and found nothing in them that a reasonable conscience could not accept.

**III. Of the news from Anakhi.** The Keeper of the Seal laid before the Council letters received within the week, reporting that a separate communion has been proclaimed in Anakhi under a reforming preacher, which men there already call the [[evt:45546|Shilesh Schism]]. The Council heard the letters read. His Majesty remarked that it was a grief, but not a surprise.

**IV. Debate.** The Lord Treasurer observed that the synod had cost the Crown a sum beyond the estimate, in lodging, provision and escorts, at a time when grain stands near two [[cur:1057|Lodhu]] and two-thirds the measure in the city markets; and he questioned whether a second synod could be afforded. The Master of Requests answered that a schism unhealed would cost more in the end than any synod, since disputes of religion are seldom confined to theology but pass soon into tolls, marriages and frontiers.

The Archpriest urged that the teaching of the Vishithmikh, being addressed to all peoples, cannot rest content while any people is divided from it; but that compassion, which is the rule of the faithful life, forbids constraint of conscience by arms. The Lord Chancellor dissented in part, holding that compassion and prudence are not opposites, and that a kingdom may lawfully guard its own peace.

**V. Resolutions.** After debate, His Majesty resolved, with the advice of the Council:

1. That no force be used against Anakhi on account of religion.

2. That the Archpriest prepare a letter to the doctors of [[pol:1656|Anakhi]], in temperate terms, leaving the door open to further conference.

3. That the Lord Treasurer render an exact account of the costs of the synod at the next sitting.

4. That these minutes be entered in the register and sealed.

The Council rose at the second hour after noon.

*Read and approved in the King's presence. Signed for the Council by the secretary.*

*[Endorsed in the hand of His Majesty:]* Seen. — [[per:44540|Khuldun]].
