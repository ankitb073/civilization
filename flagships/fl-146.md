**THE CHARTER OF MYIZISHIE**

*Granted by the Republic of [[pol:1656|Ane]], in Assembly at [[set:7|Ane]], under the presidency of Citizen [[per:44689|Mishösmof]]; given on the 28th day of Yuive, in the year 4163 of the [[mov:25|Myitiyishiu]].*

**Preamble.** Whereas the people of Ane, having in [[evt:45287|the Revolution]] of six years past put off the absolute rule of kings and taken the government upon themselves, have found that liberty, like any other inheritance, must be husbanded if it is to be enjoyed; and whereas the price of bread in the capital has risen so far that a measure of grain now costs above two [[cur:1038|Withe]], and the poor of the city are many and the fields about it few; and whereas there lies in the country of [[reg:16|Yite]], upon the coast, a tract of good land with a sheltered harbour, which for above a hundred years has acknowledged no lord and paid no tax to any state; the Assembly has resolved that a colony of free citizens be planted there, and has ordained the following articles for its government.

Let no one object, as some in the Assembly objected, that the Republic, being at war, cannot spare men for ploughs. The army has shown these twelve months what the citizens of Ane can do in the field; it is the opinion of this Assembly that they can do as much in the furrow. [[evt:45326|The present war]] will end as wars end; the colony is intended to outlast it.

**Article I. Name.** The town shall be called [[set:323|Myizishie]], and the country belonging to it the Colony of Myizishie.

**Article II. Allegiance.** The colonists remain citizens of Ane in every respect, owe obedience to the Assembly, and shall send two delegates to sit in it, with voice but without vote, until the colony shall number ten thousand free souls.

**Article III. Government.** The colony shall be administered by a Governor named by the President, for three years, and by a Council of seven elected yearly by the householders. No ordinance of the Governor shall stand which the Council has refused three times.

**Article IV. Land.** Every head of a household who goes out with the first company shall receive forty measures of arable land, free of rent for seven years; thereafter he shall pay one tenth of the produce, in kind or in Withe at the market price. No single person shall hold more than four such portions, the Assembly holding that a colony of great estates is a kingdom in disguise.

**Article V. Religion.** The priests of the Myitiyishiu shall be maintained at the public charge, and the rites performed in due order. As that faith teaches justice in dealings and moderation in all things, the Governor shall punish usury, engrossing of grain and false measure with particular severity.

**Article VI. The inhabitants.** Such of the ancient inhabitants of the country as remain shall keep the lands they actually till and the customs they actually observe. No colonist shall take their land except by purchase before the Council, nor their persons by any means whatever.

**Article VII. Trade.** The harbour shall be free to the ships of Ane without duty. The ships of other nations shall pay the duty fixed in the capital, except in years of dearth, when grain shall enter free.

**Article VIII. Defence.** Every colonist between eighteen and fifty shall keep a firearm and train upon the green once a month. The Republic shall maintain one company of regular foot at Myizishie for the first five years.

**Article IX. Amendment.** This charter may be amended by the Assembly alone, upon petition of the Council or of its own motion, but never so as to diminish the rights in Articles II, IV and VI.

**Sealing.** In witness whereof the President has set his hand and the seal of the Republic, this being [[evt:45337|the planting of the colony]] and [[evt:45338|the founding of the town]], and the clerk of the Assembly has entered it in the register of laws.

*Mishösmof, President. Countersigned by the Clerk.*
