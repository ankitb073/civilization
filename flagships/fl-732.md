*[Obverse]*

In the 9th year of [[per:3138|Tvinon]], lord of [[set:100|Kvile]].  
Before the judges at the gate of the house of [[dei:83|Shada]], the sky, the servant woman of the house of the oil-merchant stood, and spoke, and swore.

She swore by Shada, the sky, who sees.  
She swore by [[dei:87|Shutsufi]], lady of the house below, who takes the false swearer.  
She said:

> I am the servant of the house of the oil-merchant, by the well of the old gate. I was in the house in the year the men of [[pol:105|Bäni]] came.

> The men of Bäni came in the month of the threshing. They came over the fields. They were many. They were like locusts on the barley. Our men on the wall were few. I saw them from the roof: not thirty men. The lord Tvinon was with them. They fought at the gate. By evening our men were fewer.

> My master said: go down. My master said: go down into the cellar with the jars. I went down. I took the child of my master, the small girl, with me. My master and my mistress went to the house of the god. I did not see them again.

> In the cellar it was dark. There were the oil jars, the big jars, forty jars. I put the child behind the jars. I put a sack over the child. I said: do not cry. She did not cry.

> Above us the men of Bäni came into the house. They broke the door and the chests. They took the bronze lamps and the cloth. They came to the cellar door. One came down with a torch. He saw the jars. He called up: oil. Another called down: leave it, we have no carts. He went up. Shada, the sky, shut his eyes. Shutsufi did not take us.

> We were in the cellar two days and a night. We drank oil from my fingers. The smoke came down from the street. On the third day the street was quiet. I went up. The house was empty. The house of the neighbour was burning.

*[Reverse]*

> The judges ask: where is the silver of my master, the silver of the strongbox? I say: I did not take it. The men of Bäni took it. The strongbox lay in the court, its lid broken, like every box in the lane. Ask the neighbours.

> The judges ask: where is the oil? I say: the oil is in the jars. Forty jars. Count them. I did not sell one jar. The oil of my master is a hundred [[cur:71|Lyino]] and more. If I were a thief, would I sit with a hundred Lyino in the dark and drink from my fingers?

> My master came back on the fourth day. They found the child alive. My master said: you are not a servant, you are a sister. That was the day of the [[evt:2794|Sack of Khuele]]. Now it is two years after. Now his brother says I stole the silver. I did not steal it.

> After that came the [[evt:2813|Siege of Khuele]], and the [[evt:2814|Treaty of Khuele]]. The criers say the city gives tribute to Bäni for twenty and seven years. Barley is a little less than one Lyino the measure, and who has a Lyino? Everyone in the city is poor now. My master's brother is poor; that is why he says I stole.

So she spoke. So she swore by Shada and by Shutsufi.  
The child of the oil-merchant, a girl of nine years, stood before the judges and said: she put a sack over me, she said do not cry.  
The judges said: the woman is clean. Let the brother pay the fee of the judges.  
Written at the gate of the house of Shada, in the 9th year of Tvinon.
