*[Obverse.]*

To my son, who keeps the flocks in the hills above the river, say:  
thus says [[per:633|Natpimttumin]], your father, lord of [[set:61|Tsinlampiba]].

May [[dei:33|Nabudzin]], father of the river, keep you.  
May [[dei:35|Dumnu]] of the storm turn the storm away from you.  
May [[dei:36|Nidziktti]] of the grain fill your bowl.

Hear what has come upon your father's house. Hear it and do not weep, or weep and then stand up.

The men of [[pol:22|Birnadu]] came down into the land of the reeds. They came with spears, they came with shields, they came with the ram of cedar. Since the beginning of the [[evt:532|Virnado–Tsinlampiba War]] they had come, year after year, like the locust. This year they came again, more than a thousand of them.

Our men went out to meet them before the walls. Three hundreds and more went out, the young men and the old men, the potters and the fishers. At the [[evt:550|Battle of Zhinlampive]] they stood. They stood until the sun was high. The men of Birnadu fell, many of them fell. But they were many and we were few. Our line was broken. Our men came running back to the gate, and the gate could not be shut behind them.

Then came [[evt:551|the sack of Zhinlampive]]. I will tell it to you as I saw it.

They came in at the river gate. They came in at the gate of the potters. The smoke stood up over the city like a black tree. They broke the doors of the houses. They took the silver from the jars, they took the [[cur:13|Bilu]] from the strongroom, they took the oxen from the stalls and the bronze from the altars. The house of [[dei:37|Novku]] they did not spare. The wise god's door they burned.

My son, the house of tablets is burned. The tablets of my father, and of his father, and of the fathers before him: the tablets of the fields, the tablets of the oaths, the tablets of the gifts to the gods — the roof fell in on them, the fire went over them, the shelves broke, and they are broken. Who now will say whose field is whose? Who now will read the oaths that the gods heard? I stood in the ash with my staff, and I picked up a piece, and the writing on it was half gone.

I am alive. Your mother is alive. The women of the household are alive; we hid them in the reed-boats under the sacks. Of the household, four servants are dead. The old scribe is dead; he would not leave the tablets. May [[dei:38|Shedartsi]] of the house below receive him kindly. He kept the words of this city all his life.

*[Reverse.]*

Now hear my word to you. Do not come down to the city. The men of Birnadu are still in the land. Keep the flocks in the hills. Count them, and send me word of their number by the next herdsman. If the men of Birnadu come up into the hills, drive the flocks into the high valley and do not fight.

I am lord of [[pol:23|Tsinlampiba]] in the second year only. My hand is not yet strong. But I have sworn before Nabudzin and before [[dei:34|Tselni]] of the moon that this city will not lie down. We will clear the ash. We will raise the walls. We will set up the altars again and pour the right sacrifice. The wheel turns; what has fallen will rise.

Tell the priests of [[mov:5|Lelmi]] in the hill shrine: let them pour for the dead of Tsinlampiba, for the young men and the old men who stood before the walls. Let them pour water and beer and oil.

Send me barley if you have it, and send me wool. The people are hungry. Send me your news. Write it on good clay, and keep a copy, for the house of tablets is no more.

*[Colophon, on the edge.]* In the 2nd year of Natpimttumin. Sealed with the seal of the lord.
