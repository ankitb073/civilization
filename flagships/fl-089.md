*The words that [[per:18406|Tonofmalen]] spoke at the gate of [[set:6|Jenontono]], in the 28th year of Belton, on the day the first disciples were set apart. Written down by one who heard them, that they be not lost.*

Hear me, people of Jenontono. Hear me, men of [[pol:775|Tobejenga]]. Hear me, you who carry water, and you who carry spears, and you who carry nothing because you have nothing left to carry.

You have been told that all things turn on a wheel. You have been told: what goes down will come up; what ends will begin again. So the priests of [[mov:2|Jenro]] say. So their fathers said. Now hear what I say. The wheel is a lie told to the comfortable.

Remember the war. Remember it, though it is twenty years and more behind us.

At [[evt:18194|Baaliurinu]] our sons went out, and they went down.  
At [[evt:18207|Puthnimdu]] the king went out, and he went down.  
At [[evt:18220|Zhinontunu]], under these walls, at this gate where I stand, the king went out again, and he went down again.  
At [[evt:18239|Pikhtunge]] our sons went out once more, and they went down once more.

I was a young man in those years. I carried water to the wounded under this wall. I gave water to a boy who had no legs, and he asked me: which god did I not feed? I could not answer him. He died with the cup at his mouth.

Then came [[evt:18240|the treaty]]. Then the silver went out of this city, sack after sack of [[cur:352|neleth]], to the enemy who had beaten us. And the priests said: the gods were angry; give more. And we gave more. We gave to [[dei:14|Volme]], and she did not fight for us. We gave to [[dei:16|Domamnol]], and he did not see us. We gave to [[dei:17|Nedhekhte]], and the bread grew dear. The priests said: prosperity is the sign of favour. Then look at us. Whom do the gods favour? The fat man in the temple, and the enemy beyond the hills.

Now hear the word that was given to me.

There is One who is above the gods. Volme is His servant, and Domamnol is His servant, and Nedhekhte and [[dei:15|Venba]] are His servants, as the steward is the servant of the house. They are not wicked. They are small.

And time is not a wheel. Time is a road. It began, and it goes forward, and at the end of the road the One sits in judgement. He does not ask: how many oxen did you burn? He asks: whom did you lift up from the road? He asks: did you give water to the boy with no legs? He asks: whom did you release from suffering, and whom did you leave in it?

This is the whole of it. Mercy, and not sacrifice. Release, and not the wheel.

And hear this also, for it is hard. The word is not for Tobejenga alone. It is for the enemy who took our silver. It is for the men of the hills and the men of the sea. The One made them also, and will judge them also, and they too must hear. Go to them. Go without spears.

I do not speak against [[per:18034|Belton]] our king. He stood in the field when others stood behind walls. He signed the treaty so that the killing would stop. Let him rule. But let him know that above his throne there is a judgement seat, and he too will stand before it, as the water-carrier will stand.

Now I lay my hands on these who stand before me. They have left their fields and their houses. They will keep the word, and teach it, and lay their hands on others after them, in order, from mouth to mouth, so that the word is not changed. This is [[mov:58|Veshethmekh]]: the road and not the wheel. Today, at this gate, [[evt:18520|it begins]].

Go out from here, and give water.
