*[Obverse.]*

I sing to [[dei:35|Dumnu]], lord of the storm, who rides the black cloud!  
I sing to [[dei:33|Nabudzin]], the river, father of the fields!  
I sing to [[dei:34|Tselni]], the moon, who counts the months of the march!  
Hear, O gods of [[pol:14|Egu]], hear the song of your servants.

The men of [[pol:22|Birnadu]] were proud, their necks were stiff.  
They did not bring the sheep, they did not bring the silver,  
they did not bow down at the gate of Egu.  
Then Egu said: "Let them bring the sheep. Let them bring the silver.  
Let them bow down at the gate." And Dumnu heard, and Egu went out.  
They went down into [[reg:7|Tunnga]], the land of the reeds,  
they went down to Zhinlampive, the city of the ford.

**The first song of the battle.**  
At the [[evt:559|Second Battle of Zhinlampive]] Dumnu thundered.  
The men of Birnadu stood, and then the men of Birnadu fell,  
two hundreds less a few they fell, and Egu held the ford.

**The second song of the battle.**  
At the [[evt:564|Battle of Zhinlampive]] in the year that followed,  
the men of Birnadu were few, and they were fewer at sunset.  
Two hundreds of them lay down in the reeds. Of ours, few.

**The third song of the battle.**  
At the [[evt:566|Battle of Virnadhu]] the storm turned away.  
Why did Dumnu turn away? Dumnu turned away to test the hand of Egu.  
Our men lay in the mud, seven hundreds lay in the mud,  
and [[dei:38|Shedartsi]] of the dark house took them in.  
We wept. We poured water. We went out again.

**The fourth song of the battle.**  
At the [[evt:576|Battle of Zhinlampive]] two great hosts met,  
two thousands against two thousands, spear against spear.  
Two hundreds and more of Birnadu fell there, and Egu held the ford.

**The fifth song of the battle.**  
At Zhinlampive again the gods hid their faces.  
Our men lay down, many of them lay down, and the river was red.  
We wept. We poured water. We went out again.

**The sixth song of the battle.**  
At the [[evt:581|Battle of Zhinlampive]] in the first year of the king,  
host met host, and the reeds were trampled flat.  
Seven hundreds and more of Birnadu fell, and their shields lay in the reeds.

**The seventh song of the battle.**  
At the [[evt:583|Battle of Zhinlampive]] the last of their strength was spent.  
Three hundreds of them fell, and the rest threw down the spear.  
Then the men of Birnadu bowed down. They bowed down at the ford.

*[Reverse.]*

Seven times Egu went down into Tunnga.  
Five times Dumnu gave Egu the field. Twice Dumnu tested the hand of Egu.  
The hand of Egu did not fail. The hand of Egu did not fail.  
[[per:761|Lulrinraman]] the king, strong of arm, whom the storm loves,  
he too went out in this war, he too stood in the line of Egu.

At the ford, in [[set:61|Tsinlampiba]] by the water,  
the [[evt:584|Treaty of Zhinlampive]] was sworn before the gods.  
Birnadu swore by its gods, Egu swore by Nabudzin and by Dumnu.  
Now Birnadu brings the sheep. Now Birnadu brings the oxen.  
Now Birnadu brings the silver, weighed out like the [[cur:14|Niba]],  
year by year it brings, at the new moon of Tselni it brings.  
Now the gate of Egu is full, now the storehouse is full.

Who gave the field? Dumnu of the storm gave the field.  
Who gave the water on the march? Nabudzin, the river, gave it.  
Who gave the bread to the host? [[dei:36|Nidziktti]] of the grain gave it.  
Who gave counsel to the king? [[dei:37|Novku]] of wisdom gave counsel.  
Who took in our dead? Shedartsi took them in, and they rest.

As the wheel turns, so the strong are raised and the proud are bowed.  
The wheel has turned for Egu. Let the priests of [[mov:5|Lelmi]] give thanks,  
let them burn the fat, let them pour the beer, let them sing this song.

*[Colophon.]*  
Written in the 3rd year of Lulrinraman, king of Egu.  
Whoever erases this song, may Dumnu strike his house.  
Whoever sings this song, may Nabudzin water his field.
