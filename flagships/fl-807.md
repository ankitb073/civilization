*[Obverse.]*

Say to my mother, in the lane of the weavers in [[set:197|Evun]]: thus says your son, of the queen's band.

May [[dei:17|Niedhekhte]], lady of the grain, fill your jar. May [[dei:19|Yienan]] of the moon keep your door at night. May Bienva of the earth hold my father softly, where he lies. I am alive. I am alive. Read it twice.

Hear what was. You know we are fewer. When the lords of Evun stood up against [[pol:1139|Lienunushal]] and said, we will not bow, and so began the [[evt:30520|Second Lienonoshal Civil War]], they did not have spears enough. The great host stayed with Lienunushal. Only the sworn men came to us, and the sons of the sworn men. I was one. You wept. I went.

Three summers ago our lady [[per:30189|Niefputh]] led us to [[set:250|Rueren]]. You know how it ended. She lay down there, and many thousands with her, and the field was theirs. We brought her shield back to Evun, and you saw it carried through the lane.

This summer we went to Rueren again, all the host of Evun, and the queen's band with it. We of the band were twenty and four. Mother, I tell you the truth: twenty and four. Our captain was [[per:30217|Ranathven]]. Against us came the whole host of Lienunushal, with their lady Nuernenukhueth in the midst of them, many as the grains of sand in the river, many as the stars of Duemamnul. When they came over the low hill the ground shook. The boy beside me made water where he stood. I did not laugh at him. I nearly did the same.

*[Reverse.]*

We stood in the narrow place between the two ditches, where only a few can come at once. Ranathven said: here they must come to us by tens, not by thousands. And they came. They came all day. We killed, Mother. We killed until our arms would not lift. They lay in the ditches so thick that their own men walked on them. I do not know how many. More than I can count. More than our village.

But at evening they came around by the stream, behind us. Four of the band lay down then. Ranathven lay down, with three spears in him, at the end of the [[evt:30566|Battle of Rurin]]. The rest of us went back through the reeds in the dark. The field is theirs again.

So we are twenty, now, and the queen [[per:30199|Natunuernenath]] has sent word that we are heroes. The heralds say it in the square, I am told. I do not feel a hero. I feel hungry. There is no barley in this camp but what we beg from the villages, and the villages of Diebeguvebenga do not love us, for they say every army eats them, ours and theirs.

They say also that other enemies have risen against Evun on other sides, the men of Vieneynga and of Birnathu and of Kungaruishrimu. I do not know where those places are. I know there are not enough of us.

Mother, do not sell the loom. Do not sell the goat. If you must sell something, sell my good cloak; I will not need it before winter, and if I need it in winter I will have another, or I will not need it at all.

Pour water for Buevme, lady of war, at the house shrine, every new moon. Pour it for Ranathven too. He has no mother in Evun.

Your son, one of the twenty.
