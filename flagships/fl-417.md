*[Obverse. The heading is written in larger signs.]*

**Song for the [[evt:116|accession of Nurninpansheth]], to be sung at the gate, at the altar, and at the seat.**

*At the gate, by the singers:*

Open, O gate of [[set:0|Tallin]], open your two doors!  
Open, O gate of the city, the lady is coming in.  
Who is coming in? The daughter of the king is coming in.  
Who is coming in? The queen of Tallin is coming in.  
Open, O gate, open your two doors.

*At the altar, by the priests:*

Hear, O [[dei:16|Dumamnul]], lord of the high sky, who sees the roads.  
Hear, O [[dei:15|Vinba]], mother of the black earth, who holds the dead.  
Hear, O [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]], lady of the grain, who fills the storehouse.  
Hear, O [[dei:18|Eva]], lord of the fire, who keeps the hearth.  
Hear, O [[dei:19|Yenan]], lamp of the night, who counts the months.  
Hear, O [[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the spear, who eats the shields.  
All the gods of [[pol:1|Tallin]], each in your place, each with your due, hear.

The king has lain down. [[per:1|Baliun]] has lain down.  
Baliun, the strong bull, the high wall, the shepherd of the city,  
he sat upon the seat from the beginning; thirty-nine years he sat.  
Thirty-nine harvests he gathered; thirty-nine times he poured the beer at the new year.  
Now he has gone down to Vinba. Vinba, keep him well.  
We have put the bronze beside him. We have put the bread beside him.  
We have shut the door. Vinba, keep him well.

The wheel turns. What ends will begin again.  
The father has ended; in the daughter he begins again.

*At the seat, by the singers and the people:*

Here is [[per:59|Nurninpansheth]], daughter of Baliun.  
She has the eyes of her father. She has the hand of her father.  
Put the staff in her hand. She takes the staff.  
Put the cloak on her shoulders. She takes the cloak.  
Put the bronze band on her brow. She takes the band.  
Let her sit. She sits.  
She sits upon the seat of Tallin. She is the queen.

*The people answer:* She is the queen. She is the queen.

May Dumamnul give her rain in its season.  
May Nidhikhti give her barley to the knee.  
May Eva keep her hearth from cold.  
May Yenan count her months as many as the stars.  
May Vulme give her spears that do not break  
and enemies whose spears do break.  
May Vinba give her a long road before she goes down,  
a long, long road before she goes down.

She will give the gods their due; she will not forget the sacrifice.  
She will give the priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] their place; she will not forget the order.  
She will give the widow bread; she will not forget the widow.  
She will keep the boundary stones; she will not move the boundary stones.  
This she has sworn. This the gods have heard.

*At the gate again, by the singers:*

Shut, O gate of Tallin, shut your two doors.  
The queen is in her house. The city is in her hand.  
Shut, O gate, and let no enemy open you.  
Shut, O gate, and let the queen go out and come in in peace.

*[Lower edge. Colophon.]*

> Written in the 1st year of Nurninpansheth, queen of Tallin, for the house of the singers.  
> Whoever breaks this tablet, may Vulme break his shield.
