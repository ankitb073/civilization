*[Obverse]*

By [[dei:77|Pugazhu]] the sun, who sees all the land. By [[dei:73|Tidu]] of the storm, who breaks the oak. By [[dei:76|Tsilmu]] of wisdom, who teaches the teachers. By [[dei:74|Binge]], mother of the barley. By [[dei:75|Bumbi]], lady of the sea. Hear it.

Thus says [[per:135|Nakidi]], king of [[pol:3|Tagath]], son of the queen, in the first year of his seat. Thus says the king by the mouth of his regent, by the mouth of his scribe, by the mouth of the herald at the gate. Hear it, men of the city. Hear it, men of the fields. Hear it, men of the boats.

In the year before this year, the queen, the mother of the king, made a new thing in [[set:9|Tagath]]. Before, Tagath was a city only, and the queen sat as the first among the house of the elders. The elders sat in the gate. The elders quarrelled in the gate. One elder said "dig," another said "do not dig," and the canal was not dug. One elder said "build," another said "do not build," and the wall was not built.

The queen said: "Enough. Let there be one seat. Let there be one staff. Let the staff pass from the mother to the son, from the father to the son, as the barley passes from the seed to the ear." And the elders bowed. And the priests of [[mov:11|Kyif]] looked at the smoke, and the smoke went up straight. That is the [[evt:168|Kingdom Reform in Tagath]]. That is the new thing. The city is a kingdom.

In this year the queen went down to her fathers. The gods took her. The people wept. And the staff passed to her son, as she had said. The king is young. His years are ten. Therefore a regent holds the staff beside him until his arm is strong. But the seat is his. The seat is the king's.

*[Reverse]*

Now hear the words of the seat.

First: the house of the elders is not destroyed. The elders sit at the gate still. They give counsel. The king hears counsel. But the king says the word, and the word is done.

Second: every field pays to the seat one sheaf in ten. The sheaf goes to the storehouse of the king. From the storehouse the soldiers eat. From the storehouse the widows eat in the year of hunger.

Third: every man of the city gives to the seat one month in the year. He digs the canal. He builds the wall. He rows the king's boat. In that month the king feeds him.

Fourth: the measures of the market are the measures of the king. The cup of barley is the king's cup. The weight of copper is the king's weight. Whoever uses a false cup, his hand is struck. Whoever uses a false weight, his door is shut.

Fifth: the teachers are honoured. The king honours the teachers of Kyif. Let them teach in the court of the temple. No one owns the truth; let them dispute, but not with knives.

Sixth: the gods are fed first. Before the king eats, the gods eat. To Pugazhu the first loaf. To Tidu the first ox. To Binge the first sheaf. To Bumbi the first fish of the boats. To Tsilmu the first tablet of the scribes.

*[Lower edge]*

All things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again. The house of the elders ended; the seat of the king begins. Let it not end.

Whoever breaks this edict, may Tidu strike his roof. May Binge dry his field. May Bumbi sink his boat.

Written in the first year of Nakidi, king of Tagath. Set up at the gate. Read aloud at the new moon.
