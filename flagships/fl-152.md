To [[per:6688|Bedun]], king of [[pol:277|Tsinondonu]], my lord, say:

Thus says [[per:6689|Kathkhuknakhen]], your servant, the wheelwright of the river gate. At the feet of my lord seven times and seven times I fall. May [[dei:55|Dumamnul]] of the sky keep my lord. May [[dei:58|Tsithal]] of war strengthen the arm of my lord.

My lord knows the trouble of the city. Last year the gods gave us walls; that was the [[evt:6400|Founding of Tsinondonu]]. In the same year the men of Tallin came against us; that was the [[evt:6414|Tallin–Tsinondonu War]]. This year my lord went out with two hundred and fifty men against the men of Tallin, who were as many as the sand of the river. That was the [[evt:6422|Battle of Zhinontunu]]. Nearly a hundred of ours fell. My lord came back into the city. Now the men of Tallin sit around the walls. That is the [[evt:6423|Siege of Zhinontunu]]. Their fires are many at night. Our men are few.

Now hear, my lord. I have made a thing that was not before.

My lord knows the cart of the farmers. It has four wheels. The wheels are solid wood, cut from the trunk. The oxen drag it. It is slow. It is heavy. A man walking goes faster.

I have made a cart that runs. It has two wheels, not four. The wheels are not solid. I have made them with a rim, and with spokes like the rays of the sun, six spokes to a wheel, so that the wheel is light. The rim is bent ash wood, bound with rawhide while it is wet. The axle is under the floor. The floor is woven leather, so it bends and does not break. A pole goes forward from the floor. Two horses are yoked to the pole, not oxen. Horses run. Oxen walk.

Two men stand in the cart. One holds the reins. One holds the bow. The cart runs along the line of the enemy. The bowman shoots. The cart turns. The cart runs back. The enemy on foot cannot catch it. The enemy's spear cannot reach it. One cart with two men is worth twenty men on foot.

I have made one cart. I drove it on the threshing floor inside the walls, before the elders. The elders saw it. The horses ran. The cart did not break. The elders said: "This is a thing that was not before." Let my lord ask them.

My lord, give me wood. Give me ash wood and elm wood. Give me rawhide. Give me bronze for the hubs and the pins. Give me six men of the carpenters and four men of the leather-workers. Give me the horses of the palace. In one month I will make ten carts. When the men of Tallin come against the gate, let ten carts go out, and let twenty bowmen stand in them. The men of Tallin have never seen such a thing. They will be afraid.

My lord, I am your servant. I ask nothing for myself now. When the gods give my lord the victory, let my lord remember the wheelwright of the river gate. Let my lord give me a field by the river and a house within the walls. Let my lord set up my name in the house of the gods, so that when men drive the running cart they will say: "Kathkhuknakhen made it."

I have given a lamb to [[dei:56|Nabuthin]] of the river. I have given oil to [[dei:57|Bubin]] of the moon. I have poured for [[dei:59|Vinba]] of the earth, so that she holds the wheel. The priests of [[mov:8|Ligay]] read the lamb. The lamb was good.

Let my lord send his answer quickly, by the hand of my messenger. The wood must be cut before the new moon. Let my lord not be slow.

Written in the 2nd year of Bedun, in [[set:6|Tsinondonu]], the city that is besieged.
