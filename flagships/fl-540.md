*[Obverse. The tablet bears, at the top, the impression of a new cylinder seal: a jar, a beam of timber and a sheaf, with the sign for "house" between them.]*

To my son, who is on the road with the oil-jars, say:  
thus says [[per:396|Makhtenuth]], your mother, the merchant of the harbour-street of [[set:18|Lenunushal]].

With me it is well. With the storehouse it is well. With the boats it is well.  
With the timber it is well; the beams came down from the hills before the rains.  
May it be well with you. May it be well with the donkeys. May no jar be broken.

Hear, my son. Listen with both ears. A great thing is done.

On the day of the new moon I went up to the house of [[dei:16|Dumamnul]] of the sky with the elders of the harbour-street. I took a white kid. I took a jar of the first oil. I took a sheaf of the first barley. I gave them to the god. And before the god and before the elders I swore: this is a house. Not a shop, not a stall, not a woman with a donkey. A house. The house of [[hou:2|Kushin]].

Its seal is cut. You see it on this tablet. The jar for the oil, the beam for the timber, the sheaf for the grain. Every tablet of the house from this day bears it. Whoever trades with the seal of Kushin trades with the whole house, with me, with you, with the sons of your sons. That is the [[evt:337|Founding of the House of Kushin]], as the scribe of the king's steward has written it in his list.

For I went also to the steward of the king. I bowed before the seat of [[per:366|Pufnojeyshan]], king of [[pol:10|Lenunushal]]. I gave the king's gift: ten jars of oil, ten beams, ten measures of barley. The steward wrote the house in his list. Now the king knows our name. Now the tax-gatherer knows our name. Do not grumble at it. A house the king knows is a house the king protects.

Hear, my son, why I have done this.

When I was a girl of fifteen there came the year of the [[evt:285|Usurpation of Evemtan I]], when the seat changed hands and no one knew whose law would hold. In that year the stall of my mother's brother was taken and his debts were forgotten, because he was one man, and a man alone has no name that lasts. I swore then: I will make a name that lasts longer than one man. Now I have made it.

These are the words of the house. Keep them.  
Give the true measure; the god sees the false one.  
Give the good oil on top and the good oil underneath.  
Pay the boatmen on the day you promised, not the day after.  
Lend to the man you know. Do not lend to the man who swears too much.  
Count everything twice. Write everything once.  
Pour to [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]] of the grain at harvest, to [[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire at the hearth, to [[dei:19|Yenan]] of the moon when you set out by night,  
to [[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the spear, when the road is not safe,  
and to [[dei:15|Vinba]] of the earth when you buy a field. Each god in their place, each with their due, as the priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] teach. The god that is forgotten forgets.

You are twenty-one summers. You are a man. When I lie down, the house will be yours, and the seal will be yours, and your name will be Kushin before it is your own. Learn the names of the boatmen. Learn the price of oil in every market. Learn to sit silent while the other man talks.

Come home before the rains. Bring back the empty jars; they cost money.

All things turn on the wheel. What ends will begin again. I will end. The house will begin again, and again.

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> Written in Lenunushal, in the 10th year of Pufnojeyshan, under the new seal of the house.
