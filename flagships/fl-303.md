*[Obverse.]*

Praise to [[dei:14|Vulme]], lord of the spear, lord of the red field!  
Praise to [[dei:16|Dumamnul]], the high sky, who sees every road!  
Praise to [[dei:15|Vinba]], the broad earth, who drinks what is poured!  
Hear the song of [[pol:1|Tallin]], O gods, hear it and be glad.

The men of [[pol:10|Lenunushal]] sat in the land of [[reg:9|Tupujinga]],  
they sat by the wells, they sat by the fords, they sat on the roads of the donkeys.  
Since the days of the [[evt:193|First Tuvizhinge Trade War]] they sat there,  
and the bread of Tallin was small, and the [[cur:4|Niga]] lay idle in the jar.  
Then Tallin lifted its hand to Vulme, lord of the spear:  
"Vulme, see the shut wells. Vulme, see the shut roads. Open them with the spear."  
And Vulme heard. And the spears went up.

They went up in the first year, they went up in the second year,  
they went up in the third year, they went up in the fourth year,  
the men of Tallin went up, and their bronze shone like the river at noon.

*[A line is damaged.]* […] at the [[evt:237|Battle of Taulin]] the spears were broken.  
At Taulin the dust stood up like a wall, and the sun did not see us.  
Our men lay down in the dust, nine hundreds and more they lay down,  
and the women of Tallin cut their hair, and the oil was not poured.  
Why did the god turn away? The god turned away to try us.  
As the smith tries the bronze in the fire of [[dei:18|Eva]], so the god tried us.  
We gave the sheep, we gave the oxen, we gave the first of the barley to [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]].  
And Vulme turned again. And the spears went up.

At the [[evt:243|Battle of Tathriubu]] they met us, the men of Lenunushal,  
there they met us with the shield, there they met us with the axe.  
But Vulme went before us, and Dumamnul thundered over us,  
and the men of Lenunushal fell down, seven hundreds of them fell down,  
and the rest ran like goats into the hills.  
Their shields we took, their axes we took, their oxen we drove home.

*[Reverse.]*

Then the men of Tallin came to Linnshal, the walled city.  
They sat down before its gate in the [[evt:244|First Siege of Linnshal]].  
They sat while [[dei:19|Yenan]] the moon grew full, they sat while Yenan grew thin,  
the people of Linnshal ate their seed, the people of Linnshal ate their leather.  
Then the men of Tallin rose up, four thousands and three hundreds and more,  
against them came out the men of Linnshal, a thousand and two hundreds less a few.  
In the [[evt:249|Battle of Linnshal]] Vulme put the spear in our hand,  
Vulme put the shield on our arm, Vulme put the shout in our mouth.  
The gate was broken. The wall was climbed. The city was opened.

At the [[evt:250|First Sack of Linnshal]] the fire went up to the sky,  
the silver came out of their houses, the grain came out of their houses,  
their cattle came out, their bronze came out, all of it came to Tallin.  
The men of Lenunushal bowed down, they bowed their faces to the earth,  
they swore by their gods, they swore by ours, the [[evt:251|Treaty of Linnshal]] was sworn.  
Now the wells of Tupujinga are ours, the fords are ours, the roads are ours.  
Now the donkeys go out, now the donkeys come home with silver.

Who opened the roads? Vulme, lord of the spear, opened them.  
Who gave the rain on the march? Dumamnul, the high sky, gave it.  
Who held up our feet? Vinba, the broad earth, held them up.  
Who fed the host? Nidhikhti of the barley fed the host.  
As the great wheel turns, so the land has turned back to Tallin.  
What was ours is ours again. What was taken is given back.

To the dead of Taulin, water. To the dead of Tathriubu, water.  
To the dead before Linnshal, water and bread and oil.  
Let the priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] pour for them, every year let them pour.

*[Colophon.]*  
This song was set in clay in the 1st year of [[per:196|Kulufpimtuth]], queen of Tallin,  
in her house it was sung, before the gods it was sung.  
Whoever breaks this tablet, may Vulme break his spear.  
Whoever keeps this tablet, may Nidhikhti fill his jar.
