To [[per:18981|Benufmalin]], the king, my lord, lord of [[pol:779|Yide]], say: thus says [[per:19223|Bethin]], your servant, who dwells in [[set:10|Navnge]] by the river gate. May it be well with my lord. May it be well with his house, with his host, with his horses and his granaries.

My lord has heard words about me. Men have come up to the palace and said: "Bethin turns the people from the gods. Bethin says the sacrifices are empty. Bethin will not pay the tribute." Let my lord hear my own words now, and judge.

In the year of [[evt:19234|the battle at Piyikhzhidhe]], when the host of my lord was turned back and the enemy held the field, the wounded were carried into Navnge on carts. I was a scribe in the house of the grain-measurers. I went out to the carts. I gave water. I saw a man whose arm was gone call upon [[dei:14|Vvumi]] of the war, and Vvumi did not answer. I saw a boy call upon his mother, and his mother was far away. That night there was a ring around the moon of [[dei:19|Yenan]], and the priests said it was an omen of more war. But I lay awake, and a word came to me, and it was not a word of war.

This is the word. Above Dumamnul of the sky, above Yenan, above Vvumi and Vinbe and Ive, there is one Power. The gods of [[mov:2|Tsinru]] are its servants, as the governors are the servants of my lord. They are not nothing; but they are not first. And the years do not turn like a wheel, coming round and round to the same place, as the priests have taught. The years run like the river, from the spring to the sea, and at the sea every man is weighed, the king and the carter alike.

What does the Power weigh? It weighs mercy. It weighs the cup of water to the wounded man. It weighs the hand that looses a man from his suffering. The fat of the bull is good, and I do not forbid it; but the bull does not go before the Power to speak for the man who killed it. Mercy goes before it.

And this word is not for Yide alone. It is for the people beyond the river, and for the people of the enemy also, for they too are carried home on carts.

Let my lord not believe that I take the people from his service. Those who hear me pay the tribute; they pay the [[cur:406|ngevu]] at the gate; their sons go into the host when my lord calls. I have not hidden one measure of grain from the tax-gatherers. I teach in order: those who teach in my name are set apart with oil and words, as the priests are set apart, and they say only what was given to them. There is no disorder in my house.

My lord knows that the Power has been kind to Yide since that day. At [[evt:19245|Himi]] my lord himself stood at the head of the host, and the enemy turned back. At [[evt:19277|Rurin]] the enemy turned back. Now in this year at [[evt:19280|Himi]] again the enemy turned back, and the dead of the enemy were many and ours were few. I do not say Vvumi did not help. I say: who sent Vvumi?

Therefore I ask my lord three things. Let the captives of Himi be given bread and not the whip. Let those who follow [[mov:65|Kankhu]] meet by the river gate without being driven off by the temple guards. And let my lord, when he goes up to the house of Dumamnul, remember also the One above Dumamnul, even in silence.

I have written this with my own hand. The brothers who sit with me by the river gate have heard it read and have set their marks beside mine. We number few, but we are not nothing; and on the day this tablet goes up to my lord, [[evt:19356|the house of Kankhu]] will be opened in Navnge, with water for any who are thirsty.

May the Power keep my lord. May his days be long. Written in the 14th year of Benufmalin.
