*[Obverse. Incipit in larger signs.]*

> The [[evt:463|Sack of Zhinontunu]], which I saw with my eyes. I, a scribe of the house of tablets, write it, for the old tablets are broken.

Hear, you who come after. Hear, you who have not seen.

In the days of the war, the [[evt:444|First Tallin–Chinontonu War]], the men of [[pol:1|Tallin]] came down. They came down like the river in flood, they came down like locusts on the barley.

At Puthnimdu the first time we broke them. The [[evt:447|Battle of Puthnimdu]] was ours; the men of Tallin lay in the field, many, many, and we sang at the gate.  
At Puthnimdu the second time the king went out, [[per:504|Dolulrinin]] the king went out, and the gods turned their face. The field was Tallin's. We did not sing at the gate.

Then they came to [[set:6|Chinontonu]], to the walls of Chinontonu. They sat down before the walls. They sat a summer, they sat a winter. They cut the date palms. They cut the channels. The water of [[dei:56|Nabuthin]] did not come into the city. We drank from the old wells, and the old wells were bitter.

In the spring the king went out again. Few were the spears of Chinontonu; few, few, like the teeth of an old man. Many were the spears of Tallin, many as the stars of [[dei:55|Dumamnul]]'s house. I stood on the wall. I saw it. The king went out, and the king came back; the field was Tallin's. Our young men did not come back. That was the [[evt:462|Battle of Zhinontunu]]. Let it not be forgotten.

On the third day they came over the wall at the river gate.  
I saw the ladders. I saw the bronze. I saw the fire.

They went into the house of [[dei:58|Chithal]], lord of war, and threw down his spear from his hand.  
They went into the house of [[dei:60|Shineyta]] of the sea and took her silver bowls.  
They went into the house of tablets, the house of my fathers, the house of the counting and the oaths.  
They put fire to the shelves. They put fire to the wax boards. They put fire to the reed mats.  
The roof beams fell. The tablets fell. The tablets broke on the floor like potsherds.  
The lists of the kings broke. The oaths of the fathers broke. The counting of the barley broke.  
I carried out two baskets. Two baskets only. A soldier of Tallin struck me with the butt of his spear, and I lay down in the street, and I watched the smoke go up to the moon.

[[dei:57|Bubin]] of the moon, you saw it. You were full that night. You did not turn away. Remember it.

After the fire the king sat down with the lord of Tallin. They swore by the gods; I do not write their oath, for it is bitter. That was the [[evt:464|Treaty of Zhinontunu]]. Chinontonu gave land. Chinontonu gave a town. The king of Chinontonu bends his neck to Tallin. Ten years there shall be no war; so they swore.

Now it is the 19th year of [[per:504|Dolulrinin]]. The walls are mended with mud. The channels run again. A measure of barley in the market costs a [[cur:8|Yong]] and a quarter, and the widows cannot pay it. The priests of [[mov:8|Ligay]] pour to the gods each in their place, as it was in the days of our fathers. All things turn on the wheel; what ends will begin again. The house of tablets will be built again, and I will write again the lists of the kings, from my memory, as far as my memory goes.

But I say this, and let it be written first on the new shelf: it was not the gods who were weak. It was the men of Tallin who were many.

[[dei:59|Winba]] of the earth, take our dead into your lap.  
Chithal, lord of war, take back your spear.  
Remember the river gate. Remember the fire. Remember the two baskets.

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> Written in Chinontonu, in the 19th year of Dolulrinin. Whoever breaks this tablet, may Bubin hide her face from him.
