*[Obverse.]*

Hear, O [[dei:48|Kudhze]] of the high sky. Hear, O [[dei:50|Nidhinande]] of the war.  
Hear, O Dalu of the earth, O Bine of the fire, O Bumbi of the sea.  
Hear, O Düvi of the house below, who keeps our dead.  
Hear the words of the men of [[pol:426|Ninzhe]], in the 8th year of Ninzhe.

Thus say the men of Ninzhe.

We were the servants of [[pol:398|Divnüsidhi]].  
We paid the grain to Divnüsidhi. We paid the sheep to Divnüsidhi.  
Our sons carried the spear for Divnüsidhi. Our daughters wove for Divnüsidhi.  
We were not free. The gods saw it. The gods were not pleased.

In the [[evt:9675|Revolt of Ninzhe]] the men of Ninzhe stood up.  
[[per:9953|Nambave]] stood up first, and the men of Ninzhe stood up behind him.  
We said: "Ninzhe is Ninzhe. Ninzhe is not the field of another."  
That was the first year of Ninzhe. From that year we count our years.

Then came the [[evt:9676|Second Ninzhe War of Independence]].  
Six summers it went on. Six winters it went on.

In the second year [[per:9978|Nadazum]] led us against the city.  
At the [[evt:9711|Battle of Divnüzith]] the gods were with us. Their men fell.  
In the [[evt:9712|Second Sack of Divnüzith]] we took back what they took from us.

In the third year, and in the fourth year, the gods tried us.  
At Divnüzith we fell, and at Divnüzith we fell again.  
We poured water for our dead. We did not go home.

In the fifth year [[per:10002|Nakhizhi]] led us.  
At the [[evt:9750|Battle of Divnüzith]] the gods turned again to us.  
We sat down before the walls of Divnüzith, and the walls shook.

In the sixth year Nadazum led us again, and the gods gave us the field.  
In the [[evt:9761|Third Sack of Divnüzith]] the gate of the proud city was opened.  
Then the men of Divnüsidhi bowed. They swore the [[evt:9762|Treaty of Divnüzith]].  
They swore before the gods: Ninzhe is free. Ninzhe is its own.  
They swore it, and the gods heard it. The gods heard it.

*[Reverse.]*

Then the stranger came against us.  
In the [[evt:9764|First Sack of Alunzhi]] the stranger came into our city.  
At the [[evt:9771|Battle of Alunzhi]] Nakhizhi stood before the gate, and he could not hold it.  
The stranger was like the sand. We were few.  
The stranger came into [[set:221|Kalunzhe]] a second time.  
In the [[evt:9773|Conquest of Ninzhe]] the stranger said: "Ninzhe is mine."

Now hear the word of the men of Ninzhe. Hear it, O gods.  
The oath of Divnüzith was sworn before you. No spear can unswear it.  
No sack can unswear it. No stranger can unswear it.  
Ninzhe is free in the sight of Kudhze. Ninzhe is free in the sight of Nidhinande.  
The stranger sits in our house. The house is not his.  
The stranger eats our grain. The grain is not his.  
The stranger counts his years. We count ours.

This is the eighth year of Ninzhe. Next year will be the ninth.  
Every year we will count it. Every year our sons will count it.  
The wheel turns. What ends will begin again.  
What was made free once will be made free again.

Let this tablet be hidden in the wall of a house in Kalunzhe.  
Let the stranger not find it. Let our sons find it.  
Let them read it before the gods of [[mov:7|Kumbumbi]], and remember.

Whoever breaks this tablet, may Düvi open the earth under him.  
Whoever keeps this tablet, may Kudhze send rain on his field.
