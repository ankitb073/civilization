*[Obverse. A large tablet, a copy of the stone set up at the gate; the scribe's ruling divides it into ordinances.]*

Thus says [[per:56|Todifoyatl]], queen, queen of [[pol:0|Shue'ethie]], beloved of [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky, chosen of [[dei:1|Uelti]], keeper of the words.

Hear, elders. Hear, priests. Hear, men of the city and men of the villages.

In the days of my father the city was a city. The elders sat in the gate; each spoke for his own house; the villages of the plain came to the market and went home again, and paid what they pleased.  
In my thirty-seventh year I said: the city shall be a kingdom. I said it in the gate, and the elders bowed, and the priests poured oil on the stone. That was the [[evt:182|Kingdom Reform in Shue'ethie]]. Two summers have gone round since then. Now I set down the ordinances of the kingdom, so that no one may say: I did not know.

**The first ordinance.** The house of the queen is the house of the kingdom. The elders who sat in the gate shall sit in the house of the queen. They shall counsel, and the queen shall decide. Each elder shall swear by Atlie and by [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] that he will not hide his counsel and will not oppose the decision.

**The second ordinance.** The villages of the plain are the villages of the kingdom. Each village shall bring the tithe of its barley to the granary of the queen at the threshing, one sheaf of ten. The granary of the queen shall be opened in the year of hunger, to the villages first and to the city after. Whoever hides his barley, his field shall be taken.

**The third ordinance.** There shall be one measure in the kingdom. The measure of barley, the measure of oil, the weight of copper: the scribes of the queen shall make them of stone and set them in the house of Uelti. Whoever sells with a false measure, his hand shall be struck, and his goods given to the widow.

**The fourth ordinance.** The judgement of the kingdom belongs to the queen. In each village one man shall hear the small quarrels: the strayed ox, the broken pot. The great quarrels, of blood and of land, he shall bring to the gate of Shue'ethie, and the queen shall judge them.

**The fifth ordinance.** The houses of the gods shall be fed by the kingdom. The house of Atlie, the house of Uelti, the house of [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the black earth, the house of [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river, the house of [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] of the moon, the house of Nieshashta of the spear: each in their place, each with their due, as the priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] have taught since the founding. The priests shall be ordained in order, father to son, teacher to pupil, and the queen shall not set a priest over a god who has not been taught.

**The sixth ordinance.** In the day of war the men of the villages shall come to the gate with their spears, one man of five, and the queen shall feed them while they are at the gate.

**The seventh ordinance.** This tablet shall be read in the gate at each new year, so that the children learn it.

Whoever keeps these ordinances, may Muevas'e make his field fat and his house full.  
Whoever breaks these ordinances, may Niele'i turn away from his field and Lashla'e hide his face from him at night.  
Whoever breaks this stone or this tablet, may Nieshashta break his spear, and may his name be forgotten in [[set:3|Shue'ethie]].

All things turn on the wheel. What ends will begin again. The city ended; the kingdom begins.

*[Lower edge.]*

> Written in the 39th year of Todifoyatl, at the word of the queen, by the scribe of the gate.
