*I. Invocation*

Sing, tongue of [[pol:980|Yad]], a song you did not know before;  
not the old songs of the balance and the measure,  
but a song that walks in a straight road, as time walks,  
from the first morning of the world to the last tribunal.  
One is the Power that made the morning; one is the Judge at the road's end;  
and all the lesser gods our fathers fed with smoke  
are shadows cast before that light, or servants at that door.  
*Glory to the One, who goes before and waits at the end.*

*II. Of the old way*

Let us not curse the faith we leave; it taught us much.  
The [[mov:24|Udunkala]] also said the road was straight,  
and that the One was over all, and that a man may reason.  
It taught us justice in our dealings and a measure in all things.  
But measure is a cold coat in winter, and justice weighs, but does not heal.  
We had need of more than a balance. We had need of mercy.  
*Glory to the One, who goes before and waits at the end.*

*III. Of our sorrows*

Remember, city of [[set:82|Yad]], the [[evt:25052|Battle of Shyih]],  
where our sons went down before a greater host;  
remember the [[evt:25091|Battle of Dyizhih]], and the long road home;  
remember the [[evt:25092|Treaty of Dyizhih]], the lands cut from us like a limb,  
the tribute counted out in [[cur:534|Fyiu]] in a stranger's hall.  
Remember the [[evt:25255|Secession of Kankush]], our brothers turned to rivals,  
and the [[evt:25256|First Yade Civil War]], which is not yet ended.  
What did the old balance say to these? It said: endure.  
What does the new teaching say? It says: be released.  
*Glory to the One, who goes before and waits at the end.*

*IV. Of the new teaching*

Hear the word of the [[mov:62|Nidunde]], which the poor heard first:  
that suffering is not a debt we owe, but a chain the Power will loose;  
that the good life is compassion, given and received;  
that no priest holds the key of truth, but every seeker carries a lamp within,  
and reason is the oil of that lamp;  
that the gifts of the earth, the full granary and the laden ship,  
are signs of favour, and not snares, to those who share them;  
that this word is not for Yad alone, but for every people under the sky.  
*Glory to the One, who goes before and waits at the end.*

*V. Of the king*

And [[per:24725|Nanüdi]] our king, in the strength of his years,  
saw that the greater part of his people had already turned their faces,  
in the markets and the harbours, in the fields and in the schools;  
and he did not set his throne against the lamp of the many,  
but bowed his head with them, and made the people's faith the faith of the realm.  
So was the [[evt:25353|Conversion of Yade to Nidunde]] accomplished:  
not by the sword, which has done little for us, but by consent.  
*Glory to the One, who goes before and waits at the end.*

*VI. Prayer*

O Judge at the end of the road, be merciful when we arrive.  
Count our losses in the scale before our sins.  
Let the brothers who are divided from us be one with us again.  
Let the granaries of Yad be full, that we may share them.  
And when the last morning comes, and the line of time is drawn to its end,  
let it be written that in the year five hundred and fifty-four of the teaching  
a broken people chose compassion over the balance, and were healed.  
*Glory to the One, who goes before and waits at the end.*
