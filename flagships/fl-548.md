**AN OPEN LETTER**

*to the Elders and Ordained of the [[mov:58|Vishithmikhi]] in the Kingdom of [[pol:1797|Ool]], and to all who read; wherein the sentence pronounced against the writer is examined, found wanting, and returned to those who pronounced it.*

*By [[per:43382|Luishafueyatl]], formerly a reader of that communion, now of none but the One Power's. Printed at [[set:211|Ool]], 22 Isyi 4010 of the Myitsiyishio.*

---

Reverend Sirs,

On the third day of this month you caused to be read from your pulpits, and nailed to the doors of your meeting-houses, a sentence cutting me off from your communion, and forbidding the faithful to eat with me, lodge me, or buy from me. You did not summon me to answer; you did not name my error beyond calling it "pride"; and you did not, I observe, sign the paper with any name but the seal of your office. I reply in print and over my own name, because I have nothing to hide, and because I have learned that what is read from a pulpit is soon forgotten, while what is printed is kept.

**I.** You charge me with denying the authority of the ordained. I deny it plainly. Our faith teaches, and you teach it with me, that there is one Power over all things, that time runs in a line to its Judgement, and that the good life is compassion and the release of suffering. Where is it written that a man may know none of this except through you? The One Power did not wait for an Elder's hand to be laid on a head before it gave light. I hold, and shall hold, that every seeker may find the truth by the light within and by reason, and that an ordained man who will not reason has no more of the truth than his seal.

**II.** You charge me with stirring the poor. I do not deny that either. A measure of grain in the market of Ool costs this month above two [[cur:989|Isuith]]. I have seen widows count coppers for half a measure at the door of a house where the Elders dine on three courses. Our faith asks compassion. It does not, I grant, forbid wealth; but I say that the wise withdraw from wealth and appetite, and that a priesthood which grows fat in a year of dearth has already pronounced its own sentence.

**III.** You charge me with dividing the faithful in a time of war, when the armies of the [[evt:43805|Holy War of Ishwyithie against Ool]] are on our frontier and every house in the kingdom should stand together. I answer that I have urged no man to disobey Her Majesty [[per:43025|Oges]], nor to withhold a single coin of the tax, nor to shirk the muster. I am a loyal subject of the Queen and an obedient one. It is not the State I have quarrelled with. It is you.

**IV.** Therefore, Reverend Sirs, hear my sentence in return. Not by any authority of my own, for I claim none, but by the plain sense of the teaching we once held in common, I declare:

that a communion which casts out a man unheard has cut itself off from justice;

that a priesthood which claims to own the light has cut itself off from the light;

that a faith which eats while its people go hungry has cut itself off from compassion;

and that you, having done all three, stand outside the faith you claim to keep. I do not close any door against you. You have closed it yourselves.

**V.** To those who have come to hear me in the upper room in the dyers' street, and who have been threatened for it: do not be afraid. No man's hand is needed between you and the One. Keep the fasts; feed the poor; read, and think about what you read. Men already call this company the [[mov:156|Tuedinatlshi]]. Let them. A name given in scorn has carried many good things.

I remain, whatever you have written,

the servant of the One Power and of its poor,

LUISHAFUEYATL.
