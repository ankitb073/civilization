*[Obverse.]*

Thus says [[per:96|Baluthnufmalin]], lord of [[pol:10|Lenunushal]], who sat in the high seat seventeen years by the will of the gods.

Hear me, O [[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the spear.  
Hear me, O [[dei:16|Dumamnul]], the high sky.  
Hear me, O [[dei:15|Vinba]], the broad earth.  
Hear me, O [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]] of the barley.  
Hear me, O [[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire.  
Hear me, O [[dei:19|Yenan]] of the moon.  
Hear me, all you gods of [[mov:2|Jinru]], each in your place, each with your due.

There is a man. His name is [[per:147|Lomitathrelin]].  
I fed him at my table. I gave him cattle. I gave him a place at the feast.  
He sat at my right hand, and he smiled, and his heart was black.  
He gathered men in the night. He opened the gate in the night.  
He came into the house of the king with spears in the night.  
He took the staff from my hand. He sat down in the high seat.  
That is the [[evt:180|Usurpation of Lomitathrelin]]. Let the gods see it.

Now the scribes write: "In the 1st year of Lomitathrelin."  
I spit on that year. I do not count it. The gods do not count it.

*[Reverse.]*

Therefore hear my curse, O gods. Hear it and do it.

May Vulme break his spear in the day of battle.  
May Vulme turn the faces of his men toward home.  
May Dumamnul shut the sky over his fields, and send no rain.  
May Dumamnul send hail in the month of the harvest.  
May Vinba refuse to hold up his feet. May the earth open under his house.  
May Nidhikhti blight his barley. May the ear stand empty on the stalk.  
May his granary be full of mice and his jars full of dust.  
May Eva put out the fire of his hearth.  
May Eva burn the roof over his head.  
May Yenan hide the moon from his road, so he walks in the dark and stumbles.  
May his sons not sit in the high seat. May his name be scraped from the wall.  
May his wife weep. May his servants run from him.  
May his cattle sicken. May his sheep be taken by the wolf.  
May no one pour water for him when he goes down to the earth.

And whoever helped him, the men who opened the gate,  
the men who carried the spears, the men who held the torch:  
may the same curse fall on them, on their houses, on their sons.

As for me, I am sick. My strength has gone from me. I do not have long.  
I have no spear now. I have no host. I have only my mouth, and the gods.  
The mouth of a lord is heavy. The curse of a lord who was wronged is heavy.  
I lay it on him. I lay it on him.

Let this tablet be buried under the threshold of the house of the gods in [[set:18|Lenunushal]].  
Let him walk over it every day and not know it.  
Let it lie there and do its work.

Whoever digs up this tablet and breaks it, the curse is on him also.  
Whoever digs it up and reads it aloud before the gods, the curse is made new.

O gods, I have given you right sacrifice for seventeen years.  
I have poured the oil, I have burned the fat, I have kept the feasts.  
Do not let a thief sit in the place of the faithful.  
The wheel turns. What was raised will be brought low.  
Turn the wheel, O gods. Turn it upon [[per:147|Lomitathrelin]].

*[Colophon, on the edge, in a different hand.]*  
Written in the 1st year of Lomitathrelin, at the word of Baluthnufmalin.
