*[Obverse. Ruled in short entries, one to a box.]*

**In the 17th year of [[per:504|Dolulrinin]], king of [[pol:12|Chinontonu]].** The tablet of the days of the siege. Set down by a servant of the house of [[dei:55|Dumamnul]], who keeps the count of the stores.

**First day.** The men of [[pol:1|Tallin]] are before the walls. At the [[evt:457|Battle of Zhinontunu]] our men went out, and they were few, and the men of Tallin were many, more than six to one. Our men killed many of them, eight hundreds and more. But two hundreds of ours lay down, and the rest came in through the gate. The gate is shut. The bar is on the gate.

**Second day.** The men of Tallin dig a ditch around the city. They set up their tents on the hill of the threshing floors. Their smoke goes up. We hear their drums.

**Third day.** The king walked on the wall. He said: "At Puthnimdu they ran from us." It is true. In the first [[evt:447|Battle of Puthnimdu]] they ran, and the crows ate them. The people on the wall shouted. But in the next year, at the second [[evt:451|Battle of Puthnimdu]], the king himself stood in the line, and the men of Tallin were like the sand, and we gave way. The people on the wall did not speak of that.

**Fifth day.** I counted the stores of the house of the god. Barley: the great jars, eleven full, four half. Oil: six jars. Dates: two baskets. Salt fish: much. I gave out the measure to the priests and to the poor at the gate.

**Seventh day.** A sacrifice to [[dei:58|Chithal]] of the war. A white kid. The priests of [[mov:8|Ligay]] read the liver. They said it was good. They said Chithal stands on the wall.

**Ninth day.** Arrows came over the wall. A woman at the well was struck in the shoulder. She lived.

**Twelfth day.** In the market a measure of barley goes for twice what it went for last month. The traders hide their [[cur:8|Yong]] under the floors. The king sent his men to open the storehouses of the traders. There was shouting.

**Fifteenth day, the full moon.** [[dei:57|Bubin]] stood full over the camp of Tallin. Their fires were many, like stars come down. I could not count them. A child on the wall asked me: "Are there more of them than of the stars?" I said: "No. Bubin sees them all and knows their number."

*[Reverse.]*

**Eighteenth day.** They brought up a ram of wood with a roof of hides. Our men poured hot sand on it from the wall. It burned. The men of Tallin ran back. We sang the song of Chithal on the wall.

**Twentieth day.** Water. The river [[dei:56|Nabuthin]] is ours still on the side of the reeds. The women go down by the water gate at night. Nabuthin covers them with mist. May the mist not fail.

**Twenty-third day.** The barley jars: eight full. I gave out a smaller measure. The poor at the gate cursed me. I did not answer.

**Twenty-fifth day.** A man of Tallin came near the gate and called up to us in our own speech. He said: "Open, and live." The king answered him from the wall: "[[set:6|Chinontonu]] does not open." The people shouted. I shouted with them.

**Twenty-seventh day.** Sickness in the quarter of the tanners. Three dead. The priests burned cedar.

**Thirtieth day, the dark of the moon.** Rain. [[dei:55|Dumamnul]] sent rain. The ditch of the men of Tallin is full of mud. Their tents are wet. Ours also. But our walls are of baked brick, and their tents are of hide.

**Thirty-first day.** The king went up to the house of the god. He poured oil before Dumamnul. He poured oil before [[dei:59|Winba]] of the earth, that the earth may hold up our walls. I stood behind him with the jar.

**Thirty-second day.** The barley jars: seven full. The siege is not lifted. I count. I give out the measure. I write.

*[The last box on the reverse is left blank.]*
