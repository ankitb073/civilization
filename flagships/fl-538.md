*[Obverse. A merchant's tablet, small and thick, in an envelope bearing the seal of the house; the envelope was opened in antiquity and found with the tablet.]*

To my son, the son of my house, say:

Thus says [[per:349|Ulontayin]], your father, the merchant of [[set:79|Talenonoy]].

With me it is well. With the donkeys it is well. With the bales of cloth it is well. The road is dry and the river is low. May it be well with you. May it be well with your mother. May [[dei:141|Nabüdin]] of the river carry this tablet to you quickly.

Hear me, my son, and keep this tablet. Do not throw it on the heap with the tallies. Put it in the box under the floor, with the weights.

In this year, the 2nd year of [[per:355|Tyenmomloret]], our lady, I went up to the gate of the palace. I took gifts: a bolt of red cloth, a jar of oil, a bronze bowl. I bowed before the lady. I said: "Let the house of Ulontayin be a house, with its own seal and its own name." The lady looked at the gifts. The lady looked at me. She said: "Let it be." The scribes wrote it. The priests of [[mov:21|Kunelmu]] poured the oil on the doorpost. So the house was made: the house of [[hou:0|Wuelshir]], in Talenonoy. The scribes of the palace call it the [[evt:314|Founding of the House of Wuelshir]]. Remember the day.

Hear what a house is, my son. A house is not walls. Walls fall down. A house is a name and a seal and a road. The name is Wuelshir. The seal is the seal on this envelope: the horse and the bale. The road is the road your father walks.

Hear what the house carries. It carries timber down from the hills. It carries horses from the high pastures. It carries cloth from the looms of the river towns. It carries bronze. It carries spice, a little, in small jars, for the great ones. Learn the price of each. Learn it as you learned the names of the gods.

Hear what the house does not do. It does not cheat on the weight. The [[cur:6|Tyektsi]] of the house is full weight; the scale of the house is level. If a man cheats you, do not cheat him back. Write his name on a tally and do not trade with him again. [[dei:138|Tuel]] of the moon sees the scales at night. She sees them.

Hear what you owe. You owe the gods their portion. Before you open a bale, pour a little oil to [[dei:136|Duemino]], that the storm keep off the road. At harvest, give barley to [[dei:139|Nidiktti]]. The priests say: prosperity is a sign of the favour of the gods. So the house must be prosperous, so that the gods are seen to favour it. But the house must not be proud. A proud house falls.

You owe the lady her portion also. When the officers of the palace come, give them the tax, and a little more, and a cup of beer. Do not be the richest house in Talenonoy. Be the second richest. The richest house is the one the palace remembers.

The land of [[pol:19|Talenonoy]] is young. Thirteen years ago it was made, at the [[evt:292|Founding of Talenonoy]]; I was a young man; I carried stones for the wall. Now the wall stands and the house stands. You were born inside the wall. Do not forget the men who carried the stones.

I will come home before the new moon after next. If I do not come home, if [[dei:137|Shyedartsi]] takes me on the road, then the house is yours. Take the seal. Take the weights. Keep the name. Pour beer for me at the door.

Greet your mother. Greet the old woman at the loom. Feed the black donkey; he is lame.

*[Edge.]*

> Written on the road, in the 2nd year of Tyenmomloret, lady of Talenonoy.
