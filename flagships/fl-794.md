*In the twenty-eighth year of Nandange. One mother sings; the mothers answer.*

Who went down into [[reg:27|Lothe]]?  
*Our sons went down into Lothe.*  
Who came back up out of Lothe?  
*Ask the crows. Ask the earth.*

My son went down to [[set:132|Kanendo]], in the second year of the war.  
*Kanendo, Kanendo, the field of the crows.*  
The men of [[pol:238|Kavo]] stood on the high ground. My son stood below.  
He was a reed-cutter. He did not come back to cut the reeds.  
*Kanendo, the field of the crows.*

My son went down to [[set:150|Kavazho]], and the men of Nelo held the field.  
*Kavazho, Kavazho, where our men held the field.*  
They brought back his shield. They said, He fell in the winning.  
Does a son fallen in the winning eat more bread than a son fallen in the losing?  
*Kavazho, where our men held the field.*

My son went down to Kanendo with the king, in the sixth year.  
*Kanendo again. Kanendo again.*  
[[per:6218|Nandange]] went in front, the king went in front, his shield was red.  
The king came back. My son did not come back.  
*The king came back. Kanendo again.*

My son went down to Kanendo in the ninth year,  
when the field was thick with the men of [[pol:245|Nelo]], thick as barley.  
*Kanendo, the third time. The field was thick.*  
Thirty of ours for one of Kavo, the old men say. Thirty.  
I did not count them. I counted one.  
*She counted one. We count one each.*

My sons went down to [[set:145|Kapene]], two sons, in the last two years.  
*Kapene, Kapene, the end of the road.*  
The elder in the first of those years, the younger in the second.  
Then the king set his seal at Kapene, the [[evt:6101|Treaty of Yibi]],  
and silver went out of every house to Kavo, silver for the peace.  
*Silver for the peace. Sons for the war.*

[[dei:31|Kombe]], moon, you saw it all.  
[[dei:32|Nejenande]], war, you ate it all.  
Ten years the elders swore. Ten years of no war.  
*Ten years. We counted the moons.*  
A measure of barley is one [[cur:97|Lièlo]] in the market.  
A son is no price in the market. A son is not sold.  
*A son is not sold. A son is given.*

Now the ten years are full. The ten years are full.  
Now the boys who were small in the [[evt:6007|Second Nelo–Kavu War]]  
are tall in the doorway, are as tall as their brothers.  
*As tall as their brothers. Taller.*  
Nejenande, do not look at them.  
Kombe, hide them in the dark.  
King, let the seal stand. Let the seal stand.  
*Let the seal stand. We remember Kanendo.*
