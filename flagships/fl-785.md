*To my lord the owner of the herd, in the town of [[set:17|Pöme]], say: thus says your herdsman of the pastures below Linidhagshal. May [[dei:15|Venba]] of the earth keep your house, may [[dei:16|Domamnol]] of the sky be kind to your fields. In the 43rd year of [[per:8552|Shefton]], the king.*

My lord, I send this by the son of the salt-carrier, who goes up to the town. I send it because you must know, and I cannot leave the herd.

The rain has not come. In the month when the rain comes, it did not come. In the month after, it did not come. Domamnol of the sky has shut his hand. The clouds come over the hills in the morning, white, and go away in the afternoon, white. Not one drop.

The grass of the lower pasture is burned. It is yellow, it is white, it breaks under the foot like straw. The spring of the three stones is a mud. The spring of the fig tree is a mud. The river is a road of stones, and the cows walk on it.

I have taken the herd to the high valley, where the grass is better. It is better, but it is not good. Other herdsmen have come there, from the east, from the south, with their herds. They say: the grass is burned with us also. They say there is a [[evt:8604|drought in Diebeguvebenga]] from the mountains to the sea. The high valley is full. The herds fight at the water. The men fight at the water. One man of the south struck my boy with a stick. I did not strike him back. I gave him water for his cows, and he gave me salt.

Of your cows, my lord: eighty-seven at the spring count. Now eighty-one. Two calves dead of thirst on the road. One old cow fell on the stones. Three I sold at the market below Linidhagshal, for grain for the herd-boys, as you said I may do in need. The buyer gave little, because many sell. Grain at the market is a [[cur:166|Röngel]] and three-quarters the measure, and rising. Everyone sells cows. Everyone buys grain.

The milk is less. The cows give a little in the morning and none at night. The calves suck and are not full. The cheese I send you is small. Forgive me. It is all.

My lord, I ask: may I sell ten more, the old ones, before they die and are worth nothing? May I take the herd over the hill to the river-meadows of your cousin, if he will have us? Send word.

The old men of the pastures say they have never seen such a spring. The oldest of them says he has never seen the spring of the fig tree dry. He says: in the year of the [[evt:8399|Treaty of Zhiyiyzhikhti]], when the king brought home the tribute, the grass stood to a cow's belly. He says: that was a fat year.

I have made the offering to [[dei:17|Nedhekhte]] of the grain and to Domamnol. A kid, the best of the kids, on the stone of the high valley. The smoke went up straight; there is no wind. The priest of [[mov:2|Jenro]] in the village says: the gods are testing [[pol:310|Pöme]]. I say: let them test us with a little rain.

My wife sends her greeting to your wife. The herd-boys are well. They are thin, but they are well.

Your herdsman.
