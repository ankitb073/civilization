I am a man of [[pol:171|Tallen]]. I was a spearman of the king. My hair is white; my knees are weak. In the 320th year of Tallen I set down what my eyes saw. [[dei:14|Volme]], lady of the spear, hear it. Yenan of the night, hear it. Let no scribe add to it; let no scribe take from it.

**The going out.** In those days [[per:6749|Naleyon]], king of [[pol:262|Manke]], was proud. He had gone out to war, and at [[evt:6620|the battle at Mienioshtse]] his men were cut down like barley. Then the king of Tallen said: "Manke is wounded. Go out, take the land of [[reg:2|Nobe]]." So began [[evt:6680|the war of Tallen and Manke]]. We sharpened the bronze. We gave the bull to Volme. We went out.

**The first battle.** At [[set:162|Khongomno]] the general [[per:6722|Kotdon]] led us. We were many, many as the stars of Domamnol. Naleyon stood across the field, his men few, his spears short. We went against him and he did not break. Volme hid her face. My brother lay down at Khongomno; I did not find his body. I poured beer on the earth for him. Venba, hold him.

**The next year.** Again Kotdon led us to Khongomno. [[per:6961|Mafpekhjen]] of Manke came out against us. This time Volme showed her face. The men of Manke ran. We took their shields.

**The year after.** Kotdon went to the gods; he did not die by the spear. [[per:7008|Vanofmalen]] became our general. Again to Khongomno. Domekovan of Manke came out. Again the men of Manke ran. Three times we went to Khongomno; twice Khongomno was ours in the field.

**The year after that.** At [[set:54|Ponedoyedhdey]] Vanofmalen met Naleyon himself. The king of Manke ran from the field. That night we ate his sheep.

**Two years later.** Hear this. The king of Tallen himself came out, [[per:6818|Tafbafkolvan]], lord of the land. At Khongomno he went against Naleyon. Naleyon held. The king drew back. I do not say more. The gods test even kings.

**The next year.** At [[set:170|Joyeyjekhte]] the king went forward again. Naleyon stood against him and Naleyon broke. The king gave each man of us silver, Lol upon Lol, weighed out on the field.

**Four years later.** At Ponedoyedhdey again the king met Naleyon. Again Naleyon broke. My spear arm was cut that day. See, the scar is white like my hair.

**The last year.** Naleyon went to the gods; his son [[per:6838|Lomonan]] sat on his seat. At Khongomno Vanofmalen met Lomonan. Lomonan broke. Then Manke bowed its neck. At Khongomno the king Tafbafkolvan and Lomonan swore the oath before the gods. Manke gave Nobe to Tallen. Manke gave Joyeyjekhte to Tallen. Ten years the spear would sleep. We came home to [[set:0|Tallen]] singing. The women threw barley over us.

**Twelve years of war.** This is the count of it: Kotdon, Vanofmalen, the king. Khongomno, Ponedoyedhdey, Joyeyjekhte. Who can count the dead? The scribes of the king counted them; I do not count them. I count my brother.

**Now.** The king Tafbafkolvan has gone to the gods. Vanofmalen has gone to the gods. The men of my year sit by the gate and sleep in the sun.

And now the house of Tallen is broken. Three years ago [[evt:6959|Yeda rose]] in Tallen; [[evt:6960|Navnga rose]]; [[evt:6961|Duegovinga rose]]; Nulinga, Pome, Tobojenga rose, each with its own lord. The land we bled for, who holds it? The barley we threw, who gathers it?

So say the priests of [[mov:2|Jenro]]: all things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again. I say it after them. Volme, lady of the spear, turn the wheel. Eva, keep the hearth of Tallen warm. Nedhekhte, fill the jar. Let my son's son read this, and know what his fathers did at Khongomno.

Here ends the tablet of the old spearman.
