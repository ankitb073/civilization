Say to [[per:10620|Vanalon]], king of [[pol:451|Ayoto]], my servant: thus says [[per:10521|Ralokhnon]], great king, king of [[pol:310|Peme]], beloved of [[dei:14|Volme]], beloved of [[dei:16|Domamnol]].

With me it is well. With my house, my wife, my children, my chariots, my horses and my land it is very well. With the hostages of your house, who are in my hand, it is well. Hear this, and let your heart be at rest, and let your heart also be afraid.

The young men of your house came to me at [[set:17|Peme]]. They came in the month of the threshing. They came with their heads bowed. They came as the [[evt:10390|treaty]] says they shall come, the treaty that you and I set our seals to at [[set:202|Rovon]], before Domamnol who sees, before Volme who watches, before [[dei:18|Eva]] whose fire eats the oath-breaker.

I have given them a house inside the wall. I have given them bread, I have given them beer, I have given them oil for their bodies. They eat at the lower end of my table. They sit when I sit. They stand when I stand. No man of Peme has struck them. No man of Peme has mocked them. This I swear by Volme.

But hear, Vanalon. Remember how the matter went. Twice I came up against Rovon. The [[evt:10374|first time]] you yourself stood in the gate against me, and your men fell before mine as the barley falls before the sickle, and the city was given to my soldiers. The [[evt:10388|second time]] your captain stood in the gate, and he fell, and his men fell, and the city was given again to my soldiers. Ayoto is small. Peme is great. Volme has shown it twice. Will you ask her a third time?

Therefore keep the words of the tablet. The land in Ayshi that you gave me, let no man of Ayoto plough it. The tribute that is written, send it each year at the new year, for thirteen years, silver by weight against the [[cur:166|rengel]] of my storehouse, and let my scribes weigh it. Ten years there is no war between Peme and Ayoto. Ten years your gate is not broken. Keep the words, and the young men of your house will eat at my table, and grow fat, and learn the songs of Peme, and when the years are full I will send them home to you with gifts.

If the silver does not come, I will not write again. I will not send a messenger. I will send the young men of your house back to you one by one, and they will not walk.

The hostage who is the eldest among them asked for clay and a scribe, that he might send you his greeting. I granted it. These are his words, which my scribe set down:

> *To my lord and king, Vanalon: thus says your servant. I fall at the feet of my lord seven times and seven times. I am alive. The great king feeds us. The house is dry. The nights are cold. Send me a cloak. Keep the words of the tablet, my lord, keep the words of the tablet, for my sake and for the sake of all of us. May [[dei:19|Yenan]] watch over Ayoto until I see it again.*

These are his words. I added nothing. I took nothing away.

I have sent to you with this tablet one horse, a good one, as a gift from brother to servant. Send me a gift in return, and let it be fitting.

Written in the 12th year of Ralokhnon.
