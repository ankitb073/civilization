*Words of [[per:6|Shuesh'ayueve]], king of [[set:7|Ana]], set down on clay in the new signs, in the 4th year of Shuesh'ayueve.*

I.

Praise to [[dei:1|Uelti]], lord of wisdom, who opens the eye of the heart.  
Praise to Uelti, who gave the signs.  
Praise to Uelti, who put the signs into the hand of the king.  
Praise to Uelti, who put the signs into the hand of the scribe.

II.

Before the signs, a word was a bird.  
It flew out of the mouth. It flew away.  
The king spoke, and the word flew away.  
The priest spoke, and the word flew away.  
The steward counted the barley, and the count flew away.  
The old man died, and the names of his fathers flew away with him.  
This was the way of the world before the signs.

III.

Now the word is a bird in a cage of clay.  
It does not fly away. It sits. It waits.  
The king speaks, and the scribe presses the reed.  
The word sits in the clay. Tomorrow it is there.  
The next year it is there. When the king is old it is there.  
When the king's son is old it is there.

IV.

Hear what the signs keep.  
They keep the barley of the storehouse: so many measures in, so many measures out.  
They keep the oil and the wool and the oxen.  
They keep the names of the gods, every god in his place:  
[[dei:0|Atlie]] of the sky, Uelti of wisdom, [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the earth,  
[[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river, [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] of the moon, [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] of war.  
They keep the days of the feasts. They keep the words of the oath.  
A man swears by Atlie, and the signs keep it. He cannot say: I did not swear.

V.

Who made the signs? Let the singer say it.  
In the third year after [[evt:40|Ana was made a kingdom]],  
in the house of tablets by the river gate,  
the scribes of the king sat with clay and reed.  
They made a sign for the ox: an ox's head.  
They made a sign for the barley: an ear of barley.  
They made a sign for the king: a man with a staff.  
And they said: a sign for an ox is also a sound,  
and with the sounds we may write the names of men.  
So they wrote the name of the king. It is written. It is here.  
Let no man say the king did not stand over them.  
Let no man say the king did not bring the clay.  
Let no man say the king did not give the bread and the beer while they worked.  
The king gave them all these things.  
Uelti gave the thought; the king gave the bread.

VI.

Hear me, men of [[pol:6|Ana]].  
Hear me, people of [[cul:0|La'im]], of the one blood and the one speech.  
The signs are yours. They are [[scr:0|the signs of Shatl'i'ueza]].  
No other people has them. Keep them among you.  
Teach them to the sons of scribes, in order, the father to the son.  
Let the priests keep them in the house of the gods.  
Let them be passed down in order, as the rites of [[mov:0|La'im]] are passed down.

VII.

The priests say: all things turn on the wheel.  
What ends will begin again.  
But the signs say: what was spoken will remain.  
Let the wheel turn. The clay remains.

VIII.

Praise to Uelti. Praise to the scribes. Praise to the king.  
This is the [[evt:63|making of the signs]]. It is written. It is here.  
Whoever breaks this tablet, may Lashla'e hide his face from him.  
Whoever reads it, may Uelti open his eyes.
