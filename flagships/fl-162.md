**The preface of the master**

Thus says the master of the tablet-house in [[set:49|Adhgumgi]], servant of [[per:13322|Nanyef]], great king, king of [[pol:578|Khanji]]:

[[dei:28|Khudhaze]], lord of the sky, hear me. [[dei:30|Pugazhu]], lady of the sun, give light to the eyes of the boys. [[dei:31|Khumbe]], lady of the moon, give light to the lamp when they copy by night.

In the days of our fathers the scribe learned many signs, one sign for each sound and its voice together, sign upon sign, so that a boy sat ten years in the tablet-house and came out with a bent back. Then in the 13th year of Nanyef the great king, [[evt:13432|the new letters were made]] in Khanji, the letters of the [[cul:6|Khukunji]], which are called [[scr:23|Mbingi]]. Each letter is one sound of the mouth; the voice between them the reader puts in himself, as a man puts water in the bread. Few letters, many words. A boy learns them in one season.

The old scribes grumble. They say: a reader may read "king" where it is written "dog." I say: let him read the line before and the line after, and he will not mistake the great king for a dog. Let the old scribes grumble; the boys are learning.

This tablet is the exercise of a boy of the second year. I have set my marks on it where he went astray, so that the next boy may see the fault and not make it. Let him who reads it give thanks to the gods of [[mov:4|Khupande]], who keep the truth in order and pass it down.

**The exercise of the boy**

*First: the names of the gods.*

Khudhaze, lord of the sky.  
[[dei:29|Bumbe]], lord of the sea.  
Pugazhu, lady of the sun.  
Khumbe, lady of the moon.  
[[dei:32|Nizhinande]], lady of war.

*(Master: you have written Bumbe with the letter of the throat. The sea has no throat. Again!)*

Bumbe, lord of the sea.

*Second: the praise of the king.*

Nanyef, great king, king of Khanji, beloved of Nizhinande.  
In the 5th year of Nanyef the army of Khanji went out.  
At [[evt:13321|Shuthunile]] the army of Khanji struck the enemy, and the enemy fell.  
At Shuthunile the king swore [[evt:13322|the oath]]: ten years the spear sleeps.  
In the 11th year of Nanyef the army of Khanji went out against Kambizhin.  
That was [[evt:13381|the war of Kambizhin]].  
Three years Kambizhin stood; in the fourth year Kambizhin bowed.  
In the 14th year of Nanyef the king swore [[evt:13429|the oath at Khambizhinu]].  
Kambizhin gave land to Khanji.  
Kambizhin brings silver to Khanji, year upon year, for thirty-one years.

*(Master: "thirty-one," not "thirteen." Would you rob the king of eighteen years of silver? Again!)*

Kambizhin brings silver to Khanji, year upon year, for thirty-one years.

*Third: the sayings of the wise.*

The ox does not ask why the furrow is long.  
A sealed jar keeps the wine; a sealed mouth keeps the head.  
The king's word is written once; the scribe's fault is written twice.  
He who sacrifices rightly eats from a full bowl.  
All things turn on the great wheel; the boy becomes the master.

*Fourth: the letters in their order, three times.*

*(Master: the second row is crooked; the third is good. This boy will be a scribe.)*

Written in Adhgumgi, in the 15th year of Nanyef, great king, in the new letters of Khanji.
