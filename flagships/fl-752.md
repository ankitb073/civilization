*[Obverse]*

To the judge of the king, the servant of [[per:14797|Duberoyin]], king of [[pol:635|Mikhuthitsu]], who sits at the gate in [[set:28|Tsukha]], say:  
thus say the farmers of the village of the old canal, the heads of nine houses, who set their seals below.  
May [[dei:17|Nidikhki]], lady of the grain, fill the king's storehouse.  
May [[dei:15|Winba]], mother earth, give the king long days.

Hear the complaint of the farmers. Hear it, lord. Hear it.

In the days of our fathers the [[cul:13|Khunthatsumumu]] came out of [[reg:11|Kiya]] into the land of [[reg:10|Mikhuthitsu]].  
The scribes of the king write it: [[evt:14658|Migration of the Unsatlmum into Mikhukhizhi]]. We say: the Khunthatsumumu came.  
They came because of the [[evt:14657|Famine in Tsiey]]. They came hungry. They came with nothing.  
Our fathers gave them bread. Our fathers gave them beer. Our fathers said: sit by the canal.  
The king's men gave them the low fields, the wet fields, the fields that no one sowed.  
That was good. We do not complain of that. A hungry man must eat.

But they did not stop coming.  
One year a hundred houses. Another year a hundred houses more.  
Twenty years and more they came, family by family, along the road from Kiya.  
Now they are in the low fields. Now they are in the high fields.  
Now they are on the canal bank. Now they are at the head of the canal.

*[Reverse]*

Hear the first complaint. The water.  
Our water day is the day after the new moon. It was so in our fathers' days.  
Now the Khunthatsumumu open the sluice on our day. They say: we did not know.  
They say it every month. They know.

Hear the second complaint. The goats.  
Their goats come into our barley. Their goats eat the green barley.  
We drove the goats out. Their young men came with sticks. Our young men came with sticks.  
The headman bled from the head. He is a man of sixty years.

Hear the third complaint. The work.  
The lord of the estate hires them at harvest for half of what he pays us.  
They take it. They are many and they are hungry still.  
So our sons sit at home at harvest, and their sons cut the lord's barley.  
A measure of barley in the market is one [[cur:331|Nufpu]] and nearly another.  
How shall a man pay it, when the lord will not hire him?

Hear the fourth complaint, lord. It is a hard one.  
When the men of Nibanga came against us, our sons went out with the king's army.  
Our sons fell at Lininadhe. Our sons fell at Zhükhi.  
When the [[evt:14800|Sack of Zhükhi]] was done, our sons did not come home.  
And while our sons were at war, the newcomers ploughed. While our sons were at war, they moved the boundary stones.  
We say this. They say no. Let the king's judge come and measure.

We do not ask that they be driven out. Where would they go? Back to Kiya? Kiya is far.  
We ask the king's judge: come to the village of the old canal.  
Measure the fields. Set the water days on a tablet. Set the stones again.  
Give the goats to the goat-ward. Let the lord pay one wage for one day.

This is the complaint of the farmers. We are the king's men. We gave our sons to the king.  
May Dumamnul, sky, see the boundary stones.  
May Nidikhki see the green barley. May Winba keep the fields.  
In the 10th year of Duberoyin, the king, may he live.  
Nine seals. The seal of the headman is the seal with the ox.
