*The lament of the village by the river below [[set:80|Yeshtakshal]], in the 1st year of [[per:1354|Nupmaluanan]].*

Woe for [[pol:34|Yeshtakshal]], woe for the city of our fathers.  
Woe for the gate, woe for the wall, woe for the house of the lord.  
Woe for the barley of the river field. Woe for the hands that cut it.  

The men of [[pol:14|Egu]] came six summers ago, in the first year of our sorrow.  
They came to the field before the city, they came with spears.  
At the [[evt:1032|battle of Yishdhagshal]] their king himself stood,  
[[per:1168|Pantsenupmalin]] stood in the field, and our young men fell.  
Our young men fell like barley before the sickle. Woe.  

Three harvests they came and went, three harvests the fields were trodden.  
In the fourth year they came and sat down before the wall.  
The [[evt:1039|siege of Yishdhagshal]]: they sat, and we ate the seed-corn,  
they sat, and we ate the oxen, they sat, and the wall was opened.  

Our lord [[per:1262|Tsentsin]] went out to them. He put his seal on the tablet.  
He put his seal on the tablet, and they killed him before the gate.  
Woe for our lord. Woe for the seal of the city, broken.  
They wrote the [[evt:1040|Conquest of Yeshtakshal]] on their tablets,  
and our city was a city no more, but a field of Egu.  

Now the scribes of Egu come down to the village.  
They count the sheep. They count the goats. They count the children.  
They measure the field with the rope of Egu, which is shorter than our rope,  
so that our field is larger and the tax is larger.  
Woe for the rope. Woe for the field. Woe for the tax.  

They want silver. They want the [[cur:14|Niba]] of Egu, with the mark of Egu.  
We have no silver. We carry barley to the town and sell it for their silver,  
a measure for little more than half a Niba, and give the silver back to them.  
Barley goes to the town. Silver goes to Egu. Nothing comes to the village.  

In this year the king who took us went to the house below.  
Pantsenupmalin went down to the dark. We did not weep.  
Now his son sits in Egu, a child, a boy, with his mother to hold his hand.  
A child counts our sheep. A child takes our barley. Woe.  

[[dei:36|Nidziktti]] of the grain, see us. You know the barley of the river field.  
You know it is ours. You know whose hands planted it.  
Nidziktti, see the rope of Egu, how short it is.  

[[dei:38|Shedartsi]] of the house below, receive our young men,  
receive our lord Tsentsin, whose seal was broken.  
Give him a seat in the house below. Give him his own city there.  

Woe for Yeshtakshal, woe for the city of our fathers.  
The swallows nest in the gate. The men of Egu sit in the gate.  
We go down to the river field with the sickle. We cut the barley.  
We cut the barley, and we sing this, so that the barley hears.
