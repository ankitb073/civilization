title: The Weeping of Nelo by the Sea

**The women of the gate sing:**

Who is this that sits upon the shore with her hair unbound?  
Who is this that sits upon the shore and does not eat?  
It is [[pol:177|Nelo]], the lady of the four roads.  
It is Nelo, the lady of the harbour, the mother of the salt.  

**Nelo speaks:**

I was the lady of the four roads. Now the roads go past me.  
I was the mother of the salt. Now the salt is bitter in my mouth.  
[[dei:29|Bombe]] of the sea, you brought the ships to my quay.  
Bombe of the sea, why do you bring them now to another quay?  
[[dei:28|Khothaze]] of the sky, you kept the storm from my roofs.  
Khothaze, why have you opened the roof of my house?  

**The women answer:**

Lady, do you not remember? Do you not remember Yibi?  
At [[evt:5248|Yibi]] your sons went out, and many did not come home.  
At [[evt:5262|Khavazhu]] you swore the oath of the beaten,  
and you weighed out your silver to the stranger.  

**Nelo speaks:**

I remember Yibi. I remember the silver.  
I remember Yizhi, where the field was mud and blood.  
At [[evt:5318|Yizhi]] my sons went out for the gods; the gods did not go with them.  
At [[evt:5352|Daadüdhih]] my sons went out again; the stranger held the hill.  
[[dei:32|Nejenande]] of war, I gave you the ox and the ram.  
Nejenande, I gave you the ox; you gave me the dead.  
At [[evt:5364|Divnüzith]] I swore the oath of the beaten again.  
Again I weighed out my silver, my good [[cur:97|lièlo]], to the stranger.  
And the merchants of the stranger came and sat in my market,  
and they set their scales beside my scales.  

**The women answer:**

Lady, but do you not remember Azhi? Do you not remember the good year?  
At [[evt:5631|Azhi]] the enemy swore the oath of the beaten to you.  
He gave you towns; he gave you fields; he gave you the river;  
he promised you his tribute for many years.  

**Nelo speaks:**

I remember Azhi. I remember the good year.  
I held the towns of Azhi four years, four years only.  
Then came the year of the breaking, the [[evt:5663|Collapse of the Nelo State]].  
The towns I took at Azhi did not stay with me.  
They set up their own gates. They became seats of kings.  
[[pol:246|Kasheve]] sits in a town I took. It does not call me mother.  
[[pol:248|Kheshkheze]] sits in a town I took. It does not call me mother.  
[[pol:247|Piève]] has shut its road against me. It does not call me mother.  
What is given in a good year is taken in a bad one.  

**The women sing:**

Who is this that still keeps the name?  
It is [[pol:245|Nelo]], the daughter of Nelo, sitting in the old house.  
In [[set:5|Nelo]] the gate still stands; the old house still stands.  
But the daughter is small, and her granary is small.  
Once a measure of grain cost less than a lièlo in the market of Nelo;  
the grain is still cheap, but who comes to buy it?  

**Nelo speaks:**

[[dei:31|Kombe]] of the moon, you have seen it all, month by month.  
Kombe, write it on the night, for my scribes are few.  
Priests of [[mov:4|Khopande]], do not stop your songs.  
The wheel turns, you say; what ends will begin again.  
Then turn, wheel. Turn back to the four roads.  
Bring the ships of Bombe to my quay again.  
Bring the daughters home to the mother.  

**The women of the gate sing:**

Rise, lady, rise from the shore. Bind your hair. Eat bread.  
The wheel turns. We will wait with you by the sea.

*Written in the hundred and eighty-second year of Nelo, in the seventeenth year after the breaking.*
