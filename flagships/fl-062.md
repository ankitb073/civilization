To [[dei:28|Khudhas]], lord of the sky, great god of [[pol:579|Kashunji]], speak! Thus says [[per:13225|Nalüving]], your servant, the king, who holds the reins of Kashunji in your name:

You sent me to Kambizhin as your envoy. I went as your envoy. Now I bring back the report of my going, as a messenger brings back the words of the far country. Let it be set in your house, beside the tablets of my fathers.

Hear first why I went. The king of [[pol:587|Kambizhin]] held back the gifts. He sent no silver, he sent no oxen, he sent no grain to the house of Kashunji. His words were proud; his face was turned away. So I took up [[evt:13369|the war against Kambizhin]]. Four times my host went down to Hunjingivji. In the first battle I went myself, I, Nalüving, with my spear. Four times the host of Kambizhin stood, and four times it ran. At [[evt:13410|Hunjingivji in the fourth year]] they ran. At [[evt:13423|Hunjingivji in the fifth year]] they ran, and their king ran before them. [[dei:32|Nijinandi]] of the battle went at my right hand.

Then I came to the city of [[set:33|Kambizhin]]. Its gate I broke. Its storehouses I opened. This was [[evt:13424|the third time Kambizhin was sacked]], and its people sat in the dust and wept.

Hear now the words that were spoken.

On the first day the envoys of Kambizhin came out of the gate. They bowed seven times. They brought a jar of oil and a lamb. They said: "Our lord is your brother. Take your host home, and he will send gifts as before." I said: "Is the dog the brother of the lion? Go back and say to your lord: I will speak only with him."

On the second day [[per:13379|Namumbivu]], king of Kambizhin, came out of the gate. He had put off his fine robe. He bowed seven times and seven times. He said: "Take the city's silver, and let the land stay mine." I said: "The silver I have already taken. It is the land I want."

On the third day the scribes of both houses sat together, and the boundaries were spoken. These are the words. Fourteen districts in Ng'jinaguh and in Khanji, and the town of Hunjingivji with them, they go to Kashunji. Every year Kambizhin will send tribute to Kashunji, weighed in silver kavu by [[cur:290|kavu]], for thirty-six years. For ten years there will be no war between us.

Namumbivu said: "Thirty-six years is longer than my life." I said: "Then your son will pay it." He was silent. Then he said: "It is so."

So was [[evt:13425|the oath of Kambizhin]] sworn. We ate the salt. We poured oil on the stone. I swore by Khudhas of the sky, by [[dei:29|Bumbi]] of the sea, by [[dei:30|Ugash]] of the sun, by [[dei:31|Kumbi]] of the moon, by Nijinandi of the battle. Namumbivu swore by his own gods, and he swore by mine also, for they had shown him their strength.

Hear, O Khudhas. I lost few men. The king of Kambizhin lost many. I did not burn the house of his gods. I did not drive out his household. I left him his city and his seat, for a king who is left his seat pays his tribute, and a king who is driven out pays nothing.

Other kings also came to the gates of Kambizhin in that year, and took their share of it. Since then another king has swallowed what was left, and Namumbivu sits no more upon his seat. Let them have the bones. Kashunji took the best share, and the fourteen districts are held, and they were taken with your favour.

This is my report. I set it down in the twenty-third year of my kingship, in the city of [[set:46|Kashunji]], according to the law of [[mov:4|Khuandi]]. May the wheel of the years turn, and may the oath sworn at Kambizhin be remembered in your house as long as the sun comes up over the hills.
