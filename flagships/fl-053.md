To the elders of [[pol:313|Pönedöyedhdey]], my brothers, who are in the hills, say:

Thus says [[per:8331|Köbethon]], king of Pönedöyedhdey, your brother. I went down as an envoy goes down. I went down as my own envoy, for there was no other to send. Hear what was spoken.

My scribes are scattered. My scribes are dead. This tablet is written by a scribe of [[pol:306|Yeda]], in the tongue of Yeda, and the year is counted by the years of their queen, as they command. It is the 3rd year of Shetkoshenlamath. So the clay says. Let the clay say it.

Hear how it came to this. In the year that I took the seat of my fathers, Yeda came against us in the [[evt:8002|war of Yeda against Pönedöyedhdey]]. They were many as grasshoppers are many. We were few. At [[evt:8035|Nodhinge]] they came on, and we gave ground. At [[evt:8045|Navnge]] I stood myself, and the sky was against me; [[dei:16|Domamnol]] the sky-god of Yeda sent dust into the eyes of my men. At [[evt:8053|Bakhriytil]] they came on again, ten men of theirs for one man of ours. Not many of ours fell. Not many. But a man cannot hold a gate with a hand.

Then the gate of my city was opened. It was not opened by the spear. It was opened from within, by men who ate my bread. Let their names be cut from the clay. So came the [[evt:8054|sack of Ontiedzey]]. [[dei:18|Eva]] the fire-god of Yeda went through the streets. The roofs fell. The granaries burned. The women cried on the walls. I saw it. My eyes saw it.

Then the great men of Yeda said: let Köbethon come down. I came down. I came down into the camp before [[set:54|Pönedöyedhdey]], my own city, as a stranger comes. I took off my sandals. I bowed seven times and seven times.

There sat [[per:8361|Shetkoshenlamath]], queen of Yeda, on a stool of cedar. She is a child. Her feet do not reach the ground. Her great men stood about her, and they spoke for her, and she said nothing.

The great men of Yeda said: give us the two towns. I said: the two towns are the towns of my fathers. They said: give us the two towns, and the land of Mana. I said: the land of Mana feeds my people. They said: give us the two towns, and the land of Mana, or we go up into the hills.

I gave them silver. I gave them [[cur:164|Nevba]] of silver from my own neck, that their words might be soft. Their words were not soft.

So it was sworn, the [[evt:8055|peace of Ontiedzey]]. For ten years no spear shall go up between Yeda and Pönedöyedhdey. They swore by [[dei:14|Volme]], their war-goddess, by Domamnol, by Eva, by all the gods of [[mov:2|Jenro]] who turn the great wheel. I swore by the gods of my fathers. They wrote my name in their own manner; they wrote it as a king's name. That they did.

Then they took up their tents. They go back to [[set:0|Tallen]] with the spoil of my city on the backs of their asses.

Brothers, hear me. We were not beaten by men. We were beaten by the gate that was opened, and by the gods who looked away. The wheel turns. What is ended will begin again. Come down from the hills. Rebuild the granaries. Do not say that Köbethon gave away the land. Say that Köbethon kept the hills, and kept the people, and kept the name of Pönedöyedhdey.

With me it is not well. With my house it is not well. But I live. Send word quickly.
