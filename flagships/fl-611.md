In AY 2070, three days after a battle she had lost, the queen of Nilu wrote home from her camp. Her army had been driven from the field by the king of [[pol:629|Puvu]], and she wanted her people to hear the truth before rumour did. "Let Puvu call that a victory," she wrote.
