title: The Apology of Ba'amamshe, Who Took the Seat of Ana
---
Thus says [[per:149|Ba'amamshe]], king of [[pol:6|Ana]], beloved of [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky, chosen of [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] of the spear.

Hear my words, men of Ana. Hear them, priests of [[mov:0|La'im]]. Hear them, mothers of the city. Let no one say that Ba'amamshe sat in the seat by night, like a thief. I sat in it by day, before the eyes of the gods. Hear how it was, and judge.

The old king went down to the wheel. He was a good king. I poured oil for him with my own hand. His son sat in the seat after him, [[per:145|Tlamfen Nieme]], a boy, thirteen summers old. I bowed my neck to the boy. I said: "My lord." Let the priests say whether I said it. They heard it.

But the boy was a boy. His hand was small. The staff was heavy in it. The elders quarrelled in the gate. The storehouse keepers stole from the storehouse. The herdsmen of the hills said: "There is no king in Ana," and they did not bring their sheep. The grain sat in the jars and was not given out. Men went hungry in a year of good barley. Was this the will of the gods? It was not the will of the gods.

Then [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] the moon turned his face from the boy. A fever came on him in the dark of the month. The healers burned their herbs. The priests poured their oil. I sent a ram from my own fold to the house of Atlie for him. Let them say whether I sent it. They saw it. The fever did not go. The boy went down to the wheel, as his father went down. I did not lift my hand against him. Let Atlie strike me if I lifted my hand. Let Nieshashta break my spear.

Then the seat was empty. And who was there? The boy had no brother. The elders quarrelled more. Men took up spears in the street. I said: "Enough." I came to the gate with my spearmen. I sat in the seat before the men took up spears against each other. This is the [[evt:181|Usurpation of Ba'amamshe]], as my enemies name it. I name it the saving of [[set:7|Ana]].

Hear what the gods have shown since that day.

The herdsmen have come down from the hills with their sheep. The storehouse is opened and the grain is given out. A measure of barley costs less than a [[cur:0|Sovosh]] in the market of Ana, and the poor man eats. [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the earth has opened her hand. [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river has risen in his season, not too high and not too low. Is this the sign of gods who are angry? It is the sign of gods who are pleased. Prosperity is the favour of the gods. Look at it, and know.

Hear what I swear before [[dei:1|Uelti]] of wisdom.

The mother of the boy, the widow of the old king, I have not driven her out. She sits in her own house. She eats from the king's table. Her women serve her. Let no man raise his hand against her, or he will answer to me.

The priests I have not driven out. Their houses I have filled with oil and barley. Their order I keep, each god in the god's own place.

The men who quarrelled in the gate, I have not killed. I have sent them home to their fields.

So I have done. So I will do. Let the scribes write it on the clay and set it in the house of Atlie, so that the sons of my sons may read it and say: "Ba'amamshe did not take the seat. The gods gave it."

Written in the first year of Ba'amamshe, king of Ana.
