title: Hymn for the Victory at Listlitl

*To be sung in the houses of the gods, the elder choir first and the younger answering.*

Sing, priests of the [[mov:25|Mieteyeshio]], in your ordered ranks,  
sing as you were taught, the elder to the younger;  
let every god be praised in the place that is theirs,  
let no god be forgotten, and no god be praised beyond their due,  
for the gods are many, and each one keeps a station.

*Glory to the gods, each in their place;*  
*glory to Queen [[per:28881|I]], who gave each god their due.*

In the days of our fathers [[pol:1172|Miezhitl]] rose against us,  
they came up against [[pol:1212|Tomzel]] with their banners and their spears,  
and the gods turned them back, and they went home ashamed.  
Yet they did not learn their place; they did not keep their borders;  
they sat in [[reg:17|Niz]] as a thief sits in another man's house.

When the Queen took up the crown the war was already kindled.  
She did not quench it with gold, she did not buy a shameful peace;  
she called her lords, and her lords came with their sworn men,  
each lord as he was bound, each man behind his lord,  
as the stars come out in order behind the evening star.

At [[set:260|Ove]] rode [[per:29370|Yeze]], the Queen's faithful servant,  
and [[per:29248|Ai Li'itla]] stood against him and was broken.  
Before Listlitl Yeze stood a second time, and the enemy fled before him.  
Now Yeze sleeps with his fathers, and the priests have sung him home.

Seven times the hosts met before [[set:134|Listlitl]];  
seven times the river ran red beneath its walls.  
Twice the gods tried us there, as the smith tries silver in the fire,  
and once again at Ove they turned their faces from us for a season,  
so that we should not grow proud, so that we should remember who gives.  
But eight times they gave us the field, and the field was ours.

Sing of [[per:29407|Pitelmamshe]], who did not lose heart:  
at Listlitl twenty thousand of Miezhitl lay down before him,  
and their queen [[per:29291|I Tli]] went back to her halls in tears.  
Sing of him at [[set:214|Lekhtli]], where [[per:29464|Khaithi]] could not hold the road.

*Glory to the gods, each in their place;*  
*glory to the Queen, who gave each god their due.*

At Lekhtli the Queen herself went down into the battle,  
and Ai Li'itla came out to meet her, he who had fought us at Ove and Listlitl,  
and there he fell, and he did not rise again,  
and the crows of Niz feasted on the pride of Miezhitl.

In the sixteenth summer the Queen came again to Listlitl,  
and the last host of Miezhitl was scattered like chaff.  
Then I Tli came down from her seat with her nobles and her seals,  
and she set her hand to the [[evt:29917|Treaty of Listlitl]], and gave up the land:  
Listlitl she gave, and Lekhtli she gave, and Niz with its fields,  
and fifteen years of truce she swore before the gods.

You lords, do not boast; you soldiers, do not swell with pride.  
The wise turn their faces from spoil, and the gods love moderation.  
Bring the tithe of the plunder to the priests in good [[cur:687|sozhne]],  
give the widow her portion and the orphan his bread,  
let justice be done in the land that the gods have given.

Let the bells of [[set:264|Ölee]] ring from the morning to the evening;  
let the lamps burn before every altar, the great and the small;  
let the sky have its incense, and the earth its offering, and the dead their rest.

*Glory to the gods, each in their place;*  
*glory to Queen [[per:28881|I]], who gave each god their due,*  
*now and in the years that the gods appoint.*

*Here ends the hymn for the victory, made in the 15th year of I, Queen of Tomzel.*
