*[Obverse]*

Weep, O [[set:3|Shue'ethie]], weep, city of the river!  
Weep, O wall, weep, O gate, weep, O threshing floor!  
The king has gone down. The king has gone down to the wheel.  
[[per:35|Shuesh'anieme]] has gone down, and he does not come up.  

Seven times the barley has come up since he went down.  
Seven times [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the earth has opened her hand.  
Seven times the barley has come up. The king does not come up.  
Weep, O Shue'ethie. Weep again.  

He was the bull of the city. He was the tall cedar.  
He was the high wall when the enemy came.  
When the seat was empty, he sat in it.  
When the city was afraid, he stood in the gate.  
Seventeen years he sat in the seat. Seventeen years the gate held.  

[[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky, why did you take him?  
[[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river, why did you carry him away?  
Atlie said: "I did not take him. The wheel took him."  
Niele'i said: "I did not carry him. The wheel carried him."  
What ends will begin again. The wheel turns. The wheel turns.  

*[Reverse]*

The women of the house cut their hair for him.  
The men of the house put ash on their heads for him.  
The oxen did not eat their grain. The dogs lay down in the gate.  
The priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] poured oil for him in order,  
oil to Atlie, oil to Muevas'e, oil to Niele'i,  
oil to [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] the moon, who keeps the dead by night.  
They laid him down with his spear and his cup.  
They laid him down with bread for the road.  

Weep, O Shue'ethie. But hear now: do not weep too long.  

His daughter sits in his seat. [[per:56|Todifoyatl]] sits in his seat,  
the young lioness, the daughter of the bull.  
She took the staff from his cold hand. She took the seat when he went down.  
This was the [[evt:115|Accession of Todifoyatl]]. The city shouted her name.  
Eight years she has sat in the seat. Eight years the gate has held.  

O [[dei:1|Uelti]] of wisdom, put your word in her mouth, as you put it in his.  
O [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] of the spear, put your strength in her arm, as you put it in his.  
Let the enemy see her and say: "The bull is not dead. The bull stands in the gate."  
Let [[pol:0|Shue'ethie]] see her and say: "The cedar has put out a new branch."  

Weep, O Shue'ethie, for Shuesh'anieme who has gone down.  
Rejoice, O Shue'ethie, for Todifoyatl who stands up.  
The king has gone down. The queen stands up.  
What ends will begin again. The wheel turns. The wheel turns.  

*[Rubric]* To be sung by the women at the tomb, on the day of his going down, every year. Written in the 8th year of Todifoyatl.
