*[Obverse]*

In the 712th year of Tagath, before the priests of [[dei:76|Tsilmu]], lord of wisdom, at the shrine by the threshing floors of [[reg:24|Pyivi]],  
a man of the village of the two wells came and knelt and spoke. He said:

> Hear me, Tsilmu, lord of wisdom, who knows what men hide.  
> Hear me, Pugazhu, sun, who saw me run.  
> I am a deserter. Twenty-six years I have not said it. Now I say it.

> In the days of the war, the [[evt:3096|First Tagath–Nelo War]], I was a young man. I had a wife one year. The king's men came to the village. They said: [[pol:3|Tagath]] goes to take Pyivi from the men of [[pol:80|Nilu]]. Every house gives a man. My father was old. I went.

> We marched under the lord [[per:3478|Natsuthandi]]. We were many. We said: Nilu will run. We came to the place by [[set:169|Kaküni]]. That was the [[evt:3114|Battle of Ajü]].

> The men of Nilu stood. They did not run. They stood, and the arrows came down like hail on the barley. The man on my right was the potter's son of our village. He fell. The man on my left, I did not know him. He fell. In front of me men fell. Behind me men fell. The lord Natsuthandi's horn blew, and blew, and blew. I did not know what it said.

> I threw down my spear. I threw down my shield. I ran. I ran into the reeds by the stream. I lay in the water to my mouth until dark. I heard the men of Nilu walking in the reeds. Pugazhu went down. I came out. I walked by night. I did not go home. A man who runs from the king does not go home.

*[Reverse]*

> Afterward men said: of every two who stood in our line at Ajü, almost one did not come home. I do not know the number. I know the potter's son.

> I went to the far side of Pyivi. I said I was a man of another village whose house had burned. I herded goats for a widow. Later I married her daughter, though I had a wife. Tsilmu, you know this also. I say it.

> I heard of the other battles. I heard of Khavazhu. I heard of Aguga, where the lady [[per:3376|Naluyazi]] led our men and lost, and then sat down with the lady who led Nilu and made the [[evt:3129|Treaty of Aguga]]. Peace for ten years, the criers said. That was all. All those men, for ten years of peace and not one field of Pyivi.

> Then Kaküni rose up against the king, and the lady Naluyazi put it down at Dadavli. Then, eleven years ago, the [[evt:3241|Collapse of the Tagath State]]. The kingdom broke like a pot. Now there are three kingdoms where there was one. The kingdom I ran from is no more. The scribes who wrote my name are gone.

> So I say it now. My first wife, I hear, is dead. My son by her, I hear, is alive, in the village of the two wells. I would see him. I would give him the two [[cur:28|Ndül]] I have saved. I ask the priests: is a man still a deserter when the kingdom he ran from is gone?

The priests of Tsilmu heard him. They said: the gods are not a kingdom. They do not break.  
They said: bring a kid without blemish to the shrine of Binge, lady of the grain, and one Ndül to the house of Tsilmu.  
Barley is one Ndül and a fifth the measure; let the second Ndül go to your son.  
Then go to the village of the two wells, and say there what you said here.
