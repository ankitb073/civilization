*[Obverse. A small tablet, closely written; the scribe has ruled a line between each day.]*

In the 49th year of [[per:352|Tavin]], king of [[pol:9|Pumi]], beloved of [[dei:14|Vulme]], I begin this tablet. I am a scribe of the house of grain. The king's steward said: "Count the jars, count the days." I count them. Let [[dei:16|Dumamnul]] of the high sky see that I count them truly.

**The first day.** The men came back from Nilu. They came back in the evening, through the gate of the river. Few came back. The king came back; Vulme spared the king. The king did not speak. This was the [[evt:399|Battle of Nilu]]. The field was not ours. The women went up on the roof and cried out the names. Many names were cried out and not answered.

**The second day.** Dust in the west. Dust in the south. The men of [[pol:1|Tallin]] have come. They sit before our wall. Their tents are many as the stars of Dumamnul. The gate is shut. The gate is barred with cedar and with bronze. This is the beginning of the siege. This is the [[evt:400|Siege of Himi]].

**The third day.** I counted the jars of the house of grain. Barley: many jars. Oil: fewer. The steward said: "Count again." I counted again. The number did not change.

**The fifth day.** The men of Tallin call to us from below the wall. They say: "Open, and live." We do not open. They pray to Vulme as we pray to Vulme. Whom will she hear? The priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] say: she hears the one who is in the right. We are in the right. This is our land. This is the [[evt:397|First Tallin–Pumi War]]; they came for our land, we did not go for theirs.

**The eighth day.** They brought ladders in the night. Our men threw them down. Hot oil, stones, the jars of the potters. Three of ours fell from the wall. Many of theirs fell into the ditch. [[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire was with us; the oil burned well.

**The tenth day.** No water from the river gate; they hold it. The well of the temple is deep. Vinba of the earth, keep the well full.

**The thirteenth day.** I counted the jars. Barley: fewer. A measure of barley at the market now costs what a measure of oil cost before; the [[cur:5|Ineth]] buys little. The steward has closed the market. The king gives out barley from the house of grain by the measure, to each house by its mouths.

**The fifteenth day.** The new moon. We gave the white kid to [[dei:19|Yenan]] of the night. Yenan showed his face over their camp, and the priests said: a sign. A sign of what, the priests did not say.

**The eighteenth day.** A child died in the street of the weavers. The belly-sickness. Another child.

**The twentieth day.** The king went up on the wall. He stood where the men of Tallin could see him. He said to them: "I am Tavin. Forty-nine years the gods have kept me on the seat of Pumi. Go home." They shouted. He did not move. Our men shouted back.

**The twenty-second day.** I counted the jars. Barley: still some. Oil: few. Wine: none; the priests have it. The steward said: "Do not write that." I have written it. Let Dumamnul see it.

**The twenty-fifth day.** They are building something of timber before the gate. The men say: a roof, to bring the ram under. Our men sharpen the bronze. The women carry stones up the stair.

**The twenty-sixth day.** [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]] of the grain, the barley in our fields outside the wall is ripe. The men of Tallin are cutting it. They are eating our harvest before our eyes. Nidhikhti, choke them with it.

*[Reverse.]*

**The twenty-eighth day.** The gate holds. The wall holds. [[set:17|Pumi]] holds. Himi of the high gate holds. I have one tablet more of clay; when it is full I do not know what clay I will write upon. All things turn on the great wheel, say the priests; what ends will begin again. Vulme, lady of the spear, turn it for Pumi. I count the days. I count the jars. I count.
