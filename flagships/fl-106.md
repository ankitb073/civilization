[[per:33274|Tlamfin Tlyi]], least of the servants of the One, keeper of the lamp at [[set:297|Ma'itli]], to all who call themselves faithful in the Empire of [[pol:1375|Muvush]], to the priests and the ordained, to the lords and their households, to the poor and to those who keep the poor: grace from the One Power, before whom every god is a shadow and every crown a borrowed thing.

**I.** Let it be known that this letter is to be read aloud at the doors of every house of prayer, in the year 3057 of the [[mov:25|Myitiyishiu]], as the reckoning of the Empire runs, and let no priest hide it under his sleeve.

**II.** You know, brothers, that we were born in the house of the [[mov:58|Vishithmikhi]], and were washed in it, and ordained in it, and that we loved it. You know that our fathers in that faith taught that time runs in one line from the making of the world to its judgement; that there is one divine power, and the old gods are its servants or shadows; that the good life is compassion and release from suffering; that the message is for all peoples. We do not deny one word of this. We say that you have denied it, not with your mouths but with your hands.

**III.** You say the old gods are shadows, yet you light lamps before them in the market, lest the merchants be offended. Is a shadow to be fed? Is a servant to be bowed to in the presence of his master? It is written that whoever bows to the shadow turns his back to the light. We say: the One alone. There is no second. The shadows are shadows.

**IV.** You say the good life is compassion. When the armies of our lord the emperor went out to [[evt:33578|Ashmath]] and to [[evt:33587|Litlitli]], and the wounded lay in the fields in their thousands, where were you? You were in your halls, counting the tithe. When [[evt:33588|the peace of Litlitli]] was sworn and the conquered became the vassals of Muvush, did you go to them? Did you carry them bread? You did not. You said: they are not ours. But the message is for all peoples, or it is for none.

**V.** You say the priesthood keeps the truth in order. So it does, and we honour the order; but an order that keeps the truth in a locked chest has become a jailer, not a keeper.

**VI.** Therefore, having prayed nine days and fasted three, having taken counsel with the brothers at Ma'itli, we declare:

> That those priests of the Vishithmikhi who set lamps before the shadows, who stayed in their halls while the wounded lay in the field, and who say of any people "they are not ours," are cut off from the communion of the faithful.  
> That no faithful man shall take bread from their hand, nor give his children to be washed by them, nor bury his dead by their rite, until they repent openly before the people.  
> That those who repent shall be received again without price, for compassion is not sold.

**VII.** Let no one say that we rise against the Empire. To our lord [[per:33112|Yismi]], emperor in [[set:14|Muvush]], we render all that is his: the tax in [[cur:761|Antli]] at the due season, the levy, the watch on the walls, the oath of the household. Our fathers in the faith said it, and we say it again: the body owes the lord; the soul owes the One.

**VIII.** Let those who will walk with us be called [[mov:129|Myituwize]], the cut-off who cut nothing from the truth. We take no new name for the One, and no new book. We take only the old word, and do it.

**IX.** The judgement comes in its time. It will not ask what house you were washed in. It will ask whom you fed.

Given at Ma'itli, in [[evt:33712|the year of our separation]], under my hand and the seal of the lamp.
