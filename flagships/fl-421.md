*[Obverse.]*

I am [[per:2|Ba'alie'an]]. I am the king of [[pol:2|Mieshlothie]]. I was the first to sit on the seat of Mieshlothie; no man sat on it before me. Twenty-six years I sat on it. Twenty-six harvests I weighed at the gate. Twenty-six times I poured the oil to [[dei:0|Atlie]] at the new year. Let Atlie say if I failed him once.

Now hear what was done in the year they call the 1st year of Ma'she Simen.

[[per:45|Ma'she Simen]] ate my bread. Ma'she Simen sat at my left hand. Ma'she Simen held my cup. I gave him oxen. I gave him a house by the river. I gave him the spears of the east gate to command.

He took the spears of the east gate. He went in by night. He did not go in by the gate of the city; he went in by the gate of my house. He put my servants out. He put my seal on his own neck. He sat on my seat. He said to the people: "The old man is old. The old man is finished." He said to the priests: "Pour the oil for me." The priests were afraid. The priests poured the oil.

I am old. That is true. My beard is white. That is true. But the seat is not a cloak that a younger man takes from an older man in the night. The seat is given by the gods. Who gave it to Ma'she Simen? Not Atlie. Not Uelti. He gave it to himself.

Therefore hear, gods of [[mov:0|La'im]]. Hear, gods of [[set:2|Mieshlothie]]. I say the curse. Let the scribe press it deep.

*[The curse.]*

May Atlie, father of the high sky, shut the sky over him; may no rain fall on his field.  
May [[dei:1|Uelti]], keeper of words, take the words from his mouth; may he speak and not be heard.  
May [[dei:2|Muevas'e]], mother of the black earth, close her lap; may his barley not come up.  
May [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river turn the water from his canal; may his ox die of thirst.  
May [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] of the night hide his light; may he walk in the dark and find no road.  
May [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] of the red feet, lady of the spear, break his spear in his hand on the day he needs it.  

May his seat be hard under him. May his bread be sand in his mouth.  
May his sons quarrel at his door. May his name be scraped from the clay.  
As he went in by night, may he go out by night.  
As he took the seat from another, may another take the seat from him.  

*[Reverse.]*

And you who come after, scribe or priest or king: if you find this tablet, do not break it. If you break it, the curse is on you also. If you scrape the name of Ma'she Simen from it to save him, the curse is on you also.

If you read it aloud at the gate, may Atlie bless you. If you pour oil for the old king, may Muevas'e give you sons.

I, Ba'alie'an, have said it. The wheel turns, say the priests; what ends will begin again. I have seen the wheel turn twenty-six times. Let it turn once more, and turn against [[per:45|Ma'she Simen]].

*[Colophon.]*

> Written in Mieshlothie, in the house where they keep the old king, in the year they call the 1st year of Ma'she Simen. The scribe's hand shook. The king's did not.
