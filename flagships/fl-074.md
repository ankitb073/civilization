In the 397th year of [[pol:93|Mii]]. It is the last year. Let it be written so.

[[dei:0|Atlia]], lord of the sky, hear the court. [[dei:1|Uelti]], lord of wisdom, hear the court. Muevas'a, mother earth, who receives all things, hear the court. Lashla'a of the night, Niele'i of the river, Niezhashta of the spear, hear the court. The court of Mii speaks for the last time.

**What was.** Mii was great. Mii was one. From the sea to the hills, one seal; from the river to the river, one seal. The kings of Mii sat in [[set:7|Ana]], and the lords of the cities brought silver, and the priests gave the right sacrifice, and the gods were glad.

**How it was broken.** Hear how it was broken, so that the sons of our sons may know.

The gods sent [[evt:4890|the River Fever]]. It came down the water. It went into the houses. In the house of the potter one in five lay down; in the house of the lord one in five lay down. The fields had no hands.

Then our sons went out to [[evt:5000|Itlishithyi]]. They were many, as the reeds are many. A small people stood against them. Our sons were cut down like reeds. The mothers of Ana tore their hair. The gods turned their faces.

Then the lords of Miezanitie said: "We will not bring silver to Ana." That was [[evt:5219|the going-away of Miezanitie]]. Then Miezanitie came against us. At [[evt:5277|Nyinsitlasa]] the army of Mii stood, and the army of Mii was broken. Brother fought brother four more years; in the end the court brought Miezanitie back under the seal, but the wound did not close.

Then La'im rose in Namos'ash. That was [[evt:5343|the revolt of La'im]]. The court sent its army and brought La'im back, but the strength of Mii was spent.

And in the 396th year the seal of Mii was broken into six pieces. That was [[evt:5441|the breaking of Mii]]. Now six lords sit where one sat. [[pol:230|Miezanitia]] has a lord. [[pol:231|Yi'otl]] has a lord. [[pol:232|Namos'ash]] has a lord. [[pol:233|Fa'amni]] has a lord. [[pol:234|Mobosh]] has a lord. And here in Ana, in our own city, [[evt:5442|Ana has risen]] with its own lord. The court does not curse them. The court does not bless them. The court is silent.

**The decree.** Hear the last decree of the court of Mii.

The seal of Mii shall be broken with a hammer of bronze before the house of Atlia. Its pieces shall be buried under the threshold of the god, where no man will take them up.

The tablets of the court shall be carried to the house of Uelti. The scribes shall seal the door with clay. Let no new lord break the clay. What is written is written.

The soldiers of Mii are free of their oath. Let each man go to his own house. Let no man say the soldiers of Mii ran; the court released them.

The silver of the treasury, the [[cur:65|Lash'a]] that remains, shall be given to the houses of the gods, a share to each god, as is right. Let no lord take it.

The governors of the cities are free of their oath. Let them serve whom the gods give them.

The great lady of Mii, our lady, whose name the gods know, lays down the staff of her fathers. She goes to her own house. Let no man lift a hand against her; she has done no wrong but to live in the last year.

**The prayer.** The priests teach: all things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again. So the court believes. So the court says. Mii has ended. If the gods will it, Mii will begin again. When the wheel turns, let the one who finds this tablet under the clay of Uelti read it aloud, and say: once Mii was one.

[[pol:93|Mii]], farewell. Here the court of Mii is ended.
