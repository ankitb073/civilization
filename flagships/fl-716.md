*[Obverse]*

O [[dei:22|Vinba]], mother earth, broad earth, hear me.  
O [[dei:24|Navbuthen]], river, mother of the fields, hear me.  
I am the wife of a farmer of [[reg:19|Mankha]]. I set down the hungry years.

In the 21st year of [[per:9|Banathbin]], the king, the shepherd of [[pol:8|Navnga]]:  
The rain did not come. The rain did not come in the month of sowing.  
The river was low. The river showed its stones.  
We sowed the barley. The barley came up thin. The barley came up yellow.  
We cut the barley. Three baskets. Last year, nine baskets.  
My husband said: we will eat little. We ate little.

In the 22nd year of Banathbin, the king:  
The rain did not come. [[dei:21|Duminu]], storm, lord of the clouds, did not speak.  
We gave Duminu a kid. We gave Duminu the fat of the kid. Duminu did not speak.  
The barley did not come up. The lentils did not come up.  
We ate the seed barley. We ate the lentils of the seed.  
We gave the ox for two baskets of barley. We gave the copper pot for one basket.  
My husband's mother lay down. She did not rise. We put her in the ground.  
The ground was hard. The ground was hard as a pot.  
This was the [[evt:104|Famine in Mana]]. All the land was hungry.

In the 23rd year of Banathbin, the king:  
The children ate grass. The children ate the bark of the tamarisk.  
My little son lay down. He did not rise. I put him in the ground by his grandmother.  
The house of the potter is empty. The house of the reed-cutter is empty.  
Some lay down. Some took the road. Of the houses in our lane, the empty ones I count on my fingers.  
The men of the hills went away with their goats and their children.  
They said: in Mikhukhizhi there is grass. They went. They did not come back.  
The king's men came to the village from [[set:10|Navnga]].  
The king's men gave each house one basket of barley from the storehouse of the king.  
May Banathbin live. May his storehouse be full.

*[Reverse]*

In the 24th year of Banathbin, the king:  
The rain came. The rain came in the month of sowing.  
Navbuthen rose. Navbuthen covered the stones.  
We had no seed. My husband went to the village of my brother.  
My brother gave seed. We sowed. The barley came up green.  
We cut the barley. Five baskets. We wept on the threshing floor.  
We gave the first sheaf to Vinba. We gave the second sheaf to Duminu.

In the 25th year of Banathbin, the king, now:  
The barley is in the field. The barley is green. The river is full.  
I have two children. I had three.  
My husband has a new ox. The new ox is small. He works.  
The Unsatlmum, the people of the hills, still go out on the road. Every month we see them.  
Men speak of it: the [[evt:105|Migration of the Unsatlmum into Mikhukhizhi]].  
I do not know that land. I know the road is long.  
The king has sat on his seat twenty-five years since the [[evt:76|Founding of Navnga]].  
May he sit twenty-five more.

O Vinba, mother earth, do not shut your womb again.  
O Duminu, storm, speak in your season.  
O Navbuthen, river, do not show your stones.  
I have written the hungry years so my daughter will know.  
When the barley is high, let her remember the bark of the tamarisk.  
Let her remember her brother by the hard ground.  
Let her keep seed. Let her always keep seed.
