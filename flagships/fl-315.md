*[Envelope, sealed with the seal of the camp]*

To the king. To the king's hand only.

*[Tablet, obverse]*

To my lord [[per:819|Nitllu Ash]], king of [[pol:13|Tivintusa]], the bright one, the shepherd of his people, say: thus says your servant, the keeper of the tally of the host in the land of [[reg:19|Man'a]].

May [[dei:88|Mastu]] the sun shine on my lord. May [[dei:83|Sada]] of the sky stretch his hand over my lord. May [[dei:85|Nil'i]] of the river fill the canals of my lord. May the house of my lord be well. May the city of [[set:13|Tivintusa]] be well.

With the host it is not well. Let my lord hear it from his servant before he hears it from the road.

In the fifth year of my lord, in the month of the green barley, the host of my lord went down to [[set:55|Yuftuyi]]. The general [[per:827|I'a Mavi]] set the men in order: the spears in front, the bows behind, the war-carts on the left hand by the water. I counted them on the day before. Four thousand and three hundred and thirty-one were the men of my lord. I counted them with my own reed; my lord may trust the count.

Out of Yuftuyi came the men of [[pol:8|Navna]]. [[per:824|Lukhnaluni]] led them, the general of Navna, he who rides a white ass. Three thousand and one hundred and thirty-four were they; so the scouts counted them.

At the first light the spears met. The ground by the water was soft. The war-carts went down into the mud, the wheels went down into the mud. The men of Navna came round on the right hand. The men of my lord were pressed together like reeds in a bundle. At midday the host of my lord turned its back. That is the [[evt:628|Battle of Yuftuy]]. I do not hide it from my lord.

*[Reverse]*

Of the men of my lord, one thousand and two hundred and eleven did not answer at the evening count. Of the men of Navna, two hundred and forty-one lay on the field; I saw them with my eyes. The priests of the camp poured beer to [[dei:87|Sutlpi]] of the house below, so that she may receive our dead with bread and not with dust. The priests poured water to [[dei:84|Lashli'i]] of the moon. The moon rose red. The priests say: "It is a sign of anger, not of ruin."

The general I'a Mavi is whole. He has eaten bread. He has drunk water. He said to me: "Write to my lord. Say: the men of Navna stand on their own ground, and so they stood. Let my lord send more spears, and they will not stand again."

Now let my lord hear the needs of the host. The men have barley for one month, no more. Let my lord open the storehouses of [[dei:86|Shu'i]]. Let my lord send bronze for the spearheads that broke. Let my lord send silver, by the weight of the [[cur:3|Awa]], for the boatmen of the river, who will not carry without silver. The men who are wounded I have sent back on the boats with the grain-boat captains.

Since the [[evt:625|Tivintusa–Navnga War]] went out, the gods of Tivintusa have not turned away from Tivintusa. The wheel of the year is not ended. The barley of Man'a is green yet; it will be gold, and the sickle of my lord will cut it.

Let my lord write to his servant quickly. Let the answer come by the river, by the fast boat. Let my lord not be troubled in his heart.

*[Left edge]*

Written in the camp by Yuftuyi. Sealed with the seal of the camp. The tablet is not to be opened on the road.
