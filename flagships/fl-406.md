*[Obverse.]*

To [[per:13|Shuesh'ayueve]], my son, my name, the seed of my house, say: thus says [[per:6|Shuesh'ayueve]], king of [[pol:6|Ana]], your father.

With me it is not well. I say it plainly, for you are not a child to be told lies, though you are a child. My breath is short. My feet are cold. The priests of [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] have been to my bed and have gone away shaking their heads. Soon I will go down to the earth, to the lady of the black earth, and she will hold me as she holds your mother.

Hear me, my son. Your mother is not here to speak to you. She went down to Muevas'e in the year the seat of the king was raised, in the year [[evt:40|Ana became a realm]], when you were a babe at the breast. A queen mother should speak to the young king. A queen mother should stand at his right hand and say, "Do this; do not do that." You have no mother to say it. So I will say it twice: once as your father, once as your mother.

As your father I say: you will sit on the seat of Ana. The gods have given it to our house. [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky gave it, when I set the first stone of the wall of [[set:7|Ana]]. Do not let it go out of the house.

As your mother I say: you are young, and the seat is high. Do not climb it alone. [[per:14|Tlamfen Igwe]] will stand beside you. Tlamfen Igwe has eaten at my table. Tlamfen Igwe has carried my spear. I have set him over the house until your beard comes. Hear him as you would hear me. When he says, "Wait," wait. When he says, "Give," give.

As your father I say: give the gods their due, in order, as the priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] teach. Give the bull to Atlie at the new year. Give the ram to [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] when the spears go out. Give the lamp to [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] at the turning of the month. Pour beer to [[dei:3|Niele'i]] when the river rises. Do not skip a month.

As your mother I say: be gentle with the women of the house. Feed the old servants until they die. Do not shout at the scribes.

The scribes, my son. Hear this above all. In my days [[evt:54|writing was found in Ana]]. Before this, the word of the king went out of his mouth and was lost in the wind. Now it is pressed into clay, and the clay remembers. This tablet will remember me when I am in the earth. Keep the scribes. Keep the houses of the tablets. Let them write the grain that comes in and the grain that goes out, the silver weighed in [[cur:0|Sovosh]], the names of the gods and the gifts given to them. A king whose words are written is not forgotten.

*[Reverse.]*

The wheel turns, my son. What ends will begin again. I end. You begin. Do not weep too long for me; weep in order, as is fitting, and then rise and wash your face and go to the gate and sit in judgement.

May [[dei:1|Uelti]] of wisdom open your ears. May Atlie keep the city. May Muevas'e keep me, and keep your mother, and let us lie together in the black earth.

I have pressed my seal. It is enough.

*[Left edge, in another hand.]*

> This tablet the old king sealed; it was opened before the young king and before Tlamfen Igwe, in the 1st year of Shuesh'ayueve.
