*Tablet of days, kept by a scribe of the storehouse in [[set:31|Waga]]. The beginning is lost.*

[…] the barley was green. The river was full. The priests said: a good year.

**The day the mountain spoke.** In the morning there was a sound in the west. It was like a door shut in the house of the gods. It was like a door shut a second time. The cattle ran. The dogs did not bark; they lay down. The scribe went up on the roof of the storehouse. In the west was a cloud. It stood on the earth like a tree. It was black. It went up to the sky. Men said: [[evt:7940|Mount Onas has spoken]]. Men said: the mountain of the west is burning.

**The second day.** Ash fell. It fell like flour from a sieve. It was grey on the roofs, grey on the barley, grey on the backs of the oxen. The women swept it. It fell again. They swept it again.

**The third day.** The sun did not rise. It rose, and it was not seen. At noon it was like evening. Lamps were lit in the storehouse at noon. The priests of [[dei:0|Etlie]] went up to the high place and cried out. Etlie did not answer.

**The seventh day.** The sun was seen. It was red like a coal. It was small. Men looked at it and were not blinded. The scribe looked at it and was not blinded. This is not right.

**In the month of the harvest.** The barley did not ripen. The ears stood up empty. The ears were white. The rain was cold. The rain was grey with ash. In the fields of the river the farmers wept. The steward said: open the storehouse. The scribe opened the storehouse. The scribe counted out grain. The scribe counted it out by the small measure, not the great measure.

**In that month.** The old king went to his fathers. He had seen the dark sun, and he lay down, and he did not get up. Men said: the mountain took him. The priests said: do not say it.

**In that month.** [[per:8282|Mi'sömamshe]] sat on the throne of [[pol:153|Waga]]. He went up to the house of [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the earth. He poured out oil. He poured out wine. He gave a black bull. He said: Muevas'e, mother, give back the barley. The scribe was there. The scribe saw it.

**In the month after.** This was [[cli:64|the winter of the mountain]], and it came in summer. Snow fell in the hills. The old men said: we have not seen it. The old men's fathers did not see it. Frost was on the vines. The sheep died in the high pastures. The herdsmen came down into the city with nothing.

**On a day of that month.** The price of grain in the market went up, and went up. Men brought [[cur:85|i'a]] of silver to the market and carried home little. The steward sent soldiers to the market. The soldiers stood in the market.

**On the next day.** Men of [[evt:7835|the Ie'i cult]] stood in the street of the potters. They said: the old gods are angry. They said: this is for the blood of [[evt:7856|the war with Tomözel]]. They said: this is for [[evt:7923|Kheye]], which we broke, for the dead of [[evt:7922|Tunavükhini]]. The soldiers drove them out. The scribe writes it down. The scribe does not say whether they lied.

**On the new moon.** [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] the moon rose red. He was red like the sun. The priests of [[mov:0|Le'im]] cut the liver. They did not say what they saw.

**On a day after.** The scribe went down to the river. [[dei:3|Niele'i]] was grey. The fish were floating. The water was bitter.

**This day.** The scribe sets down this tablet. It is the first year of Mi'sömamshe. The sun is still small. The cold is still in the fields. Five years ago we brought home the silver of [[evt:7924|the treaty]] from Kheye, and the people sang in the streets. Now no one sings.

The priests say: all things turn on the great wheel. What ends will begin again. The scribe says: Etlie, father, let the wheel turn. Let the sun come back. Let the barley stand up full. Let the scribe count out grain by the great measure again.

*[The rest of the tablet is broken.]*
