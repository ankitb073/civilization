*[Obverse. Envelope broken; the inner tablet is complete. Five seal impressions on the edges.]*

Before [[dei:83|Sada]] of the high sky, who sees every bargain.  
Before [[dei:88|Mastu]] of the sun, who walks over every road.  
Before [[dei:85|Nil'i]] of the river, who carries every boat.  
Before [[dei:86|Shu'i]] of the grain, who fills every measure.  
Before the ordained priests of [[mov:13|Unsatlmum]], who keep the words in order.

This is the house. This is the charter of the house. The house is called [[hou:4|Sa'litlni]]. It stands in [[set:13|Tivintusa]], by the gate of the merchants, in the street of the oil-sellers. [[per:440|Min'ayuvi]] founded it in the year before this year. Min'ayuvi is the head of the house. While Min'ayuvi lives, his word is the word of the house.

Hear what each man gave into the house.

Min'ayuvi gave one hundred and twenty [[cur:3|Awa]] of silver, weighed at the gate, with the weight of the king.  
His brother gave forty Awa of silver.  
The son of his sister gave thirty Awa of silver and four donkeys with their packs.  
The oil-presser of the upper street gave sixty jars of oil, good oil, sealed.  
The smith of the bronze-quarter gave twenty minas of bronze, cast in bars.  
All this is the purse of the house. The purse is one purse. No man takes back his part while the house stands.

Hear what the house will do.

The house will send oil out to the markets in the jars of the house.  
The house will send bronze up the road on the donkeys of the house.  
The house will bring back spices from the far market, in sealed bags, counted by the mina.  
What the house gains, the house divides at the end of each year, at the feast of Shu'i, after the first sheaf is given.

Hear how the gain is divided.

First, one part of every ten parts goes to the house of Sada, to the priests, for prosperity is the sign of the gods' favour, and the gods must have their share of the sign.  
Then the rest is divided by the purse: as each man gave, so he takes. Min'ayuvi takes one part more, for he walks the road and bears the danger.

Hear what the house will not do.

No man of the house will trade in the name of the house without the word of Min'ayuvi.  
No man of the house will lend the silver of the house to a man of another house.  
No man of the house will put a false stone in the scale. If he does, let his part be taken from him, and let him be put out of the house, and let him pay twice what he stole.

If a man of the house dies, his son takes his part. If he has no son, his part returns to the purse, and his widow is fed from the house until she marries again.

If Min'ayuvi dies, the house does not die. Let the men of the house choose a head from among the kin of Min'ayuvi, before the priests, and let the house stand.

*[Reverse.]*

Whoever breaks this charter, whoever changes these words, whoever cheats the house:  
may Sada shut the sky over his fields;  
may Nil'i sink his boats;  
may [[dei:84|Lashli'i]] of the moon confound his count of the months, so that he pays his debts in the wrong season;  
may [[dei:87|Sutlpi]] of the house below open the earth for him before his time.

Witnesses: the priest of Sada; the overseer of the merchants' gate; the scribe who wrote this tablet; two elders of the street of the oil-sellers. Each has pressed his seal.

> Sealed in the 15th year of [[per:419|Cui'dilli'an]], king of [[pol:13|Tivintusa]].
