To [[per:19|Nurninpadiyath]], my daughter, lady of [[pol:9|Pumi]], who sits in the seat, say:  
Thus says [[per:10|Talomin]], your father, who sat in the seat before you.

May [[dei:16|Dumamnul]] of the high sky keep you. May [[dei:15|Vinba]] of the earth keep you. May [[dei:19|Yenan]] the moon keep you by night, when I cannot.

Hear me, my daughter. Your mother is not here to speak to you. Ten winters ago she went down to the wheel, and you were a small girl and held my hand at the fire. Since that day I have been your father and I have been your mother. So now I speak to you as your mother would have spoken. Hear it as her word. Hear it as my word. It is one word.

The priests have poured the oil on your head. The elders have put the staff in your hand. The men of Pumi have shouted your name at the gate. This is the [[evt:81|Accession of Nurninpadiyath]]. I saw it with my own eyes. I gave you the staff while my hand could still hold it. My hand cannot hold it now.

My breath is short. My legs are weak. The healers burn their herbs and shake their heads. I know what that shaking means. I am not afraid. What ends will begin again. The wheel turns. I go down to your mother.

Hear now what your mother would say to you, and what I say.

Five summers ago I built the wall of [[set:17|Pumi]] with these hands. I set the first stone. I cut the first ditch. That was the [[evt:77|Founding of Pumi]]. Before that there were huts by the river, and now there is a wall. Keep the wall. Mend it after every rain. A wall that is not mended is a gate.

Give the gods their due, each in the god's own place. Give the first sheaf to [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]] of the grain. Give the first fire of the year to [[dei:18|Eva]] of the hearth. Give a ram to [[dei:14|Vulme]] of the spear before the men go out, and a ram when they come home. Do not forget one god for another. The priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] know the order. Listen to the priests. But do not let the priests sit in your seat.

Be kind to the widows. Be hard to the thief. Do not take a man's field because you are strong and he is weak. The men of Pumi are few. Each one is a stone in the wall.

Choose a good man to stand beside you, a man who will not want the staff for himself. Your mother stood beside me. She did not want the staff. She wanted the wall to stand. Find such a one.

When you are angry, wait one night before you speak. When you are afraid, do not let the men see it. When you are glad, give thanks to the gods before you give thanks to yourself.

You are young. You have eighteen summers. I had more when I took the seat, and I was still a fool. You will be a fool sometimes too. Do not be ashamed of it. Be ashamed only if you are a fool twice in the same way.

My daughter, I have nothing more to give you. I have given you the wall and the staff and the gods. I give you also this clay, so that when you are old, and your own daughter sits at your feet, you can read it to her and say: "This is what my mother said to me, through the mouth of my father."

May the wheel bring us together again.

Written in the first year of Nurninpadiyath, lady of Pumi, by the hand of the scribe, at the word of Talomin.
