In the name of all the gods of heaven and earth, each in their place and with their due, and of the holy order of the [[mov:25|Myitiyishiu]], who keep the truth from hand to hand:

I, [[per:40013|Nistlushimin]], by the favour of the gods king of [[pol:1655|Ölie]], son of King [[per:39742|Tozhush]] of blessed memory, being now ninety years of age, weak in body but whole in mind, make this my testament, and command that it be read in the hall of my palace at [[set:264|Ölie]] before the lords and the priests, and kept thereafter in the treasury of the temple.

First, I give thanks. Sixty-three years I have sat in the seat of my father. The gods gave me long life, and I have tried not to waste it. When I took the crown the realm was as a field after a flood; I leave it as a field at harvest.

I remember the [[evt:41130|Ölie–Yisamashtsi War]], which the gods put into my hand when I was already old. At [[evt:41148|Lyismiyi]] my host prevailed. At Eol, in [[evt:41160|the first battle]], the captain of the enemy was slain, and in [[evt:41174|the second]] his successor fled. Then the lords of that people came to me at Eol, and I set my seal to the [[evt:41175|Treaty of Eol]], by which Eol itself and the country about it were joined to my crown, and an indemnity paid into my treasury in good silver. Let no one say that I took it for myself: I gave a tenth of it to the temples, as the custom is.

I remember also the [[evt:41264|Ölie–Ini War]]. At [[evt:41282|Lishimti]] and at [[evt:41307|Chezhietle]] my captains broke the enemy, and at Chezhietle their captain also was slain, so that in the end the war of the Ini melted away like snow in spring, and troubled us no further.

I remember the hard year, when the [[evt:41193|famine in Tamthal]] fell upon my people. I confess before the gods that I could not command the rain. But I commanded the granaries, and they were opened; and I ate the bread of the common soldier at my own table until the harvest came again, that no lord might say the king feasted while the land starved.

Hear now my bequests.

To my daughter, [[per:40201|Amanilyian]], whom I name my heir, I leave the crown of Ölie, the seal of the realm, and the love of a father who has watched her since her birth. To the lords of the realm I say: you swore your oath to me at my crowning; swear it now to her, and keep it as you kept it to me. A lord owes his sovereign his sword and his counsel; the sovereign owes the lord justice and protection. Let neither side forget the bargain.

To the temples of the gods I leave a thousand [[cur:917|nyinan]] of silver, for lamps and for the priests who tend them.

To the poor of the capital I leave the bread of my storehouses for forty days after my death, to be given at the palace gate without question.

To those who owe debts to the crown of less than ten nyinan, I forgive them, every one.

To my servants I leave their freedom from service, and to each a year's wage.

Last, I confess that I have loved fine horses, and fine cloth, and the praise of men, more than a wise man should; for the priests teach that the wise withdraw from wealth and appetite, and I did not withdraw as far as I ought. Let my body be buried in plain linen, without gold, and let my horses be given to the cavalry of the realm.

May the gods receive me according to my deeds, and forgive me according to their mercy. May they keep Ölie, and my daughter, and all who live under her hand.

Sealed in the palace of Ölie, in the year 3788 of the Myitiyishiu, before witnesses.
