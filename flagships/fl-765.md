*To my wife in [[set:60|Birnado]], say: thus says your husband, the spearman of the harbour street. May [[dei:140|Winba]] of the earth keep you, may Nabüdin of the river keep the children. In the 14th year of [[per:6627|Elulrinat]], the queen.*

I am at the camp by the ford, two days up the river. The scribe of the hundred writes this for two rings of copper. I tell him the words; he says my words are too many. I say: I have paid.

The men of Tallin have come into the land. This is the [[evt:6473|Tallin–Talenonoy War]], the captains say. The captains call up every man who ever held a spear for [[pol:259|Talenonoy]]. So they called me. My beard is grey. My knee is bad. They called me.

Wife, when I was young I came to Birnado with a spear. You know it; I do not hide it. I came in the days of the king [[per:6478|Shulon]], the father of our queen. I was one of the many who went down against [[pol:265|Tonnga]]. I was there at the [[evt:6272|Second Sack of Virnadhu]]. I was there when we burned the houses by the harbour gate. I do not tell the children this. I tell you, because you know.

The next year we came back. At the [[evt:6278|battle of Virnadhu]] I stood in the second rank. The men of Tonnga were few, few, and they fought like dogs at a door. My friend from my own village fell by the reeds. Then the siege, and the hunger in the city, and the gate. Then the king of Tonnga, [[per:6429|Bubnelulrinin]], came out and put his seal on the tablet before king Shulon, and went away alive. The [[evt:6280|Conquest of Tunnga]], the scribes call it. Tonnga became our land.

They gave me a strip of land by the harbour street, for my spear. They gave me a strip of land, and your father gave me you, though your brother had stood on the wall against me. Twenty-three years, wife. Twenty-three harvests. Our sons speak like Birnado men. I speak like a Birnado man. I am a Birnado man.

And now I am again a spearman of Talenonoy, against Tallin, for the land I took. This is how the gods laugh. Duemino of the storm laughs loudest.

Here in the camp barley is a [[cur:145|Badwuy]] and a half the measure, and the quartermasters give it short. I have sold my good cloak. Do not send another; send the dried fish, and the salve for my knee, by the boat of your cousin.

Tell the elder son: the plough before the feast; the east ditch before the plough. Tell the younger son: he is not to come here. If the captains come for him, he is to be at his uncle's. He is not to come.

Tell your brother, who stood on the wall against me: I ask him to keep my house. He kept his own against me once. He knows how.

The captains say the queen will send more men, and the men of Tallin will go home. The captains always say so. I do not know.

May Tuel of the moon light your door until I come. Your husband.
