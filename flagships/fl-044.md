**REPUBLIC OF [[pol:1922|YIUTL]] — OFFICIAL GAZETTE, EXTRAORDINARY**

*The following dispatch was received by wire at the Presidential Court in [[set:114|Thimisitsiy]] on 19 Ime 2826 of the Khikhishiu, and is published by order of the Council of State.*

---

General Headquarters, Army of [[reg:13|Iiyyi]], before Initl.

To His Excellency [[per:47167|Bailada]], President of the Republic, and to the Honourable Members of the Council of State.

Excellency,

I have the honour to report that the engagement before [[set:338|Initl]], which the newspapers of the capital have already begun to call the [[evt:48470|Seventh Battle of Initl]], was concluded at dusk on the day before yesterday, and that the field before the town remains in the possession of the armies of the Republic.

**1. Forces engaged.** By the returns of the Quartermaster-General, the strength of the Republic's armies on the line of Initl stood at upwards of one million two hundred thousand men of all arms. The enemy, under the command of General [[per:47237|Nitlizal]], disposed, by the best estimate of our intelligence officers, of something under half a million. Your Excellency will observe that this superiority of numbers, which the opposition press has called extravagant, was not more than was needed.

**2. Course of the action.** General [[per:47236|Shoe Tluie]], commanding in the field, opened with the bombardment of the enemy's forward works along a front of the river crossings. The infantry advanced on the third morning under cover of the heavy batteries brought forward by rail. The enemy's resistance was obstinate and, in places, admirable; I record it so that the Council may judge the measure of our men's achievement. On the fifth day the forces of [[pol:1965|Mii]] began a general withdrawal from the field upon the works of the town itself, which our cavalry and armoured trains pressed as far as the condition of the permanent way allowed. The town is not yet ours; I do not pretend otherwise.

**3. Casualties.** The returns are incomplete and I forward them with that caution. The losses of the Republic in killed, wounded and missing are presently estimated at twenty-eight thousand. The enemy's losses, from the count of prisoners, the burial parties and captured rolls, are estimated at not less than one hundred and forty thousand. I do not offer these figures as a cause for rejoicing. I offer them so that no citizen may be told that the victory was bought without cost, or that it was not a victory.

**4. The ground.** It will not have escaped the Council that the line now held by our troops is that on which the Republic fought the [[evt:48375|Sixth Battle of Initl]] eight years ago, and from which it proceeded to the [[evt:48376|Fourth Siege of Fyinan]] and the [[evt:48377|Conquest of Iiyyi]]. The men of this army know it. Many of the older sergeants fought over the same embankments. They have marched past their own former trenches, and I am told they cheered.

**5. Requirements.** The prosecution of the [[evt:48451|Yiutl–Mii War]] requires, beyond what the Ministry has already voted: (a) the replacement of rolling stock lost on the Initl branch, in particular forty locomotives; (b) the immediate dispatch of the classes of reservists called in the last decree; (c) attention to the price of bread in the depots of the rear, where a measure of grain now costs near ten [[cur:1110|linu]], a sum the soldier's wife cannot meet from his pay. I respectfully submit that the morale of the front is made in the bakeries of the capital.

**6. Conduct of the troops.** The bearing of all ranks has been beyond praise. The chaplains of the [[mov:57|Khikhishiu]] went forward with the stretcher-bearers and ministered to the enemy's wounded as to our own, in accordance with the teaching of compassion; I have received no complaint of plunder.

I have the honour to remain, Excellency, Your obedient servant,

*For the General Officer Commanding, the Chief of Staff, Army of Iiyyi.*
