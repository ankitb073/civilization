*Praise first to the One, before whom the old gods stand as servants and as shadows;  
and after the One, praise to [[per:39313|Vithnilòrath]], our sovereign lady,  
in the 35th year of her reign over [[pol:1565|Binge]], the flower-daughter, the Victorious.*  

**I. Of her root.**

As it is written, the tree is known by its fruit and the fruit by its root:  
her root is [[per:39188|Nithdun]], who took up the burden of the realm  
in an hour of trouble, when lesser men let it lie in the road.  
He bore it eleven years, and laid it down at the One's command,  
and the burden passed to a child of nine years, a flower in a hard season.  
The lords of Binge knelt to a girl, and some of them knelt smiling.  
They do not smile now.  

**II. Of her first trial.**

When she was a girl of eleven the rebels raised their banners,  
and called themselves an empire, and for six years they defied her.  
She did not hide behind her council; in her seventeenth year she broke them.  
So came [[evt:39894|the suppression of the revolt of Tungaruikhrimu]],  
and when it was done she pardoned them, every one, by her own word,  
for the teaching of [[mov:58|Vishithmikh]] is compassion and the release of suffering,  
and she had learned it before she learned the sword.  

**III. Of her mercy toward Buethshe.**

Against Buethshe also she drew her sword, in [[evt:39964|the war of Buethshe]],  
and when the One showed her that the time was not ripe, she sheathed it.  
Let foolish men call this a war unfinished. The wise call it a war unneeded.  
In that same season the One gave her [[per:39942|Pavbun]], her son,  
that the house of Nithdun should not lack an heir.  

**IV. Of the holy war.**

But when the people of Pinidhiyidhdiy closed their ears to the truth,  
she proclaimed [[evt:40035|the holy war]], for the message is for all peoples,  
and those who will not hear it must at least be made to listen.  
Her captains went before her. At [[evt:40046|Yuftuy]] the enemy broke.  
At [[evt:40065|Ontiedzey]] the enemy broke. At [[evt:40080|Yuftuy]] once more  
the enemy broke, and their captain lay among his dead.  
Then she herself put on the mail, a mother of twelve years' standing,  
and rode at the head of her vassals to [[evt:40090|Akhtse]],  
and every lord who held land of her rode behind her, as the oath requires,  
and there was no lord who stayed at home and called himself sick.  
At Akhtse the enemy did not break; the enemy was scattered like chaff,  
and by [[evt:40091|the treaty of Akhtse]] three of their towns became hers,  
with silver in recompense, and fifteen years of peace sworn before the One.  
Therefore men call her the Victorious, and the name is not flattery.  

**V. Of her hospitality.**

When the faithful of another teaching quarrelled among themselves,  
she opened her city to them, and they sat in [[set:147|Binge]] at [[evt:40110|the council]],  
and if they did not agree, it was not for want of bread or of roofs.  

**VI. Of the hungry years.**

Then came the years when the fields of Mikhukhizhi failed,  
[[evt:40002|the great hunger]], when mothers boiled the bark of trees.  
She opened the granaries of the crown. She sold her plate.  
She walked in the lower city with the almoners and was not afraid of the fever.  
Now, by the One's mercy, bread is cheap again in Binge,  
less than a [[cur:882|shithmu]] the measure, and the children are fat.  
Let no one say the queen's hand is only a sword-hand.  

**VII. Envoi.**

Go, my verses, and kneel before her as I kneel.  
Tell her that her poet asks no gift but her continued favour.  
May the One, who draws all time in a line from the first dawn to the judgement,  
find her at that judgement standing where she stands now: in the front.
