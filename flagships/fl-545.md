*In the name of the One Power, before whom the gods of the old peoples are servants and shadows. Here is recorded, by the hand of Shiish, the disputation held in the house of assembly at [[set:22|Bau]], in the year 1805 of the Kievi, between the elders of the [[mov:58|Veshethmekh]] of that city and the said Shiish; that those who come after may know what was said, and judge.*

**Of those present.** There sat the elders of the congregation of Bau, nine in number, in their places according to their ordination; and the deacons and the people in the body of the house; and, at the door, a reeve of our sovereign lady [[per:39460|Ishlishimam]], queen of [[pol:1461|Wemu]], sent from [[set:104|Yethithi]] to keep the queen's peace. For though the queen follows the [[mov:69|Kievi]], she is lady of all who dwell in her land.

**Of the question.** The eldest of the elders put the question thus: "Whether, since the [[evt:39906|Council of Pikhtunge]] failed to reconcile the congregations of Wemu, any ordination made in this kingdom without the assent of the elders abroad is lawful; and whether Shiish, who has laid hands on new deacons this year, has done what he had no right to do."

**The elders argued first,** saying: "Truth is kept by an ordained priesthood and passed down in order. Order comes down from the elders of the chief city as water comes down from the spring. Where the channel is cut, the field below is dry. The channel was cut at Pikhtunge. Therefore what Shiish pours out is not water but dust."

**I answered,** saying: "Fathers, I grant every word of your teaching and deny your conclusion. Truth is kept by an ordained priesthood and passed down in order: so it is written, and so I hold. But order is passed from hand to hand, not from city to city. The hands that were laid on my head were laid on the head of my teacher, and on his teacher's before him, back to the first who taught us. Show me where in that chain a link is broken. You can show me only that a council abroad has quarrelled with ours. Is the One Power a vassal, to change his grace with every quarrel of lords?"

**The elders replied:** "You make every congregation its own chief city. That road leads to Lus." And they reminded the assembly of the [[evt:39907|Ishlish Schism]], ten years past, when another went out from among us and set up his own altar.

**I answered:** "I am not of Lus, and I do not judge those who are; let the judgement judge them. I ask you only this. The good life is compassion and release from suffering: so we all confess. There are children in the villages of Wemu who are not received, and dying men who are not comforted, because there is no deacon to go to them, and no deacon may be made while we wait upon a council that will not sit. Is that compassion? Our teaching is for all peoples; is it not also for the people of Wemu?"

*Here the people murmured, and the reeve stood, and it ceased.*

**Of the judgement of the elders.** The eldest pronounced that Shiish, having ordained without assent, was cut off from their fellowship, with those who followed him, until they submit.

**Of what followed.** I said: "Then I go out, but I take nothing with me that I did not bring: the teaching, whole, and the order, unbroken." And I went out of the house of assembly into the street, and of the people some three hundred came after me, and we prayed together in the square, under a sky that belongs to no council. Those who went out with me are now called by the people [[mov:143|Mituth]]; and men already speak of the [[evt:40001|Mituth Schism]]. I did not seek the division; the judgement will show whose it is.

**Of our duty to the crown.** Let it be recorded that we owe, and will render, every service and due to our sovereign lady the queen, whose host is even now gone out to war; that we will pay her tithe in [[cur:808|mimi]] as before; and that we pray for her safety at every assembly.

*Witnessed by the deacons who went out with me. Written at Bau by [[per:39513|Shiish]], servant of the One Power, in the year 1805 of the Kievi.*
