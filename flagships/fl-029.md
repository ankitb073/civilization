In the name of the One Power, who orders all things from the first day toward the Judgement.

Makhkaalith, by the grace of the One, Empress of [[pol:1405|Yidhe]], to our well-beloved lords spiritual and temporal assembled in our city of [[set:0|Tallin]], and to the reverend fathers who keep the faith of [[mov:58|Vishithmikh]]: greeting, blessing, and the peace that passes from the One to all who serve Him.

Know that on the field of [[set:250|Rurin]], in this year 4523 from the founding of Tallin, the One gave into our hand the host of [[pol:1328|Pinpidhzhi]]. Their general [[per:34221|Ruwiwulun]], who last summer boasted that he had broken us, turned his back and fled, and his banners lie in the mud of the ford. Their dead were beyond counting; our heralds walked the field until nightfall and did not come to the end of them. Of our own people the losses were light, and those who fell have passed out of suffering and stand now before the Judge with clean hands. Thus is the [[evt:34777|victory of Rurin]] written, and thus we would have it read from every pulpit in the realm.

Remember, lords, why we took up this sword. It is now seven years since the [[evt:34671|war against Pienpedhzhe]] began, and the cause of it is older than our reign. In the days of our fathers Pinpidhzhi came in arms against our brother-kingdom, and was cast down at the [[evt:34562|field of Khaadhange]], and learned nothing from its chastisement. Its princes have not ceased to covet what is not theirs and to withhold what is ours. The province of [[reg:8|Diviguvivinge]] belongs to the crown of Yidhe by right of inheritance and by the gift of the One. We seek nothing but what is owed.

Nor has the road been without its trials. At the [[evt:34681|battle of Pininnushal]], in the first summer, our faithful general [[per:34179|Nigan]] was denied the field by a hair's breadth, through the craft of the enemy, who would not stand and fight in the open as believing knights should, but hid among the hedges. In the year following we came ourself before [[set:161|Linidhagushal]], and at the [[evt:34699|first battle there]] the One struck down [[per:34086|Puyimkinathvin]], the proudest of their captains, and ten thousand and more of his men with him. Nigan, of blessed memory, gave his life on that same ground two years after, faithful unto death; pray for his soul. In the year 4521 our servant [[per:34218|Ulubumen]] avenged him there, and slew [[per:34219|Kuwikalen]] at the [[evt:34732|third battle of Linidhagshal]]. If at the [[evt:34743|fourth battle]] the day went hard with us, it was not the valour of Pinpidhzhi that did it, but the rain, and the faint hearts of certain vassals who came late with half their due. Their names are known to us, and they will answer for it.

Now therefore hear our will, as you owe it to us by your oaths. As the holy writings teach: *the sword drawn in justice is a ploughshare in the hand of the One.* We command every lord who holds of the crown to send to us, before the feast of the turning of the year, the full tale of his spears and his horsemen, without excuse or delay. We command the treasurers of Tallin to raise an aid of [[cur:778|Zhiflun]] from the cities, and the reverend fathers to give from the treasuries of the houses of prayer, for this is a holy labour. Let no man say the war is long. The war is as long as the One wills it, and no longer.

Let prayers be said in every house for the souls of our dead, and for the conversion of our enemies, that the people of Pinpidhzhi may come out of darkness and know the One. We bear them no hatred. We hate only their pride.

Given under our seal, in the camp at Rurin, in the twelfth year of our reign.
