*[Obverse]*

To my lord, the overseer of the herds of the house of [[dei:0|Atlie]] in [[set:83|Khie'a]], say:  
thus says your servant, the herdsman of the high pasture, the son of the herdsman.  
May Atlie, sky, father of the house, keep my lord.  
May [[dei:2|Muevas'e]], mother earth, keep my lord's flocks.

My lord, hear the count of the flocks.  
Ewes: two hundred and ten. Last year, two hundred and forty.  
Lambs living: ninety. Lambs dead: thirty.  
She-goats: sixty. Kids living: forty.  
Rams: eight. The old ram with the split horn is dead. The wolf did not take him. The dry took him.  
The skins I have kept. The skins I send with this tablet, eleven skins, by the boy.

My lord, hear about the pasture.  
The spring at the high pasture is low. The spring is low as a cup.  
The grass is short. The grass is yellow in the month when it should be green.  
[[dei:3|Niele'i]], river, has drawn back into his bed.  
The ewes walk all day for little. At evening they come in with their ribs showing.  
The old men of the pasture sit by the fire at night and they are afraid.  
They say: it is coming back. They say: the great dry is coming back.

*[Reverse]*

My lord, you are young. You do not know the great dry. I will tell it.  
My father's father told it to my father, and my father told it to me.  
In the days of the fathers of the fathers, the sky shut.  
That was [[evt:4416|the Great Drought in Mikhukhizhi]]. It did not open for a hundred years less four.  
A hundred years less four! A man was born in it and grew old in it and died in it.  
In the days of my father's father came the [[evt:4804|Famine in Mana]].  
After it, the [[evt:4802|Famine in Tsiey]]. The people of those lands came up to our pastures to beg.  
In the youth of my father came the [[evt:5157|Famine in Khishkhih]].  
My father said: the herdsmen of that land ate their own dogs. Then they ate the leather of the tents.  
Then, when I was a young man with a beard, the sky opened. The rain came back.  
The springs filled. The grass came back. The flocks came back.  
We gave a ram to Atlie every year at the first rain. We gave the first wool to Muevas'e.

Now the rain is short again. One year short, my lord. Only one year.  
I do not say the great dry is coming back. I say the old men are afraid.  
[[dei:1|Uelti]], wisdom, knows. I do not know.

My lord, these are my requests.  
Let me take the flocks down to the river pasture of Niele'i, by the reed bed, until the rain.  
Let the house send barley for the herdsmen, three measures for each man, for the month.  
Let the house send a new ram from the city flock. The flock cannot be without a good ram.

My lord, I have been faithful. My father was faithful.  
My father kept the flocks of the house in the great dry, and he did not eat one ewe of the house.  
He ate roots. He did not eat one ewe. I will be like my father.  
May Atlie send the rain. May Muevas'e give the grass.  
Send word by the boy. Give the boy bread. He has walked far.  
In the 228th year of Khie'a.
