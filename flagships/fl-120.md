*Watches kept in the 6th year of Nyethmalen, king of Navnga. The scribe of the house of Novegu wrote them.*

**Month I.** Night of the 1st: the moon was seen, bright. The river was low. Barley was cheap. The men of Mikhuthitsu came against us. This was the [[evt:7008|First Mikhuthitsu–Navnga War]], which began last year. Another enemy came for the trade of the river. This was the [[evt:7009|First Nivi Trade War]], which also began last year. Two enemies, one year. The priests poured for [[dei:21|Duminu]] of the storm.

**Month II.** On the 9th the king, [[per:7269|Nyethmalen]], went out with two thousand six hundred men. He met the men of Mikhuthitsu in the [[evt:7025|Battle of Nivi]]. They were as many as the locusts. Seven hundred and fifty of our men fell. The king came back. His shield was split. He went up to the house of [[dei:20|Nothol]] and gave a ram.

**Month III.** Our men went out again, nearly three thousand men, against the second enemy, the men who want the river trade. They fought in the [[evt:7026|Battle of Zhiyiyzhikhti]]. Our captain fell, and nine hundred men fell with him. The women cut their hair. That night the moon was red.

**Month IV.** The men of the river trade came to [[set:10|Navnga]]. The wall was not high. They came in. They took the grain, the oil and the bronze of the house of the gods. They burned the gate. This was the [[evt:7027|Sack of Navnge]]. Five years ago, in the year of the [[evt:6960|Rise of Navnga]], [[pol:307|Navnga]] became a kingdom when the great kingdom broke. Now our own gate is burned.

**Month V.** On the 3rd a darkness came in the middle of the day. It was not cloud. The sun was like the moon. It had no heat. Grey dust fell like fine flour on the roofs, in the cisterns and on the barley. The cattle would not eat. The priests of the [[mov:3|Ryethki]] said: "[[dei:22|Vinba]] of the earth is angry." Merchants from the coast said: "A mountain has burst open. Men call it Mount Punenio." Who has heard of a mountain bursting? We write down what they said: the [[evt:6938|Eruption of Mount Punenio]].

**Month VI.** The sun rose red and set red. At noon it was pale. The barley stood green in the field and did not turn yellow. Frost in the sixth month. Our fathers did not see this. Our fathers' fathers did not see this.

**Month VII.** The barley did not ripen. The men cut it green and fed it to the cattle. Barley is dear, dearer than any man remembers. A man gives a [[cur:114|Lul]] and carries home a small sack. The river rose, and the water was grey. Fish floated in it. The priests poured for [[dei:24|Navbuthen]] of the river, but the river did not clear.

**Month VIII.** Cold. The stars were not seen for many nights. The moon was dim. A child died in the street by the burned gate. Another child died. The king opened the storehouse of the palace and gave out barley, one measure to a household. The storehouse is small.

**Month IX.** Snow. Men say it has never snowed in this month. The sea birds came inland and died on the threshing floors. The priests of [[dei:23|Shineyka]] of the sea went down to the shore and poured, and the sea was grey.

**Month X.** The sun is still pale. The dust still falls on some days, but less. The enemies have not come back. Perhaps they too are cold. Perhaps they too are hungry.

The scribe says: I do not know why the gods have done this. I do not know when it will end. The fire of Nothol is small. The wisdom of [[dei:25|Novegu]] is hidden from us. We give what we have. We wait.

Let the one who keeps this tablet keep it dry.
