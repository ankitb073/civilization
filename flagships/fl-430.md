*[Obverse. A thick tablet with a pierced corner, as if hung by a cord in a doorway.]*

Thus says [[per:439|Nabule]], lord of [[pol:18|Kadhdazhile]], who sits on the seat in [[set:65|Kadhdazhile]], in the third year of his sitting. Hear it, gods. Hear it, men. Hear it, you who are not yet born.

[[dei:105|Khudhaze]] of the high sky, witness it.  
[[dei:104|Shilimu]] of wisdom, witness it; you who know the words before they are spoken, witness it.  
[[dei:106|Kumbe]] of the moon, who counts the months, witness it.  
[[dei:107|Nizhinande]] of the spear, witness it.  
[[dei:108|Bumbe]] of the sea, who swallows what is thrown to her, witness it.

Hear first how I came to the seat, so that no one tells it crooked.

The lady [[per:340|Nanzhizhim]] sat on the seat of Kadhdazhile thirty years and more. She was full of years. Her hair was white as salt. She gave the gods their due; I say it, and let no one say I did not say it. But no child of her body was living to sit after her, no son, no daughter, no son's son. When she went down to the dead, the seat stood empty. I wept for her as a man weeps for his lady.

An empty seat is a lamb in the field; every dog runs to it. The lords of the houses drew their knives. Then I stood up. I took the seat. Some say I [[evt:365|seized it]]. Let them say it. I say the gods gave it, for the gods did not strike me down when I sat; they gave rain in its season, and the barley came up thick. Prosperity is the sign of the gods; see, the barley.

Now hear the curse. Hear it, and tremble.

Whoever comes after me, whoever is not of my seed, whoever lifts his hand against the seat of Nabule or against the seed of Nabule, whoever sits where he was not set:

may Khudhaze shut the sky over him; may no rain fall on his field;  
may Shilimu take the words from his mouth, so that he speaks and no one understands;  
may Kumbe hide his face from him, so that he loses the count of the months, and sows in the wrong month, and reaps nothing;  
may Nizhinande break his spear in his hand on the day of battle;  
may Bumbe take his ships and his fishermen, and give back nothing but the dead on the shore.

May his name be cut from the stones. May his sons beg at the gate. May his dead lie unburied. May his house be a house of owls.

And whoever breaks this tablet, whoever hides it, whoever rubs out the name of Nabule and writes his own: on him also be all these curses, twice.

*[Reverse.]*

Hear also, priests and teachers of [[mov:16|Ninzhe]]. You say that no one owns the truth; that the teachers are honoured but no teacher is master of it. Good. Then let no teacher say in the market that Nabule is a thief. Let him say what he saw: an empty seat, a filled seat, a full granary.

The wheel turns; what ends will begin again. The lady ended. I began. When I end, let my seed begin, and not another.

Let this tablet hang in the door of the house of the seat. Let every man who comes in read it, or have it read to him.

> Thus spoke Nabule, in the 3rd year of Nabule.
