To [[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the spear, who dwells in the high house of [[set:11|Buneynga]], say: thus says [[per:11483|Duebedun]], king of [[pol:476|Buneynga]], your servant, who holds the spear you gave him.

In the 7th year of Duebedun I set this tablet before your feet. I am my own envoy; I went myself, I spoke myself, I swore myself. Hear what was said.

**Why the war was.** The men of [[pol:463|Talenonoy]] were rebels. They would not bring the silver. They would not bring the barley. [[evt:11307|The war with Talenonoy]] was begun before my days; the king who sat before me went out against them, and fell, and his blood cried out from the earth to [[dei:15|Vinba]]. I took up his seat. I took up his spear.

**The fields.** At [[evt:11416|Diviminnge]] I went out. The men of Talenonoy ran before me like goats before the wolf. Their dead covered the field; I did not count them, there were too many. In the last year of the war I went out to [[evt:11435|Viririnu]]. There stood [[per:11544|Beneyen]], who had taken the seat of Talenonoy by force. You, Vulme, went before me. Beneyen fell. Beneyen lay in the dust of Viririnu. His men fell around him in heaps, two thousand, three thousand, who can count them? Mine lay down few.

**The city.** Then I went to the city of [[set:79|Talenonoy]]. Its gate was shut against me. I broke the gate. I went in. I took its silver; I took its cattle; I burned its houses and the tablets in its houses, so that its lies were burned with them. That was [[evt:11436|the fourth taking of Talenonoy]]. Three times before, the city had been taken; now four times.

**The words.** The elders of Talenonoy came out of the ashes to me. They wore sackcloth. They put dust on their heads. They said: "Our king is dead. Who will speak for us?"

I said: "Bring me the seal of Beneyen."

They brought it. It was in a box of cedar. Blood was on it still.

I said: "A king's seal does not die with the king. Let the seal of Beneyen speak for Talenonoy, as Beneyen would not speak while he lived."

They wept. They said: "Let it be so."

**The oath.** In the gate of Talenonoy we made the oath. I put my hand on the clay. The eldest of Talenonoy put the seal of Beneyen on the clay. We cut a ram for Vulme. We poured wine for [[dei:16|Dumamnul]]. We burned fat for [[dei:18|Eva]]. These are the words of [[evt:11437|the peace of Talenonoy]]:

> Talenonoy gives to Buneynga the land along the river, field and pasture, from the old boundary stones to the water.  
> Talenonoy brings silver to Buneynga every year, for fifteen years.  
> For ten years no spear of Talenonoy goes out against Buneynga.  
> Whoever breaks this oath, Vulme breaks his spear, Vinba shuts her womb to him.

**The return.** I came home to Buneynga by the river road. The silver of Talenonoy went before me in carts. I weighed it in [[cur:166|Rongel]] before the house of Vulme, so that all could see it. The people shouted. The priests sang. I gave a tenth of it to your house, Vulme, as is right.

Lady of the spear, you went before me at Diviminnge. You went before me at Viririnu. You went before me into the gate of Talenonoy. Go before me always. Let [[dei:17|Nijishchi]] fill the barns; let [[dei:19|Yenan]] light the road at night. Let my son read this tablet and know how the rebels were brought down.

Thus says Duebedun, king of Buneynga, your servant.
