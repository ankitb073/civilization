title: Daybook of a Physician in Yennga in the Year of the Spots
---
*[Obverse. A physician's daybook; each day is marked with a small wedge in the margin.]*

In the 19th year of [[per:13788|Bomakotnakhen]], king of kings in [[set:155|Yennga]], I, a physician of the house of healing by the river gate, servant of Eva of the fire and of Venba of the earth, set down the sick and what was done for them. So my teacher did. So I do.

**The first day.** A porter of the grain quay came to the house of healing. Fever. Pain in the back. I gave him the bitter drink and the cold cloth. He went home.

**The third day.** The porter came again, carried by his brothers. On his face, on his arms, on his chest: red spots, small as lentils. I have not seen this. My teacher did not write of this. I burned cedar. I called on [[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire to burn the sickness out of him.

**The fourth day.** The porter is dead. His two brothers have the fever. His wife has the fever.

**The sixth day.** Eleven sick in the street of the grain quay. All with fever; all with the spots on the third day. Three dead. The people call it the spotted death. I write that name. Let it be the name.

**The eighth day.** The spears of the king went out by the east gate. They go against Jedhjenga; this is the [[evt:13947|Second Yennga–Jedhjenga War]]. The people threw barley over them. Some of the spearmen were from the street of the grain quay. I said to the captain at the gate: "Leave those men." He did not hear me; the horns were loud. [[dei:14|Volme]], lady of the spear, keep them. Venba, do not open your mouth for them yet.

**The tenth day.** Forty sick. I no longer count by names. The house of healing is full; the sick lie in the court. The priests of [[mov:2|Jenro]] pour out to [[dei:15|Venba]] at the gate. The smoke of the cedar is in my hair and in my bread.

**The twelfth day.** I have learned this: those who wash the sick fall sick. Those who sleep in the room of the sick fall sick. The ones who stay away do not fall sick, or fall sick later. I have told the women to wash in the river after they wash the sick. I do not know if it helps. Let [[dei:16|Domamnol]] of the sky see that I try.

**The fifteenth day.** A merchant came in by boat. He says the spots are in [[set:154|Jedhjenga]]. Did our spears carry it there? Did their traders carry it here? I do not know. I write what he says.

**The eighteenth day.** A runner of the king came in. The spots are in [[set:0|Tallen]]. The spots are in [[set:10|Navnga]]. The spots are in [[set:63|Ego]] by the river.

**The twentieth day.** Barley costs more each morning. The [[cur:303|Dothtol]] buys less. The farmers will not bring their carts into the city; they leave the barley at the gate and run.

**The twenty-second day.** A second runner. The spots are in [[set:6|Jenontono]]. The spots are in [[set:60|Vernadho]]. The spots are in [[set:147|Benga]]. They say the spots go out from [[pol:580|Yennga]] along every road, like the spokes of a wheel. The priests say: all things turn on the great wheel. I did not know the wheel had spokes like these.

**The twenty-fifth day.** My assistant has the fever. He has been with me nine years. I sat with him all night. I gave him the bitter drink, the cold cloth, the cedar. I called on Eva; I called on Venba; I called on Yenan of the night, who sees the sick when no one else is awake. On his arms this morning: the spots.

**The twenty-sixth day.** I go on. The sick come. The [[evt:13851|Spotted Death]] is not ended; who knows when it will end? [[dei:17|Nedhekhte]] of the grain, feed the city. Volme, put down your spear. I write the day. I wash my hands in the river. I go back in.
