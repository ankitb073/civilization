*In the seventeenth year of Mononna.*

O [[dei:0|Atlie]], sky-father, why is your face of bronze?  
O [[dei:3|Niele'i]], river-mother, why is your bed a road of stones?  
O [[dei:2|Muevas'e]], earth-mother, why is your breast cracked?  
The granary is empty. The granary is empty.

The granary of the queen stood high as three men.  
Its door was of cedar, its seal was the seal of the queen.  
The scribe sat at its door with his tablet and his reed.  
Now the door stands open. The scribe is gone.  
The mice are gone. The sparrows are gone.  
The granary is empty. The granary is empty.

In the year the sky shut its hand,  
the barley came up, the barley lay down.  
In the year after, the barley did not come up.  
In the third year we dug for roots by the dry river.  
Hunger walked through [[reg:10|Mi'ositl]],  
hunger sat down in the houses of [[set:3|Eshue'ethie]].  
This was the great hunger, the [[evt:520|Famine in Mikhukhizhi]].  
Of eleven who ate at the hearth, one went down into the earth.  
The grandmother went down. The small son went down.  
We put barley-cakes in their hands. We had no barley-cakes.  
We put cakes of clay in their hands.  
The granary is empty. The granary is empty.

Now the river comes a little in the month of floods,  
a little, like the oil in a widow's jar.  
A measure of barley is two parts of three of an [[cur:10|Ashfan]],  
and we have no ashfan.  
We give our arm to the field of the queen.  
We give our son to the wall of the queen.  
We give our daughter to grind in the house of the priest.  
The granary is empty. The granary is empty.

O [[per:660|Mononna]], great queen, lady of the land,  
open the storehouse of the god, open the storehouse of the temple,  
the temple of the [[mov:0|La'im]], where the priests keep the seed.  
We have sacrificed. We gave the lamb. We gave the last kid.  
Prosperity is the favour of the gods: what have we done?  
What have we done, that the gods turn away their faces?

Atlie, open your hand. Niele'i, rise up in your bed.  
Muevas'e, take the seed, give back the seed.  
Then we will fill the granary. We will sing at its door.  
We will bring the first sheaf to the queen.  
We will bring the first sheaf to the god.  
The granary is empty. Let the granary not be empty.
