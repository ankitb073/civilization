*[Obverse.]*

*Leader:* Who remembers the city?  
*All:* We remember. We were children. We remember.  

*Leader:* Who remembers [[set:74|Tlaiya]] with its gate of cedar?  
*All:* We remember the gate. We remember the gate of cedar, and the bronze on it.  

*Leader:* Who remembers the smoke?  
*All:* We remember. We are the ones who breathed it.  

Hear it, [[dei:102|Nilii]], lady of the river, who ran red that day.  
Hear it, [[dei:101|Ami]], lord under the earth, who took so many in one day that his house was full.  
Hear it, Masi of the sky, who did not hide his face, who saw it all.  

In the year of the [[evt:1713|Second Sack of Tliyiya]]  
the men of [[pol:6|Ana'i]] came up the river road.  
Their spears were many as reeds. Their shields were many as the scales of a fish.  
Our fathers went out to meet them below the walls.  
Our fathers went out, and many did not come back in.  
Then the gate of cedar burned. Then the bronze ran down off it like water.  

*All:* The gate burned. The gate burned. We remember the gate.  

*Leader:* Where were you, child?  
*First voice:* I was in the oven-house with my sister. We hid in the cold oven. We heard the feet.  
*Second voice:* I was on my mother's back. She ran to the river. She did not stop until the reeds.  
*Third voice:* I was in the temple of [[dei:99|Muvasi]]. The priest put us under the altar. The priest did not come under with us.  
*All:* We remember the priest. He did not come under.  

*[Reverse.]*

They took the bronze. They took the jars of oil. They took the barley from the pits.  
They took the young men and the young women by the neck-rope.  
They burned the house of the king. They burned the house of the potter.  
They burned the house of my mother, with the loom in it, and the cloth on the loom.  
Seventeen summers ago. We count them on our hands.  

In the next year the elders went to the camp of Ana'i and put their hands under the feet of the conqueror.  
Then came the [[evt:1724|Treaty of Tliyiya]]: Tlaiya bows, Tlaiya pays.  
Every year the tribute goes out of the gate on carts.  
Every year the tax-man stands at the threshing floor.  
Barley is four [[cur:10|Ashsa]] and more the measure, and the best of it goes up the road.  
We eat the second barley. We eat the barley with the husks.  

*All:* We pay. We pay. We remember why we pay.  

Now there is a new king in the house that was rebuilt.  
[[per:2012|Mimamavi]] sits in the house. This is his first year.  
We have washed the gate-stones. We have hung the new gate, of pine, not cedar.  
May his years be long. May his granary be full.  
May the carts on the road go the other way one day.  

*Leader:* Who remembers the city?  
*All:* We remember. Our children will remember. We teach them this song.  
*Leader:* Then sing it at the river, on the ash day, every year.  
*All:* Every year. At the river. On the ash day. Until we are under the earth with Ami.
