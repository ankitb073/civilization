*[Obverse. The opening formula is complete.]*

To the priests of the storm-house in [[set:11|Buneynga]], to the keepers of the silver, to the elders who sit at the gate, say:  
thus says [[per:836|Bebedun]], king of [[pol:7|Buneynga]], the strong bull, the shepherd of the river-land, beloved of [[dei:136|Duemino]] the storm.

With me it is well. With the king's spears it is well.  
With the camp there is sickness, a little; with the oxen there is sickness, a little.  
With the gods there is no sickness. May it be well with you.

Hear, priests. Hear, elders. Two summers now the men of [[pol:19|Talenonoy]] have refused us the land of [[reg:8|Duegopuinga]]. They sit on its fields like crows on a threshing floor. They eat its barley and do not sow. The land cries out for its true lord. So I went down in the second year of the [[evt:644|Buneynga–Talenonoy War]], and the land is crying still.

Last summer I stood at [[set:62|Bimunga]], by the ford of Bimunga. The Talenonoy were more than we; their spears were like reeds in the marsh. [[per:770|Nuerloret]] led them, Nuerloret the she-wolf, Nuerloret who swears by gods she does not feed. I poured to Duemino the storm. I poured to [[dei:141|Nabüdin]] of the river, that she hold the water low. Nabüdin heard. Duemino heard. I went against Nuerloret, and Nuerloret broke. The field was ours. That was the [[evt:645|Battle of Bimnge]]; let it be written on the wall of the storm-house. Their dead were heaped like sheaves at harvest, twice our dead and more, and the river carried their shields down to the sea.

Hear now the second thing, and do not believe the lying mouths in the market.

This summer [[per:871|Eluemin]] went down again to Bimunga with the spears of the king. Nuerloret did not come out to fight like a warrior of the open field. She came at night. She came by the reed-path that the herdsmen use. She came when the moon was hidden, when [[dei:138|Tuel]] had turned her face. Who fights so? A thief fights so. A jackal fights so. Our men were sleeping in their cloaks; our men were at the pots. Eluemin rose and stood, Eluemin held the bank until the sun, and then he drew back to the hills in good order, carrying his wounded.

The field was not ours that day. I do not hide it. But I say to you: they won by a thief's trick, and they paid for it in blood, and their mourners are many in Talenonoy tonight. It was not the gods who turned from us. It was the night that hid the jackal. This also is the [[evt:651|Battle of Bimnge]]; let the scribes write that it was theft.

Therefore, keepers of the silver, open the storehouse. Send [[cur:7|Tyettku]] of good weight for the bowmen of the hills, for they will not shoot for barley alone. Send barley for the camp. Send new shafts, send sinew, send the copper-smiths.

And you, priests: feed [[dei:137|Shyedartsi]] of the underworld, that he take our dead gently and give back none of theirs. Feed [[dei:139|Nidiktti]] of the grain, that the storehouses of Buneynga stay full while the men are away. Feed Duemino, my lord, the storm; let him come down on the camp of Talenonoy with hail.

Duegopuinga is ours. It was ours, it is ours, it will be ours.  
I will stand at the ford again. I will not go home with empty hands.

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> Written in the camp above Bimunga, in the 7th year of Bebedun.  
> Let the runner who carries this tablet not sleep until he lays it at the gate.
