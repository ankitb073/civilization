*In the fifth year of Mabyiruyin. The leader sings; all answer.*

Who sings for [[set:172|Tungarvikhrim]]?  
*We sing, the ones who were left.*

[[dei:87|Shutsüfi]], lord of the ground below, open your ear.  
Eleven harvests ago, eleven, the men of [[pol:169|Bäni]] came.  
They came over the hill road with bronze, with wagons, with fire.  
Our men at the gate were a handful, a handful, a handful.  
*A handful at the gate. We remember.*

They broke the gate. They burned the lower town.  
They burned the house of [[dei:86|Shükhi]], lady of grain.  
The barley burned in the jars. It smelled like bread.  
It smelled like bread all night, and no one ate.  
This was the [[evt:5150|Sack of Tungaruikhrimu]].  
*It smelled like bread all night. We remember.*

In one year two peaces. In one year one fire.  
In that year the elders swore the [[evt:5146|First Treaty of Tungaruikhrimu]]  
to the men of the other road, and gave fields, and gave silver.  
After the fire the elders swore the [[evt:5151|Second Treaty of Tungaruikhrimu]]  
to Bäni, and bent the neck, and promised silver every year.  
Every year the [[cur:101|Lavuke]] go out of the city in a box.  
Every year the box goes down the hill road on a donkey.  
*Every year the box. We remember.*

Mother of a dead son, what did you give?  
*I gave my son.*  
Wife of a dead man, what did you give?  
*I gave my husband and my roof.*  
Child of the burned street, what did you give?  
*I was small. I gave my mother.*  
Shutsüfi, you have them. Keep them gently.  
*Keep them gently. We remember.*

Then the old lord went down to Shutsüfi,  
and [[per:5504|Mabyiruyin]] took his seat.  
The elders did not say yes. The elders did not say no.  
Then the men of Tonnga came against us.  
This is the [[evt:5208|Third Tonnga–Tungarvikhrim War]]; it is not finished.  
Last year Mabyiruyin went out to Viririnu.  
At the [[evt:5256|Battle of Viririnu]] the field was lost.  
Our sons went with him. Some of our sons are with Shutsüfi.  
*Some of our sons are with Shutsüfi. We remember.*

We built the wall again with the stones of the burned houses.  
Black stones in the wall, black stones by the gate.  
The children count them. The children ask whose house.  
We tell them. That is why we sing.

Shükhi, lady of grain, fill the jars.  
Shutsüfi, lord below, keep the dead quietly.  
Let the box be light this year.  
Let the sons come home along the road.  
*Let them come home. We remember.*
