*[Loose quires of parchment, the earlier leaves in a careful hand, the later ones cramped and abbreviated. Entries are separated by a small cross.]*

**The year 4942 from the founding of Dagafu.** Here I begin a new book, the old being full. May the One who sees the end of all things from its beginning keep this house, and keep our lady the queen, [[per:38378|Nashinzhè]], in the fourth year of her reign, and keep my lord whose bread I eat and whose fields I count.

✠ *Sowing month.* The rain came late and then too much. The lower strips by the brook lie in water, and the seed in them has rotted. My lord's steward says it is nothing. The old men say it is like the year they remember from their boyhood. I wrote down both opinions, as the Teaching bids: let every man weigh with his own scale.

✠ *Month of the green ear.* The ears are thin and black at the tip. I walked the whole of my lord's demesne with the steward and we did not find one good field. The steward was silent all the way home.

✠ *Same month.* In the market at [[set:9|Dagafu]] grain was dearer than at the turning of the year, and climbs every market-day since. A measure is now near two [[cur:837|Zhili]], and the poor do not have one. The widows of the parish came to the gate. I gave from my own bin, a little. My wife says it was too much. My wife is right, and I am not sorry.

✠ *Month of the reaping.* We reaped what there was. It filled the great barn to the height of my knee. In a good year I cannot see over the heap.

✠ *Same month.* It is not only here. A carter from the north road says it is the same through all [[reg:25|Ngzhinakhzi]]. They are already calling it the [[evt:38867|Famine in Ng'jinaguh]] at the inns, as though it were a battle with a name. The One forgive me, I wish it were. A battle ends.

✠ *Same month, the feast-day.* The preacher in the square said the famine is a judgement, for the Teaching of [[mov:24|Uthungala]] holds that prosperity is a sign of the divine favour, and therefore want is a sign of its withdrawal. I went home and reasoned it through by my own light, as we are permitted. Five years ago the old king, our lady's father, won the field at the [[evt:38896|Battle of Khambive]]; then our hosts won again at the [[evt:38908|Battle of Khambe]]; then came the [[evt:38909|Treaty of Khambe]], and new lands, and the bells rang in every steeple of [[pol:1464|Dagafu]]. If prosperity was favour then, is famine disfavour now? What did we do between Khambe and this harvest that was so wicked? I cannot find it. Perhaps the fault is older.

✠ *Month of the falling leaf.* The old men say the land has not been right since the strangers' hosts fought at the [[evt:38805|Battle of Hil]], fourteen years gone, and trampled the dikes, and the dikes were never properly mended. That is a reason I can see with my own eyes. It is not a judgement. It is a broken dike.

✠ *Same month.* My lord's steward has come for the dues. My lord takes his third as always, the queen's tax as always. I showed him the barn. He measured it himself, and he took less than his third, and he did not write it down. May the One remember it on the last day, when every ledger is opened.

✠ *Same month.* Two children of the miller's lane buried. A third sick.

✠ *Month of the first frost.* The queen has ordered the royal granaries opened in the city, they say, at a fixed price. Whether it reaches us out here I do not know. We pray for her. We pray for the spring.

✠ *Same month.* Seed-corn. We have put aside seed-corn for the sowing, under lock, and I keep the key on my own body. If we eat the seed, we eat next year. Every night someone knocks and asks. Every night I say no.

✠ *Same month, late.* Snow. The barn is half empty. The year is not over, and the winter is long. I do not know how this ends. Only the One knows the end of anything.
