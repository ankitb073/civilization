title: Lament for Talenonoy, Mother of Lands

Weep, [[dei:141|Nabüdin]], river of the city, weep in your reeds;  
weep, Winba, earth of the city, weep in your furrows;  
weep, [[dei:138|Tuel]], moon of the city, weep on the roofs at night.  
O [[set:79|Talenonoy]], mother of lands, who will speak your praise?  
O Talenonoy, I will speak it. I will speak it and weep.

Once the roads came to you as calves come to the cow.  
Once the asses of every land came to your gate with silver,  
with [[cur:46|Niba]] of silver, with copper, with barley, with oil.  
Once the elders of every land bowed down in your courts.  
Your storm-lord, [[dei:136|Duemino]], sat in his high house;  
the priests of [[mov:21|Kunelmu]] fed him at morning and at evening,  
and he was glad, and the rain came in its season.  
O Talenonoy, that was your day. That was your day.

Then the first sorrow came. Nonenga turned its face away;  
at the [[evt:2425|going-out of Nonenga]] it set its own lord on its own seat,  
and the spearmen of the city went up and brought it back with ropes.  
Then Nonenga turned its face away a second time,  
at the [[evt:2572|second going-out of Nonenga]], and again it was brought back.  
Then Myinive lifted its hand, at the [[evt:2586|rising of Myinive]],  
and Myinive too was brought back, and its rising was put down.  
But a son brought home with ropes is not a son;  
he eats the bread of the mother and hates the hand that gives it.  
O Talenonoy, your children looked at your door.

Then your spearmen went down to Linidhagshal;  
at the [[evt:2709|battle of Linidhagshal]] the spears were broken,  
the shields were left in the field, the captain did not come home.  
At the [[evt:2710|peace of Linidhagshal]] your head was bowed,  
your head was bowed before the enemy, and it was not lifted.  
Then Myinive lifted its hand again, and your spearmen went against it  
in the [[evt:2767|war of Talenonoy and Myinive]];  
Myinive was put down, but the barns of the city were emptied,  
the spearmen ate the seed-corn, and the fields were thin.  
O Talenonoy, your hands were emptied.

Then the great breaking came, the [[evt:2822|breaking of Talenonoy]].  
The one land became three lands. The one seat became three seats.  
[[pol:119|Tonnga]] keeps your walls; Tonnga sits in your house.  
[[pol:120|Nonenga]] sits on its own seat; [[pol:121|Tungaruishrimu]] sits on its own seat.  
The name of [[pol:54|Talenonoy]] went down from the roll of kings.  
Who now says "Talenonoy, the empire"? No one says it.  
Who now brings silver to the mother of lands? No one brings it.

The walls stand. The gate stands. The house of the storm-lord stands.  
But the storm-lord has turned his face; the rain is late.  
The courts are wide and the elders in them are few.  
The grass comes up between the bricks of the processional way.  
The singers sing for one land where they sang for many.  
[[dei:137|Shyedartsi]], lord of the house below, has taken the old men  
who remember the day of the city; he has taken them, and he is full.

Fifteen years I have wept, and I am not done weeping.  
In the 215th year of Talenonoy I set down this lament.  
Weep, Nabüdin. Weep, Winba. Weep, Tuel. Weep, Nidikti of the grain.  
O storm-lord, turn your face again to your house;  
we will feed you at morning, we will feed you at evening,  
we will keep your due, we will keep your order.  
O Talenonoy, mother of lands, may your children come home.
