**MINUTES OF THE GENERAL COUNCIL OF THE FAITHFUL OF [[mov:57|E'eshio]], HELD AT [[set:76|NEMETH]] IN THE KINGDOM OF [[pol:1755|TSIEY]], IN THE YEAR 2482 OF THE E'ESHIO; FAIR COPY MADE FROM THE SECRETARY'S DAY-BOOKS AND ATTESTED THIS 12 LASHLE 2483.**

*Preliminary note by the secretary.* The day-books of the [[evt:44953|Council of Nimid]] were kept in haste, in three hands, during sessions that frequently ran past the lighting of the lamps. In making this fair copy I have corrected nothing of substance. Where the day-books disagree, I have followed the majority of them and noted the variant. Where a speaker asked that his words be struck, I have struck them, but I have recorded that he asked.

**First session.** The council was opened with the recitation of the Turning, as is customary, and with the reading of the letters of summons. Present were the ordained elders of the provinces of Tsiey, the delegates of the districts lately ceded to the kingdom by the [[evt:44911|Treaty of Zhükhi]] (admitted, after some debate, with voice but without vote), and the delegation of [[pol:1901|Tamatie]], which arrived on the second day, having been detained on the road by floods.

**Second session.** The Tamatie delegation presented its articles. These were read in full and are appended. In substance they hold:

1. That the ordained succession, though it is the guardian of the truth, is not its owner, and that the elders of Tamatie may lay hands upon their own candidates without sending them to Nemeth for confirmation.
2. That compassion, which the faith places first among the virtues, is not perfected by withdrawal alone, but must be shown in the ordered performance of one's duties in the household and the city.
3. That the wheel, while it turns, is not indifferent to the direction in which men push it.

**Third and fourth sessions.** Debate on the first article. The elders of the capital answered that truth is kept by an ordained priesthood and passed down in order, and that an order which each province may break at pleasure is no order at all. A delegate of Tamatie replied that a chain is not stronger for being longer. The debate was warm.

**Fifth session.** Debate on the second and third articles. Here the parties came nearer to agreement than on any other matter. It was generally allowed that release from suffering and the duties of one's station are not enemies, and a form of words was drafted which several of the Tamatie delegation were inclined to accept. It is entered below as the *Draft of the Fifth Day*.

**Sixth session.** The council returned to the first article and could not resolve it. A proposal that Tamatie candidates be examined at Nemeth but ordained at home was rejected by the capital as a concession of principle and by Tamatie as a humiliation in practice. The vote was taken by provinces. The articles were not received. The Tamatie delegation withdrew before the closing prayer, which was said without them. I record, because it was remarked upon, that they bowed to the presiding elder on leaving.

**Resolution.** The council affirms the ordained succession; commends the *Draft of the Fifth Day* to the provinces for study; and deplores, without condemning, the withdrawal of Tamatie.

*Secretary's note on the present copy.* Since the council rose, it has become known that the elders of Tamatie have proceeded to ordain upon their own authority, and that a separate body now gathers under them. This is the [[evt:44954|Ishyidig Schism]], as it is already called in the streets. It is not the secretary's office to judge whether the council could have prevented it. It is his office to record that it tried for six sessions, and that the one article on which it would not yield was the one on which the faith itself, as received, does not permit it to yield.

This fair copy is respectfully presented to His Majesty [[per:44316|Mielelianuo]], in the first year of his reign, with the prayer that he will read the Draft of the Fifth Day, and that the wheel, in its turning, may bring the brethren of Tamatie round again.

*Attested at Nemeth, 12 Lashle 2483 of the E'eshio. The charges of the council, reckoned in [[cur:1032|Ta]], are entered in the treasury's separate account.*
