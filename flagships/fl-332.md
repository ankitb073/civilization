*[Obverse. Ruled in sections; each section closes with the refrain.]*

Praise, praise to [[dei:21|Duminu]], lord of the storm, who rides the black cloud!  
Praise to [[dei:20|Nothol]] of the fire, who eats the fat of the offering!  
Praise to Vinba, mother of the furrow; praise to Novegu, the wise, who counts the stars!  
Praise to the king, [[per:1092|Kamagonen]], the great bull of [[pol:8|Navnga]]!

*Refrain:* The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.

The men of [[pol:13|Kivinkuthakhu]] shut their hand.  
In the days of the fathers they bowed; in the [[evt:672|First Navnga–Tivintusa War]] they bowed,  
they swore the oath, they brought the silver, they brought the oxen.  
Then their hand grew hard, then their mouth grew proud.  
They said: "We owe nothing to Navnga." They lied before the gods.

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

Kamagonen rose up in the city of the river, he rose up in the house of the king.  
He girded the bronze, he lifted the spear, he went down into [[reg:11|Kiya]].  
At Ene he went down; [[per:1022|Nitsulu Kash]] stood before him.  
The spears of Navnga were many, many as the locusts of summer.  
Nitsulu Kash broke. The field was Navnga's. That was the [[evt:875|Battle of Ene]].

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

At the walls of [[set:13|Kivinkuthakhu]], the king went down a second time.  
Nitsulu Kash stood before him a second time.  
Duminu thundered over the walls; Nothol licked at the gates.  
For each of ours that fell, three of theirs fell.  
Nitsulu Kash broke. The field was Navnga's. That was the [[evt:885|Battle of Tivintukhe]].

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

At [[set:72|Khuthmithi]], by the reed-water, [[per:1156|Pavbukhlulrinen]], the king's hand,  
the swift one, the strong-armed, stood against Nitsulu Kash a third time.  
[[dei:24|Navbuthen]] of the river lay down quiet in her bed; the war-carts crossed dry-shod.  
Nitsulu Kash broke. The field was Navnga's. That was the [[evt:891|Battle of Ometh]].

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

Twice at Kakhikhnakhath the storm turned its face from us.  
Do not ask the singer of it. Duminu tries his sons as the smith tries the bronze:  
with fire, and again with fire. The bronze that is not broken is good bronze.

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

Then in the eighth summer the king went down a last time, to [[set:76|Nimiki]].  
Never were the spears of Navnga so many; the dust of them hid the hills.  
[[per:1157|Katsula]] stood before him, Katsula the stubborn, Katsula the boaster of Kakhikhnakhath.  
Hard was the day. Many of ours lay down; let the mothers of Navnga weep for them.  
But Katsula broke at evening, and the field was Navnga's.  
That was the [[evt:914|Battle of Nimid]]; remember it, remember it.

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

Then they sat down at Nimiki, Kamagonen sat down, Nitsulu Kash sat down.  
They swore by Duminu above, they swore by Vinba below.  
Kivinkuthakhu gave the land of Kiya. Kivinkuthakhu opened the road of the traders.  
Kivinkuthakhu will bring the silver, year upon year, [[cur:2|Mulshi]] of good weight,  
into the storehouse of Navnga, into the house of the gods.  
So was the [[evt:915|Treaty of Nimid]] sworn, and the spears were laid down.

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

Now, priests, kill the white bull for Duminu.  
Now, women, pour the oil for Vinba of the furrow.  
Now, young men, dance before the house of Kamagonen,  
for he is the bull who does not tire, the shepherd who does not sleep,  
in the seventeenth year of his reign he has made the wheel turn true.

*The wheel turns, the wheel turns; what was owed is paid.*

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> To be sung at the gate of the storm-house at the new moon, in the 17th year of Kamagonen.
