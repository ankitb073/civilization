title: Hymn of the Single Road, for the Second Year of the Teaching in Teshivi

*I. Prelude, to be sung by one voice*

The road was always one road, though we walked it in circles;  
the light was always one light, though we gave it many names.  
Now the lamp is lifted in [[set:146|Teshivi]], and the hall is seen entire,  
and the shadows on the wall are known for what they are.

*II. Of the old altars, by the full choir*

We do not mock the altars of our fathers, nor the hands that built them;  
the fathers prayed in the dark, and the dark was all they had.  
The gods of [[mov:7|Kumbumbi]] were servants who forgot their master,  
stewards who sat in the house and called the house their own.  
Let the stewards be honoured as stewards; let the house return to its lord.  
The smoke of the old fires rises still, and drifts, and is gone;  
but what was burned in them was given to the One, though no one knew it.

*III. Of the Teacher, by one voice*

There came a teacher from the hills, a walker of the long roads,  
who carried neither sword nor seal, and owned nothing but a bowl.  
In the year the [[evt:19657|founding of the Tsiumu faith]] was proclaimed,  
the teacher said: "No one owns the truth. Not I. Not you. Not the throne.  
Truth is a river: drink, and pass the cup, and do not dam it."  
And the poor drank, and the merchants drank, and the soldiers drank,  
and the cup came at last to the hall of the queen.

*IV. Of the queen, by the full choir*

Blessed is [[per:19289|Namage]], who sat in the seat of judgement,  
who heard the teachers in her hall with the doors left open,  
who weighed their words as a just judge weighs the evidence,  
and did not ask what the new teaching would pay the treasury.  
In the thirtieth year of her reign we sing her, and not in flattery:  
for a ruler is measured by what she lays down, not what she gathers.

*V. Of the turning, by one voice and the choir answering*

Two winters past, the kingdom of [[pol:837|Teshivi]] turned its face.  
*Two winters past, and the road was made straight.*  
This was the [[evt:19782|conversion of Teshivi to Tsiumu]],  
*not by the spear, but by the open door.*  
The old shrines were not burned; they were swept, and lamps were set in them.  
*The old names were not cursed; they were set aside, like a child's clothes.*

*VI. Of the teaching, by the full choir*

Time runs in a line, from the first word to the last judgement;  
it does not turn upon a wheel, it does not come again.  
Therefore each day is given once, and once only;  
therefore spend it in mercy, for you will not be lent it twice.  
The wise withdraw from wealth and appetite, as a swimmer drops his stone:  
what is a purse of [[cur:438|ngade]] on the far bank of the river?  
Release the grip of the hand, and the hand is free to help.  
Release the grip of the heart, and the heart is free to grieve with the grieving.  
Compassion is the road; release from suffering is the road's end;  
and the road is open to every people under the sky.

*VII. Envoi, by one voice*

Go out, then, from Teshivi, you who have heard;  
the teaching is not ours to keep, for no one owns the truth.  
Carry it to the cities of the plain, and to the herdsmen in the hills,  
and to those who still pour wine to the stewards of the house.  
Do not threaten them. Do not buy them. Open the door, and wait.  
So the Teacher waited for us; so the One waited for the Teacher.

*Here ends the hymn of the single road, written in the 30th year of Namage, for the gathering of the faithful of [[mov:68|Tsiumu]] at the lamp-lighting.*
