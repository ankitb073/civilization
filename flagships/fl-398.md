*[Obverse. The song is set out in lines, with a stroke at the end of each. A refrain is marked in the margin with a sign like a footprint.]*

We are the [[cul:9|Myenibe]], the walking people,  
we are the people of the long road.  
Hear our song, O [[dei:141|Nabüdin]] of the river, who let us cross;  
hear our song, O [[dei:140|Vinba]] of the earth, who let us lie down.  

In [[reg:15|Bene]] our fathers' fields lay, in Bene by the old wells.  
The sky shut its mouth. [[dei:136|Dueminu]] of the storm did not speak.  
No cloud came, no rain came, the barley stood up white and dry.  
[[dei:139|Nidikhti]] of the grain turned her face, and the ear was empty.  
That was [[evt:13312|the famine]]. Of every ten, one lay down.  
Of every ten, one went down to [[dei:137|Shyedartsi]] in the house below.  

*Walk, Myenibe, walk; the road is long, the road is long.*  

The old men said: we will wait. The young women said: we will go.  
We took the pots, we took the goats, we took the bones of the grandmothers.  
We took the seed-corn sewn in the hems of our cloaks.  
We went out from Bene in the year of the white barley,  
family by family, village by village, one road and then another road.  

How many were we? Ten times ten thousand were we,  
ten times ten thousand on the road, and the dust of us went up to the sky.  
How long did we walk? Twenty summers and two we came in,  
the first came in when the hunger began; the last came in last year.  

*Walk, Myenibe, walk; the road is long, the road is long.*  

We came to the river. Nabüdin was full. We gave her a white kid.  
We came across. We came into [[reg:7|Tunnga]], the green land.  
The men of [[pol:466|Turamtu]] stood at the fords with spears.  
We bowed our heads. We opened our hands. We said: we are hungry.  
And the lords of Turamtu said: sit down, and work, and give the gods their due.  

We sat down in Tunnga. We dug. We built. We gave the gods their due.  
To Nidikhti, the first sheaf. To Vinba, the black ewe.  
To [[dei:138|Tuel]] of the moon, the lamp at the month's turning.  
We pay the tax in [[cur:249|Idin]], weighed before the priests of [[mov:21|Khunewmu]].  
Our sons carry the spears of Turamtu; our daughters marry in Turamtu.  
The walking people have stopped walking.  

*Sit, Myenibe, sit; the road is ended, the road is ended.*  

Now sits [[per:13387|Antseshiwtut]] in [[set:16|Turamtu]], the lady of the spear,  
she who broke the enemy at [[evt:13573|Himi]] with her own spear,  
in the eleventh year of her sitting, may her years be many.  
She has given us the green land. She has given us the water.  

And now, this year, her spears go down to Bene;  
[[evt:13618|the war with Bene]] has begun, in the land of the old wells, the land of our fathers.  
What shall the Myenibe sing? We do not know what to sing.  
We sing for the queen who fed us. We sing for the wells we left.  
O Dueminu, go before the spears of Turamtu.  
O Vinba, be gentle with the dead of Bene, who are our dead.  

*Walk, Myenibe, walk; the road turns back, the road turns back.*  

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> Sung at the harvest in Tunnga, in the 11th year of Antseshiwtut.
