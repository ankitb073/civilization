*[Obverse]*

Weep, village below [[set:23|Unuzhüdi]]. Weep, fields by the town wall.  
Hear us, [[dei:65|Tono]], moon, father of the night, who has seen two masters come.  
Hear us, [[dei:66|Shyidartsi]], lady of the house below, who keeps our fathers.

We were [[pol:520|Unuzhüdi]]. Our grandfathers were Unuzhüdi.  
Then the men of [[pol:449|Kvile]] came. The old men remember it.  
They came at Lunge. They came at the town. They were like the reeds of the river.  
The men of the town on the wall were a few score, and twenty of them were ours. Twenty men. The old men count them on their fingers.  
The twenty did not hold the wall. How should a few score hold a wall?

Then the kings sat down at Unuzhüdi. That was the [[evt:11949|Conquest of Unushuki]].  
[[per:11791|Tubantsin]], king of Kvile, set his seal.  
Our lady [[per:12064|Khulufmagonath]] set her seal. She was our lady one year.  
One year she was our lady, and she gave the land. Two winters after, she died.  
The old women say she died of the seal. The old women say foolish things.

Then we were Kvile. Thirty-one harvests we were Kvile.  
The scribes of Kvile wrote our fields. The tax-men of Kvile weighed our barley.  
They weighed it in [[cur:236|Shüvi]], their silver. A measure of barley is a Shüvi and two thirds of another.  
Our sons went in the levy of Kvile. Our daughters married men of Kvile.  
We learned to say: may the king of Kvile live. We said it. We said it every feast.

*[Reverse]*

Now hear the new weeping. Three years ago it came.  
In the third year of [[per:12349|Dvidyiyshan]], king of Kvile, other strangers came.  
They came against Unuzhüdi. The men of Kvile in the town were few.  
That was the [[evt:12319|Battle of Unushti]]. Kvile did not hold. Kvile ran.  
The same year the city of Kvile itself was sacked by yet other strangers, they say.  
The same year the king sat down three times and set his seal three times.  
That was the [[evt:12320|Treaty of Unushti]]. The criers said: the town is given.  
The town is given to the strangers. The town on our fields is not Kvile's.

And we? Whose are we? The criers did not say.  
The scribes of Kvile do not come. Other scribes came, and wrote, and went away.  
We do not know their seal. We do not know their king's name.  
So we still count the years by Dvidyiyshan, because no one has told us another.

The old men say: our fathers had one master and lost him.  
We had a second master, and he lost us.  
What is a village? A village is a thing that is given in a treaty.  
A village is a line on a tablet that a king draws, sitting far away.

Weep, village. Weep for the name of Unuzhüdi that our grandfathers spoke.  
Weep for the name of Kvile that our fathers learned.  
Tono, moon, you see the new masters too. Tell them our fields are good.  
Tell them the well is sweet. Tell them we pay.  
Shyidartsi, keep our fathers below. Do not tell them whose we are.  
In the 6th year of Dvidyiyshan.
