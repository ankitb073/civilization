*[Column i. The upper part is broken away.]*

[…] the storm-cloud […]  
[…] and the barley lay flat before it.  

Sing, O [[dei:136|Duemino]], lord of the storm, whose voice is the thunder,  
sing, O [[dei:141|Nabüdin]] of the river, who carries the boats down,  
sing of the spears of [[pol:19|Talenonoy]] that went down to Duegohuinga,  
that went down to the land of [[reg:8|Duegohuinga]] to take the tribute,  
the tribute that was owed, the tribute that was not given.  

The lords of [[pol:33|Buneynga]] shut their hands.  
They shut their hands on the silver, they shut their hands on the grain.  
"We owe nothing," they said. "We give nothing," they said.  
Then the spears of Talenonoy went down, as the storm goes down.  

Who led the spears? [[per:1159|Mamagonin]] led the spears,  
Mamagonin of the bronze cap, Mamagonin the patient,  
he who poured the beer for Duemino at the going-out,  
he who gave the black ram to [[dei:137|Shyedartsi]] below.  

Who stood against him? [[per:1149|Lonuerninat]] stood against him,  
Lonuerninat of Buneynga, the woman with the iron heart,  
and with her the few, the few only, two thousand spears and no more.  
Few were her spears; the way was narrow; the wall was high.  

*[Column ii.]*

At [[evt:880|Biniynge]], before the wall of [[set:11|Buneynga]],  
in the second year of the going-down, Mamagonin came before the wall.  
His spears were as many as the barley-stalks; for each of hers he had four.  
Yet they held the narrow place from morning to evening; yet ours too lay down.  
Weep, mothers of Talenonoy. They were dear, and they lay down.  
But in the evening they gave back behind the wall. The field was Talenonoy's.  

At [[evt:895|Bimnge]], by the houses of [[set:62|Bimunga]],  
in the fourth year, Mamagonin came again, and again she stood.  
Again the few; again the narrow place; again our dead.  
Lonuerninat did not bend. Lonuerninat did not bow.  
But the storm does not stop for one reed. The field was Talenonoy's.  

At [[evt:901|Nudhuruigirimu]], by Nodoruekrim of the hills,  
in the fifth year, the third time, they met.  
Her spears were few; ours were fewer than before, and wiser.  
Mamagonin went up by the goat-path; he came down from the rocks.  
The field was Talenonoy's. Three times, three times the field was ours.  

*[Some lines lost. Traces: "[…] the wall of Biniynge […]", "[…] no bread […]".]*

[…] and the lords of Buneynga came out to the camp,  
the lords of Buneynga came out with their hands open.  
Their hands were shut before; now their hands were open.  
At [[evt:903|Biniynge]] they swore it, by the river and by the storm:  
the tribute owed, the tribute given, year by year, in its season.  

*[Column iii.]*

O you who hear this song, remember the cost.  
The tribute of Buneynga was bought with the blood of Talenonoy.  
Seven hundred and more of ours, one for every two of theirs, went down to Shyedartsi,  
went down to the house below, where the beer is dust.  
Let the singer not hide it. A true song does not hide it.  
[[dei:140|Winba]] of the earth drank deep; she drank from our cup too.  

Now Mamagonin too has gone down to the house below.  
He sleeps in the earth; the black ram was given for him.  
And [[per:1228|Larnut]] sits in [[set:79|Talenonoy]], our lady, the eighth year of her sitting,  
she who made the peace with Virnado, she who keeps the storehouse full.  
Every year the silver comes up from Buneynga; every year she weighs it.  
Let it be weighed in [[cur:6|Tyektsi]], let it be weighed before the priests.  

Give the first sheaf to [[dei:139|Nidiktti]]. Give the lamp to [[dei:138|Tuel]].  
Give the beer to Duemino, who goes before the spears.  
Remember Biniynge. Remember the narrow place. Remember the few.
