*[Obverse. Small tablet, closely written; the signs are old in form and some are uncertain.]*

Hear, O [[dei:55|Dumamnul]], lord of the high sky.  
Hear, O [[dei:59|Winba]], mother of the earth.  
Hear, O [[dei:57|Bubin]], lamp of the night, who saw it.

I write what I saw. I write it with the signs. The signs were given to [[pol:11|Poyonga]] in the days of the king's father; that is the [[evt:137|invention of writing in Poyonga]], and the scribes of the house of tablets set down the barley, the sheep, the oil, the silver. Now I set down this.

The men of [[pol:1|Tallin]] came against us, year after year they came. The king's father went out against them; he went out, he did not come back. Winba took him. His son, [[per:110|Nyetluban]], our lord, was a child. He sat upon the seat of his father, and his feet did not reach the floor.

In the next year the men of Tallin came again. They came to the walls of [[set:68|Poyonga]]. They were many: three of theirs for one of ours. Our captain went out from the gate with the men of the city. I saw them go out. Our captain was killed on that field; the men of the city broke. More of ours fell than of theirs. I do not write their number; Bubin knows their number.

Then the men of Tallin came to the gate. The gate was opened.

I saw this: the men of Tallin in the street of the potters.  
I saw this: the men of Tallin in the street of the weavers.  
I saw this: the men of Tallin in the court of the house of [[dei:58|Chithal]], and Chithal did not rise against them.  
I saw the silver taken out of the storehouse, the [[cur:1|inet]] in baskets, the cups and the rings in baskets.  
I saw the oxen driven out of the city.  
I saw the barley carried out of the city.  
I saw the women taken by the hand and led out of the city.

And I saw this, the worst thing: fire in the house of tablets.  
The roof beams burned; the roof beams fell.  
The tablets of the barley were broken under the beams.  
The tablets of the sheep were broken under the beams.  
The tablets of the debts, the tablets of the boundaries, the tablets of the oaths, all were broken.  
All that the scribes had written since the signs were given, the fire ate it.  
What was counted is not counted. What was owed, who knows it now?  
The scribes wept in the ash. I wept in the ash.

*[Reverse.]*

Then the men of Tallin sat down with our lord. Our lord was a child; the elders spoke for him. This was the treaty: four districts went from Poyonga to Tallin, four districts with their fields and their wells. Ten years of peace were sworn. The men of Tallin went away to their own city with their baskets.

Now it is the second year of our lord. The walls are standing. The gate is mended. The fields are sown. [[dei:56|Nabuthin]] of the river rose in his season; he did not forget us. The priests of [[mov:8|Ligay]] say: all things turn on the wheel; what ends will begin again. I say it also.

But the tablets do not begin again. The scribes sit in the court with fresh clay and write the barley again, the sheep again, from the mouths of the old men. The old men do not agree. One says four measures, one says six. This is what the fire did.

So I write this tablet, and I will not put it in the house of tablets. I will put it in the earth, under the floor of my house, where Winba keeps it. Let it lie there. Let someone find it.

> Written in the 2nd year of Nyetluban, lord of Poyonga. Whoever breaks this tablet, may [[dei:60|Shineyta]] of the sea take him and not give him back.
