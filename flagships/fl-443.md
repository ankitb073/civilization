*[Tablet I, obverse]*

Tablet of the words of the council of the faithful of the [[mov:58|Vieshethmekh]], which sat in [[set:177|Piekhkunga]], in the house of assembly by the temple, in the 12th year of [[per:38573|Diekathrelen]], king of [[pol:1645|Piekhkunga]]. Written by the secretary of the council, who was sworn to write what was said and not what was wished.

**The first day.**

The king came in and sat upon the low seat, not the high one, as is right in the house of the One. The high priest stood and blessed the assembly. The lamp before the altar was lit and burned without smoke. The priests of the omens said: "The One is attentive." The king said: "Let it be so."

Present: the high priest, who presided; the priests of the districts; the teachers of the great school; the elders of the city. Present also: the envoys of [[pol:1497|Elekha]], with their own priests, five in number, who sat apart on the left hand.

The high priest said: "We have come together to make one faith of two quarrels. Let each say what is in his heart, and let the secretary write it."

**The first matter: of the One and the old gods.**

The eldest of the envoys of Elekha rose and said: "We honour the One. But the gods of our fathers have their own place and their own due, and we will not call them shadows. A shadow is not fed. Our gods are fed."

The teacher of the great school answered: "The old gods are servants of the One; a servant is fed at his master's table. We do not starve them; we place them."

The envoy said: "You place them below the door." There was murmuring. The high priest struck the floor with his staff, and the murmuring ceased.

**The second matter: of ordination.**

The priest of the eastern district said: "Truth is kept by an ordained priesthood and passed down in order, from hand to hand. Who laid hands on the priests of Elekha? Name the hands." The envoys did not name them. One said: "The hands are known in Elekha." The priest said: "Then the truth is known in Elekha only."

*[Tablet II, obverse]*

**The second day.**

The lamp before the altar guttered at the first hour. The priests of the omens disputed whether this was a sign. Some said it was the wind from the door. The secretary writes that the door was open.

**The third matter: of the end of days.**

The high priest said: "Time runs in a line from the making of the world to the judgement. Will Elekha confess the judgement?" The eldest envoy said: "We do not know the beginning, and we do not presume to know the end. We ask only for justice in dealings and moderation in all things." The teacher of the school said: "Moderation is not a creed." The envoy said: "It has kept us in peace longer than your creed has kept you."

Then the king spoke, and all were silent. The king said: "I have fed you for three days from my own storehouse, and paid your lodging in good [[cur:792|Mafke]]. I would pay twice as much for one faith. But I cannot buy it. Let the faithful be compassionate, as the teaching commands, and let no one go out of this house cursing another."

**The decision.**

The high priest asked: "Will Elekha accept the order of the council?" The envoys said: "We will carry the words home. We cannot accept them here." The high priest asked a second time. The envoys answered the same. The high priest asked a third time. The envoys rose and bowed to the king, and not to the altar, and went out.

So the [[evt:39203|Council of Pikhtunge]] ended without agreement. The faithful of Piekhkunga remain in the order of the council. The envoys of Elekha remain outside it.

*[Tablet II, reverse]*

The secretary has written what was said. What was muttered at the door is not written. May the One, who judges at the end, judge between them, since the council could not.
