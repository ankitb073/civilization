*A prayer to be said at the third hour in every house of [[pol:1532|Ayagu]], for so long as the sickness endures. Written in the year 3978 of the [[mov:24|Uthungala]].*

O One Power, who began the world and will judge it at the end of days; Lord before whom the old gods are as candles before the sun; Thou who seest the inner light in every heart, and needest no go-between: hear us, for we are brought very low.

Thou hast given us much. Thou didst make our fields fat and our markets loud; Thou didst give our princes victory, and the land of Nsinakhizi was joined to our crown at the [[evt:38733|conquest of Nsinakhizi]]; our [[cur:849|Nalu]] was good in every market. And we, seeing Thy favour, mistook it for our own deserving. We were not moderate. We were not just in our dealings. We weighed with a heavy hand and sold with a light one. When our soldiers burned the books at the [[evt:38722|sack of Khambizhinu]], we did not weep. Therefore Thou hast turned Thy face, and the sign of Thy favour is taken from us, and the sign of Thy judgement is set in its place.

For the Grey Sickness began in our own streets, in [[set:93|Ayagu]], Thy city, three years past; and it went out from our gates along the roads of the merchants, as fire goes along a dry hedge; and there is no city on those roads that has not been touched. A third part of the people is gone. The bells are not rung, for there are not enough to ring them. The grave-diggers are buried by other grave-diggers.

Have mercy, Lord, on [[set:5|Nilu]].  
Have mercy on [[set:210|Pagunde]] and on [[set:256|Khambe]].  
Have mercy on [[set:165|Shyith]] and on [[set:124|Athekhizi]].  
Have mercy on [[set:132|Anindu]], on [[set:271|Big]], on [[set:140|Athnambi]].  
Have mercy on [[set:109|Dunjènil]] and on [[set:191|Shimbayi]].  
Have mercy on [[set:38|Jyijinjivji]] and on [[set:296|Gunge]].  
Have mercy on all the cities of the road, whose names we do not know, whose dead we cannot count.  
Have mercy also on our enemies, for the sickness has made them our brothers.

We confess before Thee that the [[evt:38669|Grey Sickness]] is just. We do not ask Thee to be unjust. We ask Thee to be merciful, which is the greater justice.

Strengthen our lord [[per:38194|Naavu]], newly set upon the throne, that he may govern well in a dark hour: that he may open the granaries to the poor, set honest weights in the markets, and keep the roads of the merchants shut until Thy hand is lifted. Let him not seek new victories while his people are dying; let him seek Thee.

Strengthen the physicians, who go where others will not go. Strengthen the mothers who nurse the sick and are not paid. Strengthen those who carry the dead.

Give to every one of us, in the silence of our own hearts, the light by which we may see our faults, for Thou hast taught that every seeker may find the truth within; and when we have seen them, give us the will to mend them.

And when Thou hast judged us, and found us wanting, and still forgiven us, then let the sign of Thy favour return: let the fields be fat again, and the markets loud, and let us this time remember from whose hand they came.

We are dust, and Thou art the One. Thy will be done in Ayagu, as at the end of days. So be it.
