In the name of the One, who was before the beginning and who waits at the end of the road, before whose seat every king shall stand bareheaded among the beggars. Amen.

I, [[per:34426|Tukulven]], son of Bimlith of blessed memory, of the house of Nuftafbafin, by the grace of the One, Emperor of [[pol:1405|Yidhe]], lord of [[set:0|Tallin]] and of the lands that were added to it in my days, being weak in body but, thanks be to the One, sound in mind, in the fifty-third year of my reign and the year 4612 from the founding of Tallin, make this my testament, that my children and my vassals and the priests of the true faith may know my will and keep it.

**Of my life.** I came to the throne a child of nine years, when my lady mother was taken from us, and the realm was then small and beset. Of the troubles of those years I will not write; they were the fault of other men, and I have forgiven them, as the faith commands. When I came into my own strength I took up the sword that the One had set in my hand, and He did not let it fall.

**Of my wars.** When the enemy came into our lands, twice I went out at the head of my host and twice he fled before me. And when a neighbouring king was proud against us, my armies went down at [[evt:35604|Baaliurinu]], and at [[evt:35613|Puthnimdu]], where the captain of the enemy fell, and the One gave us the field. Then I came to Zhinontunu, the city of the gate, where the first teacher spoke the word that we hold, and by [[evt:35614|the taking of that city]] I brought the holy place of our faith under the protection of the faithful. There I made [[evt:35615|the treaty]], and the king of that country became my vassal, and the lands along his border were joined to Yidhe. Let my heirs keep them.

And when the city of Ban refused the peace I offered, I made [[evt:35712|war upon Ban]]. At [[evt:35724|Khash]], at [[evt:35731|Lininadhe]], at [[evt:35741|Nivanga]], my armies prevailed. [[evt:35742|Nivanga was entered]], and [[evt:35743|Ban was joined to my realm]] for ever. I did not seek these wars. I sought only order, and the release of the peoples from the suffering of disorder, which is the beginning of mercy.

**Of my sorrows.** Four sons the One gave me, and four sons He took back before me, one in his youth and three in their childhood. I do not ask why. I have laid them in the house of prayer at Tallin, and I go to them.

**Of the succession.** I leave the crown of Yidhe and all its lands to my daughter [[per:34883|Yilorath]], the eldest of my living children, who has sat at my council these many years and knows its business better than my ministers. Let every vassal who holds land of me come to her within forty days of my death and put his hands between hers, as his fathers put theirs between mine. Whoever withholds his homage, let him be accounted a traitor before men and a perjurer before the One.

To the Empress my wife I leave the palace by the river and its revenues for her lifetime. To my younger daughter I leave a dowry fitting the daughter of an emperor, to be paid from my treasury in [[cur:778|zhiflun]] of good weight.

**Of my soul.** To the priests of the [[mov:58|Vishithmikh]] I leave the tenth part of my treasure, that they may feed the poor at the gates of every house of prayer in the realm for a year after my death, and pray for me. For the faith teaches that the good life is the life of compassion; and if in my wars I caused suffering that I did not see, let these alms release some part of it, and let the One weigh them against it.

Let my body be buried without gold.

Witnessed by the high priest and the lords of the council, and sealed with my seal.
