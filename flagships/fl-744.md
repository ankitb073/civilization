*[Obverse. A refrain is marked in the margin with a sign like a flame.]*

Sing it, you who came out of the smoke.  
Sing it, you who came out of [[set:4|Nelo]] with ash in your hair.  
Hear us, [[dei:19|Yenan]], moon, who looked down that night and did not turn away.  
Hear us, [[dei:18|Eba]], fire, who was let loose and did not know his own house.  

In the fifth year of [[per:19786|Revven]], our king, the shield of Nelo,  
the men of [[pol:777|Tavlen]] came up against us.  
They were many as the barley; we were few as the olive trees on the hill.  
Our men went out before the walls. Our men stood. Our men did not stand.  
That was the [[evt:20183|Battle of Nilu]]. The field by the walls was red.  
The gates were shut. The gates were broken. The night was loud.  

*Nelo, Nelo, mother of the river towns,*  
*where are your doors? Where are your children?*  

They came into the street of the bakers. They came into the street of the weavers.  
They came into the court of the temple and took the gold of [[dei:17|Nedhekhte]], lady of the grain.  
They took the oxen. They took the girls at the well. They took the bronze from the doors.  
And Eba ran on the roofs. Eba ran from roof to roof like a boy on a feast day.  

They set fire to the house of tablets.  
There the scribes kept the names of our fathers, the fields of our fathers,  
the debts and the gifts, the oaths of the kings, the boundaries of the land.  
The tablets of clay were fired in the fire and cracked in the fire.  
The tablets fell in the dust and the dust was the names of our fathers.  
Who will say now whose field is whose? Who will say now who owes and who is owed?  
A scribe stood in the door of the house of tablets and wept, and they killed him there.  

*Nelo, Nelo, mother of the river towns,*  
*where are your doors? Where are your children?*  

*[Reverse]*

That was the [[evt:20184|Sack of Nilu]]. We came out of the smoke.  
We came out by the water gate. We sat by the river and did not speak.  
In the morning the city was standing, but it was black. The city was standing, but it was empty.  

Then the king sat down with the men of Tavlen. That was the [[evt:20185|Treaty of Nilu]].  
The criers said: the river towns go to Tavlen. The criers said: tribute to Tavlen,  
tribute every year for twenty-two years.  
My son will be grown when the tribute is ended. My son is three years old.  

Yet hear this too, sing this too: the same year the king went out against other enemies,  
and at the [[evt:20181|Battle of Binge]] he broke them, Revven, the shield of Nelo,  
and last year they made peace and bowed to him.  
So the gods take with one hand and give with the other. The wheel turns.  

Now we have come back. We have cleared the street of the bakers.  
The new roof beams are white; the old walls are black under them.  
A measure of barley in the market is an [[cur:418|Orol]] and a half.  
We pay it. We grind it. We bake it in ovens built of the broken tablets.  

*Nelo, Nelo, mother of the river towns,*  
*your doors are new. Your children are few.*  

O Yenan, moon, count us at night; we are fewer than we were.  
O Nedhekhte, lady of the grain, fill the fields that have no names now.  
[[dei:15|Benva]], earth, keep the boundaries yourself, since the tablets cannot.  
In the seventh year of Revven we sing this. Let our children sing it after us.
