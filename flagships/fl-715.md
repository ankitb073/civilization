*[Sherd 1.]*

O [[dei:74|Binge]], lady of the grain, lady of the full jar, lady of the threshing floor,  
hear me. I am small. I scratch with a nail on a broken pot.  
I have no bull. I have no ram. I have no barley-cake.  
I have this sherd. Take this sherd.  

In the 11th year of [[per:9897|Nange]], king in [[set:33|Kambizhin]], king of the wide land,  
I scratch this. The scribe will not write for me. I have nothing to give the scribe.  
So I scratch it myself, badly. Read it anyway.  

*[Sherd 2.]*

Hear what was. The sky was brass. The sky was brass and the earth was a hearth.  
[[dei:77|Pugazhu]] of the sun stood over the fields, and he did not go down.  
The barley came up and lay down. The barley came up and lay down.  
The wells of [[reg:26|Khantsi]] gave mud. Then they gave nothing.  
The old men said: it is [[evt:9582|the Great Drought in Pyivi]], it has come over the hills to us.  
The old men said: it came before, in their fathers' days. It went away.  

Then the hunger came. Then the [[evt:9689|Famine in Khanji]].  
Two years it sat in the house. Two years it ate at our table.  

*[Sherd 3.]*

I had a husband. I had three children. I had a goat with a bell.  
The goat I gave for one basket of barley. One basket, and the bell.  
My bronze pin I gave for half a basket. My mother's bronze pin.  
My little son I gave to the earth. My little daughter I gave to the earth.  
I did not give them. The hunger took them. I say it plainly.  

In our lane there were eight houses. Now one has no smoke.  
The family of the potter went away with the wanderers on the road,  
with the herds and the carts, and they did not look back.  
The road is long. I do not know if they are living.  

*[Sherd 4.]*

O [[dei:73|Tithu]], lord of the storm, black-bearded, loud one, rider of the cloud,  
you were asleep. Wake now. Wake now and shout over the hills.  
O Tithu, break the brass sky with your axe. Let the water fall out of it.  
O Binge, lady of the grain, the rain is not yours, I know.  
Speak to Tithu for me. You sit near him at the feast. Speak to him.  

The priests say the wheel turns. What ends will begin again.  
The hunger ended. Let it not begin again. Let the wheel turn the other way.  
I have kept the rites of [[mov:11|Kyif]] as I could. I poured water when I had no beer.  
I poured dust when I had no water. Count it to me.  

*[Sherd 5.]*

This year there is a little barley. It is thin. It stands.  
My husband says: do not praise it, the gods will hear and be jealous.  
I do not praise it. I only say: it stands.  
My last child is a girl. She is seven summers. Her ribs are going under again.  

O Binge, keep the barley standing. O Tithu, bring the dark cloud.  
O Pugazhu, lord of the sun, go down at evening as you should, and sleep long.  
I will break this pot at your door. I will break it so you will remember.  
Remember the woman of the eight houses. Remember her lane.
