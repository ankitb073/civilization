In the 290th year of Luthe. Thus says the court of [[pol:134|Luthe]], the house of the king, the elders who stand before the seat, the keepers of the seal, the keepers of the granary, the keepers of the door. Hear it, gods. Hear it, men. Hear it, you who come after.

[[dei:73|Tithu]] of the storm, hear us. [[dei:74|Binge]] of the grain, hear us. Bumbi of the sea, hear us. [[dei:76|Tsilmu]] of wisdom, hear us. Pugazhu of the sun, who has looked on Luthe for two hundred and ninety years, hear us. We do not lie before you.

The king our lord has laid down the seat. He has gone out from the palace. He sits in his own house and does not come out. The seat of Luthe is empty. The seal of Luthe is in our hands. This is the last time the seal will be pressed.

Hear how it came to this. The king our lord went out to Aguga against the enemy. [[evt:5439|At Aguga]] the gods turned their face from us. [[evt:5454|At Aguga]] a second time the gods turned their face from us. [[evt:5460|At Aguga]] a third time the gods turned their face from us. [[evt:5473|At Dadavli]] the king our lord went out again, and the young men of Luthe lay down in the fields of Dadavli and did not rise. Then the king our lord set his seal on [[evt:5474|the tablet of Dadavli]], and the tribute went out from Luthe year upon year, silver upon silver, and the granaries grew thin, and the land grew thin.

Then the cloak of Luthe was torn. [[evt:5573|The house of Luthe was broken]] in the 288th year of Luthe, and six men took six pieces of the cloak. In [[set:9|Tagath]], our city, where the seat stands, a lord sits who is lord of [[pol:237|Tagath]] only. In [[pol:238|Kavu]] a lord sits. In [[pol:239|Nkinakhuzi]] a lord sits. In [[pol:240|Mbilu]] a lord sits. In [[pol:241|Mbil]] a lord sits. In [[pol:242|Tatavli]], where the tablet of the tribute was sealed, a lord sits. Six seats where there was one. Six fires where there was one hearth.

Therefore the court decrees, and these are its last words.

Let the fire on the hearth of the court be put out with milk and not with water, as is done when a father dies.

Let the images of the gods be carried out of the house of the king, each to its own house, Tithu to the house of Tithu, Binge to the house of Binge, Bumbi to the house of Bumbi, Tsilmu to the house of Tsilmu, Pugazhu to the house of Pugazhu. Let the teachers of [[mov:11|Kyif]] go with them and sing the old words, for no one owns the truth, but the old words must not be lost.

Let the tablets of the court be sealed in jars. Let the jars be set beneath the threshold of the house of Tsilmu. Let no man break them. Let him who breaks them have no son.

Let the silver that remains in the treasury, the [[cur:99|ngüzhin]] that the tribute did not take, be weighed out to the widows of Aguga and the widows of Dadavli, each widow her portion, until it is gone.

Let the scribes, the cupbearers, the doorkeepers and the guards of the gate be loosed from their oath to the seat of Luthe. Let each go to the lord of the land where his house stands, and serve him well. The court does not curse them.

Let the six lords remember that they ate the bread of Luthe. Let them pour water for the kings of Luthe at the new moon. If they pour, may Binge fill their granaries. If they do not pour, may Tithu break their roofs.

The great wheel turns. What ends will begin again. The court of Luthe has ended. Let it begin again when the gods will it.

The seal is pressed. The seal is broken. The tablet is finished.
