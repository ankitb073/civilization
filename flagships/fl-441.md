*Written by the king's son in his own hand, to be sung at the lighting of the lamps on the night the marriage was made, now kept for the second time.*

*I. Invocation*

[[dei:18|Eba]], lord of the hearth-fire, who first lit this lamp between us,  
burn on, though the wind is up and the shutters knock;  
[[dei:19|Yienan]], who rose full on the night we were joined,  
rise full again, and look on us without reproach;  
[[dei:17|Niedhekhte]], lady of the grain, who heaped the marriage-table,  
forgive us that the table is plainer now. The granaries go to the army.

*II. The day remembered*

Two years ago the gates of [[set:18|Lienonoshal]] stood open,  
and the road from [[pol:1142|Viemonga]] was strewn with green boughs.  
You came in a litter hung with the dyed cloth of your country,  
and your father rode beside you, grey and upright, a king among kings.  
The priests of [[mov:2|Zhienro]] read the order of the rite,  
each god in place, each due paid, nothing omitted, nothing added;  
and my father, [[per:25759|Talediemenen]] the king, took your father's hand,  
and the two houses swore before [[dei:16|Duemamnol]] of the sky  
that what was joined that day would not be put asunder by men.  
This was the [[evt:26229|marriage of Talediemenen II and Nofath]],  
and I was the second of that name, and you were [[per:25907|Nofath]], and we were young.

*III. The turning*

The wise say that all things turn upon a great wheel,  
and that what ends will begin again. I believed it as a boy  
the way one believes the sea is wide, without having crossed it.  
Now I have seen a little of its turning.  
Your father has gone down to [[dei:15|Bienva]], and lies in the earth of his city;  
another sits in his seat; and the oath that two kings swore  
is weighed by other hands than theirs. The heralds have gone out.  
War with your father's city is proclaimed in the market,  
and the young men of [[pol:1139|Lienonoshal]] are drilling in the square below our window.

*IV. To the bride, in the measured voice*

I will not tell you that my father is wrong; he is my father and my king,  
and a son who sings against his father sings against the order of the gods.  
I will not tell you that your country is wrong; it is your country,  
and you have wept for it in the night where you thought I did not hear.  
I will tell you only this: that a marriage is not a treaty.  
A treaty is kept as long as it profits; a marriage is kept as long as there is breath.  
Let the captains count their spears and their [[cur:568|tonio]];  
I have counted otherwise, and the sum comes out the same each time.

*V. Prayer*

[[dei:14|Buevme]], lady of the spear, I do not ask you to turn aside;  
the war is yours, and you will do with it as you will.  
I ask only this: when the spears are laid down, whichever way they lie,  
let there be a house left standing in which this lamp can burn,  
and a table, however plain, at which the two of us may sit.

*VI. Envoi*

Sing it, then, as it was sung on the first night:  
the gates open, the boughs green, the lamp lit, the wheel turning.  
What ends will begin again. Let it begin with us.

*Given at Lienonoshal, in the year 3797 from the founding of Lienonoshal.*
