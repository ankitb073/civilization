**THE ROYAL CHARTER FOR THE PLANTATION OF TATHTSIDI**

*Given under the Great Seal at Kankush, and printed by authority for the instruction of all who shall take ship.*

[[per:44645|Nakagum]], by the providence of the One Power, Queen of [[pol:1915|Kankush]], to all to whom these presents shall come, greeting.

**Preamble.** Whereas it is not yet twenty years since the old dominion of Kankush [[evt:45123|fell asunder]], and this realm [[evt:45124|was raised up]] out of its ruins by the labour of our predecessors; and whereas by the blessing of the One this kingdom has since been settled in peace, its laws restored, and [[evt:45191|its own coin struck]] for the first time in the memory of its people; and whereas divers of our loyal subjects, being crowded in their holdings at home and desirous of honest labour, have petitioned us for leave to pass over the sea and plant themselves in the country of [[reg:7|Kyinna]]; We, having considered the matter in Council, and judging that a realm which does not grow will diminish, do grant and ordain as follows.

**Article I. Of the Place.** The said subjects shall have leave to settle at the harbour of [[set:322|Tathtsidi]], and upon the land lying about it so far as they may peaceably occupy and till, which land is hereby declared part of the dominions of the Crown of Kankush for ever, and the settlement thereon [[evt:45310|founded]] shall be called a town of this kingdom and not a foreign place.

**Article II. Of Government.** The plantation shall be governed by a Governor named by Us and answerable to Us alone, assisted by a Council of six of the planters chosen yearly by the heads of households. No law shall be made there contrary to the laws of this realm; and in any matter not provided for, the custom of [[set:97|Kankush]] shall be followed.

**Article III. Of Religion.** The planters shall maintain the worship of the [[mov:162|Duthladul]], and shall build a house of teaching before they build a house of trade. And since the message of the One is for all peoples, they shall instruct such of the inhabitants of Kyinna as are willing to hear, by teaching and example and never by force; for teachers are honoured, but no one owns the truth, and a faith that is driven in by the sword is driven out by the next sword.

**Article IV. Of Land.** Every man who goes over at his own charge shall receive fifty measures of land, and as much again for every grown son; every man who is carried at the charge of the Crown shall serve the Crown in the common works for three years, and shall then receive half that portion. No man shall hold more land than he and his household can till, for the wise withdraw from appetite, and a plantation of great estates and idle masters is a plantation already in decay.

**Article V. Of Trade.** All goods shipped from the plantation shall come first to the port of Kankush, and pay there the ordinary duties; and the planters shall be free of all duties outward for seven years, to give them a beginning. Accounts in the plantation shall be kept in [[cur:1060|kunde]] of the royal mint, and no foreign coin shall be current there by law.

**Article VI. Of Defence.** Every planter between sixteen and sixty shall keep arms and come to the muster at the Governor's call; but the Governor shall begin no war against any people of that country without Our express command, on pain of recall.

**Article VII. Of Duty.** The planters, though they dwell across the sea, remain Our subjects in every respect, owing Us the same allegiance, and enjoying the same protection, as if they dwelt within sight of Our palace. What they build, they build for the realm; what the realm owes them, it shall pay.

In witness whereof We have caused these Our letters to be made patent, and the [[evt:45309|plantation of Tathtsidi]] to be entered in the rolls of the kingdom.

Given at Our palace of Kankush, the 28 Nigu 42 of the Duthladul, in the second year of Our reign.

*By the Queen herself, in Council.*
