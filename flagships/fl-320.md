*[Column i. The upper edge is lost.]*

[…] in the reeds by the river […]  
[…] and the herons rose up.

Sing, O singer, of the spears of [[pol:25|Yida]],  
sing of the nine harvests of the tribute war,  
when the great house said to [[pol:10|Lenunushal]]: "Bring silver, bring oxen,  
bring the barley of [[reg:9|Tupujinga]] to the storehouse of [[set:0|Tallin]]."  
And Lenunushal said: "We will not bring it."  
Then [[dei:14|Vulme]] of the bronze arm took up her spear.

[[per:894|Jeytathrelin]] the grey general went out first,  
the grey general went out to the town of [[set:18|Lenunushal]].  
[[per:901|Nasheyshalath]], queen of Lenunushal, stood in the reeds.  
The reeds were full of her spears. The water was full of her spears.  
Two thousand eight hundred and thirty of Yida lay down in the water.  
They did not rise. Weep for them, women of Tallin, weep for them.  
That year the field was not ours. Vulme turned her face.

*[Column ii]*

The next year [[per:955|Kokulufath]] went out, the queen of the tall spear,  
the elder daughter of the house, whose word was like a bull's horn.  
To Lenunushal she went out; she went to the same reeds.  
Nasheyshalath stood against her, and Nasheyshalath did not stand.  
Eight hundred and forty of Lenunushal lay down in the water.  
The field was Yida's. The field was Vulme's.

Jeytathrelin went out again, the grey general,  
to [[set:6|Jinontonu]] he went out, by its walls he stood.  
[[per:962|Takulvan]], the shield of Lenunushal, stood against him.  
Takulvan stood. Takulvan did not stand.  
The field was Yida's. The field was Vulme's.

[…] two lines lost […]

Kokulufath went out to [[set:67|Duninga]], the queen of the tall spear,  
Takulvan stood against her, the shield of Lenunushal.  
One thousand and seventy-one of Lenunushal lay down by Duninga.  
The field was Yida's. The field was Vulme's.

*[Column iii]*

Then Kokulufath went down to the wheel, full of honour, and the house wept.  
Then [[per:957|Shenshetkuth]] took up the spear of her sister,  
Shenshetkuth the young lioness, she who is our queen this fortieth year.  
To Duninga she went out, to the same field.  
[[per:990|Kulvatovan]], the son of Nasheyshalath, the new king of Lenunushal, stood against her.  
One thousand two hundred and thirty-three of Lenunushal lay down.  
The field was Yida's. The field was Vulme's.

In the ninth harvest Shenshetkuth went out to Jinontonu.  
Takulvan stood against her, the shield of Lenunushal.  
A third time he stood in the field. A third time he did not stand.  
Takulvan fell on that field; he did not rise; the shield was broken.  
One thousand three hundred and four of Lenunushal lay down with him.  
The field was Yida's. The field was Vulme's.

Then Kulvatovan came out of his tent with his hands open.  
Before the walls of Jinontonu he put his hand to the clay,  
and Shenshetkuth put her hand to the clay.  
This was the [[evt:726|Treaty of Zhinontunu]]. Hear it and remember it.  
Then the silver came to Tallin. Then the oxen came to Tallin.  
The barley of Tupujinga went up into the storehouse.

*[Column iv]*

Give the first sheaf to [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]], lady of the grain.  
Give the first ram to Vulme. Give the second ram to [[dei:16|Dumamnul]] of the high sky.  
Let the priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] pour in order, each god in the god's own place.

Hear it, young men who went out last summer to the field of Virathkhanu,  
who went out to the walls of Himi:  
the spears of Yida went out, and the spears of Yida came home.  
The wheel turns. What went down comes up again.  
Shenshetkuth was young then. Shenshetkuth is our queen now.  
May [[dei:19|Yenan]] the moon keep her. May […]

*[The rest of the column is broken away.]*

*[Colophon on the left edge:]* Second tablet of the Song of the Tribute. Written in the 40th year of Shenshetkuth.
