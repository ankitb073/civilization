*[Obverse. Heading in large signs.]*

> These are the words that [[per:1937|A'inmamshe]], king of [[pol:67|La'im]], spoke at the gate of [[set:108|Ashash]], on the day of the new barley, in the 6th year of A'inmamshe. The scribe of the gate wrote them as they were spoken.

Men of La'im! Women of La'im! Sons of the free!  
Look at me. I am A'inmamshe. Five summers ago I was a man with a spear and nothing else.  
Look at yourselves. Five summers ago you were servants of [[pol:52|Tomözel]].

Do you remember? I remember.  
The tax-man of Tomözel came at harvest; he took the first sheaf and the second sheaf.  
The captain of Tomözel came at the new moon; he took your sons for his spears.  
The priest of Tomözel came at the feast; he poured first for his own city, and last for ours.  
We bowed. We bowed. Our fathers bowed, and their fathers bowed.

Then, in the first year, we stood up. That was the [[evt:1629|Revolt of La'im]].  
I stood up in Ashash, and Ashash stood up with me.  
I said: the land of [[reg:1|Yisamashti]] does not belong to Tomözel; it belongs to those who plough it.  
I said it at this gate, at this very gate, and you shouted.

Hear what came after. Let the children hear it. Let the scribes write it twice.

At [[set:3|Eshue'ethie]], by the walls of Eshue'ethie, the men of Tomözel came out against us.  
[[per:1961|Sho'e Yueve]] the lion went out against them; [[per:1962|Yiedi'iluesh'a]] stood against him.  
Many were the spears of Tomözel, few were the spears of La'im.  
But Yiedi'iluesh'a broke, and the field was La'im's. That was the [[evt:1652|Battle of Ishwyithie]].  
Sho'e Yueve sleeps now with the fathers; he lay down last year, and the gods have him.  
Pour for him! Pour for Sho'e Yueve!

At Eshue'ethie a second time I went out myself. Yiedi'iluesh'a stood against me a second time.  
Again they were more than we. Again they broke.  
Their dead lay in the barley like sheaves after the sickle; for each of ours, five of theirs, and more.  
That was the [[evt:1655|Battle of Ishwyithie]], and after it we sat down before the walls;  
we sat down in the [[evt:1656|Siege of Ishwyithie]], and the men of Tomözel did not sleep.

At [[set:83|Khie'a]], in the last summer, [[per:1975|Yueppafoyatl]] came against me.  
Now we were more than they. Now the wheel had turned.  
Yueppafoyatl broke. Yueppafoyatl ran. The field was La'im's. That was the [[evt:1661|Battle of Kheye]].

Then the men of Tomözel came to Eshue'ethie with their hands open.  
They sat down with me. They swore by the gods. I swore by the gods.  
La'im is free. La'im pays no sheaf. La'im gives no son.  
That was the [[evt:1663|Treaty of Ishwyithie]]; the tablet of it is in the house of the gods.

Hear me. They say: A'inmamshe was lucky. I say: [[dei:5|Nieshashta]], lady of the spear, went before me.  
They say: Tomözel was weak. I say: Tomözel was strong, and we were stronger.  
They say: few of ours died. I say: every one of ours is counted, and every one is mourned,  
and for every one of ours that lay down, three of Tomözel lay down.  
Who says otherwise is a man of Tomözel, and lies.

Now hear what I ask.  
The gods brought us out; feed the gods. Pour to [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky. Pour to Nieshashta.  
Pour to Muevas'e of the earth, who drank the blood of Tomözel and was not sick.  
The walls of Ashash must be strong; carry stones. The granary of Ashash must be full; bring sheaves.  
Enemies do not sleep. A free people must not sleep either.

All things turn on the wheel. What ends will begin again.  
We were servants; the wheel turned. Let it never turn back.  
Men of La'im! Women of La'im! Say it with me: La'im is free!

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> And the people said it, three times. Written at the gate of Ashash in the 6th year of A'inmamshe.
