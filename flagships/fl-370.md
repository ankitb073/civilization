*[Obverse. A large tablet with the royal seal impressed three times along the upper edge. Copies were evidently sent to several temples; this one bears the docket of a provincial house.]*

Thus says [[per:19884|Shail]], queen of [[pol:867|Mane]], the anointed one, who was washed in the water of the old houses as a girl and who poured the first oil on the altars in the year she took the throne. To the priests of the houses of [[mov:2|Tlindu]] in every town of Mane, to the keepers of the fire, to the keepers of the grain-stores, to the singers of the moon: say to them.

With me it is well. With my house it is well. With my city [[set:13|Tsivintuha]] it is well. With you, may it be well.

Hear me, for I write to you not as a stranger writes, but as one of your own. I knelt with you. I fasted with you on the nights of the dark moon. I read the livers of the rams with you in the court of the sky-father, and the livers spoke true. Do not say, "The queen has forgotten us." I have not forgotten.

Hear now what was done, and why it was done.

For many years the new teaching has gone up and down the roads of Mane. You have seen it. The teachers of [[mov:57|I'ishiu]] came first to the river-quays, then to the market-towns, then to the villages of the hills. They spoke of one power above all powers, and of a life of compassion and release from suffering. The porters heard them. The weavers heard them. The soldiers of my guard heard them. In the town-courts, when I went out to judge, I saw the sign of the new teaching on the doorposts, house after house, more than I saw the old signs.

In the winter of this year the elders of the towns came to me, one after another. They said: "Our people pray to the One. Let the queen pray with her people." I asked the omens. I did not ask once; I asked three times. The first ram was clean. The second ram was clean. On the third night the moon rose red and then white, and the old women said: a thing is ending, and a thing is beginning.

Therefore I have done it. Mane has taken up I'ishiu as the faith of the kingdom. This is the [[evt:20162|Conversion of Mane to I'ishiu]], and it is sealed with my seal, in the sixteenth year of my reign.

Now hear what I say to you, my brothers and sisters of the old houses, and keep it in your hearts.

The teachers of I'ishiu say that the old gods are the servants of the One, or its shadows on the wall. I do not dispute with them. But I say to you: a servant is not cast out of the house. The lady of the grain still stands in the field. The lord of the fire still sits in the hearth. Your altars will not be broken by any hand of mine. Whoever breaks them, let him answer to the queen.

The bread of the old houses shall not be taken from you in my days. The temple fields that feed you, they are yours. The silver that the crown has paid to your houses at the new year, so many [[cur:454|Ihal]] to each house as was written by my fathers, it will be paid this year as before. Let no priest go hungry because the queen has knelt in a new place.

And I ask one thing of you in return. Do not curse the teachers in the market. Do not set the people against the people. The wheel turns; your own fathers taught me that, and the new teachers say it also. What ends will begin again. Let it turn without blood.

If you will not hear me in this, then hear me in the old way: the omens were asked, and the omens answered. I am the anointed one still. I have spoken.

*[Lower edge.]*

> Written at Tsivintuha, in the 16th year of Shail, queen of Mane.  
> Let the priest who receives this tablet read it aloud in the court of his house, before the altar, and set it in the store with the old tablets.
