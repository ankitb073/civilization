*[Prism fragment; the first column begins mid-sentence.]*

[…] and the gods of the land, who share the world each in his place, gave me the spear, and I did not let it fall.

I am [[per:17664|Narniyen]], the great king, king of [[pol:758|Tungarvikhrem]], king of the four roads, in the forty-third year of my kingship. Hear how I made the peace with [[pol:799|Nodorvikhrimu]], and how my envoys went and came, and what the gods showed.

For nine summers the men of Nodorvikhrimu held the land that was mine to take, and would not yield it. They were proud. They shut their gates. They mocked my messengers. Therefore I went down against them in the [[evt:18117|war]] that the gods had set before me.

At [[evt:18224|Viririnu]] I went down myself, with my chariots and my spearmen. Their army stood against me on the slope. I broke it. I scattered it. I left their dead for the kites; they lay so thick that the kites grew fat and could not fly. Of mine, few fell.

In that same year my army went into their city, and [[evt:18225|the sack]] of it was great. Their storehouses I emptied. Their house of tablets I burned, that their lying chronicles should be ashes. Let them write their lies again, if they have clay.

In the next year they gathered once more under [[per:18124|Elelen]], and came out to [[evt:18243|Khuele]]. My general went against them. Elelen fled from the field, and his men fell behind him like barley behind the sickle, more than I could number. Then my army sat down before their city a second time, and [[evt:18244|the siege]] was laid, and no grain went in.

**The first going of the envoys.** Then I sent my envoy, a man of my household, wise in speech, to the gate of Nodorvikhrimu. Before he went, the priests opened a ram, and the liver was whole and bright, without a spot. They said: the gods are pleased; the gate will open. My envoy went. He came back and said: "They ask, 'What will the great king take?' I said: 'All the land of the war, and the towns in it.' They tore their garments. They said: 'Then we will die on the wall.'"

**The second going.** I sent him again. Before he went, an eagle flew from the right hand over the camp and did not return. The priests said: the enemy's heart has flown from him. My envoy came back and said: "They ask, 'Will the great king leave us our own city?' I said: 'The great king is just, and moderate in all things, as the priests of [[mov:25|Myigudogüyshuye]] teach. He does not want your city. He wants the land of the war, and Khuele, and Viririnu, and the fields between them.' They wept. They said: 'Let Elelen come out and swear.'"

**The swearing.** Then I went myself to the field before [[set:151|Nodorvikhrimu]]. Elelen came out with his elders. He bowed. He did not look at my face. We swore before the gods of both peoples, and the priests poured the wine, and the [[evt:18245|treaty]] was pressed into clay and baked in my fire.

This is what Elelen gave me: the land of the war, all of it; the towns of Khuele and of Viririnu and the third town that lies between; the fields and the wells and the shepherds' paths. Ten summers there shall be no spear between us. If he breaks the oath, the gods will break him.

I did not take silver for my own house. The wise withdraw from wealth and appetite. I gave the silver of the spoil, weighed in [[cur:392|Müvi]], to the houses of the gods in [[set:172|Tungarvikhrem]], and to the widows of my soldiers, and to the priests who read the liver truly.

*[Column ends. Colophon on the base of the prism.]*

> Written in the 43rd year of Narniyen, king of Tungarvikhrem. Whoever erases my name, may the gods erase his.
