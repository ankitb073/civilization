Thus says [[per:8661|Tafvafmagonin]], the great king, king of [[pol:285|Vani]], beloved of [[dei:64|Nidi]], whom [[dei:63|Dvimamnul]] of the sky has set upon the throne:

Hear me, men of Vani. Hear me, men of [[pol:383|Tsayiy]]. Hear me, you gods who stand about this place.

Forty-five years I have sat upon the throne of [[set:100|Kvile]]. Forty-five years I have poured the beer and slaughtered the ox. Forty-five years the gods have not turned their faces from me.

Who began this war? Tsayiy began it. Tsayiy shut its gates against my messengers. Tsayiy sent away my gifts. Tsayiy spoke proud words against Vani. Therefore I took up [[evt:8673|the war against Tsayiy]], and eight years I did not lay it down.

Five times the host of Vani came before Tsayiy. Five times the shields were set in rows before these walls. Once the gods tested us, and we went back to the river. Four times Nidi gave us the field. In the first battle their captain fell before my spear, and his bronze was carried to Kvile. In [[evt:8768|the battle before Tsayiy in the last year]] I myself stood at the head of the host, I, Tafvafmagonin, with the spear of my fathers in my hand, and the men of Tsayiy fled before me like goats before the wolf. In [[evt:8786|the battle of this year]] my captain went up against them, and the field was ours by noon.

Their dead were as the sand of the river. Their dead were ten thousand and more. My own dead were few; the gods kept them. Their widows wail on the walls; my widows are not many.

Then I came to this city. Its gate I broke, its storehouses I opened, its oxen and its silver I took for the gods of Vani. So was [[evt:8787|the sack of Tsayiy]] done, and it was the fourth time this city has bowed. Let Tsayiy remember it. Let the children of Tsayiy remember it.

Now hear the words of the peace.

[[per:9069|Iva Mav]], king of Tsayiy, stands before me. He has come out of the gate. He has bowed his head. He has put dust upon it. Good. I do not strike a man who bows.

These are the words.

For ten years there will be no war between Vani and Tsayiy. Ten years the spear of Vani will sleep, if Tsayiy keeps its oath. If Tsayiy breaks its oath, the spear of Vani will wake.

So is [[evt:8788|the peace sworn at Tsayiy]]. I swear it. Iva Mav swears it. We eat the salt together. We pour the blood of the lamb upon the earth.

Hear, O Nidi, lady of war: you gave the victory; keep the peace.  
Hear, O Dvimamnul, lord of the sky: you have seen it; remember it.  
Hear, O [[dei:62|Novigu]], lady of wisdom: give the scribes true tablets.  
Hear, O [[dei:65|Tono]], lord of the moon: count the ten years.  
Hear, O [[dei:66|Shyidartsi]], lady of the underworld: take the oath-breaker down to the dark.

The silver of the sack will be weighed in the house of the gods at Kvile, niva by [[cur:91|niva]], and the gods will have their portion, and the host will have its portion, and the widows of Vani will have their portion.

Thus says Tafvafmagonin, king of Vani, in the forty-fifth year of his kingship, in [[set:74|Tsayiy]], in the presence of the gods of [[mov:9|Myinive]] and of [[per:9069|Iva Mav]], king of Tsayiy. Whoever breaks this tablet, may his name be broken.
