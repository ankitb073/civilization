*A hymn of the [[mov:70|Vimekhulu]], to be sung at the lamp-lighting, composed in the 7th year of [[per:20626|Naadhash]], king of [[pol:793|Nambe]].*

**I.**

Not to the sky, though the sky is wide;  
not to the sea, though the sea is deep;  
not to the moon, though the moon is fair:  
to the Light behind them all we sing.

You have no temple, and every house is yours.  
You have no altar, and every hearth is yours.  
You have no name that the mouth can hold,  
yet the mouth is not silent before you.

**II.**

See how the gods stand at your gate as stewards stand:  
[[dei:104|Shilim]], who weighs our words, weighs them by your scale;  
[[dei:105|Khudhaze]] spreads the sky as a servant spreads a cloth;  
[[dei:106|Umbe]] carries the lamp of the night on your errand;  
[[dei:108|Bumbe]] the sea keeps the measure you set for her;  
and [[dei:107|Nizhinande]], lord of the spear, lays down his spear at your threshold.  
We do not cast down the gods of our fathers.  
We set them in their places, and see the house they serve.

**III.**

All things turn upon the wheel.  
The grain falls, and the grain rises green.  
The city burns, and the city is built.  
The quarrel ends, and is forgotten, and is forgiven.  
What is a man, that he should grip the spoke  
and cry against the turning?

**IV.**

Remember, citizens, the years before the teaching.  
Remember [[evt:20688|the going-out of Anjith]],  
when men of our own speech set up a seat against the seat of Nambe,  
and few as they were, they would not be persuaded.  
Remember [[evt:20722|Yilish]], where brother's blood ran on brother's field.  
Remember [[evt:20723|the ending of that revolt]],  
when the land was gathered in, and the king's father  
forgave those who had risen, and sent them home.  
Mercy was the first stone. Concord was not yet built upon it.

**V.**

Then in the sixth year of Naadhash,  
in the colonnade of [[set:85|Nambe]], beside the fish-market,  
[[per:20682|Navande]] stood up and spoke.  
He did not say: the truth is mine.  
He said: the truth is given; I am not its owner but its porter.  
Teachers are to be honoured, and no one owns the light.

He said: do your duty. Keep order. Make the right sacrifice.  
He said: the wise withdraw from wealth and appetite;  
the [[cur:409|Kam]] in the strongbox does not weigh a soul.  
He said: this is not for Nambe only;  
the stranger at the gate is a guest of the same Light.

So [[evt:20846|the teaching began]] in the year before this year,  
and those who heard it went home and lit a lamp,  
and in the morning lit another for their neighbour.

**VI.**

O Light behind the gods, keep the city.  
Keep the king in his seat, and his judgement straight.  
Keep the priests of [[mov:16|Ninzhe]] at their altars, for they serve your stewards.  
Keep Navande, your porter, while his feet can walk.  
Keep the ships that go out, and the strangers that come in.  
Keep the poor man's lamp, which is as bright as the rich man's.

And when the wheel brings us round again to darkness,  
let one lamp be found burning in Nambe,  
and let it be ours, the lamp of the [[mov:70|Vimekhulu]].
