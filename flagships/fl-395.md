*[Obverse]*

We are the [[cul:10|Khuduli]]. We walked.  
We are the Khuduli. We walked from the old land.  
Hear it, [[dei:77|Pugazhu]], sun who walked before us.  
Hear it, [[dei:73|Tithu]], storm who walked behind us.  
Hear it, [[dei:74|Binge]], mother of the barley, who turned her face.

In [[reg:26|Khantsi]] were the houses of our fathers.  
In Khantsi were the threshing floors of our fathers.  
In Khantsi were the graves of our fathers, by the white stones.  
The barley was tall in Khantsi. The barley was gold in Khantsi.

Then the rain did not come. Then the rain did not come.  
The sky was bronze. The ground was a potsherd.  
The barley came up and lay down. The barley came up and died.  
The ox ate the thatch. The child ate the bark.  
That was the [[evt:9689|Famine in Khanji]]; two years it sat upon us.  
Of every eight who sat at the hearth, one did not rise.  
Binge, mother, why did you turn your face?  
We gave you the first sheaf. We gave you the first loaf.  
Binge did not answer. The rain did not come.

We are the Khuduli. We walked.

*[Reverse]*

The old men said: "Stay. The graves are here."  
The young women said: "Go. The children are here."  
So we took the children. So we took the seed corn in the jar.  
So we took the cooking stones and the god of the doorpost.  
We left the graves. We left the white stones. We walked.

We walked by the dry river. We walked by the salt pan.  
We walked in the season of heat and in the season of cold.  
One year we walked; ten years we walked; twenty years we walked.  
The father died on the road, and the son walked.  
The son grew a beard on the road, and his son walked.  
How many were we? The scribes of the king counted us at the fords:  
a hundred thousand, and again a hundred thousand, nearly; they put down the reed.  
[[dei:75|Bumbi]] of the sea, we did not see you, but we heard of you.  
[[dei:76|Tsilmu]] of wisdom, you showed us the road by the stars.

We are the Khuduli. We walked.

*[Upper edge and left edge]*

We came down into [[reg:25|Nkinakhuzi]].  
In Nkinakhuzi the river was full. In Nkinakhuzi the grass was green.  
The men of the land looked at us. The men of the land said: "Who are these?"  
And the king's men said: "These are the children of [[pol:405|Kambizhin]]. Let them sit."  
The king's men gave us the low fields by the water.  
The king's men gave us seed from the storehouse of [[set:33|Kambizhin]].

Now the king is [[per:10131|Nandithim]], who was born in the year of the hunger,  
born in the year the barley died, and he lives, and he is great.  
In the seventh year of Nandithim we have built houses.  
In the seventh year of Nandithim we have cut the first sheaf.  
We gave the first sheaf to Binge. This time she turned her face to us.  
We gave the first loaf to Pugazhu. The sun stood over the field.

The road is ended. The [[evt:9690|Migration of the Khudule into Ng'jinaguh]] is ended.  
The wheel turns; what ends will begin again.  
Let it not begin again. Let the rain come in its season.  
We are the Khuduli. We walked. Now we sit.
