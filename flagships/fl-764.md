*[Obverse]*

Weep for [[set:45|Nonina]]. Weep for the town and the villages around it.  
Weep, [[dei:65|Tono]], moon, father of the night, who sees the walls.  
Weep, [[dei:66|Shiyidaditli]], lady of the house below, who has many of our sons.  
We were [[pol:200|Nonina]]. Now we are [[pol:162|Piviyo]].  
We were Nonina. The name is gone from the gate.

The old men say: seven years they fought, and seven years Piviyo came.  
First at Hiviyu. Then at Viradhuthpi.  
At the [[evt:4730|Battle of Viradhuthpi]] our captain fell. Our captain fell with the spear in him.  
Our men were few. Our men were fewer than the men of Piviyo.  
They came home with no captain. They came home with the captain on a shield.

Then Piviyo came to the town. Then the [[evt:4731|Siege of Nunnga]].  
They sat down before the gate. They cut the trees. They cut the vines.  
They drove off the goats of the villages. They ate our barley in their camp.  
We in the villages hid in the reeds. We ate roots. We watched the smoke.  
Then the gate was opened. Then the kings sat down together.

That was the [[evt:4732|Conquest of Noninga]].  
The king of Piviyo, [[per:4953|A'ash]], set his seal. Our king set his seal.  
The criers said: Nonina is no more. All the land of Nonina is Piviyo.  
Thirteen harvests ago. Thirteen harvests we have given to Piviyo.

*[Reverse]*

The men of Piviyo came with their scales. They came with their scribes.  
They wrote the fields. They wrote the goats. They wrote the sons.  
They take the tax in [[cur:106|Ilsha]], weighed on their scales, the silver of Piviyo.  
Where shall a farmer of Nonina find silver? He sells his barley to the men of Piviyo.  
He sells it at their price, and pays them back their own silver. So the silver goes round.  
So the silver goes round, and the barley goes away.

Then four years ago came a strange thing.  
The criers said: the [[evt:4821|Collapse of the Piviyo State]]. The house of A'ash is fallen.  
We laughed in the village. We said: now Nonina will stand up.  
Nonina did not stand up. The criers came again in the same year.  
The criers said: the [[evt:4822|Rise of Piviyo]]. Piviyo stands again. Piviyo has new lords.  
The new lords sent new scribes. The new scribes have new seals.  
The tax is the same tax. The scales are the same scales.  
The masters change their faces. The masters do not change their hands.

Now it is the hundredth year of Piviyo, the scribes say. A hundred years!  
They make a feast at the town. They make us carry the barley for the feast.  
We carry the barley. We do not eat at the feast.

Weep for Nonina. Weep for the name that is gone from the gate.  
Novigwü, lady of wisdom, teach us to be quiet.  
Nidi, lady of war, you were not with us at Viradhuthpi.  
Shiyidaditli, keep our captain in the house below. Give him water there.  
Tono, moon, look on the village. We are still here.  
We are still here, under the new name, under the new seal.
