*[Envelope, sealed with the queen's seal: a woman holding a spear between two lions.]*

To the steward who keeps my house in [[set:7|Ana]], speak. Thus says [[per:973|Sho'e Ash]], queen of [[pol:6|Ana]], beloved of [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] the red-footed, chosen of [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky, lady of the river and the four roads.

With me all is well. With the army all is well. With my oxen and my baggage all is well. May it be well with you, with my house, with my granaries, with my children.

Hear what the gods have done.

The men of [[pol:29|Mieshlothie]] are oath-breakers. In the days of my father, who took the seat of Ana by the will of the gods (what the jealous call the [[evt:692|Usurpation of Esiesh Igwe]]), the spears of my father went out against them. At the [[evt:696|Battle of Sase]] his spears broke them. At the [[evt:697|Second Sack of Myishlithie]] his spears opened their gate. At the [[evt:698|Treaty of Myishlithie]] they swore before Atlie and before Nieshashta: silver to Ana, thirty years; the road open to Ana, thirty years. Thirty years they swore. Twenty years and one they kept it. Now they have a new house and a new seat, and they say: "The oath of the old house does not bind the new." Is a man's debt forgiven because he moves to his brother's roof? Atlie heard their oath. Atlie hears their lie.

So I went out. So began the [[evt:769|Ana–Mieshlothie War]]. I gave the white bull to Nieshashta. I gave oil to Niele'i of the river at the crossing. I did not hold back.

Now hear. At [[set:102|Sa'sa]], at the old place where the spears of my father broke them, my general [[per:1062|Tlamfen Movi]] went out before me with the spears of Ana. Their general [[per:1063|I'kwil Tlie]] came out against him. They were proud. They were few. They stood behind their wall of shields and mocked us. Nieshashta ran ahead of my spears. Their shields were broken. Their dead lay on the field of Sa'sa like threshed straw; I did not count them, for who counts straw? I'kwil Tlie ran. This was the [[evt:777|Battle of Sase]], as in my father's day. The field is Ana's.

Of our dead I do not write in this letter. The scribes count them. Let their mothers be given barley from my granary, and oil, as is right. I have sworn it.

Then we went up to [[set:2|Mieshlothie]] itself. The gate was opened a third time. This is the [[evt:778|Third Sack of Myishlithie]]. I have taken their bronze. I have taken their oxen. I have taken the silver that was owed, and the silver that would have been owed, and I have weighed it out in [[cur:0|Sovosh]] on the field. Their elders came out with dust on their heads. I did not receive them. Let them come again with more dust.

It was not I who began this. They began it with a lie. Let Uelti, lord of wisdom, write it in the book of the gods: Mieshlothie broke the oath; Ana kept it.

The war is not ended. The land of [[reg:5|Yi'otl]] is not yet settled. The men of Mieshlothie still hold the hill forts. I will not come home before the harvest.

Therefore send me: barley, three hundred measures. Oil, sixty jars. Sandals, as many as the leather-workers can make. Arrows of reed. Send them by the river road and not by the hill road. Let the priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] pour out to Muevas'e of the black earth every new moon, that she may open her mouth for the dead of Mieshlothie and close it for ours. Let Lashla'e of the night watch over my house.

Do not let the queen's women weep in the courtyard. There is nothing to weep for. Tell them: the queen is victorious.

*[Colophon.]*

> In the 17th year of Sho'e Ash, queen in Ana. Written in the camp before Mieshlothie, in the month of threshing.
