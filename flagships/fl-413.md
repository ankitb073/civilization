*[Obverse. Rubric:]* Song of the day of the seat. The singers sing it; the people answer.

Hear, [[dei:0|Atlie]], father of the high sky, who spreads the blue cloak over Ana.  
Hear, [[dei:2|Muevas'e]], mother of the black earth, who opens her lap to the barley.  
Hear, [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river, who brings the water to the fields of Mii.  
Hear, [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] of the night, who counts the months like a shepherd counts his sheep.  
Hear, [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] of the red feet, lady of the spear, who guards the gate.  
Hear, [[dei:1|Uelti]], keeper of words, who gave the signs to the scribes of Ana.  

*The people answer: Hear us, hear us, gods of Ana.*  

The king went to the gods. [[per:13|Shuesh'ayueve]] went to the gods.  
The lord of the river-land, the strong one, lay down in his youth.  
His seat was empty; the city was afraid.  
The ox did not low; the dove did not call; the brewer did not brew.  
Then Atlie looked down from the high sky.  
Then Atlie said: "Let the seat not be empty."  

*The people answer: Let the seat not be empty.*  

Who is she that comes up to the seat?  
She is the daughter of the king. She is small as a sheaf of barley.  
She is [[per:42|I'kwil Yueve]], the queen of [[pol:6|Ana]].  
Her feet do not reach the step of the seat. The gods hold her feet.  
Her hand does not reach around the staff. The gods hold her hand.  
Small is the queen of Ana. Great is the queen of Ana.  

*The people answer: Small is the queen; great is the queen.*  

Who is she that stands beside the seat?  
She is the mother of the queen; she is the wife of the king who went to the gods.  
She is [[per:41|Foyatlilie'an]], who holds the staff until the small hand is grown.  
Wise is Foyatlilie'an, like Uelti; watchful is Foyatlilie'an, like Lashla'e.  
She weighs the grain at the gate. She hears the cases at the gate.  
She keeps the seat warm for her daughter.  

*The people answer: Keep the seat warm, keep the seat warm.*  

This was the [[evt:100|Accession of I'kwil Yueve]], in the city of [[set:7|Ana]],  
in the house of the gods, before the priests of [[mov:0|La'im]].  
They poured the oil on her head. They put the river-reed in her hand.  
They put the bronze band on her brow, and the band was too wide;  
they bound it with wool, that it would not fall. The people laughed for joy.  
The bull went to Atlie. The ewe went to Muevas'e. The fish went to Niele'i.  

Now hear a new thing, a thing our fathers did not have.  
In the days of the king's father the signs were found;  
this was the [[evt:63|Invention of the Shatl'i'ueza Script]].  
Then the scribes of Ana learned to set words in the clay;  
this was the [[evt:54|Invention of writing in Ana]].  
Our fathers' kings were sung, and the song blew away.  
The name of this queen is pressed into the clay. The name will not blow away.  
Uelti, keeper of words, keep her name.  

*The people answer: Keep her name, keep her name.*  

Now it is the third year of I'kwil Yueve.  
The barley came up. The river came up. The silver of Ana is heavy in the scale;  
the [[cur:0|Sovosh]] is full weight in the market, no one clips it.  
The gods have seen the small queen. The gods are pleased.  

May Nieshashta guard her gate. May Muevas'e fill her granary.  
May Lashla'e count long months for her. May Atlie spread his cloak over her.  
All things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again.  
The king went down; the queen came up. The wheel has turned well.  

*The people answer: The wheel has turned well. Long be the days of I'kwil Yueve.*
