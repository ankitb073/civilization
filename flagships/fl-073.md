Say to the great king, my brother, the lord of the land where I have come: thus says [[per:4451|Bepimttumin]], who was king of [[pol:58|Egu]], your servant, your brother, who has no house.

May it be well with you. May it be well with your house, your sons, your horses, your fields. With me it is not well. With me it is not well. I sit in the guest-house that you have given me, and I eat your bread, and my tears are mixed with the bread.

Hear, my brother, how it was with me, and judge whether the fault is mine.

In the days of my fathers the gods turned away from Egu. At [[evt:3765|Yinishme]] the army of Egu went down and did not come back. At Yinishme again it went down. Then at [[evt:4068|Nuwkennga]] it went down, and at [[evt:4095|Nuwkennga]] a second time, and the king who sat on the throne before me could not hold the land. The land shook like a reed in the river. Then the gods put the throne into my hand. Men say I took it. I say: it lay in the dust, and I lifted it up, and no one else would lift it. Let [[dei:37|Novku]], who knows all things, judge between me and those who say otherwise.

Fourteen years I held the throne of Egu. Fourteen years I held it, my brother. I drove the enemy from the hills, and he paid me silver. I went out against the enemy of the river country, and he gave me land, and he gave me tribute. My storehouses were full. The [[cur:52|ushut]] of Egu was good silver, and the merchants took it gladly. I fed the gods of [[mov:5|Lelmi]]. I fed [[dei:35|Dumnu]] of the storm, I fed [[dei:33|Nabudzin]] of the river, I fed [[dei:36|Nidziktti]] of the grain. Not one feast day did I let go by.

And in my fifteenth year the governors of my cities rose against me. Those whom I had made great rose against me. They had eaten at my table. They had sworn by my life. In one season they broke their oaths, every one of them.

The men of [[pol:185|Tomethel]] closed their gate and made a king for themselves.  
The men of [[pol:186|Birnadu]] closed their gate and made a king for themselves.  
The men of [[pol:187|Runiy]] closed their gate and made a king for themselves.  
The men of [[pol:188|Yishamashti]] closed their gate and made a king for themselves.

And in [[set:63|Egu]] itself, my own city, the city of my fathers' gods, they opened the palace to my enemies, and they set up [[evt:4214|a new lordship of Egu]] that does not know my name. Thus [[evt:4213|the kingdom of Egu was broken]] into five pieces, as a pot is broken that falls from the roof.

Who did this? Not the enemy. The enemy I had beaten. Not hunger. The storehouses were full. Treachery did it. Treachery and the anger of the gods. Perhaps [[dei:38|Shedarsi]], lady of the house below, was not fed enough. Perhaps a priest spoke the words crookedly. I do not know. I have asked the diviners. The diviners do not answer me; they too have gone over to the new lords.

I went out by night by the river gate, with the women of my household and a few servants who did not leave me. We took nothing but what we carried. My seal I took, and this clay I took, that I might write to you.

Now, my brother, I ask you. Do not send me back to them. They will kill me. Give me a house, give me a field, give me men. Give me only a few men, and the gods will turn again, for the wheel turns, and what ends begins again. When I sit on the throne of Egu again, I will remember you. Your gift I will repay seven times seven.

Written in the 15th year of Bepimttumin. I still count my years. Let no one take that from me.
