*[Obverse. The upper edge is chipped; the first line is half lost.]*

[…] the wheel, the great wheel […]  
what ends will begin again, what begins will end.

Hear, O [[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the spear, you who eat the shields,  
hear, O [[dei:16|Dumamnul]], lord of the high sky, you who see the roads,  
hear, O [[dei:15|Vinba]], mother of the black earth, you who drink the fallen,  
hear the song of the fathers of [[pol:1|Tallin]], the song of the four fields.

The men of [[pol:9|Pumi]] sat on the road of the traders,  
on the road of the donkeys they sat, on the road of the copper they sat.  
They took the tithe of the bales, they took the tithe of the jars.  
The silver did not come to Tallin; the oil did not go out from Tallin.  
Then the king said: "Shall Pumi hold the road that the gods made wide?"  
And the priests turned the wheel, and the spears went down to [[reg:8|Duvekopuvinga]].

The first field: the [[evt:325|battle of Himi]], under the walls of Himi,  
[[per:383|Liruvin]] of the bright helm stood against [[per:384|Lokutnakhin]],  
Lokutnakhin, captain of the Pumi men, the hard one, the stubborn one.  
Five thousand were the spears of Tallin; two thousand the spears of Pumi.  
Hard was the day. Many of ours lay down in the reeds; Vinba drank them.  
But Lokutnakhin turned his back. The field was Tallin's.

The second field: the [[evt:327|battle of Himi]], at Himi a second time,  
[[per:327|Tethtun]] the king went down, Tethtun of the long years,  
he poured beer to Vulme at the place of the spears,  
he poured oil to Dumamnul at the place of the standards.  
Lokutnakhin stood a second time. Lokutnakhin fled a second time.  
The field was Tallin's.

The third field: the [[evt:330|battle of Nilu]], by the water of Nilu,  
Tethtun the king stood against [[per:394|Pantun]], captain of the Pumi spears.  
Six thousand were with Tethtun, and fewer than a thousand with Pantun.  
Pantun was like a reed before the flood; Pantun was like chaff before the wind.  
The field was Tallin's.

*[About fifteen lines are broken. Traces: "[…] the bronze of Eva […]", "[…] they did not return […]".]*

[…] the fourth field: the [[evt:340|battle of Himi]], the third time at Himi,  
[[per:399|Lukhmanen]] the swift stood against Lokutnakhin a third time.  
Three times Lokutnakhin had come to Himi; three times he had drawn up his shields.  
Both armies bled; both armies left their sons in the ditch.  
But the standards of Tallin went up on the bank. The field was Tallin's.

Then Tethtun the king sat down at Himi, and the chief of Pumi sat down before him.  
They swore by the gods of the sky, they swore by the gods of the earth.  
This was the [[evt:341|treaty of Himi]], this was the oath:  
Five districts Pumi gave to Tallin, five districts with their wells.  
Silver Pumi swore to give, silver every year, fourteen years of silver.  
The road of the traders was opened; the men of Tallin walked in the market of Pumi.  
Ten years of peace they swore; ten years the spears were to sleep.

*[Reverse.]*

Four fields, and four times the field was Tallin's.  
Let the singer say it twice: four fields, and the field was Tallin's.

What ends will begin again, says the priest of [[mov:2|Jinru]].  
The fathers took the road, and the sons took the city.  
In our days the spears went down again; in our days was the [[evt:406|conquest of Pumi]],  
twice the walls of Himi were girdled with our spears, and Pumi is the house of Tallin.  
The wheel has turned. Who will turn it back?

Now the king is a child, and the gods hold his hand.  
[[dei:19|Yenan]] the moon keeps watch over his cradle;  
Nidhikhti fills his granaries; Eva guards his hearth.  
Grow, O king, grow tall as the spear of Vulme,  
grow, and hear the song of your fathers, the song of the four fields.

*[Lower edge. Colophon.]*

> Written in the house of the singers at Tallin, in the 6th year of [[per:517|Jingunin]].  
> Whoever breaks this tablet, may Vulme break his shield, may Vinba refuse his bones.
