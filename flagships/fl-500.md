*[A rough tablet, unbaked; the clay is poor and the signs are uneven, as if written on the knee.]*

To the priests of the house of [[dei:16|Domamnol]] in [[set:17|Peme]], to the keepers of the bones of the kings, to those who stayed when I did not, say:  
thus says [[per:10857|Lokholon]], who was great king of [[pol:310|Peme]], who is great king still in the eyes of the gods, who sleeps tonight in a herdsman's hut.

With me it is not well. With my house it is not well. With the land it is not well.  
Do not write to me that it is well. I have had enough of lying tablets.

Hear, priests. The great house is broken. The pot that our fathers made is broken into six pieces, and every piece calls itself a pot.  
In the house of my fathers in Peme sits the lord of [[pol:475|Devegobevenga]].  
The lord of [[pol:476|Beneynga]] has put on a king's cap.  
The lord of [[pol:477|Togannga]] has done it. The lord of [[pol:478|Peyekhjeda]] has done it.  
The lord of [[pol:479|Noltennga]] has done it, and the lord of [[pol:480|Nebe]], whom I fed at my own table.  
Six kings where there was one king. Six seals where there was one seal.

They were my governors. They ate my bread. They swore by [[dei:14|Volme]] of the spear and by Domamnol of the sky to keep my roads and send my tribute. They kept the roads, but for themselves. They sent the tribute, but to themselves. When I called the muster, no one came. When I sent the runners, the runners did not come back. This is not the work of enemies. This is the work of the oath-breakers. May the gods remember every oath.

And the gods: why did they turn away? I asked Domamnol, and the sky was silent. I asked [[dei:17|Nedhekhte]] of the grain, and the harvest was thin. I asked [[dei:19|Yenan]] of the moon, and he hid his face for three nights, and on the fourth night I went out by the little gate with twelve men and a donkey.

It did not begin with me. Let it be written that it did not begin with me.  
In the days of my grandfathers, at the [[evt:10458|Battle of Diviminnge]] and at the [[evt:10492|Battle of Nuwkennga]], our spears were many and the enemy's were few, and still the enemy broke us.  
When I was in my cradle, my mother the queen went out to the [[evt:10620|Battle of Ontiedzey]]; she went out with a great host, and she came back with a small one.  
Again at Ontiedzey, at the [[evt:10636|Battle of Ontiedzey]], our spears went out and did not come back.  
From those years the house was cracked. I sat on a cracked seat for sixteen summers, and I held it together with my hands. Now my hands are empty.

Hear what I ask.  
Keep the house of Domamnol. Keep the bones of my mother and my father; do not let the new lord move them.  
Hide the old vessels of [[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire under the floor of the inner room.  
Feed the gods each in their place, each with their due, as the priests of [[mov:2|Jenro]] have always fed them; do not let them go hungry because the king is gone.  
If the new lord of Peme asks you for my name, give it to him. It is still my name.

To my wife say: I am alive. I have no wound. The twelve men are faithful. I have silver, a few [[cur:166|Rengel]] in a bag, and my mother's ring.

All things turn on the great wheel. What ends will begin again.  
The pot is broken; but the clay is the same clay.  
I will not die in a herdsman's hut.

*[Edge.]*

> Written in the 17th year of Lokholon, in the hills, by the king's own hand, because the scribe ran.
