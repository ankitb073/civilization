Hear, O [[dei:63|Divmamnül]] of the high sky! Hear, O [[dei:64|Nidi]] of the spear! Hear, O Nwevgwü of wisdom, O Tweno of the moon, O Shiydadtli who keeps the dead!  
Hear, all the gods of [[mov:9|Miynba]], each in the god's own place. Hear, and set your seal on this clay.

[[pol:633|Ya'cuam]] is not the slave of [[pol:617|Vakha]].  
Ya'cuam does not bring its barley to Vakha.  
Ya'cuam does not bring its sheep to Vakha.  
Ya'cuam does not bow its neck to Vakha.  
From this day and for all days, Ya'cuam is Ya'cuam.

Hear how it was.

The men of Vakha sat on the neck of Ya'cuam. They took the barley from the floor. They took the oxen from the stall. They took the sons for their spears. Nine harvests ago [[per:14350|Lwesh'ayüp]] the bold stood up in the gate of [[set:240|Tü'a'i'osh]]. He said: "Enough." The men of Ya'cuam stood up with him. This is the [[evt:14389|Revolt of Ya'cuam]]. That day he sat in the seat. That day he was our king.

Then [[per:14173|I'pil Foyatl]], king of Vakha, sat in Tü'a'i'osh with six hundred spears.  
Lwesh'ayüp went out against him with twenty-nine men. Twenty-nine went out against six hundred.  
The field was not ours. But the twenty-nine did not run.  
The next year Lwesh'ayüp went out against the general of Vakha. The field was not ours. The men of Ya'cuam did not run.

In the fifth year [[per:14382|Ilshazh'igw'a]] the strong, the general of the king, went out against I'pil Foyatl by Tü'a'i'osh.  
He broke him. I'pil Foyatl fell on that field. He did not rise.  
This was the [[evt:14436|Battle of Tiaiosh]] that Nidi gave us.

Then [[per:14183|Ba'enieme]] sat in the seat of Vakha, the queen of Vakha.  
Twice Lwesh'ayüp went out against her by Tü'a'i'osh. Twice the field was not ours.  
The third time Ilshazh'igw'a went out against her. The third time the field was ours.  
Then the spears of Ya'cuam went down to [[set:31|Vakha]], to her own city.  
They ringed its wall. They sat before its gate. This was the [[evt:14482|Siege of Bakh]].

In this year, the tenth year of the king, Lwesh'ayüp went out to Tünavükhnit.  
[[per:14383|Yi Lwesh'a]], the general of Vakha, stood against him.  
Seventy-one of Vakha lay down. Twenty of ours lay down.  
The field was ours. The field was Nidi's.  
This was the [[evt:14488|Battle of Tunavükhini]].

Then the spears of Ya'cuam went down to Vakha a second time.  
They ringed the wall again. The queen looked out from the wall and saw the spears.  
She came out of the gate. She put her hand to the clay.  
Lwesh'ayüp put his hand to the clay.  
This is the [[evt:14490|Treaty of Bakh]]. Hear it, and remember it.

So ends the [[evt:14390|Seventh Ya'cuam War of Independence]].  
Seven times our fathers stood up. Seven times the wheel turned.  
Now the wheel has turned to us. What ended has begun again.

The land of [[reg:14|Yimla]] where our fathers are buried, it is ours.  
The threshing floor of Tü'a'i'osh, it is ours.  
The gate, the wall, the storehouse, the altar, they are ours.  
Let the priests pour in order, a ram to Nidi, a ram to Divmamnül, bread to the dead.

Hear also, O gods: the spears of Va'mi come now against us, in the [[evt:14487|Va'mi–Ya'cuam War]].  
We did not bow to Vakha. We will not bow to Va'mi.  
Stand with us, Nidi, as you stood at Tünavükhnit.

Whoever breaks this tablet, may Shiydadtli take his name.  
Whoever moves this tablet from the gate, may the moon not light his road.  
Set in the gate of Tü'a'i'osh in the 10th year of Lwesh'ayüp, king of Ya'cuam.
