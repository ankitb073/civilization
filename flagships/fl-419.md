*[Obverse]*

Ai, [[per:54|Totatpen]]. Ai, Totatpen. Ai, the lord who went down young.  
The reed-pipe says his name. The drum says his name.  
The women of [[set:68|Poyonga]] say his name on the roofs.  
Ai, Totatpen, ai.

[[dei:55|Dumamnul]], father of the sky, why did you not hold him up?  
[[dei:56|Nabuthin]], lord of the river, why did you not carry him?  
[[dei:57|Bubin]], lady of the moon, you saw him go down by night.  
[[dei:59|Winba]], mother of the ground, you opened and took him.  
Winba, mother, keep him soft. Winba, mother, keep him warm.

He was the lord of [[pol:11|Poyonga]], the new city, the city of the new brick.  
The bricks of its walls were not yet dry when he took the staff.  
Seven years he held the staff. Seven years, only seven.  
In the first year he cut the canal. In the second year he raised the gate.  
In the third year he gave the house of the gods a door of cedar.  
In the seventh year he lay down, and did not rise.

He was not old. His beard was black.  
He was not weak. His arm drew the bow.  
No spear struck him. No enemy struck him.  
The sickness came in the night like a thief, and took him from the house.  
Thirty years he had seen, no more. Ai, Totatpen, ai.

*[Reverse]*

His wife, [[per:65|Donathat]], tore her garment.  
Donathat put ashes on her head. Donathat sat on the ground.  
His son, [[per:66|Tatrelchifin]], was a boy of ten years.  
The boy stood by the tomb. The boy did not weep before the people.

Then the priests of [[mov:8|Ligay]] said: "The seat must not be empty.  
The gods must have their smoke. The wheel must turn."  
And they set the boy upon the seat of his father.  
That was the [[evt:125|Accession of Tatrelchifin]], in the year the lord went down.  
And because the boy was small, his mother held the staff for him.  
Donathat held the staff. Donathat sat at the gate.  
Six years she held it, as a mother holds the cup for a child,  
and when the boy had grown, she put the staff in his hand.

Ai, Totatpen, see your son. He is a man now.  
His beard is coming. His arm draws the bow.  
In the ninth year of his seat he pours beer at your tomb.  
He pours beer and oil. He burns fat for [[dei:58|Chithal]] of the spear.  
He gives fish to [[dei:60|Shineyta]] of the sea.

Born in the year Poyonga was founded, he has the city's luck.  
The [[evt:110|Founding of Poyonga]] and the birth of the boy were one year.  
So the old women say; so the singer says.

*[Lower edge]*

All things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again.  
Ai, Totatpen, begin again. Begin again in your son.  
Ai, Totatpen. Ai, Totatpen. Let the pipe be laid down.  
Sung at the tomb in the 9th year of [[per:66|Tatrelchifin]]. Written by the singer.
