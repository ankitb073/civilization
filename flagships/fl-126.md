**In the 14th year of [[per:5044|Pa'ish]] the king, in the month of sowing.**

The rain did not come. The sky of [[dei:83|Sad]] is white like a pot. We sowed the barley in the dust. My father said: in my young days the rain came in this month. My father is old. Since [[evt:4416|the drought began]] in the days of his youth, the rain has come thin, and thinner, and in some years not at all. This year it does not come.

**In the month of the first heat.**

The canal is low. The river of [[dei:85|Niw'i]] has gone down into its bed; the mud shows. We carried water in jars to the barley. The barley came up a hand high and lay down yellow. We gave a lamb to Niw'i. We gave beer to [[dei:86|Shu]] of the grain. The priests of [[mov:13|Unsatlmum]] say: give more. We gave more.

**In the month of the second heat.**

A measure of barley in the market of [[set:13|Tivintusa]] costs near four [[cur:101|law'e]]. Who has four law'e? The potter has not. The weaver has not. My neighbour sold his ox. My neighbour sold his door. The children of the lane go to the temple gate in the morning for the bread of the gods, and come back without it.

**In the same month.**

The host of the king went out. [[per:5044|Pa'ish]] the king went out at the head of it, to [[evt:4826|the war against Tonnga]], to take the land of Tunnge. The carts went out full of barley. The granary of the king is open for the host. It is not open for us.

I do not speak against the king. The king is strong. In the days of my youth the men of Tiy came against us, and at [[evt:4725|Mane]] the king broke them, and at [[evt:4743|Mienioshtse]] they swore the peace and went home. The king is a lion. But a lion does not eat dust.

**In the month of the threshing.**

There is nothing to thresh. The threshing floor is bare. My mother died. She gave her bread to my children and she died. We buried her by the east wall. The diggers asked a price; we paid it in cloth.

The famine is in all [[reg:19|Man'e]]. It is in the villages; it is in the town. The men of the hills come down to the town and lie by the walls. In the morning some of them do not get up.

**In the same month.**

News came from the host. At [[evt:4844|Viririnu]] the king fought the men of Tonnga, and Tonnga fled, and many of them were killed. The heralds cried it in the square. The people cheered a little. Then they went back to the temple gate.

**In the month of the long nights.**

The priests read the liver. They say Shu is angry because the fields were not given their due in the good years. They say [[dei:88|Mastu]] of the sun is too strong; the sun has eaten the rain. They say [[dei:84|Lashli]] of the moon will turn the year. Who knows? I have given what I had. I have nothing more to give.

My wife is weak. My son is weak. My daughter does not cry any more, and that frightens me more than the crying.

**In the last month of the year.**

The king has sent barley from his granary to the temple gate. It is said a caravan came from the coast. Each household had one measure. One measure for a household for a month. We ate it slowly.

I write this on clay so that if my house does not see the spring, someone will know that in the 14th year of Pa'ish the sky was white, [[evt:4804|the hunger came upon Man'e]], and the people of [[pol:167|Tivintusa]] gave their lambs and their beer and their mothers, and still the rain did not come.

O Sad of the sky, open. O Niw'i of the river, rise. O Shu of the grain, remember us. We are your servants. We have always been your servants. Let the wheel of the seasons turn.
