*[Obverse. Large tablet, sealed with the seal of the great house; the seal has been struck through with a single line.]*

In the 389th year of Mane. Thus says the court of the great house of [[pol:312|Mane]], the scribes of the house of tablets, the keepers of the granaries, the elders of the gate, in the city of [[set:13|Tivintuha]]. Let it be heard in the four quarters. Let it be heard on the roads. This is the last word of the great house. After this word there is no word.

Hear, [[dei:25|Nabikh]], lord of wisdom, keeper of the tablets. Hear, Nadul of the fire. Hear, Dumin of the storm. Hear, [[dei:22|Binbe]] of the black earth, Shiniyte of the sea, Navbudin of the river. You gave the great house to our fathers. Three hundred and eighty-eight years it stood. Now hear its last word.

**What befell.** In the days of our fathers the men of Isamia came. At the [[evt:10534|Battle of Likhtli]] our spears were broken; our general lay down. At the [[evt:10558|Battle of Iazhash]] our spears were broken a second time. Then they came to our own gate. This was the [[evt:10559|Second Sack of Tivintukhe]]. The great house was emptied; the great house was filled again; but it was not filled as before.

In our own days the men of Ana came. At the [[evt:10853|Battle of Mivimäkhi]] our spears were broken. The field was Ana's.

Two years ago our spears went out against Yuftoy, under our own walls. This was the [[evt:10941|Battle of Tivintukhe]]. The general lay down. Many spears did not come back. The granaries were empty. Barley was bought for silver, measure by measure, at a price the fathers never heard of; the [[cur:184|Ushe]] bought little.

Then the governors of the provinces looked at the great house and saw that it was empty. Then each governor shut his gate. This was the [[evt:10943|Collapse of the Man'e State]]. Five seats stand where one seat stood.

**The five seats.** Let it be written, that no one may say the great house did not know:

[[pol:485|Mane]] holds the seat in Tivintuha, the city of the great house.  
[[pol:486|Nimit]] holds its own seat.  
[[pol:487|Nish]] holds its own seat.  
[[pol:488|Ihazhash]] holds its own seat.  
[[pol:489|Cuivuna]] holds its own seat.  

**The decree.** Therefore the court of the great house decrees, and it is the last decree:

The governors are released from their oath to the great house. Let them swear to whom they will.  
The soldiers of the great house are released. Let each go to his own quarter with his spear and his cloak.  
The tribute is not owed to the great house. Let no collector go out on the roads in its name.  
The tablets of the great house shall be sealed in the house of tablets. Let no one break the seals. Let the priests of [[mov:3|Diyithti]] keep the key.  
The gold of the gods stays in the house of the gods. It is not the great house's gold. Whoever takes it, Nadul burn his hand.  
The seal of the great house is struck through. Let no one press it into clay again.  

**The prayer.** All things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again. The great house is ended. Let it begin again, in the days of the sons of our sons, in whatever seat the gods choose. Nabikh, remember the great house. Binbe, cover its dead. Navbudin, carry its ash to the sea. Shiniyte, keep it there until the wheel turns.

*[Reverse.]*

> Written by the last of the scribes of the great house, in Tivintuha, in the 389th year of Mane. The scribe has broken the stylus.
