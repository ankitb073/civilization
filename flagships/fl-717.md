*Sung at the granary door in the year 3009 of the [[mov:25|Myitiyishiu]], when the harvest failed.*

I went to the granary, the door stood wide,  
I went to the granary, and nothing inside,  
nothing but the mouse, and the mouse was thin,  
nothing but the wind, and the wind came in.  

*Empty the granary, empty the bin,*  
*many the gods, and the bread is thin.*  

Steward, good steward, where is the grain?  
"Gone with the carts and gone with the wain,  
gone to the king's men under the wall;  
the king must eat, and his horses all."  

My father went out with the levy of spears  
to the [[evt:32975|battle of Tlomesh]], in my tenth year;  
he lies in the field where the barley grew,  
and his brother fell in the [[evt:32985|battle of Toiyoe]].  
So the plough stood still and the thistle came,  
and the steward wrote down the rent the same.  

*Empty the granary, empty the bin,*  
*many the gods, and the bread is thin.*  

They broke the walls two years agone,  
at the [[evt:33106|sack of Isuizhwunogath]] the bells rang on;  
the carts went by with the spoil and the gold,  
and came back empty for our seed and our fold.  
The war is not finished, the criers say;  
my brother is gone where my father lay.  

At the harvest fair a measure was sold  
for three parts of a [[cur:749|Sozhne]], fairly told;  
now the dealer laughs and asks for two,  
and turns his back when the bargain's through.  
"Justice in dealings," the holy books read,  
"moderation in all things"; let the dealer be fed  
on justice and moderation, and give us bread.  

*Empty the granary, empty the bin,*  
*many the gods, and the bread is thin.*  

Lord [[per:32252|Nitllu Mov]], victorious lord,  
you have taken the town with fire and sword;  
take now the granary key in your hand,  
and look in the bin, and see how we stand.  

In [[set:264|Ölie]] the bakers bake white and brown,  
the bells of the priests ring over the town;  
in Iiyyi the children are put to bed  
with water and a story, and no bread.  

Now it is [[evt:33063|famine in Iiyyi]] from the ford to the hill,  
the mill wheel is idle, the miller sits still,  
the widow is boiling the nettle and dock,  
the shepherd is eating the last of his flock.  

"[[pol:1371|Tomzel]] is great!" the heralds cry;  
Tomzel is great, and the children cry.  
"The wise withdraw from wealth and appetite":  
then we are the wisest in Tomzel tonight.  

Gods of the field, each in your place,  
turn to Iiyyi a kinder face;  
priests of the order, who keep what is true,  
carry our grief up the steps for us too.  

I went to the granary, the door stood wide,  
I went to the granary, and nothing inside.  

*Empty the granary, empty the bin,*  
*many the gods, and the bread is thin.*
