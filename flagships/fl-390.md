title: A Prayer for the Hidden Altar

*[Written small on a folded leaf, found sewn into the lining of a garment. The hand is careful and unpractised.]*

To be said in the dark, by one lamp, with the door barred and the window covered. If the lamp must be put out, say it without the lamp. The gods do not need to see the words; they see the mouth that speaks them.

Lord of the high sky, father of the light, you who stand over the city though they have taken down your roof:  
hear us.

Lady of the black earth, you who hold the dead in your lap and give back the grain in its season:  
hear us.

Lord of the river, who carries the boats and the sorrows of the city away:  
hear us.

Lord of the moon, who keeps the count of the months, who keeps the lamp when there is no other lamp:  
hear us.

Keeper of words, lord of wisdom, who knows the hymns that we are forgetting:  
hear us, and remember them for us.

Lady of the spear, who does not strike for us, and whom we do not ask to strike:  
hear us, and be patient with us, as we are patient.

We are few now. Two years ago the house of the gods in [[set:114|Tamesitsiyi]] was shut by order, and the priests who had been ordained in the old line, from hand to hand, from father to son of the altar, were driven out of it. Since then the followers of [[mov:0|Leim]] have been fined in the market, struck from the rolls of the guilds, and named in the streets as worshippers of shadows. My neighbour paid a fine in [[cur:639|Tluetlsi]] that would have bought his household's bread for half a year. My cousin was made to stand at the gate with a placard. This is [[evt:27884|the persecution]], and we bear it.

The priests of [[mov:57|Khikhishio]] say that there is one power only, and that you are its servants, or its shadows on the wall. They say it kindly; they speak much of compassion and of release from suffering. Yet it was by their counsel that our altar was shut, and it is their compassion that fines my neighbour. If you are shadows, then who casts you? And if you are servants, why may a servant not be thanked?

We do not ask you to burn the city. We do not ask the Lady of the spear to go out against [[pol:1166|Tamesitsiyi]], for it is our city too; our fathers are buried in it, and the earth that holds them is yours. We ask only this: that you do not forget us, as we have not forgotten you.

Turn the heart of the queen, [[per:27178|Owotluee]], who sits in judgement over us. She is not cruel by nature, men say; she listens to those who stand nearest to her. Let someone stand near her who will speak for the old altar. Let her remember that the wheel turns, as her own priests confess; that what ends will begin again; that the faith which is pressed down today may stand upright tomorrow, and the faith which stands upright today may be pressed down.

Give us patience, which is the sacrifice of the poor. We cannot give you a bull; we give you a crust of bread and a drop of oil. We cannot give you a hymn sung aloud; we give you a hymn whispered. Accept it as you accepted the bull.

Let our children learn your names in secret, so that they may say them aloud when the wheel has turned.

Let the dead hear that we have not changed our gods.

Lord of the moon, keep the lamp. Lady of the earth, keep the dead. Lord of the sky, keep the city.

Now put out the lamp, and go home by the back lane.

*Written in the year 914 of the Khikhishio, by one of the faithful, for the faithful.*
