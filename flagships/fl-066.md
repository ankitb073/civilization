*Words spoken by [[per:18084|Yi To'a]] in the gate of [[set:14|Movosh]], in the 15th year of Yi To'a, when the peace was sworn; written down by the scribes of both houses.*

Hear me, lords of [[pol:756|Ana]]. Hear me, elders of [[pol:789|Mii]]. Hear me, priests, who keep the truth in order and pass it down. Let the scribes of Ana write, and let the scribes of Mii write, and let neither add a word the other has not heard.

I who speak am Yi To'a, emperor of Ana, king of Mii. In my right hand is the seal of Ana. In my left hand is the seal of Mii. The gods have given both to me.

Five and twenty years ago the crown of Mii was set on my head, when I was a boy and others held the reins for me. Fourteen years ago my father went to the gods, and the crown of Ana came to me in [[set:7|Ana]], the city of the harbour. Since that day I have been one man with two houses, as a man may have two fields and one plough.

But two fields quarrel over the water. The lords of Mii said: "Mii bows to no one; Mii pays no silver." The lords of Ana said: "Mii owes Ana its due." Each house came to me and said: "Judge for us." I said: "A king cannot judge between his own two hands. Let the gods judge." So [[evt:18425|the third war of Ana and Mii]] began.

The priests read the birds. The birds flew from the side of Mii toward the side of Ana. The priests said: "The gods have already spoken." The lords of Mii would not hear it.

**Of Likhtli.** At [[evt:18478|Likhtli]] the host of Ana went out under my banner, and the host of Mii went out under my banner also. I stood between them and gave the word. Hear it, you who say that a king cannot fight himself: I fought, and I won, and I lost, on one day. But the host of Ana was as the sand, and the host of Mii was as a handful of pebbles. The gods judged. Ana prevailed. The men of Mii were cut down in their ranks; few went home.

**Of Tiyinmiw.** The lords of Mii still would not hear. In this year I went to Mii's side, to see whether the gods would change their word if the king stood with the weaker hand. At [[evt:18491|Tiyinmiw]] my general led the host of Ana against me. Again Ana prevailed. Again the gods said the same word. I am not ashamed of it. A king who is beaten by his own servant has a good servant.

**Of this city.** Then the host of Ana came and sat before Movosh. [[evt:18492|Eight times]] this old city has seen an army before its walls; this time no house was burned, no tablet was broken. I forbade it. Would a man burn his own granary?

**Of the oath.** Now hear the words of [[evt:18493|the peace of Movosh]]:

> The markets of Mii are open to the merchants of Ana.  
> For ten years no spear is raised between them.

Today I set my right hand to this tablet as emperor of Ana. Today I set my left hand to this tablet as king of Mii. The scribes have seen it. The priests have seen it. If either house breaks this oath, let the gods judge it as they judged at Likhtli and at Tiyinmiw.

The teachers of [[mov:25|Mie'to'ieshio]] say: ask for justice in dealings and moderation in all things; the wise withdraw from wealth and appetite. I have not asked of Mii all that Ana could take. I have asked what is just.

Lords of Ana, do not boast over Mii; you boast over your own brother. Elders of Mii, do not weep; your king is the emperor of Ana. The quarrel of the two fields is ended. The water runs to both.

Let it be written in [[set:14|Movosh]] and in Ana. So says Yi To'a.
