By [[dei:62|Novigu]] of wisdom, who opens the mouth of the scribe; by [[dei:63|Dvimamnul]] of the sky, who sees all lands; by [[dei:66|Shyidartsi]] of the underworld, who keeps the dead: I write.

I write what I saw with my eyes. I write what my father told me, and what his father told him. I do not add. I do not take away. Let the gods of [[mov:9|Myinive]] judge the tablet.

In the 232nd year of [[pol:169|Bani]] the king's army went down to Nudhuruigirimu. The army of Bani was turned back at [[evt:6171|Nudhuruigirimu]]. My father's father carried the water skins. He came home. Many did not come home.

In the 234th year of Bani the army went to Linidhagshal. The army of Bani was turned back at [[evt:6185|Linidhagshal]]. Again the widows cut their hair in Kvile.

In the 241st year of Bani the lord of Duegowinga lifted his head. This was the [[evt:6224|Revolt of Duegowinga]]. He did not send the silver. He did not send the oxen. He shut his gate.

In the 264th year of Bani the men of Lona lifted their heads. This was the [[evt:6426|Revolt of Lona]]. They too shut their gate. The king sent messengers; the messengers came back with nothing in their hands.

So the land was sick, as a tree is sick when the worm is in the root. The leaves were green; the root was eaten.

In the 288th year of Bani the king said: "Go up against Mankha." This was the [[evt:6625|Bäni–Mankha War]]. I was a young scribe. I went with the army. I counted the bread. I counted the spears.

In the 289th year of Bani we came to Zhiyiyzhikhti. The men of Mankha were few. We were many. Yet [[dei:64|Nidi]] of the war was not with us. At [[evt:6646|Zhiyiyzhikhti]] the army of Bani was turned back. I saw the dead lying on the slope. I counted them until my hand was tired, and I did not finish.

We came home. The governors did not come to the feast of the new year. Their seats were empty in the hall. Their gifts did not come. The weights of the palace, the [[cur:91|niva]] of silver, were no longer brought in.

In the 293rd year of Bani the land broke. I saw it. In one year it broke, as a pot breaks. This was the [[evt:6685|Collapse of the Bäni State]].

[[pol:286|Ibun]] went out from the king, in the city where Duegowinga had once shut its gate.  
[[pol:287|Lininada]] went out from the king.  
[[pol:288|Tungarvikhrim]] went out from the king.  
Only Kvile held. Only [[set:100|Kvile]] held, and the lands that still answered to it, and the house of the gods, and the people of the old seat.

No one finished the war against Mankha. The spears were left in the field. The men went home to their own lords. Each man went home to his own lord, and the lords were many.

Who did this? The governors did this, who ate the king's bread and shut their gates. And the gods did this, for the offerings were not right; for Nidi turned her face at Nudhuruigirimu, and turned it again at Zhiyiyzhikhti.

Now it is the 312th year of Bani. My hair is grey. In Kvile the house of [[pol:285|Bani]] still stands. The fire is fed. The priests pour the beer at the new moon to [[dei:65|Tono]]. The name of Bani is not forgotten.

The wheel turns. What ends will begin again. Let the scribe who comes after me read this, and know that it was not the spear of the enemy that broke Bani. It was the shut gate. It was the empty seat.

Written in the 312th year of Bani, in Kvile, by the hand of a scribe who saw it.
