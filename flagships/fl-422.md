*[Obverse.]*

Thus says [[per:51|Niestlitlue'e]], king of [[pol:6|Ana]]. I tell the deeds of [[dei:5|Nieshashta]], my lady, and the deeds of [[dei:0|Atlie]], my lord; let every man hear them, and let him not say afterwards, "I did not hear."

Nieshashta took me by the hand when I was young. My father was not a king. My father was a man of the gate, a keeper of the bronze. But the goddess saw me. She put the spear in my hand. In the days of the old king, who set the first stone of [[set:7|Ana]], I stood at the gate with the spear. In the days of his son I stood at the gate with the spear. I was not a king's son. I was the gate.

Hear what came to the house of the kings.

The son of the old king went down to [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] before his time. He left a daughter, [[per:42|I'kwil Yueve]], a little girl, a child of four summers. They set her on the seat. Her feet did not reach the floor. Others spoke for her; the child said nothing. Seven years she sat so.

Was it well with Ana in those years? It was not well. The men of the palace quarrelled at the door. One said, "Give the silver to this house." Another said, "Give the silver to that house." The [[cur:0|Sovosh]] went out of the storeroom and did not come back. The granary was opened by night. The priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] came to the gate and said, "Who will give the bull to Atlie at the new year? A child cannot hold the knife."

And the child was sick. Hear it: I did not make her sick. The gods made her sick. The fever came in the river-month, as it comes to many children. The physicians came, the priests came, they gave the black kid to Muevas'e. Muevas'e took the kid. Muevas'e took the child also. In this year she went down to the earth, the little queen, and the women of the house cut their hair for her. I cut my hair for her. Let no man say I did not weep.

But the seat could not stand empty. Hear it: a seat that stands empty is a seat that every man runs to. Already the men of the palace were drawing knives in the dark. Already they were sending to this house and that house. Then I went in. I went in with the spear, as I had always stood at the gate with the spear. I took the seat so that no man should take it with blood. Was there blood? There was little. Let the priests say there was little.

My enemies say, "Niestlitlue'e took what was not his." I say: what was it? A seat with no one on it, a city with no hand over it, a storeroom with the door open. I did not take a crown from a king's head. I took up a spear that was lying in the road.

Now hear what I have done.

I have closed the storeroom by night. I have counted the silver before the scribes. I have given the bull to Atlie at the new year with my own hand. I have given the ram to Nieshashta. I have poured beer to [[dei:3|Niele'i]] at the river-gate. I have kept the lamp of [[dei:4|Lashla'e]]. The gods have taken my gifts; the omens are good. Prosperity is the sign of the gods' favour; see, the barley is high this year.

*[Reverse.]*

Therefore let the men of Ana not say in the market, "The usurper." Let them say, "The man of the gate, whom Nieshashta chose." Let them hear these words and give the gods their due, and let them not plot.

Whoever after me changes these words, whoever breaks this tablet, whoever says that Niestlitlue'e did harm to the child: may Atlie shut the sky over him, may Muevas'e shut the earth under him, may [[dei:1|Uelti]] take the words out of his mouth.

> Written in the 1st year of Niestlitlue'e, king in Ana.
