*[Obverse, column i.]*

Weep, daughters of [[set:60|Virnado]], go down to the water and weep,  
weep at the bank of [[dei:33|Navutsin]], where the reeds bow their heads,  
pour out the water, pour out the beer, pour out the sweet oil,  
for the men who went out to Tonnga and did not come back.  

*Who will raise them? [[dei:38|Shedartsi]] holds them.*  
*Who will bring them home? The river does not carry them.*  

In the days of [[per:681|Tettkaelit]], queen of Virnado, lady of the bronze,  
the men of [[pol:23|Tsinlampiva]] closed their hands.  
They did not send the silver. They did not send the barley.  
The queen spoke in the house of the gods: "Let Tsinlampiva bring its silver."  
Dumnu of the storm spoke from the clouds; the priests of [[mov:5|Lelmi]] said, "Go."  
So began the [[evt:532|Virnado–Tsinlampiba War]]. So the spears went out.  

In the third year, by the walls of [[set:61|Tsinlampiva]],  
[[per:711|Pimttupabukin]] the steadfast led the men of Virnado.  
[[per:632|Puptun]], lord of Tsinlampiva, came out against him.  
Fourteen of ours lay down in the barley; they did not rise.  
Fifty-six of theirs lay down; and Puptun lay down with them.  
This was the [[evt:536|Battle of Zhinlampive]]. The field was Virnado's.  

*Who will raise them? Shedartsi holds them.*  
*Who will bring them home? The river does not carry them.*  

In the fifth year the queen herself went out, Tettkaelit of the bronze helm.  
[[per:732|Nuptalen]] stood against her with few spears, with stubborn spears.  
Hear it, daughters of Virnado, for this was the bitter field:  
forty-eight of ours lay down, sixty of theirs.  
Yet the field was Virnado's. Yet the gate of Tsinlampiva was opened.  
Their house of tablets burned; their words went up in smoke.  
This was the [[evt:551|First Sack of Zhinlampive]]. The oxen were driven out.  

*[Column ii.]*

In the sixth year [[per:734|Tatdomnon]] went against [[per:735|Mapavukin]],  
thirty-three of ours lay down, one hundred and eighteen of theirs.  
The city was ringed; no barley went in, no water went in.  
This was the [[evt:556|First Siege of Zhinlampive]].  
Then Tsinlampiva bowed its neck. The oath was sworn at Zhinlampive:  
the land of [[reg:7|Tonnga]] for Virnado, the silver for Virnado,  
fourteen years of silver, pami upon pami weighed out,  
ten years of the sleeping spear. This was the [[evt:557|Treaty of Zhinlampive]].  

Count them, scribe. Count them on the clay, the men of Virnado:  
fourteen, and forty-eight, and thirty-three.  
Ninety-five. Say it again: ninety-five.  
Ninety-five hearths without a man. Ninety-five mothers.  

*Who will raise them? Shedartsi holds them.*  
*Who will bring them home? The river does not carry them.*  

And in that same year the spears of Egu came down into Tonnga.  
The queen went out against them. The queen did not come back.  
Tettkaelit, lady of the bronze, lay down in the land she had won.  
One hundred and ninety-one lay down with her; with the ninety-five, near three hundred.  
Her son sits on her seat, [[per:742|Tapulon]], a child, small as a sheaf.  
The [[evt:549|First Egu–Virnado War]] is not ended. The spears are still out.  

*[Reverse.]*

O Novku, lord of wisdom, set wisdom in the heart of the child.  
O Tselni of the night, light the road under the earth for the fallen.  
O Nitsiktti of the grain, feed the widows of Virnado.  
Shedartsi, lady below, you have taken enough. Close your mouth.  

All things turn on the great wheel. What ends will begin again.  
The barley is cut and the barley comes up. Let the men of Virnado come up.  

*Who will raise them? Shedartsi holds them.*  
*Who will bring them home? The river does not carry them.*  

> In the 2nd year of Tapulon, king in Virnado. Sung at the water's edge on the day of pouring.
