*[Obverse. A tablet of the petition type, with a blank space left at the top for a seal that was never impressed.]*

To [[per:121|Sho'e Tlue'e]], lord of [[set:7|Ana]], the king, say:

Thus say the heads of the houses of Ana, the elders of the gate, your servants, who bow down to the king seven times and seven times.

May [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky keep the king.  
May [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river fill the king's canals.  
May [[dei:1|Uelti]], lord of wisdom, open the king's ear.  
May [[dei:2|Muevas'e]], mother of the black earth, make the king's fields heavy.

Hear the words of your servants. Do not be angry. We are not rebels. We do not lift the hand against the king. We lift the clay.

In the days of the fathers, Ana was a city. The lord of Ana sat in the gate, and the elders sat with him. When there was a matter, the elders spoke, and the lord heard. When there was a judgement, the elders weighed it, and the lord gave it. The lord was the first of the elders; the lord was the shepherd of the city. So it was in the days of the fathers. So it was in the days of the fathers' fathers.

Then, two years ago, in the days of your father, the word went out from the house of the lord. The heralds cried it in the streets. They cried: "Ana is not a city. [[pol:6|Ana]] is a kingdom. The lord of Ana is a king." This was the [[evt:161|Kingdom Reform in Ana]]. Your father did it; your father is gone down to Muevas'e; we do not speak against your father.

But hear what the reform has done.

The elders do not sit in the gate. The king's men sit in the gate.  
The elders do not weigh the judgement. The king's scribe weighs it.  
The king's men come to the fields with the measuring cord. They measure the fields of the city; they measure the fields of the villages; they write the fields on the clay.  
The king's men come to the storehouse. They take the barley for the king's house. They take the [[cur:0|sovosh]] for the king's house. The barley of the city goes to the villages; the silver of the city goes to the soldiers.  
The young men are counted for the king's levy. They are sent to guard the far fields, the fields that were not ours, the villages that were not ours.  
The priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] are told to say "king" in the prayers. They did not say "king" before. The gods knew the lord of Ana by the name of lord. Will the gods know him now?

We are afraid, O king. We are afraid that the city is become a field among many fields, and Ana is become a village among villages. We are afraid that the gods of the city will not know the city.

*[Reverse.]*

So we ask the king. Hear us, O king, and do it, and the gods will see it.

Let the elders sit in the gate again, beside the king's men, if the king wills it.  
Let the tithe of the city stay in the storehouse of the city, as in the days of the fathers.  
Let the young men of Ana guard the walls of Ana.  
Let the priests say the prayers as the fathers said them.  
Then the city will love the king, and the king will be great, as the lord of Ana was great.

All things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again. The days of the fathers have ended; let them begin again in the days of the king.

[[dei:5|Nieshashta]], lady of the spear, we do not call on you. We call on [[dei:4|Lashla'e]], who sees by night, to see our hearts, that they are true to the king.

> Written in the 2nd year of Sho'e Tlue'e, at the gate of Ana, by the hand of a scribe of the elders. Whoever breaks this tablet before the king has heard it, may Atlie withhold the rain from him.
