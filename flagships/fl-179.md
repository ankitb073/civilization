title: Song of the Bear Who Conquers

Sing, men of [[set:10|Navnga]], sing at the gate when the beer is poured,  
sing of [[per:3868|Dulomin]], the son-bear, the Conqueror, the bear of his mother.  
Who conquered at Khuele? The Conqueror conquered.  
Where was the bear? The bear was at home.  

**I.**

At [[evt:3695|Khuele]] the captain went out, and the captain won.  
The bear was at home. The bear was eating honey.  
At [[evt:3707|Zhiyiyzhikhti]] the captain went out, and the captain won,  
and the captain fell on the field with a spear in him.  
The bear was at home. The bear was eating honey.  
Then the bear went out to Zhiyiyzhikhti, when the field was swept,  
and he set his seal on [[evt:3708|the tablet of Zhiyiyzhikhti]],  
and he took three lands, and the silver of the enemy, and ten years of quiet.  
Who conquered at Zhiyiyzhikhti? The Conqueror conquered.  
Pour water for the captain. The captain is thirsty in the dark.  

**II.**

In those same years another enemy came against [[pol:40|Navnga]], and the bear himself went out.  
Hear, O [[dei:24|Navbuthen]], hear, O river, what the bear did.  
At [[evt:3696|Shivange]] the bear had nearly two men for every one of theirs.  
The bear went in. The bear came out. Seven thousand of ours stayed in.  
Who lost at Shivange? The enemy lost, says the scribe; the scribe is paid.  

At [[evt:3709|Shivange]] again, the next year, the bear went out,  
and this time the bear won. Let it be written. We are honest men.  
One field in the bear's own paw. Hail the Conqueror!  

At [[evt:3713|Taulin]] the bear went out with thirteen thousand.  
The enemy came with five thousand and a cart.  
Of ours, two thousand and four hundred went down to the dark.  
Of theirs, two hundred. Count them on your fingers. You need no more fingers.  
Where was the bear's luck? The bear's luck was at home, eating honey.  

**III.**

Then the bear grew wise, and stayed at home, and sent the captain.  
At [[evt:3727|Nilinge]] the captain lost. Even captains lose.  
At [[evt:3760|Vuvu]] the captain won.  
Then the bear went out to Vuvu, when the field was swept,  
and sat down with the enemy, and set his seal on [[evt:3761|the tablet of Vuvu]]:  
what was theirs is theirs, what was ours is ours, and ten years of quiet.  
Six years of war, and everything as it was before.  
Who conquered at Vuvu? The Conqueror conquered nothing, gloriously.  

**IV.**

The bear is a great maker of friendships.  
Bäni looked at Mikhushitsu. Mikhushitsu looked at Bäni.  
Then they both looked at the bear, and they embraced like brothers,  
and swore [[evt:3697|their friendship]] for thirty years, against Navnga.  
Who made them friends? The Conqueror made them friends.  
Hail the bear! Even his enemies are grateful to him.  

**V.**

Now hear the people, and the gods hear with them.  
[[dei:20|Nothol]], keep the fire in our hearths, though the bear sits nearest to it.  
[[dei:25|Novegu]], give wisdom to the captains, since the bear has none to spare.  
Navbuthen, rise in your season, and fill the fields, and lower the price of barley,  
for a measure costs more than a [[cur:26|rafri]] now, and the bear eats first.  
Gods of [[mov:3|Ryethki]], keep Navnga. The bear is a season. Navnga is the year.  

Sung in the 32nd year of Dulomin, at the gate, by many mouths,  
and if the bear asks who sang it, every mouth was eating honey.
