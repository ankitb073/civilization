*[Obverse. A large tablet, carefully ruled; to be read aloud at the gate.]*

Thus says [[per:35|Shuesh'anieme]], king of [[pol:0|Shue'ethie]], beloved of [[dei:5|Nieshashta]], lady of the spear.  
I tell the words of my kingship. Let the elders hear them. Let the priests hear them. Let the sons of the sons hear them, and let no one say: it was not so.

I was the son of a man of the city. I was not the son of a king. I do not hide it. Let the scribe write it first: I was not the son of a king.

But Nieshashta, my lady, took my hand when I was a child. When I was sick, she raised me. When I went out with the spear, she went before me. In the days of the young city, in the years after the [[evt:34|Founding of Shue'ethie]], I stood on the wall with the captains, and my lady stood with me. Ask the old men. They will say: it was so.

Hear now about [[per:16|Ma'she Lieze]], the king.  
He was a child when he sat on the throne. He was the king, and I bowed to him.  
I bowed to him when he was small. I bowed to him when he grew.  
I carried his spear. I kept his gate. I did not lift my hand against him.

But a child cannot hold the city. The elders spoke in his name, and the elders did not agree. One said: go to the river. One said: go to the hills. The granary was opened to friends and shut to the poor. The gate stood with no captain. The city was sick, and no one gave it medicine.

Then [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] of the moon called the king down. Ma'she Lieze lay down in his house in his fifteenth summer, and he did not rise. The fever took him; the gods took him. I was not in the house. I did not lift my hand against him. Whoever says that I did, may [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the black earth open her mouth and swallow him; may [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river turn away from his field.

I wept for him. I cut my hair. I gave a white ox for his going.

Then the elders sat down to choose, and the elders did not agree. Three days they did not agree. The men of the city stood in the square with their spears. The women stood on the roofs. There was no king in [[set:3|Shue'ethie]], and the gods were not fed.

On the fourth day I went up to the house of Nieshashta. I asked my lady: shall I? And my lady said: go up.  
I went up to the house of [[dei:0|Atlie]] of the high sky. I asked him: shall I? And the bird flew on the right hand.  
I went up to the house of [[dei:1|Uelti]], keeper of the words. I asked him: shall I? And the oil spread on the water.  
Three times the gods said: go up. So I went up. I sat down on the throne, and the men in the square lifted their spears.

I have not taken the throne from a living king. I have taken it from an empty house. Let the scribe write it: from an empty house.

The widow of the king I have kept in honour. She sits in her own house; her servants are her own; her bread comes from my granary. Let no one say she is shamed.

Now, you who come after me: if a child sits on the throne, let a strong hand stand beside him. If the elders do not agree, let the gods choose. And when you pour to the gods of [[mov:0|La'im]], each in their place, each with their due, pour also for Ma'she Lieze, who was my king.

All things turn on the wheel. What ends will begin again. The house of kings ended; the house of kings begins again.

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> Written in Shue'ethie in the 1st year of Shuesh'anieme. Whoever breaks this tablet, may Nieshashta break his spear.
