To the elders of [[set:108|Ashash]] and to the priests of the house of [[dei:0|Atlie]], say: thus says [[per:1937|A'inmamshe]], king of [[pol:67|La'im]], who was his own envoy.

In the 7th year of A'inmamshe this is written, so that the sons of La'im may know how La'im was made free, and who made it free.

Atlie, lord of the sky, saw it. [[dei:1|Uelti]], lord of wisdom, saw it. Lashla'e, lord of the moon, saw it by night. Let them bear witness that every word is true.

**The war.** I rose in Ashash. I said: La'im will not bow its neck to [[pol:52|Tomözel]]. La'im will not carry its barley to Tomözel. For four years La'im fought. At [[evt:1655|the field of Eshue'ethie]] I went against the men of Tomözel. They were many, we were few. [[dei:5|Nieshashta]], lady of war, went before me. The men of Tomözel fell like reeds before the sickle; a thousand of them lay down. Of mine, a handful. At [[evt:1661|Kheye]] I went against them again. Again they fell; again they ran. Twice I sat before the walls of [[set:3|Eshue'ethie]]; in [[evt:1656|the first siege]] and in the second I sat there, and the men of Tomözel did not come out.

**The going in.** Then [[per:1726|Bozin'ash]], king of Tomözel, sent a man to the gate. The man said: "The king will speak with the rebel." I said: "Tell the king: the king of La'im will speak with the king of Tomözel." The man went back. He came again. He said: "The king will speak with the king."

I went in with ten men. I took no gold; I took a spear. I took bread and salt, as is right.

**The words.** Bozin'ash sat in the gate. His beard was white; his eyes were old. He said: "You were my servant. Your fathers were the servants of my fathers."

I said: "My fathers were your servants. My sons will not be your servants."

He said: "Bring me silver every year, and I will call you king."

I said: "I bring you no silver. Nieshashta has called me king at Eshue'ethie and at Kheye. Ask her if you doubt it."

He said: "Give me the land by the river."

I said: "The river is Niele'i's. He gave it to La'im. Ask him."

He was silent a long time. His counsellors spoke in his ear. Then he said: "Let it be so. La'im is La'im; Tomözel is Tomözel."

**The oath.** We cut a lamb. We poured the blood on the earth for Muevas'e. Bozin'ash put his hand on the tablet; I put my hand on the tablet. These are the words of [[evt:1663|the oath of Eshue'ethie]]:

> La'im is free of Tomözel, from this day.  
> For ten years no spear of Tomözel goes against La'im,  
> and no spear of La'im goes against Tomözel.  
> Whoever breaks this oath, Atlie strikes him from the sky,  
> Muevas'e opens under his feet.

So [[evt:1630|the war of La'im]] was ended.

**After.** I came home to Ashash. The women sang. The priests gave a bull to Atlie.

Bozin'ash has gone to Muevas'e since that day. His oath remains. I honour it.

In the year after the oath strangers came and sat before Ashash. That was [[evt:1669|another matter]], and the gods test even a free people. I gave them a little, as a man throws bread to dogs to quiet them, and they went away. Let no one say La'im bowed its neck. La'im has not bowed its neck to [[pol:52|Tomözel]], and La'im will not.

Thus says A'inmamshe, king of [[pol:67|La'im]]. Uelti, keep these words.
