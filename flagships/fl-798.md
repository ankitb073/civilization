*In the thirteenth year of Shim'aluesh'a. The mothers sing at the threshing floor.*

Muema, Muema, the field of the mothers' sons.  
Six times the men of [[pol:555|Tlayiyan]] went down to [[set:192|Muema]].  
Six times we stood at the door. Six times we counted.  
*Muema, Muema. We counted.*

The first time, Muema went to the men of [[pol:575|Niz]].  
The second time, Muema went to the men of Niz.  
Twice they held the field. Twice we carried our sons up the hill.  
*Twice they held the field. Twice we carried.*

Then Muema was ours, and ours, and ours.  
The captains went in front, [[per:13314|Ima]] the grey, Ima the hard,  
and the field was ours, and the ditch was full.  
*The field was ours. The ditch was full.*

Our queen [[per:13336|Shim'aluesh'a]], the brave one, went out herself,  
she went out to [[set:40|Litlatlti]] with her spear,  
and the men of Niz broke before her, and we sang in the street.  
And in the last year Muema again, Muema the sixth time,  
and then the gate of Litlatlti, and then the seal.  
*Muema the sixth time. Then the seal.*

The [[evt:13325|Treaty of Litlitli]] was sworn. Niz bent the neck.  
Every year the silver of Niz comes up the road.  
[[cur:262|Ima]], they call the weight. Ima, they called the captain.  
The captains spent our sons. The silver cannot buy them back.  
*The silver cannot buy them back.*

[[dei:5|Niezhashta]], lady of war, you ate well in those years.  
[[dei:4|Lashla'a]], moon, you saw the ditch at Muema.  
Ten years we have been quiet. Ten years the barley.  
Ten years the boys grew. The small ones grew.  
*The small ones grew. Ten years.*

Now the drum. Now the drum again.  
The queen's men came to the threshing floor with the tablet of names.  
They read the names of the boys who were small in the [[evt:13245|Second Tlayiyan–Nizh War]].  
They are not small. They are tall in the doorway.  
This is the [[evt:13448|Tlayiyan–Tsukha War]]. Tsukha is far. Who has seen Tsukha?  
*Who has seen Tsukha? Nobody has come back from it yet.*

Mother of the reed-cutter, what do you give?  
*I give my second son.*  
Mother of the potter, what do you give?  
*I give my last son. I have no other.*  
Mother of no one, what do you give?  
*I give my voice. I sing with you.*

Niezhashta, look at the old men. Do not look at the boys.  
Lashla'a, count them going. Count them coming back.  
Let the count be the same. Let the count be the same.  
*Muema, Muema. Let the count be the same.*
