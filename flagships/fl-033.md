*To be sung in the houses of prayer of [[pol:1243|Khishkhis]] on the day of thanksgiving, in the 26th year of Navavun, by order of the king.*

I.

Praise to the One, who has no second,  
whose servants are the old powers of earth and sky,  
who turns the Wheel, and the Wheel does not stop.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

II.

Seventeen winters our fathers and sons went out,  
seventeen springs they did not come home to plough,  
for the One had said: let the land of [[reg:20|Khishkhis]] be one land,  
and let the people of [[pol:1277|Puf]] hear the teaching.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

III.

Did not the One first speak to them in hunger?  
In [[evt:34068|the famine of Daadüdhih]] He spoke to them,  
and they ate their seed-corn and did not listen.  
Then He spoke to them with the sword of His servants,  
and in the [[evt:34263|holy war]] they listened at last.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

IV.

Sing of [[per:33789|Nabuli]], the faithful lord,  
who went down at [[evt:34302|Mungu]] with the banners of the king,  
and the unbelievers lay in the fields like sheaves,  
near fifteen thousand, and no man to bind them.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

V.

Sing of our lord the king, [[per:33712|Navavun]], shepherd of the faithful,  
who at [[evt:34352|Bandidadhu]] had one man for every two of theirs,  
and the false queen [[per:33737|Naluwzhu]] fled before him,  
for the few who have the One are more than the many who have Him not.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

VI.

Sing also of the bitter years, and do not be ashamed:  
the Wheel goes down before it comes up.  
Sing of [[per:33813|Nafif]], who fell at Mungu in the second battle,  
who gave his body to the earth and his soul to the One,  
whose sword was laid on the altar in [[set:85|Nambi]].  
Let no tongue say his blood was wasted. The Wheel has turned.

VII.

Sing of the king at [[evt:34391|Luyügih]], where once we had wept,  
where the field that had drunk our blood drank theirs,  
five thousand of them, and the king stood among the banners  
and gave thanks bareheaded, as the poor man gives thanks.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

VIII.

Sing of [[per:33873|Nalimivi]], the patient lord, the grey sword,  
who at [[evt:34404|Luluyi]] broke the host of Puf,  
and there fell [[per:33846|Nambava]], their captain, who had boasted at Mungu and at Hivu.  
His boasting is in the dust, and the dust is the One's.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

IX.

Sing of Nalimivi at [[evt:34414|Agni]], in this same year,  
where the last host of Puf came out and did not go home,  
and their lords came with ropes about their necks  
and with bowed heads to the tent of the king.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

X.

At Agni the [[evt:34415|peace]] was written,  
and the lands that were theirs are ours, and the lands are the One's.  
Let the faithful go up into them without greed,  
for the wise do not grasp at silver, nor count their [[cur:711|nidhath]] at the altar,  
but they build houses of prayer, and teach, and the stranger is made a brother.  
Praise to the One. The Wheel has turned.

XI.

Lord of the Wheel, keep our king Navavun,  
keep his lords and his captains, keep the ploughmen who come home,  
keep the widows of Mungu and of Dambali and of Hivu,  
and keep the people of Puf, who are now our people,  
that they may learn the way of [[mov:70|Vimikhulu]] and live.

Praise to the One, who has no second.  
The Wheel has turned. The Wheel will turn. Amen, amen.
