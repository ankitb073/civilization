To the Great Queen, to [[per:5808|Kalidath]], lady of [[set:0|Tallen]], beloved of [[dei:14|Vulme]], beloved of [[dei:15|Vinba]], my lady, say:

Thus says your servant, the scribe who walks with the army of the Queen.

With my lady may it be well. With the house of my lady may it be well. With her storehouses, with her fields, with her city may it be very well. Before [[dei:16|Dumamnul]] of the sky and before [[dei:19|Yenan]] of the moon I bow seven times and seven times.

This is the third year that the army of [[pol:171|Tallen]] walks in the land of [[reg:16|Yeda]]. My lady sent us out. We went out. We crossed the land. We drank its water. We ate its grain.

Now hear, my lady. The men of [[pol:165|Yeda]] came out against us. They came near the town of [[set:135|Virathkanu]]. They came with their spears, they came with their shields, they came with their banners. They were many. Twenty-two thousand nine hundred and seventy-seven men they were. We were twelve thousand two hundred and seventeen men. For each man of Tallen there stood two men of Yeda. The young men were afraid. The young men said: "The men of Yeda are many."

[[per:5983|Dudhithshin]], the commander of my lady, did not fear. He stood before the army. He said: "Vulme goes before us. Vinba holds our feet. Who are the men of Yeda? They are grass." He killed a ram before the army. The priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] read the ram. The ram was good.

At dawn we went down. We struck the men of Yeda. We struck them on the right. We struck them on the left. We struck them in the middle. [[per:5984|Juyethkovelen]], the commander of Yeda, stood a little; then he turned his face from the field, and his men turned with him. We ran after them until the sun stood high. We ran after them until the sun went down.

We counted the dead of Yeda. We cut the right hands and counted them before Dudhithshin. Nine thousand five hundred and sixty-eight hands we counted. Nine thousand five hundred and sixty-eight men of Yeda lie at Virathkanu. Let the birds eat them.

We counted the dead of my lady. Two thousand eight hundred and fifty-nine men of Tallen fell. We burned them. We gave them to [[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire, as is right. We poured beer for them. Their names are written on a second tablet. Let my lady send it to their fathers.

So it was at the [[evt:5564|Battle of Nunuunge]]. So it was at the [[evt:5565|Third Sack of Ramtuunge]]. So it was at the [[evt:5566|Conquest of Ramthulnga]], when my lady set her seal on the land. So it is now. My lady's hand is heavy. Her enemies lie down.

But hear, my lady. The war is not finished. The towns of Yeda are not yet in my lady's hand. The men of Yeda are still in their walls. They say: "We will come out again." Let them come out.

Now the army is hungry. The grain of this land is burned. The men eat the grain of [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]] slowly, one measure for two men. Let my lady send grain. Let my lady send donkeys loaded with grain. Let my lady send silver, [[cur:114|Lol]] for the men who carry spears, so that they do not say: "The Queen has forgotten us." Let my lady send oil for the wounded.

I have sent the spoil of Virathkanu to my lady: shields, spears, bronze. Let my lady give it to the house of Vulme.

May Vulme keep my lady. May Vinba keep my lady. May Eva keep the fire of my lady's house. May Yenan count the days of [[per:5808|Kalidath]] long. Let my lady write to her servant. Let my lady not be silent.

Written in the 21st year of Kalidath, Great Queen of Tallen.
