To the steward of the king's house in [[set:7|Ana]], and to the priests who keep the wheel of [[mov:0|La'im]], say:

Thus says [[per:3289|E'a Mue'a]], the king, the great one of [[pol:93|Mii]], beloved of [[dei:0|Atlie]] the sky, beloved of [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] the spear-bearer, your lord.

With me it is well. With my house it is well. With my spearmen, my oxen and my asses it is well. Before Atlie and before Nieshashta I fall at the feet of the gods seven times and seven times.

Hear the words of the king. In my nineteenth year I took up the spear in the [[evt:3084|war of Mii against Yi'otl]]. I took it up because the men of [[pol:30|Yi'otl]] closed the roads, because they held back the grain, because their king spoke proud words against my fathers. [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] the earth showed me in a dream the fields of [[reg:5|Yi'otl]], heavy with barley, and said: these are given to you. So I went down.

In my twentieth year my general [[per:3461|Nitllo Ash]], my servant, went down to the plain by [[set:15|Miezanitie]]. There at the [[evt:3094|field of Myizinithie]] came out [[per:3424|Esiesh Ada]], king of Yi'otl. He did not come out as a king comes out. He came out as a thief comes out, by the river, by the reeds, in the dark of [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] the moon. His men were many, many as locusts are many. Yet my servant struck them. Of theirs we slew three thousand; of theirs we slew four thousand; their dead lay in the barley like cut sheaves. Of ours few fell. But the moon was dark, and the god turned his face, and my servant drew back to the camp. Let no one in Ana say otherwise. Let the elders who say otherwise hold their tongues.

Now in my twenty-first year I, the king, have come down myself. I have brought fifteen thousand men of Mii, men of the hills, men of the river towns, men who eat bread from my hand. I stand again before Myizinithie. I have given to Nieshashta a white bull. I have given to Nieshashta a black ram. The priest opened the liver and the liver was good. The priest looked at the birds and the birds flew to the right hand.

Esiesh Ada sits behind his ditch. He is afraid. He sends no gift; he sends no word. His spearmen come out at night and cut the throats of my oxen, and run back like dogs. Is this the way of a king? It is the way of a dog.

Tomorrow I go out against him at the [[evt:3105|battle of Myizinithie]]. The wheel turns. What ends will begin again. As Yi'otl was lifted up, so it will be cast down; as Mii bowed its head in the dark of the moon, so it will lift its head in the light.

Therefore hear and do quickly. Send me grain, two hundred loads of grain. Send me bronze for the spear-heads. Send me from the house of the king a thousand [[cur:65|Lash'a]] of silver, that the levies may be paid and not grumble. Let the priests pour water to [[dei:3|Niele'i]] the river at the ford of Ana. Let them burn fat before [[dei:1|Uelti]] the wise, that my counsel may be wise. Let the women keep the lamp before the moon, and not let it go out.

Do not delay. Do not say: the road is long. The road is not long for the servant who loves his lord. Send quickly. Send quickly. With me it is well.
