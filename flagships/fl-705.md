*In the 16th year of [[per:3620|Vasada]], the queen, the shield of [[pol:108|Va'mi]].*

**The day of the new moon of sowing.** I went down to the river. [[dei:6|Nili]] the river is low. Nili the river is low, and the reeds stand on cracked mud. The ditch of our field is dry. I carried water in the jar, I carried water on my head, I poured it on the barley. The barley drank. The barley was thirsty again.

**The fifth day.** [[dei:8|Nish'il]] of the sky gives no cloud. Nish'il gives no cloud, and [[dei:9|Shimintlo]] of the storm sleeps in his house. [[dei:10|Masto]] the sun stands over the field like a guest who does not go home.

**The tenth day.** My husband is not here. My husband went with the queen's men against the men of Tallin. In the year that is gone he stood in the [[evt:3567|battle of Nuwkennga]]. The queen herself stood there, they say, the queen herself, and the men of Tallin were many, many like locusts on the barley. He came home with a cut on his hand. At the barley harvest the queen's men took him again.

**The fifteenth day, the full moon.** I went to the market by the gate. A measure of barley: six [[cur:73|Khoni]] and half a Khoni. In my mother's time a measure was one Khoni. One Khoni, and the seller gave a handful over. Now the seller gives nothing over. Now the seller looks at my hands.

I gave the bronze pin of my mother. I gave the bronze pin, and I carried home half a measure.

**The sixteenth day.** The queen's tax-man came to the village. He said: the silver goes to the east, the silver goes to Ash, as was sworn at the [[evt:3550|Treaty of Ash]]. Every year the silver goes. He took two goats. He took two goats and the good blanket. He said: the queen weeps for you. I did not see the queen weep.

**The twentieth day.** The ox of our neighbour died. We ate the ox of our neighbour. The children of the street came with bowls, and the neighbour's wife gave to every bowl. May [[dei:7|Yub]] of the fire warm her hearth for ever. May Yub of the fire never go out in her house.

**The twenty-second day.** My little daughter does not cry any more. This frightens me more than her crying. I chew the bran soft and put it in her mouth. She sleeps. I sit by her and count her breaths.

**The twenty-fifth day.** They say there is [[evt:3522|famine in Ayshi]] from the hills to the river. They say the people of the upper villages walk to the town with their pots on their backs, and sit by the gate, and the gate is shut. They say the granary of the queen is opened in the town of [[set:26|Va'mi]]. They say it is not opened. I do not know what is true. I know what is in my jar.

**The last day of the month.** I made a cake of the last flour. I did not eat it. I gave it to Nili at the river's edge, at the place where my mother gave.

Nili, river, father of the barley, come back.  
Nish'il, father of the clouds, open your hand.  
Shimintlo, lord of the storm, wake.  
Masto, go home, go home and sleep.

Tomorrow I go to the field again. The barley is yellow before its time.
