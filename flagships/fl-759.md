*THE LIGHT NIVBE; or, WHO SHAVED THE COIN? A new comic song, as sung with great applause in the beer-cellars by the tram depot. To the air of "The Stoker's Polka." Price: one old copper, no new ones accepted. 28 Nuw 2545 of the Vishithmikh.*

**I.**  
Oh, the papers came out on a Tuesday at dawn,  
With a notice in black from the Treasury lawn:  
"For reasons of State, and the War, and the rest,  
The [[cur:1028|Nivbe]] will now be minted *lighter*, and *best*."  

*Chorus:*  
*So hold up your Nivbe and look at it twice,*  
*It's the same little face at a much littler price;*  
*Four grams and a half was the weight of your pay,*  
*And four and a twentieth's the weight of today!*  

**II.**  
Now my landlord came round with his book and his pen,  
And he says, "It's the same rent. A Nivbe is ten."  
"But the Nivbe's grown thin, sir!" I said with a frown.  
"Then pay me eleven," he says, "or move out of town."  

**III.**  
The baker in Ropewalk Street weighs out his loaf,  
And he's shaved off a tenth, like the Mint, the old oaf;  
"If the Palace can do it," he says, "so can I."  
And the measure of grain's gone past three. Ask him why!  

*Chorus.*  

**IV.**  
They say it's the [[evt:46364|Tallin–Fuiyu War]] that's to blame,  
That the guns must be fed, and the generals the same;  
And the ministers say, in the [[mov:58|Vishithmikh]] way,  
"Let compassion endure what the Treasury can't pay."  

**V.**  
Then there's our [[per:45641|Balmaanin]], who came in last year  
By the side door, they say, with a smile ear to ear;  
Now the coin's grown as thin as his title, I've read:  
It's lighter in silver, and lighter in head!  

*Chorus.*  

**VI.**  
The priest in the chapel says, "Brethren, take heart,  
For the Judge at the End weighs the soul, not the part;  
No coin will be counted, the Scales will be fair."  
Well, I hope they're more honest than the Mint's over there.  

**VII.**  
Oh, the dockers have struck and the tram-girls have too,  
And the telegraph boys have a meeting at two;  
And the Ministry clerks, on their silver-shaved pay,  
Are as sore as the rest, though they daren't say it, they say.  

**VIII.**  
So here's to the [[evt:46375|Debasement of the Nivbe]], my friends,  
It's a fine bit of statecraft, the way that it ends:  
With the rich man's in gold, and the poor man's in tin,  
And the Treasury's laughing, and taking it in.  

*Chorus:*  
*So hold up your Nivbe and look at it thrice,*  
*It's the same little face at a much littler price;*  
*And if [[pol:1785|Tallin]] keeps shaving the coin that we've got,*  
*By the year of the Judgement there'll be nothing to shave. Not a jot!*
