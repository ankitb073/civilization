**OPEN LETTER OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC TO THE SOLDIERS OF MBILU IN SHYIYU**

*To be read aloud at the head of every company, and posted in every barracks, hospital and coaling station of the expeditionary force. Printed by order at the State Press, [[set:136|Mbilu]], 13 Bine 4709 of the Uthungala.*

Soldiers of the Republic!

The despatch-boat has brought me the news from [[reg:11|Shyiyu]], and I write to you the same night, before the newspapers of the capital have set their type, because it is to you and not to them that the nation owes its thanks.

You have won the [[evt:46266|Battle of Nimid]].

You have done what the old kings of [[pol:1674|Mbilu]] never dared to attempt. Three years ago the people of this country rose and put an end to the throne; that was the [[evt:46232|Mbilu Revolution]], and many of you marched in it. Then you were citizens demanding your rights. Today you are citizens defending them, far from home, under a sun your fathers never saw.

Let no one tell you this war was of our choosing. When the Republic put forward its lawful title in Shyiyu (the [[evt:46252|Mbilu Claim against Nemeth]], laid before the world in good order and in plain words) the government of [[pol:1941|Nimifu]] answered with contempt, and then with arms. It told its people that the soldiers of a republic would not fight. You have answered that lie. So began the [[evt:46253|First Mbilu Colonial War in Tsiey]], and so, carried by your courage, it goes on.

Consider what you have done. Under General [[per:45521|Navièvi]], whom the Republic trusts and the enemy has learned to fear, you came up before Nimid in such strength as the enemy had never imagined. The enemy's general, [[per:45354|Minishu]], had chosen his ground and dug his trenches. He could not hold them. The returns of our staff give his losses at more than twenty-five thousand men, and every officer who walked the field tells me that the true figure is far higher; the enemy, who counts his dead in secret, will never publish it.

Our own losses I will not hide behind a figure. They were heavy. They were borne by men who did not ask whether the ground was easy before they crossed it. Every name will be printed; every widow will receive her pension in full and in good [[cur:926|Dathu]], not in promises. I have signed the order tonight. I have also signed an order raising the field allowance of every soldier in Shyiyu by one part in five from the first of next month. A Republic that asks for its citizens' blood will not quibble with them over their bread.

Now you stand before the walls of [[set:76|Nimifu]] itself. The [[evt:46267|Siege of Nimid]] has begun. I do not tell you it will be short. I tell you that it will be conducted by an army that has already shown it can do what its enemies call impossible, and that every resource of the nation (its foundries, its canals, its fleets) is turned toward you.

The teachers of the [[mov:24|Uthungala]] tell us that time runs in a line toward a final judgment, and that justice in our dealings is what we shall be judged by. I accept that judgment for this war. We came to Shyiyu with a lawful claim, refused; we fight a government that chose to fight; and we will bring to the people of that country, when the guns are silent, the same order and the same law that we fought for at home.

Soldiers! The Republic is proud of you. Your mothers are proud of you. Keep your powder dry, your discipline strict and your conscience clean.

Long live the army in Shyiyu! Long live the Republic!

[[per:45529|Navande]]  
*President of the Republic of Mbilu*
