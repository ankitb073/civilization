To the chief priest of [[dei:0|Atlyi]] in [[set:83|Khiy'a]], my father in the god, say: thus says [[per:7906|Fimcuonvmalin]], your son, who was king.

May Atlyi keep you. May [[dei:1|Ivilti]] give you wisdom. May Mivzhas'i hold up your feet. I am not well. My house is not well. My land is not well.

In the 3rd year of Fimcuonvmalin I write to you, and I do not know whether there is a 4th year.

Hear me, my father. I did not lose [[pol:274|Khiy'a]]. Khiy'a was lost before I sat on the seat. I sat on the seat two years, and the seat was already cracked under me.

Before my father's days the army went out to [[evt:7217|Lininadhe]]. The gods were not with it. It came home broken. Then in my father's days the strangers came against us at [[evt:7469|Yizamashti]], and our men fell. They came again at [[evt:7490|Shidyun]], and my father stood there with his own body, and our men fell. Was it his fault? It was not his fault. The gods turned their faces.

Then my father's general went out to [[evt:7661|Bathshi]]. He had many men; the enemy had few. He was broken. I was a boy; I saw the wounded come in at the gate. The next year my father went to the enemy and set his hand on [[evt:7667|the oath of Bau]]. He came home and did not speak for many days. He said to me: "My son, I have bought ten years. Use them." I have not had ten years.

My father went to Mivzhas'i. I sat on the seat. The governors came to me in the first year. They knelt. They put dust on their heads. They swore by Atlyi, by Ivilti, by [[dei:5|Niyshashta]] of the spear. In the third year they broke the oath.

Hear their names, my father, and curse them in the house of the god. One lord, who ate bread at my father's table: [[evt:7750|Val'a has risen]] under him. Another, who held my father's cup: [[evt:7751|Yimla has risen]] under him. Another, who carried my father's spear: [[evt:7752|Viririnu has risen]] under him. And worst of all, worse than all: in Khiy'a itself, in my own city, in the house where I was born, [[evt:7749|Tomözel has risen]], and a stranger sleeps in my bed.

So [[evt:7748|the house of Khiy'a is broken]] into four, as a pot is broken. Who can mend a pot? Only the potter. Only the gods.

I did not run, my father. I went out so that the blood of the king would not be spilled on the threshold of Atlyi. Tell them that in Khiy'a. Tell them I did not run.

Here I eat the bread of strangers. Grain is dear everywhere; in Khiy'a, they tell me, a measure of barley costs three [[cur:110|Tina]] and more. My men have sold their bronze. My servants weep. I sit by the door and look toward Khiy'a.

My father, the priests teach: all things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again. Is it so? Then let the wheel turn quickly. Let [[dei:3|Niyl'i]] carry my prayer down the river to your house. Let [[dei:4|Lashla'i]] see my tears by night.

Give the bull to Atlyi for me. I have no bull. Give the barley to Mivzhas'i for me. I have no barley. Pour the oil for my father. I have no oil.

Write to me, my father. Tell me whether the house of Atlyi still stands. Tell me whether they still say my name in [[pol:274|Khiy'a]].

Thus says Fimcuonvmalin, your son, who was king.
