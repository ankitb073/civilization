*[Obverse. A hymn tablet of the temple singers. Marginal strokes mark the lines sung by the chorus, here printed in italics.]*

[[dei:16|Dumamnul]], father of the high sky, look down!  
[[dei:15|Vinba]], mother of the black earth, look up!  
Between the sky and the earth stands the seat of [[set:18|Lenunushal]].  
Who sits on the seat? Hear it, Dumamnul. Hear it, Vinba.  
*Balshiymagunin sits on the seat.*  

The old lord has gone down. [[per:11|Balshiymagunin]] has gone down,  
the first lord of [[pol:10|Lenunushal]], who set the doorposts of the kingdom,  
who sat on the seat thirty summers, who counted the barley thirty harvests.  
He was full of years. He lay down in his house. He went down to Vinba.  
Vinba, mother, hold him. Vinba, mother, he is yours.  
*The old lord has gone down.*  

Weep for him three days, women of Lenunushal.  
Weep for him at the gate, weep for him at the well.  
On the fourth day wash your faces. On the fourth day put on the bright cloth.  
For the wheel turns. The wheel does not stop.  
What ends will begin again; so say the priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]].  
*What ends will begin again.*  

See, it begins again! See, the name comes back!  
The father was Balshiymagunin; the son is Balshiymagunin.  
The father went down; the son goes up.  
The name went down into the earth; the name comes up out of the earth,  
as the barley goes down as seed and comes up as barley.  
[[dei:17|Nidhikhti]], lady of the grain, this is your doing.  
*The name comes back.*  

He is no boy. He is a man of forty summers.  
He has stood in the gate beside his father. He has sat in the judging beside his father.  
He has poured the oil beside his father, he has cut the ram beside his father.  
He knows the count of the barley. He knows the boundary-stones.  
He knows the names of the gods, each in his place, each in her place.  
*He knows the way.*  

Now the priests bring the oil. Pour it, priests!  
[[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire, light the lamp over his head.  
[[dei:19|Yenan]] of the moon, keep watch over his nights.  
[[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the spear, put the spear into his hand.  
Dumamnul, father, put the sky over him like a roof.  
Vinba, mother, put the earth under him like a floor.  
*Pour the oil.*  

The oil is on his head. The staff is in his hand. The cloth is on his shoulders.  
He sits on the seat of his father. He sits on it well.  
This is the [[evt:124|Accession of Balshiymagunin II]], let the scribes write it so:  
the second of the name, the son of the first.  
*He sits on the seat.*  

*[Reverse.]*

Hear it, river! Hear it, threshing floor! Hear it, sheepfold!  
Hear it, you who carry water, you who grind the grain, you who herd the goats!  
Your lord is [[per:64|Balshiymagunin]]. Bow down.  
Your lord is Balshiymagunin. Stand up and sing.  

Let his barley be heavy. Let his goats be many.  
Let his storehouse be full, for a full storehouse is the favour of the gods.  
Let the enemy see his spear and turn back at the boundary-stone.  
Let him sit long on the seat, as his father sat long.  
And when the wheel turns again, let a Balshiymagunin come up after him.  
*Let it turn. Let it come back.*  

*[Lower edge. Colophon.]*

> Sung at the anointing in the house of the gods in Lenunushal, in the 1st year of Balshiymagunin.  
> The singer who changes a word of this hymn, may Eva take the breath from his lamp.
