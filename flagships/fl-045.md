*To be sung in all halls, depots and assembly rooms on the 18th of Mbivi, 2535 of the Vimikhulu. Printed by the Office of Public Song, [[set:85|Nambi]]. Tune: the Rolling Wheel.*

**I.**

Hear the wire from the south, hear the hammer and the bell,  
Hear the whistle of the engines on the long iron line;  
They are singing of the soldiers of [[pol:1638|Khishkhih]],  
Who went down into the wheat-lands, and came home in their time.

*Refrain:*  
*Turn, great Wheel, and bring the morning;*  
*What ends below begins again above;*  
*One Power over all the nations,*  
*One people in its duty and its love.*

**II.**

It was not for gold we marched, nor for the fat of other lands,  
For the wise man turns from appetite, as the teachers say;  
We marched because the border-lords of [[pol:1952|Yithumashdi]]  
Had shut the roads of the province against the people's day.

So the [[evt:48410|war against Yisamashtsi]] was proclaimed in every square,  
And the posters of the Party called the factories to arms;  
The shells came off the presses like the loaves come off the ovens,  
And the girls who filled the cartridges sang louder than the alarms.

*Refrain.*

**III.**

At [[set:232|Yyiju]] by the river stood the enemy entrenched,  
And [[per:47194|Fijaladha]] his captain swore that he would never yield;  
But our [[per:47099|Nalizhih]] came on, through the smoke and through the wire,  
And at [[evt:48457|Yyixi]] the foe gave up the field.

Seven hundred and fifty thousand of the enemy lay down,  
Count them, comrades, count them, write the figure on the wall!  
And if our own were many, they are written in the Wheel,  
Which turns them into morning, and remembers them all.

*Refrain.*

**IV.**

Then at [[set:31|Baaga]], at the crossing, came the hour of the hammer,  
And [[per:47233|Avath]] brought his columns to the last ditch of his land;  
Nalizhih, the iron marshal, broke his centre in the morning,  
And by evening at [[evt:48465|Bakh]] there was no one left to stand.

On to [[set:30|Namuzash]], to the harbour, to the cranes and to the warehouse,  
Where the [[evt:48466|sack of Namosash]] paid the debt of all their lies;  
And there at the [[evt:48467|peace of Namosash]] they signed away the province,  
And bent the knee to Khishkhih, and lowered their eyes.

*Refrain.*

**V.**

Now the fields of [[reg:1|Yithumashdi]] send their grain along our railways,  
And Baaga flies the banner that was raised by the people's hand;  
And the enemy is vassal to the Party and the nation,  
And for twenty years no rifle shall be lifted in that land.

Glory to the Chairman, to [[per:47238|Nadaazum]], who keeps it,  
Glory to the Party and the order of the State,  
Glory to the worker who gave his last [[cur:1109|Agni]],  
Glory to the soldier at the southern gate.

**VI.**

Teachers of [[mov:70|Vimikhulu]], light the lamps in every quarter;  
Mothers, set the bread out; there is bread enough for all.  
What was broken is remade, what was ended is beginning,  
And the Wheel goes round for Khishkhih, and will not let us fall.

*Refrain, twice, all standing.*
