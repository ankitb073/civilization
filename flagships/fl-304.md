*[Obverse]*

Praise to [[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the spear, whose arm is bronze, whose eye is fire!  
Praise to [[dei:16|Dumamnul]], father of the high sky, who looks down on the fields of men!  
Praise to [[dei:15|Vinba]], mother of the earth, who opens her hand and shuts her hand!  
Hear the song of [[pol:1|Tallin]]. Hear the song of the spears.  

Ten harvests the spears went out. Ten harvests the spears came home.  
They went down into [[reg:16|Yida]], the good land, the land of barley,  
the land the men of [[pol:11|Poyonga]] held in a closed hand.  
Vulme said: "Open the hand." And the spears of Tallin opened it.  

[[per:196|Kulufpimtuth]] the queen, the lioness of Tallin, went out first.  
At [[set:56|Vuvo]] she went out, by the walls of Vuvo she stood.  
[[per:268|Ladhilatu]], queen of Poyonga, stood against her.  
Five hundred and one men of Poyonga lay down. They did not rise.  
The field was Tallin's. The field was Vulme's.  

In that year the city of [[set:68|Poyonga]] was opened.  
Its storehouses were opened, its oil jars were carried out,  
its bronze went to Tallin on the backs of donkeys.  
This is the [[evt:265|Second Sack of Poynge]]. Hear it and remember it.  

At Noni the sky was dark.  
Of Noni we do not sing. Vulme turned her face for a season,  
as [[dei:19|Yenan]] the moon turns his face, and comes again, and comes again.  

Kulufpimtuth went down to the wheel, full of years.  
[[per:199|Panshedominin]] sat in her seat, the great bull, the king of Tallin.  
He poured oil to [[dei:18|Eva]] of the hearth. He poured blood to Vulme of the spear.  
He said: "Go out again." And the spears went out again.  

*[Reverse]*

[[per:244|Pavbukhununin]], the old spear, the grey spear of Tallin,  
went out to [[set:77|Nodhinga]], against Ladhilatu he went out.  
Ladhilatu stood. Ladhilatu did not stand.  
The field was Tallin's. The field was Vulme's.  

At [[set:66|Shivanga]] the king himself went out, Panshedominin went out.  
[[per:309|Badon]] of Poyonga stood against him with his shields.  
Four hundred and sixty-six men of Poyonga lay down. They did not rise.  
The field was Tallin's. The field was Vulme's.  

Then Pavbukhununin went out a second time, to the city of Poyonga.  
He stood before the gate; he stood before the wall.  
A third time Ladhilatu came out against the spears of Tallin,  
a third time she went back behind her wall.  
The wall was ringed with spears. The gate was ringed with spears.  
The gate was shut. The gate was opened.  

Ladhilatu came out with her hands empty.  
She put her hand to the clay before Panshedominin, before the king she put it.  
Then her life was given back to Vulme, who had asked for it.  
This is the [[evt:288|Conquest of Poyonga]]. Hear it and remember it.  

Now Yida is Tallin's, from the river to the hills.  
Now the barley of Yida goes up to the storehouses of Tallin.  
Give the first sheaf to [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]], lady of the grain.  
Give the first ram to Vulme, the second ram to Dumamnul, the third ram to Vinba.  
Let the priests pour in order, each god in the god's own place.  

What ends will begin again. The wheel turns. The wheel turns.  
Poyonga is ended. Tallin is not ended.  
Praise to Vulme! Praise to Vulme! Praise to the spears of Tallin!  

*[Colophon]* Written in the 3rd year of Panshedominin, king of Tallin, in the house of the singers.
