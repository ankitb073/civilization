*In the fourteenth year of A'i Ash.*

Weep, river [[dei:109|Nili]], weep for [[set:131|Tilcui]],  
weep for the city of [[dei:110|Masto]], the white city,  
the city of the threshing floors, the city of the singing women.  
Weep, river Nili. Weep.

They came, the men of [[pol:17|Wal'a]], they came,  
they came in the month of the green barley,  
like locusts on the green barley they came,  
like locusts they came, they came.

Our lord [[per:1136|A'i Ash]] went out, he went out by the river gate,  
he went out with the spearmen, with the men of the city he went out.  
At the [[evt:991|Battle of Shiüzhi]] the field was lost, the field was lost.  
Seven men of Wal'a stood against each man of ours.  
They were many. They were many.

Then the gate was broken. Then the gate was broken.  
This was the [[evt:992|First Sack of Shiüzhi]]. May there be no second.  
They took the bronze from the house of Masto.  
They took the grain from the storehouse, the oxen from the stall.  
They took the young women by the hair.  
The roof-beams burned, the reed roofs burned,  
the smoke went up to Masto, and Masto did not look down.

*Mother, where is our house?*  
*Our house is ash, my child.*  
*Mother, where is my father?*  
*Your father lies with the dead by the gate.*

In the year after they came again, they sat down before the walls,  
they sat down in the [[evt:995|Fourth Siege of Shiüzhi]].  
We ate the grain of the burned storehouse,  
black grain, bitter grain, grain that tasted of smoke.  
In the market a measure of barley was near one [[cur:20|Sazhon]].  
Who had a sazhon? The sazhon went with the bronze.

Then A'i Ash went out with empty hands,  
he went out to the men of Wal'a, he bowed his head,  
he set his seal on the clay of the [[evt:996|Treaty of Shiüzhi]].  
Silver to Wal'a, every year silver.  
The fields by the river to Wal'a.  
The city of Masto bends its neck to Wal'a.

Yet we live. We few live, and we sing.  
We cut reeds for the roof again.  
We sow the black field again.  
The women go down to the river again with jars on their heads,  
and the river is the same river.

Weep, river Nili, and then be still.  
Masto, look down on Tilcui again.  
Look down, look down.
