title: What I Saw When Kavu Came the Second Time

*[Obverse.]*

Hear, you who come after. Hear what I saw with my eyes. I do not tell what another man told me. I tell what my eyes saw, in [[set:115|Unjinlathu]], the city of the wheel, the city of the high wall.

[[dei:28|Khuthaze]] of the sky, see that I speak truly. [[dei:30|Pugazhu]] of the sun, you saw it also; you stood over it all the day. [[dei:31|Kumbe]] of the moon, you saw it in the night. Let the gods be my witnesses.

The men of [[pol:15|Kavu]] came against us. They came in the summer, they came with spears, they came with bows, they came with fire. Six summers they had come and gone, come and gone, like the locust. Before our wall they stood. Our spearmen went out against them at [[evt:510|Unzhinlathu]], and the gods turned their faces from us, and the field was Kavu's. That was the year before this one.

Then they came in at the gate. That was [[evt:511|the first sack]]. They took the grain from the storerooms. They took the oxen. They took the bronze from the house of [[dei:32|Nijinande]]. We wept, and we built again. We said: the wheel turns. We said: what ends will begin again. We gave the ram to Nijinande, we gave the fish to [[dei:29|Bumbe]] of the sea, and we built again.

*[Reverse.]*

In the next summer they came again. Again our spearmen went out. Again at [[evt:515|Unzhinlathu]], before our own wall, the field was Kavu's. I stood on the wall. I saw the dust go up. I saw our men come back running, and some did not come back.

Then [[evt:516|the second sack]]. Hear it.

The gate was broken at evening. The men of Kavu came in with torches. They came in shouting the names of their own gods, which I will not write on this clay. They went from house to house. They went into the house of the potters; they broke the pots. They went into the house of the weavers; they carried out the cloth. They went into the house of the god; they pulled down the bronze from the door-posts, they poured out the oil on the floor of the god. The priest stood in the door. The priest said, "This is the house of Nijinande." They pushed the priest down into the oil.

I hid in a pit under the threshing-floor, with my mother and my two children. All night the city burned. All night the dogs barked and the women cried out. The smoke went up to Khuthaze. Khuthaze was silent. I said: why are you silent, lord of the sky? I did not hear an answer.

In the morning we came out. The storerooms were empty. The granary was black. The house of the god had no door. They had taken the young men in ropes; they had taken the cattle; they had taken the silver, every ring, every bar.

Then the lords of Kavu sat down in the house of our chief. That was [[evt:517|the conquest of Unjinlathu]]; they took our land under their hand. [[pol:24|Unjinlathu]] bows to Kavu now. Our chief's seat is under their feet.

But hear this, you who come after. The priests of [[mov:4|Khupande]] say: the wheel turns. What ends will begin again. The house of the god will have a door again. The granary will be full again. Kavu came twice; Kavu will not stand forever. Remember Unjinlathu. Remember the two sacks. Do not forget the names of the gods.

*[Lower edge.]*

> Written in the 31st year of Unjinlathu. Whoever breaks this tablet, may Kumbe hide his face from him.
