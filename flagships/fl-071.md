Thus says [[per:4431|Makhshishenmumath]], queen of [[pol:61|Tallin]], daughter of the king who sat in the seat before her, servant of [[dei:16|Dumamnul]] of the sky, beloved of [[dei:14|Vulme]] of the battle:

Hear, you priests. Hear, you scribes. Hear, you men of the palace who are still in the palace.

The land of Tallin was one land. From the river to the hills it was one land, under one seat, under one seal. My father held it. My fathers' fathers held it. Three hundred years and more the kings of Tallin held it.

Now the land is broken. It is broken like a pot dropped on the threshing floor.

[[pol:172|Duvegobuvinga]] has gone out from me.  
[[pol:173|Jukuti]] has gone out from me.  
[[pol:174|Noltennga]] has gone out from me.  
[[pol:175|Yinnga]] has gone out from me.  
And in [[set:0|Tallin]], in my own city, at my own gate, another man sits and calls himself lord of [[pol:171|Tallin]].

Who did this? I did not do this. The governors did this, the men who ate bread at my table, the men whose sons I named in the palace. They took the silver of the tribute and did not send it. They swore the oath before the gods and spat upon it. They sent me sweet words, and behind the words they sharpened the spear.

And the gods did this. Since the days of my fathers' fathers the gods have turned their faces from Tallin. At [[evt:3691|Rile]] they turned their faces. At [[evt:3709|Shivange]] they turned their faces. At [[evt:3760|Vuvu]] they turned their faces. And the [[evt:3665|Marsh Pestilence]] came up out of the reeds; it went into every house; the living were too few to carry out the dead. Since that day the land has been sick.

The land was sick when I took the seat. Three years I have sat in the seat. Three years I have poured to the gods, morning and evening, oil and beer and the fat of oxen. Was it enough? It was not enough. The fault is not in my hand. The fault is in the hands of the faithless.

Therefore hear the last decree of the court of Tallin.

I decree: let the fire of [[dei:18|Eva]] in the house of the gods not go out. Let the priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] keep it burning, whoever sits at the gate.

I decree: let the silver of the palace storehouse, the [[cur:48|menmu]] weighed and sealed, be carried to the house of Dumamnul and laid before the god. Let no rebel weigh it. Let no rebel count it.

I decree: let the tablets of the kings be sealed in jars and set beneath the floor of the house of the gods, so that the names of the kings of Tallin are not lost.

I decree: let the grain of the palace be given to the widows of the city, in the name of [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]] of the grain.

I decree: let the men of my guard go home to their houses. Let no man die at the gate for a seat that is broken.

The wheel turns. What ends will begin again: so the priests say, and so I say. I do not know the day. The gods know the day.

Whoever breaks this tablet, whoever scrapes my name from it, whoever lays a hand on the silver of the god: may Vulme break his spear in battle; may Dumamnul withhold the rain from his field; may Eva burn his house; may his name be forgotten as the name of Tallin is now trampled in the dust.

Last decree of the court, in the 3rd year of Makhshishenmumath, the year of the [[evt:4077|Collapse of the Tallin State]].
