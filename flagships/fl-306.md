*[Obverse. Ruled in four columns; the first line is written large.]*

Weep, O [[set:0|Tallin]], mother of spears, weep for your sons.  
Weep, O house of the king, weep for the sons of the house.  
Weep, O threshing floor, for the reapers have not come home.

[[dei:19|Yenan]] of the night, white lord of the moon, count them with your light.  
[[dei:15|Vinba]] of the black earth, mother below, open your lap to them.  
They went down into [[reg:8|Duvekopuvinga]]; they did not come up out of Duvekopuvinga.  
They went down with bronze; the bronze came home without them.

Hear it, you who sit at the gate. Hear it, you who grind at the stone.  
The men of [[pol:9|Pumi]] sat on the good land. The men of Pumi did not bow.  
So the spears of [[pol:1|Tallin]] went down, in the year of the long road,  
and that was the [[evt:397|First Tallin–Pumi War]], three summers long.

At Nilu, by the walls of [[set:4|Nelo]], they fought the [[evt:399|Battle of Nilu]].  
[[per:499|Ngulen]] the strong-armed stood against [[per:352|Tavin]], lord of Pumi.  
Seven thousand and more were ours. The field was Tallin's.  
But one thousand and three of ours lay down in the barley.  
One thousand and three did not rise. Who will reap their fields?  
Weep, O threshing floor. The field was Tallin's; the sons were Vinba's.

At Himi, by the walls of [[set:17|Pumi]], the spears sat down in a ring.  
The men sat in the ring, they ate their bread cold, the dust was their bed.  
That was the [[evt:400|Siege of Himi]], and the gate did not open.

At Himi, in the next summer, they fought the [[evt:404|Battle of Himi]]. She went down herself,  
[[per:487|Makhshikoth]], lady of Tallin, mother of the king, the bronze-girded,  
she stood against [[per:511|Nunenin]], the general of Pumi.  
She poured oil to [[dei:14|Vulme]] of the spears, she poured beer to Vulme at the ford.  
Vulme heard her. Nunenin broke. The field was Tallin's.  
But eight hundred and eighty and one of ours lay down by the wall.  
Eight hundred and eighty and one did not rise. Who will herd their goats?  
Weep, O sheepfold. The field was Tallin's; the sons were Vinba's.

*[About ten lines damaged. Legible: "[…] the gate […]", "[…] the ring a second time […]".]*

Then Tavin came out of the gate. Tavin, lord of Pumi, came out.  
He set his hand in the hand of Makhshikoth before the gods of the sky,  
and Pumi was given to Tallin, the land and the water of it.  
That was the [[evt:406|Conquest of Pumi]]. Let the singer say it twice:  
Pumi was given to Tallin, the land and the water of it.

*[Reverse.]*

Count them, Yenan, count them, lord of the moon.  
One thousand, eight hundred, eighty and four: so many of ours.  
The scribe counted them, the priest counted them, the mothers counted them.  
The mothers counted wrong, for each mother counted only one.

[[dei:18|Eva]] of the hearth, burn the fat of the ram for them.  
Nidhikhti of the grain, put barley in their mouths.  
Let them not go hungry on the road below. Let them not go cold.  
Let the barley sprout over them in Duvekopuvinga, the good land, the land we hold.

What ends will begin again, says the priest.  
The wheel turns; the reaper becomes the seed, says the priest.  
So says the priest. But the threshing floor is empty, and the mothers sit in the dust.

Weep, O Tallin, mother of spears, weep for your sons.  
Weep, O house of the king. Weep, O threshing floor.  
The reapers have not come home.

*[Lower edge. Colophon.]*

> Written in the 10th year of [[per:517|Jingunin]], king in Tallin, son of Makhshikoth.  
> Sung at the new moon in the month of the barley-cutting.  
> Whoever breaks this tablet, may Vinba close her lap to him.
