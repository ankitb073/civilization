*[Obverse.]*

*One mother:* Where is my son?  
*All the mothers:* At Vatlshi. At Val'a. Under the earth.  

*One mother:* Who sent him?  
*All the mothers:* The king's house sent him. The king's house in Khiy'a.  

*One mother:* Where is the king's house?  
*All the mothers:* Fallen. Fallen. Fallen like our sons.  

Hear us, [[dei:2|Mivzhas'i]], mother of the earth. You are a mother. You have our sons.  
Hear us, [[dei:5|Niyshashta]], lady of war. You are a woman. You took them anyway.  
Hear us, Lashla'i of the moon, who sees the fields at night where they lie.  

In the year of the [[evt:7641|Khyeka–Va'mi War]] the heralds came to the villages.  
They cried in the square: the land is ours, go and take it.  
They gave our sons a spear of bronze and a shield of hide.  
They gave our sons a bag of barley for nine days.  
We baked the bread. We sewed the cloaks. We stood at the gate.  
We said: come back. They said: we will come back.  

*All the mothers:* They said: we will come back.  

At [[set:139|Vatlshi]], in the second summer, they met the men of [[pol:294|Va'mi]].  
Our sons were many. Our sons were three for every one of theirs.  
But the men of Va'mi stood like a wall of stone,  
and our sons broke on the wall like water on the stone.  
[[per:8017|I'cuil Yüb]] the general led them. I'cuil Yüb came home. Our sons did not.  

*[Reverse.]*

At [[set:22|Val'a]], in the third summer, they met the men of Va'mi again.  
The ones who were left from Vatlshi, and the young ones, the second levy, the boys.  
My youngest was in the second levy. He had no beard. He had a beard of dust.  
Again the wall. Again the water on the stone.  

Then came the [[evt:7667|Treaty of Bau]]: no land, no silver, nothing. Only peace.  
Only peace, for which we did not need to send them.  

The scribes of the king counted: three thousand eight hundred and forty-six.  
Three thousand eight hundred and forty-six sons. They wrote it on the clay.  
We do not count so. We count one. Each of us counts one, or two, or three.  

*All the mothers:* We count one. We count one.  

Now hear what is new, Mivzhas'i. Hear it, Niyshashta.  
Last year the king's house fell. It is the [[evt:7748|Collapse of the Khyeka State]].  
The governors made themselves kings. The towns shut their gates against each other.  
At Val'a there is a king now, they say, where our sons lie.  
Who will pour the water for our sons now? Who will keep their names?  
The king's house that sent them is gone. The clay with the count on it is broken.  

Barley is three [[cur:110|Tina]] and more the measure, and the roads are not safe.  
We are old. Our hands are not strong for the plough.  
Our daughters plough. Our daughters' husbands are dead too, or gone to the new kings.  

So we keep the names. We sing them at the new moon, at the stone by the well.  
We will sing them until our mouths are earth.  

*One mother:* Where is my son?  
*All the mothers:* At Vatlshi. At Val'a. In our mouths. In this song.
