*The dead of the gate quarter of [[set:7|Ana]], put into the earth by my hand, in the 1st year of [[per:3853|E'a Mue'a]], king of [[pol:93|Mii]].*

**Month of the barley harvest.**

Day 2. One man, a potter, of the street of the kilns. The shaking, then the fever. Two days. By the east wall.  
Day 2. One woman, his wife. The same. By her husband.  
Day 4. One girl, a child of the water-carrier. The shaking. By the east wall.

*Note. The water-carrier gave half a [[cur:65|Lash'a]] and a jar of beer. I took the silver for the priest. I drank the beer.*

Day 5. One man, a soldier, come home from the [[evt:3519|battle of Myizinithie]] with spoil on his back. The shaking. His spoil did not save him. By the east wall.  
Day 5. One man, his brother. The same.  
Day 6. One old woman, a seller of onions. The shaking. By the east wall.  
Day 6. One boy. Name not known. Found by the gate.

*Note. They call it the shaking sickness. The priests call it the [[evt:3463|Shaking Pestilence]]. It begins in the hands: the hand shakes so that the cup spills. Then the whole body. Then the fever. Then I come.*

Day 8. Two men, a woman, three children: the household of the weaver. The same. One pit, by the east wall. The east wall is full.  
Day 9. One man, a scribe of the granary. The same. In the new ground beyond the ditch.  
Day 9. One woman, a midwife. The same. She brought my son into the world. In the new ground, at the head of the row, with her bronze pin on her.

*Note. The old king went to his fathers this year, and the new king sits in the house of kings. The drums beat for the old king. We did not dig for him. The king's men dig for the king.*

Day 11. Four. Day 12. Six. Day 13. Nine.

*Note. Bread is one Lash'a and a tenth the measure. The bakers of the gate quarter have shut their doors and gone up the river. A man gave me his house for my labour. What shall I do with a house? I have a house.*

*Note. [[dei:4|Lashla'e]] of the moon, count them. You see them at night, you count them. [[dei:3|Niele'i]] of the river, wash them. [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the earth, open your mouth, receive them. Muevas'e, mother, you are fed. Muevas'e, mother, be content.*

Day 14. Seven. Day 15. Ten. I do not write the names of the children now. There is not room on the tablet.

Day 16. Twelve. My son digs with me. He is fourteen years. He does not speak. He digs.

*Note. The priests of [[mov:0|La'im]] went to the high house of [[dei:0|Atlie]] and gave a white bull. The smoke went up straight. The people said: Atlie has heard. Day 17: eleven.*

Day 18. Eleven. Day 19. Thirteen. Day 20. Eleven. The new ground beyond the ditch is half full. I have marked another ground beyond it, by the road to the river.

*Note. My hands shake tonight. It is the cold. It is the long work. It is the cold.*
