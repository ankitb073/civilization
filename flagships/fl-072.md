This is the tablet of the breaking of [[pol:80|Nelo]]. I saw it with my eyes. I, a scribe of the house of [[dei:28|Khothaze]] in the city of [[set:5|Nelo]], wrote it with my hand. Let it be kept in the house of the god. Let the one who reads it pour water for me.

Hear the beginning of the evil. In the days of the fathers of our fathers, the throne of Nelo was taken by a hand that was not given it. That was the [[evt:3752|Usurpation of Nagovo I]]. The priests did not anoint him. The gods saw it. The gods were silent, but they did not forget.

Hear the second evil. In the days of our fathers the white sickness came. That was [[evt:3876|The White Pestilence]]. It came into the city of Nelo. It came into the houses. Of every four men, one died. The grain stood in the field and no one cut it. The dead lay in the street and no one buried them. My father's father carried the dead to the river on his back, and then he lay down and died.

Hear the third evil. Kagene would not bring its gifts. Kagene set up its own throne. That was the [[evt:3937|Secession of Kagene]]. The great king took it back, but the wound did not close.

Hear the fourth evil. Again the throne was taken by a hand that was not given it. That was the [[evt:4026|Usurpation of Nashenjè]]. He sat on the throne seventeen years. His armies went out. His armies won. The enemy bowed down before him. But the gods do not count victories. The gods count oaths.

Now hear what I saw.

In the year of the breaking I was a young scribe. I sat in the gate of the palace with my tablets. The new year came. The governors did not come.

The men of [[pol:178|Povo]] did not come. The men of [[pol:179|Kavo]] did not come. The men of [[pol:180|Kagene]] did not come. The men of [[pol:181|Kangoshe]] did not come. The men of [[pol:182|Yathe]] did not come. Their gifts did not come. The storehouses were not filled. The [[cur:69|Datho]] did not come into the treasury.

Then messengers came. One messenger said: "Povo has set up a throne." One messenger said: "Kavo has set up a throne." One messenger said: "In Kagene, in Kangoshe, in Yathe, a man sits on a throne." The great king sat in the palace. He did not go out. He did not send an army. The army had gone home.

I went up on the wall. I looked to the east. I looked to the west. I looked to the south. I looked to the north. The roads were empty. The great kingdom of Nelo, which had been one, was six.

On that day the priests of [[dei:30|Pogazho]] did not light the fire. On that day the boats of [[dei:29|Bombe]] did not go out. That night [[dei:31|Kombe]] hid her face behind cloud. [[dei:32|Nejenande]] of war did not hear us.

Hear what is now. Eighteen years have passed. The throne in the city remains. A king sits on it. The house of [[pol:177|Nelo]] stands, and the fields around the city are ours. In these last years our king sent out his army, and the same enemy bowed down again. The gods have not forgotten us.

The [[mov:4|Khopande]] teach that all things turn on the great wheel, that what ends will begin again. The kingdom was one. The kingdom is six. The wheel turns. May the six be one again. Let Khothaze see it. Let Khothaze do it.

Let the one who reads this tablet not break the oath of the throne. Let him not take a throne that was not given him. Let him give each god their due. The fathers took what was not given, and Nelo was broken.

Written in the city of Nelo, in the 320th year of Nelo.
