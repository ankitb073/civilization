*[Obverse. Set up in the gate of the house of the gods; a copy was sent to each village.]*

Hear it, [[dei:63|Dvimamnul]] of the high sky. Hear it, [[dei:62|Novigu]] of wisdom, who knows the words. Hear it, [[dei:64|Nidi]] of the spear, who went before us. Hear it, Tono of the moon, who counts the months. Hear it, [[dei:66|Shyidartsi]] of the house below, who holds our dead.

Hear it, men of [[pol:402|Pviyo]]. Hear it, men of [[pol:372|Yimola]]. Hear it, every man who passes this gate.

Pviyo does not bow. The neck of Pviyo is not bent. The grain of Pviyo does not go down the road to Yimola. The oxen of Pviyo do not go down the road. The young men of Pviyo do not go down the road to carry the baskets of Yimola. The land of [[reg:15|Vani]] is Pviyo's land. Say it, and say it twice: Pviyo does not bow.

Hear how it was before. Our fathers bowed. Our fathers' fathers bowed. The men of Yimola came every year at the cutting of the barley. They took the tenth sheaf and the fifth sheaf. They took the fat ox. They took the young men for their walls. Our fathers said: it is the way of the wheel. Our fathers wept and bowed.

Then [[per:9455|Vyinufmalin]] stood up in [[set:105|Pviyo]]. He would not bow. In that year [[evt:9139|Pviyo rose]], and he became king, and the men of Pviyo stood up with him.

Hear the deeds of the war.

At [[evt:9153|Niuzafammas]] the first time, Vyinufmalin went against [[per:9345|Kikukil Shimkamu]] of Yimola. One and twenty men went with him, sworn to his hand, and two thousand spears came behind them, against more than a thousand. Six hundred and more of ours lay down. Of theirs, near two hundred lay down. The field was Yimola's, but the name was ours. Let the singers sing the one and twenty.

At [[evt:9168|Xath]], by the houses of Shathi, Vyinufmalin went against Kikukil Shimkamu a second time. Our spears were more than theirs. The field was Pviyo's.

At Niuzafammas the second time, [[per:9484|Vutalen]] led us against Kikukil Shimkamu. Nidi turned her face that day. The field was Yimola's. We wept, and did not bow.

At Xath the second time, Vyinufmalin went against Kikukil Shimkamu again, the third time those two met. The field was Pviyo's.

At [[evt:9182|Niuzafammas]] the third time, [[per:9493|Tuvanan]] led us against [[per:9361|Kilshashukikuka]] of Yimola. Their dead were more than ours. The field was Pviyo's. Then the spears of Pviyo went into Nilthapammeth, and we took back from its storerooms the tenth sheaf and the fifth sheaf and the fat ox. That was [[evt:9183|the sack]], and it was just.

Six years we fought. Five times we met them. Three times the field was ours. And where the field was ours, their dead were more than ours.

*[Reverse.]*

Then at Nilthapammeth Vyinufmalin sat down, and Kilshashukikuka sat down before him. They swore by the gods of the sky and the gods of the earth. That is [[evt:9184|the oath of Niuzafammas]]. Yimola has sworn it. Yimola has bowed its head to the oath. From this day the road of tribute is closed.

Therefore hear these words, and keep them.

Pviyo has its own king. Pviyo has its own gate. Pviyo gives its own sheaf to its own gods, by the hands of its own priests of [[mov:9|Myinive]], in the order that is fitting.

Whoever says, "Pviyo bows to Yimola": may Dvimamnul shut the sky over him. May Shyidartsi open the earth under him. May Novigu take his words away.

Whoever breaks this tablet: may Nidi break his spear, and may the moon not rise for him.

> Written in the 6th year of Vyinufmalin, king of Pviyo, who did not bow.
