*[Front.]*

O [[dei:19|Yenan]], lord of the moon, white lord, lord of the night road,  
lord who sees the house when the lamp is out,  
hear me. I stand at your door. I have washed my hands. I have washed my feet.  
I am the widow of the dyer of the lane of the vats, by the east gate of [[set:0|Tallen]].  
I have no name worth saying. You know my house. You have looked in at the window.  

In the 11th year of [[per:11102|Olotafbafen]], queen in Tallen, lady of [[pol:439|Yeda]],  
in the month of the first figs,  
the [[evt:11004|Red Plague]] came into the city.  
It came in the lane of the vats first. So the people say. They say it was the dye.  
It was not the dye. My husband dyed for twenty years and it was never the dye.  

It came as a redness on the neck. Then a redness on the chest.  
Then the fever. Then the thirst. Then the sleeping that is not sleeping.  
My husband lay down on the fourth day of the moon. On the ninth day of the moon he did not get up.  
I gave him to [[dei:15|Venba]], lady of the earth. She has him.  
I did not wash his body. The priests said: do not wash. Burn the cloth.  
I burned the cloth. I burned his good blue cloth. Forgive me, husband.  

*[Back.]*

Now hear my vow, Yenan. Hear it and hold it as the moon holds the tide.  

I have two sons. I have one daughter. The daughter is at the breast.  
The redness is not on them. I look every morning. I look every night by your light.  
If you keep them, lord of the moon,  
if the redness does not come on the neck of my sons,  
if the redness does not come on the neck of my daughter,  
until the plague goes out of Tallen as it came in,  

then I will give you a white kid at the full moon.  
Then I will give you a jar of oil at the dark of the moon.  
Then I will give you three [[cur:241|Noype]] of silver, the silver of the dye-shop,  
which I have in a cloth under the floor, which no one knows.  
Now you know. I have told no one but you.  

And I will give you this: every month, on the night of the full moon,  
I will set a lamp in the window of the lane of the vats  
so you will know the house, so you will not pass it by.  

O [[dei:18|Eva]], lord of the fire, eat the cloth of the sick, eat it all.  
O Venba, lady of the earth, you have my husband. That is enough. That is enough.  

The price of bread is high. Barley is a Noype and more the measure  
and the carts do not come in from the villages.  
The neighbours shut their doors. The neighbours do not speak across the lane.  
The priests of [[mov:2|Jenro]] go up and down with smoke and bells.  
They say the wheel turns. They say what ends will begin again.  
I do not want it to begin again. I want it to end.  

Yenan, white lord, see my lamp. Keep my three.  
I have said it. Let the clay hold it. Let the fire harden it.  
I will come back at the full moon, and the next, and the next.
