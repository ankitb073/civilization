*A song in praise of our lord the king, made for the feast of his forty-third year upon the throne, and sung before him in the great hall of [[set:147|Binge]].*

Hear, lords and knights, and you who hold your lands  
by service of the sword and of the hand;  
hear, priests who keep the Light from age to age;  
hear, commons, and be glad: I sing the king.  

I sing of [[per:41070|Livdun]], whom the Light has crowned,  
the son of [[per:41042|Maavuthin]], of the blood of kings,  
who forty years and three has held the seat  
of [[pol:1710|Binge]], and has not let it fall.  

The Light is One; the old gods are its shadows;  
and as the sun above the shadows stands,  
so stands our king above the lords of earth,  
and as the sun gives warmth, so gives the king.  

Who has not heard of [[evt:41740|Lunge]]? There the foe  
stood thick as locusts on the summer field,  
and there our king's host fell on them, few to many,  
and sixty thousand of the foe lay down.  
The heralds of the foe still weep  
to tell it, and their mothers curse the name.  

And if the Light, who tries the just like gold,  
gave sorrow to us also, at [[evt:41731|Unushti]]  
and at [[evt:41819|Nilithna]], what of it?  
The saint is proved by trial, and so the king.  
He did not rage, he did not curse the heavens;  
he weighed the blood of Binge against the silver,  
and chose to spend the silver, not the blood:  
so was the [[evt:41820|Treaty of Nilithna]] sealed,  
a peace of mercy, bought with [[cur:956|tuthshi]], not with sons.  
Which other king has loved his people so?  

Now hear of the [[evt:41882|Binge–Biniynge War]],  
where the false lords of Biniynge raised their heads.  
At [[evt:41898|Yiranunyirin]] our host came down,  
and the lord of Biniynge fled from his own field;  
and in this year, at [[evt:41921|Yiranunyirin]] again,  
the rebels broke like pots beneath the wheel.  
Let the ravens of that country sing the rest.  

Nor is the king's sword sharp for war alone.  
The Light is jealous of its truth, and he  
is jealous for the Light. When in this year  
the error of the I'ishiu crept in  
like rust into the armour of the realm,  
he scoured it out: so let the scribes record  
the [[evt:41932|Persecution of the I'ishiu in Binge]],  
that the priests of [[mov:58|Vishithmikh]] might keep  
the truth unbroken, passed from hand to hand.  
Compassion is the Light's; but so is order;  
the surgeon cuts that he may heal the limb.  

Behold beside the throne his daughter fair,  
[[per:41092|Maatungath]], the jewel of his house,  
in whom the wisdom of her father shines  
as the moon shines with the borrowed light of day.  
The lords bow down to her, and so they should.  

O lords, you hold your fiefs of such a king.  
Your oath is not a chain, it is a crown;  
your service is not bondage, it is honour.  
Ride when he calls, and counsel when he asks,  
and pay your dues at the appointed days,  
and he will give you justice and defence,  
as the Light gives the faithful its reward.  

The line of time runs from the first day on  
to judgement; and on that day, when the scales are set,  
let the deeds of [[per:41070|Livdun]] be the first weighed.  
I do not fear for him. I fear for those  
who, having seen such greatness, did not praise it.  

*Written in the 43rd year of Livdun, our lord, whom the Light preserve.*
