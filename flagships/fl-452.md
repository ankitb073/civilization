**REPUBLIC OF MANA.**

**REPORT OF THE GOVERNOR FOR THE LODE TERRITORIES ON THE LATE OPERATIONS AND THE WITHDRAWAL OF THE ESTABLISHMENT.**

*Presented to the Ministry, [[set:13|Tzeventoa]], and ordered to be printed. 10 Mua 2601 of the Eheshio.*

---

Sir,

I have the honour to submit, as required by the regulations, my report upon the conduct and conclusion of the [[evt:46030|Mana Colonial War in Luthe]], together with the returns of strength and casualties, and such observations as my office permits.

**1. Origins.** The Ministry will not need reminding that the operations arose from the [[evt:46029|Mana Claim against Luthe]], which the Republic advanced in good faith and on sound title, and which the chiefs of [[reg:27|Lode]] refused to acknowledge. The decision to enforce that claim by arms was taken at Tzeventoa. It is not for me to review it.

**2. Strength.** I must place on record, with all respect, the following figures. The column of the Republic under Colonel [[per:45323|Voshkheyof]] at no time during three campaigning seasons exceeded thirty-five officers and men. Its strength at the three principal engagements was 35, 30 and 31. Opposed to it, according to the estimates of my intelligence officers, which I consider moderate, were bodies of 87,444, 104,109 and 75,034 respectively. I repeatedly applied by wire for reinforcement. The replies are on file.

**3. The engagements.** All three were fought before the native town of [[set:25|Khavo]] and are officially styled as follows:

- The [[evt:46035|Battle of Kavu]], first season. Enemy commanded by [[per:45259|Navavon]]. Our losses, 6; the enemy's, 5,718.
- The [[evt:46042|Battle of Kavu]], second season. Enemy commanded by [[per:45185|Nalale]]. Our losses, 6; the enemy's, 5,291.
- The [[evt:46055|Battle of Kavu]], third season. Enemy again under Navavon. Our losses, 12; the enemy's, 7,229.

The total of our dead for the war is therefore twenty-four. The enemy's, by careful count of the burial parties, is 18,238. I invite the Ministry to consider whether any column in the history of the Republic has inflicted such loss at such cost, and to weigh that ratio when the conduct of Colonel Voshkheyof and his men comes, as I understand it will, to be discussed in the Chamber.

**4. The result.** It would nevertheless be dishonest to describe the outcome otherwise than as it was. On each of the three occasions the column, having exhausted its ammunition against an enemy who did not cease to come on, was obliged to retire upon its base, and the field remained with [[pol:1826|Lode]]. After the third season it was clear to me, and I so telegraphed, that the position could not be held without a force some hundreds of times larger than that provided. The Government of the Republic drew the same conclusion.

**5. The peace.** The [[evt:46056|Treaty of Kavu]] was accordingly concluded at Khavo between His Excellency [[per:45307|Melianuo]], for the Republic, and Nalale for the chiefs of Lode. Its terms are known to the Ministry: the Republic renounces its claim, pays an indemnity, and observes a truce of twenty years. I signed nothing and was consulted on nothing, but I carried out the withdrawal in good order. The last of the establishment's stores were embarked within the period stipulated.

**6. Observations.** I offer three:

(a) The enemy are numerous, brave and well led. Navavon and Nalale handled very great bodies of men with a skill that I should be glad to see in our own staff college.

(b) A colonial policy that is willing to claim a country but not to garrison it is not a policy but a wager. The Republic has lost this wager.

(c) I would ask that the twenty-four be honoured by name in the rolls. They were sent to do an impossible thing and very nearly did it.

**7. Religious.** The chaplain of the [[mov:57|Eheshio]] attached to the column read the funeral office over our dead and, at my order, over theirs. He reminded us that the wheel turns, and that what has ended may begin again. I record his view without endorsing it as policy.

I have the honour to be, Sir, your obedient servant,

THE GOVERNOR.
