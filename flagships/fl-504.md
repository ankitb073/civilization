*[Obverse]*

In the 475th year of Tanchethi.  
Before [[dei:10|Masto]] of the sun, who sees all lands.  
Before [[dei:8|Nish'il]] of the high sky, who stretches over all lands.  
Before [[dei:6|Nili]] of the river, who runs through all lands.  
Before [[dei:7|Yof]] of the fire, who burns on the hearth of the Great House and has not gone out.

Thus decrees the Great House of [[pol:291|Tanchethi]], the house of the empire, the house of four hundred and seventy-five years.  
Hear it, scribes. Hear it, priests of [[mov:1|Ya'kam]]. Hear it, keepers of the storehouse and keepers of the gate.  
Hear it, and press it into the clay, and set the clay in the house of Masto, where the first tablets of the house were set.

The lands of the Great House were one land.  
Now they are six lands. Hear their names.

[[pol:526|Los]] has its own gate.  
[[pol:527|Wal'e]] has its own gate.  
[[pol:528|Yisamashchi]] has its own gate.  
[[pol:529|Wa'mi]] has its own gate.  
[[pol:530|Muel'i]] has its own gate.  
And [[pol:525|Tanchethi]] keeps the gate of [[set:152|Tanchethi]], the old city, the city of the throne.

This is the [[evt:11672|Collapse of the Tantethi State]]. Two years ago it was done. Two years the Great House has waited. Two years the governors have not come. The tribute has not come. The barley has not come. The silver has not come.

Hear now why it was so. The Great House does not hide it.

First, three times in six years the seat changed hands, and the hand that took it was not the hand the gods had given. This began with the [[evt:11141|Usurpation of Mi'epayal]], and the days after it, and the days after those.  
Then at Shidyun the spears of the house were broken. This was the [[evt:11252|Battle of Shidyun]]. Many men did not come home.  
Then the men of Vani stood up and said: "We are not of the house." This was the [[evt:11302|Revolt of Vani]].  
When the seat shakes, the house shakes. When the house shakes, the walls fall away.

*[Reverse]*

Therefore the Great House decrees.

The Great House sends no more governors to the five lands. It does not ask for their tribute. What it cannot take, it releases. Let it not be said that the five lands were taken from the house; let it be said that the house set them down, as an old man sets down a load he has carried long.

The seals of the empire, the seal of the six lands and the seal of the river road, let them be broken before the altar of Masto, so that no false scribe may press them on false clay.

The standards of the empire, let them be laid in the house of [[dei:11|Nizhashte]] of the spear, with the spears of the old kings.

The scribes of the empire, who wrote for six lands, let them write now for one. Let each be paid what is owed him, in good [[cur:157|Ishke]], from the storehouse of the city, before the new moon.

The roll of the provinces, let it be kept in the house of tablets. Let it not be broken. Let the sons of our sons read it and know how wide the house once was.

The priests of Ya'kam, let them pour in order, each god in the god's own place, as they have poured for four hundred and seventy-five years. Let the fire of Yof not go out.

Hear also this. The priests say that all things turn on a great wheel; that what ends will begin again. The Great House believes it. The Great House waits for the wheel. It does not weep. It does not curse. It waits.

This is the last decree of the court of the six lands. The decrees after this are the decrees of the city.

Whoever breaks this tablet, may [[dei:9|Shiminto]] of the storm break his roof and his name.
