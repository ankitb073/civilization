*Obverse.*

These are the words of a scribe of the house of [[dei:16|Domamnol]] in [[set:0|Tallen]], who saw these things with his eyes and wrote them with his hand. Let him who reads them pour water for the scribe.

In the 287th year of Tallen: the army of [[pol:171|Tallen]] went out to [[evt:6696|Khungumnu]]. The army was great. The army was like the grass of the field. The army went down. The army did not come back. The men who came back came back without their shields. In that year the women of Tallen cut their hair.

In the 292nd year of Tallen: the army went out again to [[evt:6717|Khungumnu]]. The army went down again. The king's men said: the ground was bad, the river was high. The priests said: [[dei:14|Volme]] has turned her face. The scribe says: Volme turned her face.

In those years the silver went out of Tallen and did not come in. The [[cur:114|lol]] that was full weight became light. The merchants weighed it twice. The merchants weighed it three times. The governors of the cities sent less grain. The governors of the cities sent less silver. They said: the road is bad. They said: the river is low. They lied.

In the 317th year of Tallen: [[dei:19|Yenan]] the moon was red at his rising, three nights, four nights. The diviners cut the liver. The liver was spotted. They told the king. The king did not hear.

In that year [[evt:6958|the land of Tallen was broken]]. Hear how it was broken.  
The governors of the cities met in secret, each in his own city.  
In one month they rose. In one month they shut the gates.  
[[pol:306|Yeda]] rose. [[pol:307|Navnga]] rose. [[pol:308|Dovegobovenga]] rose.  
[[pol:309|Nolenga]] rose. [[pol:310|Pome]] rose. [[pol:311|Tobojenga]] rose.  
Six they were. Six heads where there had been one head.  
Each said: I am king. Each said: my city is a kingdom.  
Each took the oath of his own gate, and forgot the oath of Tallen.

And in Tallen itself, the city of the great king, the house of the gods, the lords of Yeda opened the gates and [[evt:6959|set up their seat]] in the palace. The scribe saw it. The scribe stood in the street of the potters and saw it. The banners of Tallen were taken down from the gate. Other banners were put up. The people did not shout. The people stood and were silent. Only the dogs barked.

*Reverse.*

In the 318th year of Tallen: the roads were shut. A merchant who went from Navnga to Pome paid a toll at every gate, and the toll was more than the goods. The roads were empty. The grass grew in the roads.

In the 321st year of Tallen: strangers came. The men of other lands came against Dovegobovenga. The men of other lands came against Navnga. They came for silver, they came for land. And the cities did not help one another. Brother did not go out to help brother. They were all men of Tallen. They all swore by the same gods. They all poured to [[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire and to [[dei:15|Venba]] of the earth. And still each shut his gate and watched his brother's field burn.

In the 323rd year of Tallen: the scribe writes this tablet. The scribe is old. The scribe still counts the years of Tallen, though there is no Tallen. Let the lords of Yeda say what they will. Let the lords of Pome say what they will. The years of Tallen are counted in the house of Domamnol.

The priests of [[mov:2|Jenro]] say: all things turn on the wheel. What ends will begin again. The scribe says: may it begin again while the scribe lives. May the scribe see one king in Tallen. May he see the six heads become one head.

Let this tablet be set in the house of Domamnol. Let no one break it. Whoever breaks it, may Volme break him. Whoever keeps it, may Domamnol keep him.
