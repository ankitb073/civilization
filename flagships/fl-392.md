*[Rubric, in a smaller hand:] To be said in the dark, by those who keep the old way of the [[mov:27|Anatofpel]], when two or three are gathered and the door is barred. Let no lamp be lit while it is spoken. Written in the year which the priests of the [[mov:58|Veshethmekh]] call 905.*

O One who turns the wheel, whom no temple holds and no priest may sell,  
hear us, who have no temple now and want no priest but you.  

We do not come before you with gold, for we have none; and if we had it, we would give it away, as your teaching bids us. We do not come with incense, for the smell of it would betray us to the street. We come with what is left to us: our breath, our names, and the dark of this cellar. Accept these, for they are all we have, and they are honest.

You know, O One, how it has been with us since the man [[per:28450|Medhelonen]] put his hand upon the throne of [[pol:1141|Peme]] which was not his to take. You saw the [[evt:28887|Usurpation of Medhelonen I]]; you saw the old king's servants scattered and the new king's servants set in their places. And in the very next year you saw the edict go out, and the [[evt:28917|Persecution of the Anatofpil in Peme]] begin. Our meeting-house by the river gate was shut with a beam and a seal. Our books were carried into the square of [[set:17|Peme]] and burned before the people, and the priests of the established faith stood by and called it mercy.

We do not ask you to curse them. We have learned from you that hatred is a second chain, and that he who wears it is twice bound. We ask only that you remember them as they are, and remember us as we are, and judge between us when the wheel comes round.

Remember the widow who hid three of us under her loom and would not take a single [[cur:615|Tokhlo]] for her trouble.  
Remember the boy who carried word along the roofs by night.  
Remember the elder who was taken at the well, and did not speak our names, though they asked him three times.  
Remember the ones who did speak them, for they were afraid, and fear is also a kind of suffering.  

Now the kingdom is torn. Tallen has broken away from the throne in the [[evt:28907|Secession of Tallen]], and the [[evt:28908|Peme Civil War]] fills the roads with soldiers. Some among us whisper that this is your hand, come at last to scourge the usurper. We do not know it. We do not presume to read your purposes in the burning of other men's villages. The soldiers who march past our door are sons of mothers, whichever banner they carry; and the grain that rots in the trampled field is grain that would have fed the poor of both sides.

Therefore we pray: let the war end quickly, by whatever road you choose. Let the edict fall, or let it be forgotten. Let the meeting-house be opened, if not for us, then for our children.

And if it is not opened, let us bear it. Teach us that what ends begins again; that the seed is not lost because it lies a season in the dark; that a people which has been scattered may be gathered on the next turn of the wheel as surely as the barley comes again after the reaping.

Keep us from pride, if we are spared.  
Keep us from despair, if we are not.  
Keep the door barred and our voices low.  
Keep the One in our hearts, where no edict reaches.  

*[Here each says his own name, without sound.]*

So be it, now and on every turning.
