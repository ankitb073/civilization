**The first song**

Dumamnul of the sky has turned his face from [[pol:25|Yida]].  
Dumamnul has turned his face; the rain falls where it will.  
[[dei:15|Vinba]] of the earth has let go of the foundations.  
Vinba has let go; the walls stand, but they do not stand together.  
[[dei:18|Eva]] of the fire has gone out on the hearth of the king.  
Eva has gone out; the hearth of the king is cold ash.  
Yenan of the moon counts the months, but no one writes them for Yida.  

*Its refrain:* O Yida, mother of cities, who will count your years now?  
O Yida, who will count your years?

**The second song**

Our sons went out to Bimnge, to the field of reeds.  
At [[evt:1322|Bimnge]] they fell; the enemy struck from behind.  
Our sons went out to Biniynge, to the bank of the river.  
At [[evt:1339|Biniynge]] they fell; the river took them, the river did not give them back.  
Again our sons went out to Bimnge, to the field of reeds.  
At [[evt:1440|Bimnge]] they fell a second time; the reeds grew tall over them.  
Again our sons went out, to Ture, to the field of stones.  
At [[evt:1458|Ture]] they fell, and the stones were red.  
[[dei:14|Vulme]] of war did not walk with them.  
Vulme of war did not lift his shield over them.  
The mothers of Yida tore their garments at the gate.  
The mothers of Yida sat in the dust and did not rise.  

*Its refrain:* O Yida, mother of cities, who will count your sons now?

**The third song**

In those days the seat of the king was not held in order.  
A hand that was not given the seat reached for the seat.  
In the [[evt:1553|usurpation of Talon]] the house quarrelled with the house.  
The brother did not know the brother. The steward did not know the lord.  
The priests of [[mov:2|Jinru]] poured the beer, but the gods did not drink it.  
The priests slaughtered the ox, but the smoke went sideways.  
Then came the year of the breaking, the [[evt:1589|Collapse of the Yida State]].  
Then the one house became six houses.  

*Its refrain:* O Yida, mother of cities, who will hold your hand now?

**The fourth song**

[[pol:61|Tallin]] keeps the house of the king, but not the kingdom of the king.  
In [[set:0|Tallin]] the gate still stands, but the roads do not come to it.  
[[pol:62|Buneynga]] has set up its own gate and its own scribes.  
[[pol:63|Pumi]] has set up its own gate and its own weights.  
[[pol:64|Tupujinga]] has shut its road and takes the toll of the boats.  
[[pol:65|Jinontonu]] has turned its face to the hills.  
[[pol:66|Punennushal]] has forgotten the name of its mother.  
Six daughters, and each says: "I was never a daughter."  
Six houses, and each says: "I was never one house."  

In the days of Yida a man came to the market with one [[cur:19|tunrun]],  
and he went home with nearly two measures of grain on his ass.  
Now in each market there is a different weight.  
Now in each gate there is a different toll.  
The merchant weeps, and the ass weeps with him.  

*Its refrain:* O Yida, mother of cities, who will weigh true silver now?

**The fifth song**

But the wheel turns. So say the priests of Jinru; so said our fathers.  
What ends will begin again. What breaks will be bound again.  
Turn, wheel, turn, and bring back the one house.  
Dumamnul, turn your face again. Vinba, take hold of the foundations again.  
Eva, kindle the hearth again in the house of the king.  
Then the six will be one, and the mothers will rise from the dust.  

*Its refrain:* O Yida, mother of cities, we count your years still.  
We count your years still.

*Written in the two hundred and sixty-fifth year of Yida, in the seventeenth year after the breaking.*
