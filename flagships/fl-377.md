[[per:23874|Lobiethneth]], by the will of heaven queen of [[pol:1013|Diebegovebenga]], and until this year first priestess of the wheel and keeper of the royal altars, to the elders of the priesthood of [[mov:2|Zhienro]] in [[set:11|Vieneynga]] and in every temple of the kingdom: greeting, and peace, and the remembrance of old fellowship.

You will have heard the proclamation before this letter reaches you, for news runs faster than the queen's couriers, and ill news fastest of all. I do not write to repeat it. I write because I was one of you. I was anointed at your altars when I was a girl; I learned the turning of the seasons from your mouths; for six years of my reign I have poured the libation at the new year with my own hand. It would be a poor return for that teaching if I let you learn from a herald what I owe you in my own words.

It is true. In this year the crown and the kingdom have received the teaching of [[mov:58|Vieshethmekh]], and the altars of the court will be served henceforth by its ordained. This is the [[evt:24192|conversion of Diebegovebenga to Veshethmekh]], and I will not pretend to you that it was done in a moment of passion or against my judgement. I weighed it for longer than you know.

Hear my reasons, and then judge me as you will.

You taught me that the gods share the world, each in his place and with his due, and that the realm belongs to one people. But the realm I rule is not one people. The river towns, the hill clans, the merchants who have settled in the capital from beyond the frontier: these keep other altars, or none, and our rites have never sought them, for our rites do not seek. A crown that cannot speak to half its subjects in the language of their souls rules them only in the language of its tax-gatherers. The new teaching is for all peoples. It goes out to them; it does not wait at the temple door.

You taught me also that prosperity is the sign of divine favour. I have looked at the poor of Vieneynga in the winter of a dear year, and I could not believe that heaven had simply withdrawn its favour from every one of them. The new teaching says that the good life is compassion and release from suffering. Forgive me: I found that I already believed it.

Now hear what I do not do. I do not curse the gods you served. The teaching I have received holds that there is one divine power, and that the old gods are its servants. Your gods are not cast out; they are set in their right order, as stewards in a great house. Nor do I scatter your order. You have kept the truth by ordination, passed from hand to hand in due succession; so does the new priesthood. Any among you who will receive its orders shall be received with honour, and shall keep his rank.

To those who will not, I make this promise under my seal. No temple shall be pulled down by royal command. No priest shall lose his house. Each elder of the wheel shall receive from the treasury a pension of [[cur:551|niesh]], paid at the new year as the libation was paid, for as long as he lives. I will not buy your consciences; I will only refuse to let you starve for them.

You taught me, lastly, that all things turn on a great wheel, and what ends will begin again. If that is true, then you will not grieve too bitterly at what has ended in me. And if it is not true, as I now hold, then each of us has one life only, and I would not have us spend it as enemies.

Pray for me in whatever manner you can. I shall pray for you.

Given at Vieneynga under the royal seal, in the year 488 of the Vieshethmekh.
