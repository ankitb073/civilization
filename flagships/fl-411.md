title: Lament for Nakatha, Father of the Seat of Nilu

*[Column I]*

Weep, O [[set:5|Nilu]], city of the reed-gate. Weep, O house of the seat.  
The king has gone down. The king has gone down into the dark.  
[[per:5|Nakatha]] has gone down, Nakatha the first, who set up the seat.  
Weep, O river. Weep, O barley. Weep, O ox in the stall.

[[dei:28|Khuthaze]], father of the sky, lower your face.  
[[dei:30|Pugazhu]], lady of the sun, cover your light with a cloth.  
[[dei:31|Kumbe]], lady of the moon, do not come up tonight.  
[[dei:29|Bumbe]] of the sea, let your waves be still, let them weep.  
[[dei:32|Nijinande]], lady of the spear, lay your spear down on the ground.  
The king who fed you has gone down.

*[Column II]*

Who raised the wall of Nilu high? Nakatha raised it.  
Who dug the canal of Nilu? Nakatha dug it.  
Who set the first brick of the house of the gods? Nakatha set it.  
Who gave the priests their bread, the gods their smoke? Nakatha gave it.  
Before him there was no seat. Before him the old men sat in the gate and quarrelled.  
He made the seat. He sat on the seat nineteen years.  
He sat on the seat, and the barley was tall. He sat on the seat, and the ewes bore twins.  
Now the seat is cold. Now the seat is cold.

His hair was white like the salt of the shore.  
His hand was heavy like the stone of the threshold.  
His voice was the voice of a bull at the gate.  
Now his hair is in the dust. Now his hand is still. Now his voice is not heard.  
Weep, O Nilu. Weep, O house of the seat.

*[Column III]*

The priests of [[mov:4|Khupande]] washed him with water.  
The priests of Khupande anointed him with oil.  
They put the bronze cup in his hand, the bronze knife at his side.  
They put bread for the road, beer for the road, a lamp for the road.  
They closed the door of the tomb. They sealed it with the seal of the king.  
All things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again.  
Let him begin again. Let him begin again in the house of the fathers.

Then the son stood up. Then [[per:31|Nakatha]] the son stood up.  
He took the staff of his father. He sat on the seat of his father.  
That was the [[evt:85|Accession of Nakatha II]]: the son in the place of the father.  
The people bowed. The priests poured. The gods were given their due.  
Weep no more, O Nilu; the seat is not empty.

*[Column IV]*

But the wheel turned again, and swiftly. Five years the son sat.  
The son also went down into the dark, to the house of his father.  
And in that same year his son was born, and was set upon the seat:  
[[per:40|Nakatha]], the child, the grandson of the builder, the third of the name.  
Twelve years he has now sat. He is small, but the seat is his.  
Grow, O Nakatha. Grow tall like the barley that your grandfather sowed.  
Grow heavy-handed like the stone of the threshold.

May [[pol:5|Nilu]] stand while the river runs.  
May the house of Nakatha stand while the sun goes up.  
Weep for the father. Bow to the son of the son.  
The lament is ended. Let the reed-pipe be laid down.

*[Colophon]*

Copied in the 12th year of Nakatha, from the old tablet of the house of the gods, by the hand of the singer.
