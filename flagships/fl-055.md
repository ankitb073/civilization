Thus says [[per:9442|Mimkhishiuf]], the great queen, queen of [[pol:312|Man'e]], lady of [[set:13|Tivintuha]], beloved of [[dei:20|Nadul]] of the fire and of [[dei:21|Dumin]] of the storm:

Hear the words of this tablet. Hear how the king of Tiy bowed his head, and how I gave him peace.

The kings of [[pol:362|Tiy]] were proud. They sent no gifts to my house. They sent no silver, no oxen, no oil. My messengers went to them and said, "Send gifts," and they did not send. Therefore in my 5th year I took up the spear. I went out against Tiy. So began the [[evt:9170|war of Man'e and Tiy]].

In my 8th year I came to [[set:76|Nimit]], their city. I took it. I carried off its silver and its oxen; this was the [[evt:9195|sack of Nimit]]. In my 11th year I came to Nimit again. Again I took it, and again I carried off its silver.

In my 10th year, at [[evt:9205|Lishkhu]], the river rose against my soldiers. [[dei:24|Navbudin]] of the river was angry, for the offering was not right; my soldiers were drowned in the mud and not slain by the sword. The men of Tiy did not conquer at Lishkhu. The river conquered. I made the offering right, and the river was quiet.

In my 12th year, at [[evt:9220|Ometh]], my soldiers fell upon the men of Tiy. The men of Tiy fell by the thousand, by the thousand they fell; their dead filled the valley from side to side. My general fell there also, my servant, my lion. I poured oil for him. I gave his house fields.

Then I sat down before Nimit a third time. I cut its water. I cut its grain. And [[per:9260|Mi'maf]], king of Tiy, sent his messenger to me.

Thus said the messenger of Mi'maf: "Thus says Mi'maf: The great queen is my mother; I am her son. Let the great queen draw back her spear from Nimit. What does my mother ask?"

I sent my own messenger back to him, and I put these words in his mouth: "Thus says Mimkhishiuf: Give me the land along the river, from the hills to the water. Give me tribute of silver every year, thirty years and three years. And for ten years let no spear be lifted between Man'e and Tiy."

My messenger went, and my messenger came back. Thus said my messenger: "Mi'maf says: The word of the great queen is heavy." And I answered: "Is it heavier than the dead of Ometh?"

Three times the messengers went, and three times they came. On the third time Mi'maf said: "Let it be as the great queen says."

Then we went into Nimit, he and I. Before Nadul of the fire, before Dumin of the storm, before [[dei:22|Binbe]] of the earth, before [[dei:23|Shiniyte]] of the sea, before Navbudin of the river, before [[dei:25|Nabikh]] of wisdom, before all the gods of [[mov:3|Diyithti]] who sit each in their place, we swore. I set my seal. Mi'maf set his seal. This is the [[evt:9222|treaty of Nimid]], sworn in [[set:76|Nimit]].

The land along the river is mine. The silver of Tiy will be weighed in [[cur:184|ush'e]] in my house in Tivintuha, year upon year. The men of Tiy will plough, and they will bring to me.

The wheel turns. What was proud is made low; what was low is lifted up. The gods saw the pride of Tiy, and the gods gave Tiy into my hand.

Whoever breaks the words of this tablet, whether he is a king of Tiy or a king of Man'e, may Dumin break his house with the storm. May Binbe shut her womb against his fields. May Nadul eat his name in the fire. May his sons beg at the gates of strangers.

Written in the 14th year of Mimkhishiuf.
