*[Obverse]*

Weep, village. Weep, village by the walls of [[set:68|Poyonga]].  
Weep, threshing floor. Weep, well with the old stone.  
We were the people of [[pol:11|Poyonga]]. We were the people of the lady.  
Now we are the people of [[pol:1|Tallin]]. Now we are the people of the tax.

Hear it, [[dei:17|Nidhikhti]], lady of the barley, who knew us before.  
Hear it, [[dei:19|Yenan]], moon, who counts the nights.  
Hear it, Vinba, earth, who holds our fathers.

The old men remember. The old men sit at the well and remember.  
They say: in the days of the lady [[per:268|Ladhilatu]], the barley was ours.  
They say: she went out against Tallin at Vuvu. Vuvu was bad.  
They say: then at the [[evt:279|Battle of Nin]] we broke them. The men of Tallin ran.  
At Nin we broke them. The old men say it. The old men say it every evening.  
After Nin the men of Tallin came again. They came again and again.  
Five years after Nin they came, and the land was theirs.

That was the [[evt:288|Conquest of Poyonga]].  
The king of Tallin, [[per:199|Panshedominin]], sat in Poyonga. Our lady sat before him.  
She set her seal on the tablet. She gave the land.  
Then they killed her. She gave the land, and they killed her.  
The old women do not say it loud. The old women say it with their hands over their mouths.

*[Reverse]*

Thirty-five years. Thirty-five harvests to Tallin.  
The men of the king come at the threshing. They count the sheaves.  
They count the sheaves of the field and the sheaves of the field's edge.  
They take the tenth. They take the tenth and the tenth of the tenth.  
They weigh the silver in [[cur:4|Niga]], their silver, on their scales.  
A measure of barley is a Niga and a little more, and the tax-men say: cheap.  
To the tax-men it is cheap. To the tax-men all things are cheap.

The young men go in the king's levy. The young men go out to the wars of Tallin.  
They went in the [[evt:323|First Diebeguvebenga Trade War]]. They went far.  
Some came back. Some did not come back. The ones who came back swear by Tallin.  
The children play at Tallin and Poyonga, and the children of Poyonga play Tallin.

The king is [[per:327|Tethtun]], in the 29th year of Tethtun.  
He was born in the year our lady died. He does not know her name.  
He sits in Tallin. He has not seen our well. He has not seen our threshing floor.  
May he live. We say: may he live. The tax-men are listening.

Weep, village. The barley is good this year. The barley is not ours.  
Weep, village. The well is full. The well is the king's well.  
Weep, threshing floor, for the sheaves that go away on the carts.  
Nidhikhti, lady of the barley, you knew us before.  
Know us still. Know us under the new name.  
Vulme, lady of war, you broke Tallin once at Nin.  
We do not ask you for that now. We ask only the barley. We ask only the rain.  
Weep, village. Then rise, and go to the field.
