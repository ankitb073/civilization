**Obverse**

[[dei:14|Vulme]], lady of the bronze spear, hear the singer!  
[[dei:16|Dumamnul]], father of the wide sky, hear the singer!  
Eva, flame of the altar, take the fat of the bull!  
In the 1st year of [[per:1804|Ngithnoth]] the queen, the singer lifts his voice.  
He sings [[evt:1417|the war of Yida and Egu]].  
He sings the silver that comes from Egu.

Egu was proud. Egu shut its hand.  
Egu would not carry the silver to [[set:0|Tallin]].  
Egu stood in the land of [[reg:8|Duvekopuvinga]] and would not bow its neck.  
The king who has gone to the gods, the father of Ngithnoth, raised his hand.  
He said: "Go out, Yida. Bring me the silver of Egu."  
[[pol:25|Yida]] went out. Vulme went before Yida.

Who goes before Yida? Vulme goes before Yida.  
Who falls before Yida? Egu falls before Yida.

At [[set:149|Punennushal]] [[per:1689|Shikhyin]] the general met [[per:1752|Tapubapudun]] of Egu.  
Man lay down beside man; the dead of Egu were as the dead of Yida.  
But the field was Yida's. Vulme gave the field to Yida.

At Bimunga the queen of Egu stood in the road.  
Yida drew back, as the ox draws back from the furrow to draw breath.  
The gods tested Yida. Yida did not break.

At Buneynga Shikhyin went forward again.  
[[per:1744|Vamun]] of Egu ran before him as the hare runs before the hound.  
The field was Yida's. Vulme gave the field to Yida.

At Tora the earth was thirsty.  
[[dei:15|Vinba]] the earth asked for blood, and Yida gave it.  
Who refuses Vinba her due? Not Yida.

At Tathrelbu Shikhyin went forward a third time.  
Tapubapudun turned his back. Tapubapudun counted his dead.  
The field was Yida's. Vulme gave the field to Yida.

Who goes before Yida? Vulme goes before Yida.  
Who falls before Yida? Egu falls before Yida.

**Reverse**

At [[set:24|Jevampiva]] [[per:1797|Nemun]] the general came.  
Vamun of Egu stood against him a second time.  
Nemun struck. Vamun fell. Vamun does not see the moon.  
Of Yida few lay down; of Egu three thousand lay down, and more.  
Now Jevampiva is the city of Yida. Its gate opens to Yida.

At Runiy [[per:1798|Ununshefin]] the general came.  
Tapubapudun of Egu stood against him and ran again.  
The field was Yida's. Vulme gave the field to Yida.

At [[set:75|Sheney]] [[per:1799|Magunjeyshan]] the general came.  
Tapubapudun of Egu stood against him for the last time.  
His shield was broken; his spear was broken; he lay down in the dust.  
The dogs of Sheney know his face.

Then Egu bowed its neck. Then Egu opened its hand.  
At Sheney the queen of Egu set her hand to the tablet.  
The king who has gone to the gods set his hand to the tablet.  
[[evt:1486|The oath of Sheney]] was sworn before Dumamnul and before Yenan of the night.  
Egu gave cities. Egu gave land.  
Egu will bring silver to Yida for forty years, year upon year.  
For ten years the spear sleeps between them.

[[pol:14|Egu]], bring the silver! Weigh it out in [[cur:19|Tunrun]]!  
Bring it to Tallin, to the house of the queen.  
Nidhikhti, fill the granaries of Yida!  
Eva, burn bright on the altar of Yida!  
Vulme, lady of the bronze spear, rest your spear in the house of Ngithnoth.

Who goes before Yida? Vulme goes before Yida.  
Who falls before Yida? Egu falls before Yida.  
Let the singer sing it in the first year of Ngithnoth.  
Let the singer's son sing it after him.
