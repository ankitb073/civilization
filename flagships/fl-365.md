**ROYAL MINT OF FUVU — OFFICE OF THE MINT MASTER**

**Nilu, 28 Shiènzhè 4844 of the Uthungala**

**To:** The Treasury Committee of the Chamber, and by their leave to the Council of His Majesty [[per:46388|Nashuzzhe]], King of [[pol:1988|Fuvu]]

**Subject:** Execution of the Ordinance reducing the silver standard of the [[cur:1130|Zhizi]]; technical particulars; observations

*Not for publication. Twelve copies, numbered.*

**1. The Ordinance.** I have the honour to report that the Ordinance of the present month has been received at the Mint and that its provisions are in course of execution. By its terms the Zhizi, which has contained since the reform of six years ago 5.259 grammes of fine silver, shall henceforth contain 3.874 grammes. The reduction amounts to 1.385 grammes in each coin, or a little above twenty-six parts in the hundred.

**2. The comparison the Committee will be asked for.** I set the figures down plainly so that no member is caught unprepared. Before the [[evt:47571|Debasement of the Zhizi]] of six years ago the coin held 7.7 grammes of fine silver. It now holds 3.874. A Zhizi struck under the new Ordinance therefore carries rather more than one half of the silver of a Zhizi as it was first issued, seventeen years ago. The newspapers of Nilu possess the same arithmetic; it is better that the Committee hear it first from the Mint.

**3. Causes, as stated in the preamble.** The preamble of the Ordinance cites the charges remaining from the campaigns at Khambe, at the [[evt:47439|Battle of Khambe]] of twenty-three years ago and at the [[evt:47555|Battle of Khambe]] of fourteen years ago, whose pensions, indemnities to families, and loans at interest have not diminished as was forecast. It cites also the costs of the field at Khadambe, where the arms of the Kingdom were victorious at the [[evt:47591|Battle of Khadambe]] but where the [[evt:47592|Treaty of Khadambe]] restored no territory and brought no indemnity, only a truce of twenty years. A victory that pays nothing must still be paid for. The Mint does not question these figures. It observes only that silver cannot be found in a preamble.

**4. Technical particulars.**

(a) The existing dies are to be retired by the end of the next month and defaced in the presence of two inspectors of the Treasury.

(b) Old Zhizi brought to the Mint will be received at their silver weight, not at their face, until the date of demonetisation to be fixed by the Committee.

(c) The steam presses of the Nilu works can deliver the new coinage at the rate required, provided that the coal allowance is restored to its former level. I have written separately on the coal.

**5. Prices.** The measure of grain stands at present at about 1.18 Zhizi in the markets of [[set:5|Nilu]]. The Committee should expect, on the experience of the last reduction, that the price of grain in the new coin will rise before the wages of those who buy it. I make no forecast of the figure; the last time, every forecast was too low.

**6. Observation.** Since the [[evt:47622|Fuvu Revolution]], the Chamber and not the Palace orders the standard of the coin. I hold this to be a just arrangement, and I wish it to be recorded that the Mint has executed the Ordinance faithfully. I wish it also to be recorded that the teachers of the [[mov:24|Uthungala]] ask of us justice in dealings and moderation in all things. A coin is a promise made by the Kingdom to the poorest person who handles it. With this [[evt:47661|Debasement of the Zhizi]] we have twice in six years made that promise smaller. I do not advise a third occasion. I am not certain the public would believe a fourth.

**7. Recommendation.** That the Committee publish, at the same hour as the Ordinance, the exact silver content of the new coin, so that rumour shall not set it lower than the truth.

I have the honour to remain the Committee's obedient servant,

*The Master of the Royal Mint*

*Enclosures: (i) assay of trial pieces; (ii) schedule of retired dies; (iii) note on the coal allowance.*
