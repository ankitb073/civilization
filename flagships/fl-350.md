*[Obverse.]*

To the queen of [[pol:6|Ana]], say: thus says [[per:1050|Esiesh Foyatl]], queen of [[pol:29|Mieshlothie]], the lady of the wall, whom [[dei:0|Atlie]] set up when the old house fell.

With me it is well. I say it to you first, so that you hear it: with me it is well. You said in your heart, "The queen of Mieshlothie is under the ashes." I am not under the ashes. I stand on my feet. My hand writes this clay.

Hear what your men did, and hear it twice.

At [[evt:777|Sase]] your spears met my spears. I do not hide it: the field was yours. My men came back to me with their shields on their backs. Atlie turned his face. [[dei:5|Nieshashta]] of the spear walked with you that day. So be it; the field is the goddess's to give.

But then your men came to [[set:2|Mieshlothie]]. They came to the wall of my city, the wall that my workmen raised again in the first year of my sitting, in the year of [[evt:751|the collapse]], when the old house fell and every lord took his own road. I raised the wall. I gathered the scattered. I gave the gods their due again. And your men came, and they broke it.

They came in at the gate at night. They carried fire. They went into the house of [[dei:1|Uelti]], where the tablets are kept in order, and they threw down the tablets. They went into the house of [[dei:3|Niele'i]] by the river-gate, and they carried out the silver bowls of the river. They went into the storerooms; they took the barley, they took the oil, they took the wool. They went into the houses of the people; they took the cooking-pots, they took the daughters.

This is [[evt:778|the third sack of Myishlithie]]. Three times now the city has been broken. Three times the wheel has crushed it. Do you think the scribes will not write it? The scribes write it. The scribes of Mieshlothie, those who live, are writing it now.

*[Reverse.]*

Where was I? I will tell you, so that you know the gods kept me. I went out by the water-gate with my women and the priests of [[dei:4|Lashla'e]], carrying the lamp of the moon in a pot, so that the lamp should not go out. We stood in the reeds to our knees. We saw the roofs burn. We heard the shouting of your men and the crying of mine. The lamp did not go out. Lashla'e did not let it go out.

Now hear what I ask, and do it if you fear the gods.

You and I give the bull to the same Atlie. You and I pour the beer to the same Niele'i. You and I learn from the same priests of [[mov:0|La'im]], who say: all things turn on the great wheel; what ends will begin again. Then hear the priests. Give back the silver bowls of Niele'i. Give back the tablets of Uelti that your men carried off. Give back the priests' children that your men tied with ropes. Take the barley if you must; barley grows again. Do not take the gods' things. The gods remember.

If you do not give them back, then [[dei:2|Muevas'e]] of the earth will remember it when your own harvest is cut. Then Atlie of the sky will remember it when your own city stands under the clouds. I do not curse you. I only say what the priests say.

The war is not ended. My spearmen are gathering in the villages. My granaries in the hills are not empty. Mieshlothie is not under the ashes, as I am not under the ashes.

Send me an answer by this runner. If he does not return, I will know your answer.

*[Left edge.]*

> Written in the 6th year of Esiesh Foyatl, queen of Mieshlothie, in the camp by the water.
