*[Obverse. Letter tablet, found still in its clay envelope; the envelope bears a royal seal, partly rubbed.]*

To the overseer of the storehouses of the river country, my servant, say:

Thus says [[per:789|Dolulrinin]], king of [[pol:12|Chinontonu]], your lord.

I am alive. The king is alive. Let your heart not fall down. Let the heart of the river country not fall down. May [[dei:55|Dumamnul]] of the high sky keep you, may [[dei:56|Nabuthin]] of the river keep your fields, may [[dei:59|Winba]] of the earth keep your house and your sons.

Hear what was done to the house of your lord.

The men of [[pol:25|Yida]] came out against me. Yida, who took the seven districts in the days when I was a boy; Yida, who took Dininge from us by the oath; Yida was not satisfied. They came again with a great host, as many as the locusts, as many as the barley-ears. Four of theirs for one of mine.

I went out against them. I, the king, went out at the head of my men. At the [[evt:662|battle of Dininge]] I stood against them. Hear me, and let the scribes of the river country hear: more of theirs lay down than of mine. My spearmen killed them in the ditch, my spearmen killed them on the slope. But they were many, and the field was theirs. I went back to my city with those who lived.

Then the men of Yida came to [[set:6|Chinontonu]], the city of my fathers. They came to the wall. They came over the wall.

They went into the house of [[dei:58|Chithal]]. They took the bronze spear of the god from its stand.  
They went into the house of [[dei:57|Bubin]]. They took the silver of the moon from its box.  
They went into my storehouse. They took the [[cur:8|yong]], the weighed silver, the rings and the cups.  
They went into my granary. They took the barley; what they could not carry they burned.  
They went into the streets. What they did in the streets I will not write on this tablet.

I was in the inner house with my guard. The gods hid me. The men of Yida did not find the king. When they had filled their carts, they went out of the city, and the dust of their carts was on the road three days.

Now hear what you must do.

Send barley, a hundred loads of barley, on the river, on the boats. The city has no bread.  
Send oxen, the oxen of the king's fields, every yoke of them. The ploughs stand still.  
Send oil for the lamps of the gods. The lamps of the gods are dark.  
Send the men of the levy, every man who can carry a basket of earth. The wall must stand up again before the rains.  
Do not keep back one load. Do not keep back one yoke. If you keep back, the king will know it.

And say to the priests of [[mov:8|Ligay]] in the river country: let them make the offering for the city. Let them give a white bull to Dumamnul, let them pour beer to Nabuthin, let them pour oil to [[dei:60|Shineyta]] of the sea. All things turn on the wheel; what ends will begin again. The house of my fathers has stood since the days of the first kings. It will not fall in my days.

Do not believe the words of the traders who come up from the city. They say the king is dead. The king is not dead. They say the city is empty. The city is not empty. They say the gods have turned from Chinontonu. The gods have not turned; I am here.

*[Reverse, lower edge.]*

> Sent in the 27th year of Dolulrinin, in the month of the reaping, by the hand of a runner. Whoever breaks this tablet before the overseer reads it, may Chithal break his arm.
