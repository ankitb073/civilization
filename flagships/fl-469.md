In the name of the gods who share the world, each in their place and each owed their due, and under the turning of the great wheel, by which what is ended begins again:

We, the lords spiritual of the [[mov:38|Zhivinjis]], the lords temporal, the knights, the burgesses of the towns and the free commons of [[pol:1547|Khunilili]], assembled in the hall of [[set:306|Ambudhi]] in the year 4 from the founding of Ambudhi, make known to all princes, prelates and peoples, present and to come, the things that follow.

**I. Of the oath, and to whom it was owed.** Our fathers held their lands in the country of [[reg:20|Khishkhis]] of the lords of [[pol:1511|Nzhinduf]], by homage and by fealty, and rendered them service of the sword and of the purse as the custom of the land required. This we do not deny. A vassal who denies his lord's due is a thief before the gods. But fealty is owed to the lawful lord and to his lawful heir, and not to whoever sits in his chair. It is written that the oath binds the hand that gives it to the hand that receives it, and to no other hand.

**II. Of the usurper.** Eight years since, [[per:35971|Nadadhjuth]] seized the seat of Nzhinduf by force, against the order of succession that the priests keep and pass down. To him no lord of Khunilili ever knelt, no knight ever swore, and no town ever opened its gate in homage. Yet he demanded of us the service due to the lawful lord; he sent his bailiffs into our valleys; he took the tithe of the temples for his soldiers; he weighed our grain with false weights, contrary to the justice in dealings that the gods require; and when our elders petitioned him, he answered with the rope.

**III. Of the rising.** Therefore, in the year of the founding, the lords and commons of Khunilili rose under [[per:36005|Nashüli]], whom we acclaimed our king at Ambudhi before the altars. This was the [[evt:36483|Revolt of Khunilili]], and from it came the [[evt:36484|Khunilili War of Independence]], which we did not seek and which we did not refuse.

**IV. Of the judgment of arms.** The gods have since judged between us, three times, in the field.

At the [[evt:36525|First Battle of Ambudhi]] the king went out against the usurper beneath the walls of Ambudhi and drove him from the field with heavy loss.

At the [[evt:36528|Battle of Nzhinduh]] the lord [[per:36040|Nabuli]] carried the war to the gates of [[set:227|Nzhinduf]] itself, and broke the usurper's army before them, though the ground cost us dear; and our host sat down before the city in the [[evt:36529|Ninth Siege of Nzhinduh]], as so many hosts before ours have sat.

At the [[evt:36533|Battle of Nzhinduh]] in this present year our host met the usurper a third time, the king at its head, and a third time prevailed. The city was entered. In the [[evt:36534|Sack of Nzhinduh]] its archives burned, and with them, we hold, the rolls of every false service the usurper had written against our names. We do not rejoice at fire; but we do not weep for those rolls.

**V. Of the peace.** At Nzhinduf, before the priests of both peoples, the usurper and our king [[per:36005|Nashüli]] set their seals to the [[evt:36535|Treaty of Nzhinduh]], by which the lordship of Nzhinduf over Khunilili is ended forever, and a truce of fifteen years is sworn between the two crowns.

**VI. The declaration.** Wherefore we declare: that Khunilili is a free kingdom, holding of no lord on earth, but only of the gods; that Nashüli is our king and his heirs after him, according to the law of succession that the priests keep; that every oath sworn by any of us to the usurper under force is void; and that whoever shall hereafter attempt to bring Khunilili again under the yoke of Nzhinduf shall be held a breaker of oaths before gods and men.

To this we set our seals, in the hall of Ambudhi, the priests first, the lords after, the towns after them, and the commons by their elected men; and we pray the gods who keep the great wheel to hold it steady upon this day.
