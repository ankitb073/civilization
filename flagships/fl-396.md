*[Obverse. The refrain is written in full only at the first and last; elsewhere a sign of repetition stands for it.]*

We are the [[cul:1|Yakkamu]], the people of the long road.  
We are the Yakkamu, who carried our fathers' bones.  
Hear us, [[dei:0|Katsuya]], lord of the high sky.  
Hear us, [[dei:2|Muivathka]], mother of the black earth.

*Refrain:* We walked, we walked; the road was long; the gods walked with us.

In [[reg:14|Yimola]] the barley stood up green,  
in Yimola the barley lay down brown.  
The rain did not come. The rain did not come.  
The ox died in the furrow. The child died at the breast.  
That was the [[evt:11774|Famine in Yimla]], the hunger of the dry years;  
of every eight that ate at the hearth, one ate no more.

We walked, we walked; the road was long; the gods walked with us.

We took up the hearth-stones. We took up the grinding-stones.  
We took up the bones of the fathers in baskets of reed.  
We put the old women on the donkeys. We put the children on our backs.  
We said to [[dei:3|Nyiliki]] of the river: show us water.  
We said to [[dei:4|Lashulaka]] of the moon: show us the road at night.  
Nyiliki showed us water. Lashulaka showed us the road.

We walked, we walked; the road was long; the gods walked with us.

Three hundred thousand we were, and more,  
as many as the reeds, as many as the locusts,  
but we did not eat the fields of others; we asked.  
We stood at the border of [[pol:494|Mikhuthitsu]] with our hands open.  
We said: we are not spears, we are mouths.  
We said: give us land, and we will give you barley.

We walked, we walked; the road was long; the gods walked with us.

Then the queen heard us, [[per:11995|Pedyuyalath]] heard us,  
the queen who sits in [[set:47|Lininade]], the mother of the land.  
The spears of Mikhuthitsu had gone down into [[reg:1|Yithamashudi]] before us,  
in the [[evt:11857|Holy War of Mikhuthitsu against Le'im]] they had gone down,  
the field of Shithiy was theirs, the [[evt:11884|Battle of Shithiy]] was theirs.  
She said: Yithamashudi is wide. Let the Yakkamu sit down in it.  
She said: let them pay the due in barley and in [[cur:257|Pimku]].  
She said: let them pour to the gods of the land.

We walked, we walked; the road was long; the gods walked with us.

Seventeen summers we came down, band after band,  
the first band in the year after her crowning, the last band last summer.  
The last band came in at harvest; we went out to meet them with beer.  
We counted them. We wept. We counted the empty places.  
Now the bones of the fathers lie in the earth of Yithamashudi.  
Now the hearth-stones are set down. Now the grinding-stones turn.

O [[dei:5|Nyishashuda]], lady of the spear, we ask nothing of you.  
Turn your face to the queen's enemies; turn your face away from us.  
O [[dei:1|Kuiludi]], keeper of wisdom, teach our sons the words of this land.  
O Katsuya, O Muivathka, we pour to you at the new stones,  
each god in their place, each god with their due, as the priests of [[mov:0|Lakimu]] teach.

All things turn on the wheel; what ends will begin again.  
The road ended. The field begins.

*We walked, we walked; the road was long; the gods walked with us.*

*[Lower edge.]*

> Sung at the setting of the hearth-stones, in the 19th year of Pedyuyalath.
