*[Obverse]*

To my mother say: thus says your son, the apprentice of the potter in [[set:92|Tambakh]].  
May [[dei:77|Pugazhu]], the sun, keep you. May [[dei:74|Binge]], lady of the barley, fill your jar.  
May the house be well. May my sisters be well. May the goat be well.

Mother, I am well. I am well. Do not weep for me.  
The tablet you sent by the salt-carrier came to me. The scribe at the gate read it to me.  
Now I send this one by the same carrier. He is an honest man. Give him beer.

Mother, you ask: is the town as it was? It is not as it was.  
You remember when I was small, when the [[evt:1514|Shaking Sickness]] began in Tambakh.  
You remember how you took me and my sisters out to my uncle's village.  
You remember how my father stayed for the kiln, and how the kiln went cold.  
Now I am back in Tambakh. Now I walk in the lanes where the sickness was.  
Many doors are shut. Many doors have no one behind them.  
In the lane of the potters there were eleven kilns. Now there are four kilns.  
My master says: the sickness went out of Tambakh along the roads.  
It went to [[set:9|Tagath]], to [[set:34|Tatunde]], to [[set:141|Kitsunambi]], to all the great towns.  
The men of the caravans carried it in their mouths.  
Then it went away. It went away two years ago. The old women say it is sleeping.  
I do not say it is sleeping. I say it is gone.

*[Reverse]*

Mother, hear my news. It is good news.  
My master is the potter by the east gate, the one with the cut hand.  
His sons lay down in the sickness. His sons did not rise.  
He has no son at the wheel. He has me at the wheel.  
He says: you are slow, but you are here. That is better than fast and in the ground.  
He gives me bread and onions. He gives me beer on the feast of Binge.  
He gives me a sleeping place by the kiln, where it is warm.  
Hands are few in Tambakh. A man who can carry clay is paid like a man who can shape it.  
My master says: in the year before the sickness he paid a boy half of what he pays me.

A measure of barley in the market is not quite one [[cur:28|Ndül]].  
I have saved two Ndül and a piece. I keep them in the wall behind the kiln.  
At the end of the year I will bring them to you. Buy the second goat.  
Do not give them to my uncle. My uncle has enough.

Mother, I made a jar with my own hands. It did not crack in the kiln.  
My master turned it and looked and said: Binge will drink from that.  
I took it to the shrine of [[dei:76|Tsilmu]], lord of wisdom, and set it there for my father.  
I did not see my father put in the ground. I set the jar for him.

Now [[per:1876|Namyivadu]] is king, in the fifth year of his kingship.  
The heralds say: the roads are open, the caravans come.  
The caravans come. The barley comes. The town fills up again a little.  
New people come from the villages, as I came. They take the empty houses.

Mother, do not fear for me. Pugazhu rises over Tambakh every morning, as over the village.  
Kiss my sisters. Feed the goat. Pray to Binge for the rain.  
I will come at the harvest, if my master allows.  
Thus says your son. The tablet is finished.
