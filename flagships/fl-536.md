*[Spoken by the king before the gate of Kavu, on the day of the swearing, and pressed into the clay by the scribes of Tagath.]*

Hear me, [[dei:77|Pugazhu]] of the sun, who sees the oath!  
Hear me, [[dei:73|Tithu]] of the storm, who breaks the oath-breaker!  
Hear me, [[dei:76|Tsilmu]] of wisdom, [[dei:74|Binge]] of the grain, Bumbi of the sea!  
Hear me, men of [[pol:237|Tagath]]! Hear me, men of [[pol:335|Kavu]]! Hear me, men of [[pol:334|Nilu]], who came to help Kavu and found no help there!

I am [[per:8675|Nafumbumbi]], king of Tagath, son of a king, king from my mother's lap. Twenty-seven years I have sat in the seat of [[set:9|Tagath]]. In none of them did Tagath bow its neck. In this one, Kavu bows its neck.

Three years ago I said to Kavu: "Give the tribute that is owed." Kavu said: "We owe nothing." So began the [[evt:8556|Third Tagath–Kavu War]]. Three times now the spears of Tagath have come against Kavu. Three times Kavu has learned.

Hear how it went.

Last year my general went up against the city, and [[per:8502|Nanitsi]], king of Kavu, came out against him with his spears. The men of Kavu fell in the ditches like barley under the sickle. That was the [[evt:8590|Battle of Kavu]]. Then the gate of Kavu was opened, and the storehouses of Kavu were carried away to Tagath.

This year I went out myself. At Khanindu the spears of Kavu stood against me. I went into them. I broke them. Their dead lay one on another until the crows were tired. That was the [[evt:8597|Battle of Khanindu]]. Then the gate of Kavu was opened a second time. That was the [[evt:8598|Sack of Kavu]]. Look at the gate, men of Kavu. It is black. Who made it black? Your own pride made it black.

Now hear the oath. Hear it, gods. Hear it, Nanitsi.

The land of the border towns, from the river to the hills, it is Tagath's. Kavu does not send its governors there. Kavu does not take barley there.

Every year at the harvest Kavu sends tribute to Tagath, silver weighed by the scribes of Tagath. Not one year, not ten years, but thirty-six years. Let the sons of Kavu learn to count to thirty-six.

The merchants of Tagath go into the market of Kavu. No toll is taken from them that is not taken from the men of Kavu.

For ten years no spear of Kavu is lifted against Tagath. For ten years no spear of Nilu is lifted against Tagath. If a spear is lifted, let Tithu break the arm that lifts it.

This is the [[evt:8599|Treaty of Kavu]]. I put my hand to the clay. Nanitsi puts his hand to the clay. The men of Nilu stand by and see it.

Do not weep, men of Kavu. I am not a wolf. I did not burn the houses of your gods. I did not take your king's head; he stands here beside me, and he will go back into his own hall tonight. The teachers say no man owns the truth, and I do not say I own it. But I own the field of Khanindu, and I own the gate of Kavu, and the gods have shown whose side they are on. Prosperity is the sign of their favour. Look at Tagath. Look at Kavu. Judge.

The wheel turns. What ends will begin again. Today the war ends. Today the tribute begins.

Pour the oil, priests of [[mov:11|Kyif]]. Pour it to each god in the god's own place. Bring the ox. Let the people of Tagath and Kavu eat the ox together, and let every man who eats it remember the oath.

Pugazhu sees. Tithu hears. It is sworn.
