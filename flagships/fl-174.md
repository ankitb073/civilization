Sing, O singers of the wine-house, sing of [[per:16110|Natlatl]] the king,  
in the thirty-ninth year of his sitting, the year of his widest belt.  
Bow down, bow down, you people of [[pol:649|Eshueethie]]:  
the king has won another battle, and he did not get his sandals dusty.

Hear of [[evt:16358|Thimisitsiy]], the first of the great deeds we sing.  
Twenty thousand spears went out against a village of farmers;  
thirteen hundred farmers stood behind a ditch with their hoes.  
Three thousand and more of our sons lay down in that ditch for ever,  
and two hundred farmers lay down beside them. A victory!  
The heralds cried it in the market. The widows did not cry it.  
Bow down, bow down: the king was at dinner that day.

Then the soldiers went into Itlyitlinuise and carried off its pots.  
That was [[evt:16359|the second sack of Itlyitlinuise]], for the first had left some pots.  
The city swore to pay tribute; the city swore to bow;  
so was [[evt:16360|the oath of Itlyitlinuise]] written in the king's great book,  
and the tribute came in moa by moa to the treasury in [[set:3|Eshueethie]],  
and went out again moa by [[cur:343|moa]] to the cooks of the palace.

Hear of [[evt:16400|the war against Nivanga]], the second of his great deeds.  
At [[evt:16427|Nivanga]] twenty-three thousand marched on fewer than two thousand.  
At [[evt:16431|Nivanga]] the next year thirty-five thousand marched on the same.  
The men of Nivanga were stubborn as goats; they made us pay for every wall.  
Then came [[evt:16432|the siege of Nivanga]], and the walls were starved, not stormed,  
and [[evt:16433|the oath of Nivanga]] was sworn, and three districts were given,  
three districts of thistles, and tribute for thirty-five years.  
Bow down, bow down: the king has three more districts of thistles.

Now hear of the third of his great deeds, the newest and the finest.  
Now the king has taken up [[evt:16510|the war against Veni]].  
At [[evt:16523|Hiviyu]] our host went out, nineteen thousand and more,  
against thirty-two men of Veni. Thirty-two! Count them on your fingers.  
Count them on your fingers and toes, and on the fingers of your wife.  
And when the day was over, the thirty-two were beaten,  
and nine of them were dead; and three thousand of ours were dead.  
The priests read the liver and said: an omen of great glory.  
I read the liver and said: an omen of a bad general.

Bow down, bow down. The king did not see Hiviyu.  
The king did not see Nivanga, nor Thimisitsiy, nor the ditch.  
The king saw his dinner. The king saw his tribute.  
The king saw his son Natlatl grow fat beside him at the table,  
and said: "This one shall be king after me, and win as I have won."  
May the gods forbid that he win as his father won.

The priests of [[mov:25|Mietseyeshio]] teach us moderation in all things;  
they teach that the wise man withdraws from wealth and appetite.  
Who has told the king? Let someone tell the king.  
Let someone tell him gently, after dinner, when he is sleepy.

O gods of the old houses, each in your place and with your due,  
we do not ask you to strike the king; he is our king.  
We ask you only this: count the dead as carefully as he counts the tribute.  
Count the sons of Eshueethie in the ditch at Thimisitsiy,  
in the mud before Nivanga, in the field of Hiviyu.  
Count them, and set the number beside the number of the thirty-two.

Bow down, bow down, you people of Eshueethie.  
Sing it softly in the wine-house, and do not sing it near the palace.  
Written in the thirty-ninth year of [[per:16110|Natlatl]], by one who has not yet been caught.
