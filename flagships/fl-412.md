title: Hymn for the Child-King Nakatha, Crowned in His Mother's Arms

*[Obverse. For the singers at the gate of the high house; the responses are marked "All".]*

O [[dei:28|Khuthaze]], father of the wide sky, look down!  
O [[dei:30|Pugazhu]], lady of the sun, look down!  
Look down on [[set:5|Nilu]], the city of the harbour, the young city,  
the city that our fathers built, twenty-four summers built, on the shore of the sea.

*All:* Look down, look down; the king is in the house.

The old king has gone to the gods.  
[[per:31|Nakatha]] has gone to the gods, Nakatha the father, Nakatha the builder,  
he who set the stones of the harbour, he who set the stones of the gate.  
He lay down in the month of the barley; he did not rise.  
The women cut their hair for him. The oxen did not go out to the field.  
Weep for him, O Nilu; weep, and then be still.

*All:* Look down, look down; the king is in the house.

For the wheel turns. What ends will begin again.  
The father lay down, and the son rose up.  
The son of Nakatha is Nakatha. The name goes on, the house goes on.  
[[per:40|Nakatha]], the little one, the new-born, the king!  
He came out of his mother in the year of his father's going;  
the gods took one Nakatha and gave one Nakatha.  
Who has seen such a thing? The gods have done it.

*All:* Look down, look down; the king is in the house.

He is small, but the throne is not too large for him.  
He is small, but the sky is above him.  
His hand cannot hold the spear; [[dei:32|Nijinande]] holds it for him.  
His mouth cannot speak the law; his mother speaks it for him.

O [[per:36|Nakavgi]], wife of the king who has gone, mother of the king who is,  
you sit at the right of the throne, you hold the king on your knees.  
The seal of the house is on your wrist; the word of the house is in your mouth.  
Speak, O Nakavgi, until the child can speak.  
Judge, O Nakavgi, until the child can judge.  
Let the elders bow to you. Let the captains bow to you.  
Let the merchants of the harbour bow to you, and bring their tithe.

*All:* Look down, look down; the king is in the house.

Now pour the oil on the head of the king; a little oil, for the head is small.  
Now hang the gold at the neck of the king; a little gold, for the neck is small.  
Now lift the king before the people, his mother lifts him, Nakavgi lifts him:  
behold Nakatha, the third of the name, behold him, O Nilu!

O [[dei:29|Bumbe]] of the sea, let the boats come in to his harbour.  
O [[dei:31|Kumbe]] of the moon, let her watch over his cradle at night.  
O Nijinande, lady of the spear, keep the enemy far from his walls.  
O Khuthaze, O Pugazhu, give him long days, many days,  
days as many as the stones of the harbour, as the shells on the shore.

*All:* Look down, look down; the king is in the house.

Priests of [[mov:4|Khupande]], keepers of the order, kill the white bull!  
Each god in their place, each god with their due, as the fathers taught.  
Young men, beat the drum. Young women, lift the song.  
The king is a child, and Nilu is a child; they will grow together.

*All:* Look down, look down; the king is in the house.

*[Reverse.]*

> Sung at the gate of the high house of Nilu, in the 1st year of Nakatha.
