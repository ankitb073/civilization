*In the 6th year of [[per:14886|Nangatsu]], king of [[pol:630|Ndulamlule]].*

**The first day of the month of planting.** The fifth year without rain. Five years [[dei:48|Kudze]] of the sky has shut his door. Five years the furrow is dust. I planted the millet. I planted the millet in the dust, a handful, and covered it, and said the words to [[dei:51|Talu]] of the earth. Talu, mother, keep it. Talu, mother, do not eat it.

**The third day.** I went to the well. The rope is long now, longer than my arms can count. The bucket comes up half mud. My son held the rope with me. He is nine years old. He is small like a boy of six.

**The seventh day.** The men say the hunger is finished. The priests in the town said: the [[evt:14939|famine in Daadüdhih]] is finished, give thanks. Is it finished? Grain is four [[cur:334|Guvu]] and a quarter the measure. In the year my son was born it was one Guvu and a little more. Who has four Guvu? The headman has four Guvu.

**The ninth day.** I counted the houses of our village. Twenty-one houses. Three are empty. In the house of the potter all are under the earth. In the house of my husband's sister, the husband and two children. The people of the house by the threshing floor went away towards the river towns and did not send word. [[dei:49|Tuvi]] of the underworld, you have eaten well. Tuvi, you have eaten enough.

**The twelfth day.** A woman came along the road from the east with a goat and no children. She said in [[reg:21|Tadudüvi]] all the hill country is the same. She said the old men there say the trouble began with the [[evt:14832|battle of Shyih]], when the boats stopped coming up the river with salt. I do not know. I gave her water. She gave me the goat's milk, a little bowl. We sat in the shade of the wall until the sun went down.

**The fifteenth day.** The king's men came to count the granary. The king's granary in the town of [[set:86|Ndulamlule]] gives one bowl a day to the widows, they say. My husband walked there and came back with nothing. The line was long like a river, he said, and the river did not move. He says the king does not see the villages. He says it quietly, by the fire.

**The twentieth day.** The headman's wife sent a basket of dates for the children. She sent it at night, so the others would not see. I will not forget it. I will not forget it when the rain comes.

**The twenty-fourth day.** A cloud. One cloud over the hills, white like a goat. All the village stood in the road and looked. It went away. [[dei:53|Bine]] of the fire, you are strong this year. Bine, go and sleep. We have no wood to feed you and no meat to burn for you.

**The last day of the month.** The millet is up, three fingers high. The leaves are curling. I carry water every morning, and my son carries the small jar. They say the [[cli:90|Great Drought]] will break when the king makes the great sacrifice. They say it will break next year. They said that last year. They said it the year before.

Kudze, father, open the door.  
Talu, mother, keep the seed.  
Tuvi, you have eaten enough.
