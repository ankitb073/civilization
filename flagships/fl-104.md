[[per:28045|Wemaliean]], servant of the One Power and teacher of the congregation at [[set:260|Ove]], to the overseers and ordained elders of the [[mov:58|Veshethmekhi]] in [[pol:1213|Movosh]], and to every believer who still listens to them: peace to whoever loves the truth more than the chair he sits in.

You have written to the congregations that I am a breaker of order and a sower of division. You have read your letters aloud in the houses of prayer at [[set:14|Movosh]] and forbidden the faithful to give me bread. Very well. A charge made in public deserves a public answer. I make that answer here, so that the faithful may judge between us. For although we both hold that truth is kept in an ordained order, no order was ever ordained to keep a falsehood.

On many things you and I are agreed. There is one Power over all things, and the old gods are its servants or its shadows. The good life is the life of mercy and of release from suffering. The word is for every people and must be carried to every people. I have not departed from any of this. I have ordained no one except by the laying-on of hands, in due order, as I was ordained myself.

We are divided on one point only. On that one point you are wrong, and the error does great harm. You teach that time is a road which runs once from the making of the world to a single judgement at its end, and never comes back. Look up from your tablets, overseers. Look at the year, which dies in winter and is born in spring. Look at the moon, which is eaten away and grows full again. Look at the grain, which is buried and rises. Everything the One has made speaks of the wheel. What ends begins again. Judgement does not wait at the end of a road. It comes in every turn of the wheel, to every soul, again and again, until mercy has released it.

Consider what your doctrine does to the believer. If there is only one judgement, and it is far off, a man postpones his mercy, as a debtor postpones a distant debt. If judgement comes in every turn, then today's cruelty is answered today, and mercy cannot wait. Your teaching makes men careless. Ours makes them watchful.

Even the war teaches the wheel. In the second year the army of Movosh won at the [[evt:28340|Battle of Listlitl]], and in the third year it won there again. This year, on that same field, it was thrown back by a small force. The proud cry out against fortune. The wise see the wheel turning, and pray.

Therefore hear the sentence of the congregation at Ove:

> The overseers who teach that time runs once and does not return, and who forbid the faithful to see the wheel the One has set turning, have cut themselves off from the communion of the faithful at Ove. We do not share bread with them in the houses of prayer, and we do not receive their ordinations, until they confess the wheel. Let any believer who wishes to stand with the truth come to Ove and be received.

Hear also what this sentence does not say. It does not touch the bodies or the goods of those it names. Anyone who strikes an overseer, or takes so much as a single [[cur:656|Khove]] from his house, strikes the teaching of mercy and is no member of ours. We want no favour from the magistrates and no sword from the crown. We pray for the young king [[per:27959|Oe Tluee]] and for those who govern in his name. The priests of the [[mov:25|Mieteyeshio]] keep the king's altars, and we do not quarrel with them. Our quarrel is with those who share our faith and have misread it.

Our opponents have already given this a name, [[evt:28458|the Totheyi Schism]], as if we had torn something whole. We have torn nothing. We have only refused to be carried further from the truth. The congregation that holds to the wheel is called the [[mov:108|Totheyi]], and it keeps its doors open to all.

May the One turn your hearts as it turns the year.

Given at Ove, in the year 2590 of the Mieteyeshio.
