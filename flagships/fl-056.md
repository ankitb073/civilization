Hear me, men of [[set:17|Peme]]! Hear me, priests of the house of [[mov:2|Jenro]]! Hear me, envoys of [[pol:343|Ego]], who stand before my throne with dust on your heads!

I am [[per:9678|Voltalen]], the great king, king of [[pol:310|Peme]], king of the four banks of the river, beloved of [[dei:14|Volme]], beloved of [[dei:15|Venba]], whom [[dei:16|Domamnol]] set on the throne. Forty-five years I have sat on the throne of my fathers. Forty-five years the gods have not turned their face from me.

Hear what Ego did. Ego withheld its gifts. Ego shut its gates and did not send its silver. Ego said: "Who is the king of Peme, that we should bring him gifts?" So I raised my hand against Ego. The gods saw it. The gods went before me.

My army went down to Ushdhnge. Twenty thousand spears went down. The men of Ego came out with not three thousand. They were scattered like chaff on the threshing floor. [[per:9980|Jevekote]], the lady of Ego, fled back behind her walls. That was the [[evt:9839|Battle of Ushdhnge]].

My army went down to Ego. Again they came out. Again they were scattered. We broke the gate of Ego. We went in. That was the [[evt:9852|Fourth Sack of Igu]]. Three times strangers broke that city in the days of old; the fourth time the hand of Peme broke it.

Ego did not learn. Ego shut its gates again. Then I, Voltalen, the king, went down myself. I went before my army with the spear of Volme in my hand. At the gates of Ego I struck them. I shut them in. I sat down before the city. Their bread failed. Their water failed. Then Jevekote sent her men to me with dust on their heads. They said: "Let the king of Peme take what he will, but let him leave us our lives."

So the peace was made at Ego, in my forty-third year, and I set my seal on it, and Jevekote set her seal on it. Today, in the house of the gods at Peme, the tablet of the peace is set before the gods, and the oath is sworn again before all the people.

These are the words of the [[evt:9864|Treaty of Igu]]:

> The fields on the border, which Ego held, are the fields of Peme.  
> Each year Ego shall bring twelve talents of silver to the palace of Peme, for thirty-six years.  
> Ego is the servant of Peme. When the king of Peme goes to war, Ego shall go with him.  
> The merchants of Peme shall go into Ego and come out again, and no one shall shut the road before them.  
> For ten years no spear shall be lifted between Peme and Ego.

Envoys of Ego, put your hands on the tablet. Swear by the gods of Peme, and swear by your own gods.

Whoever breaks this oath, may Volme break his spear in his hand. May Venba open her mouth and swallow his house. May Domamnol shut up the rain. May [[dei:17|Nedhekhte]] blight his grain in the ear. May [[dei:18|Eva]] burn his storehouse and his roof. May [[dei:19|Yenan]] hide his face from him by night, so that he walks in darkness until his death.

Whoever keeps this oath, may Nedhekhte fill his granary. May Eva warm his hearth. May his sons sit after him.

Men of Peme, the war is ended. Your king has given you the fields of Ego and the silver of Ego. Bring a ram for Volme. Bring a ewe for Venba. Bring barley and oil for Nedhekhte. Let no one go hungry at this feast. The grain is in the storehouse, and the [[cur:166|Rengel]] is in the treasury.

The gods went before me. The gods brought Ego to my feet. Let it be written on the tablet. Let the tablet be set up in the house of the gods. Let whoever reads it in later days know that Voltalen was king.

Written in the 45th year of Voltalen.
