*To be said in all the temples of [[pol:1497|Ölie]] on the day the gates were first shut, by order of the priesthood, in the seventeenth year of our lord [[per:37767|Ie Shimin]]. The priest says the versicle; the people answer.*

O you many gods who share the world, each in your place and each with your due,  
you who keep the sky and the deep and the threshold of the house:  
hear the people of Ölie, who come before you with ash on their heads.  

For seven years the [[evt:38362|Blue Ague]] walked among us.  
It came in at no gate; it was found at the market and in the bed.  
It turned the lips blue and the breath short and the hands cold,  
and it took the father from the plough and the child from the breast.  
In the city of our lord it took a third part of the people. In all the cities, more than two thousand thousand it took.  
We could not dig fast enough. We buried by night, and named them in the morning.  

*Remember, O gods, the cities where it walked.*  
**We remember them.**  

It began in [[set:264|Ölie]], in the seat of our lord, in the city of the realm.  
**Have mercy on Ölie.**  
It went down the roads of the merchants to [[set:57|Fyinane]] and to [[set:272|Izeyoa]].  
**Have mercy on Fyinane and Izeyoa.**  
It went to [[set:7|Ane]] and to [[set:36|Isuizhuunogath]], and the priests there died at the altar.  
**Have mercy on Ane and Isuizhuunogath.**  
It went to [[set:81|Ienitie]] and to [[set:190|Tlomesh]], to [[set:120|Tiyinmiwe]] and to [[set:74|Tliyian]].  
**Have mercy on them all.**  
It went last to [[set:159|Foyitlityi]], and there it lay down and slept.  
**Let it sleep, and never wake.**  

*Confess, O people, why the gods were angry.*  
**We confess it.**  

After the victory at [[evt:38393|Khuele]] we were lifted up.  
After the [[evt:38394|Treaty of Khuele]] we counted the new lands as a miser counts coin.  
We said: "Prosperity is ours by our own strength."  
The teachers of [[mov:25|Myitiyishiu]] told us: justice in dealings, moderation in all things;  
the wise withdraw from wealth and appetite. We did not withdraw.  
The merchant raised the price of bread while the sick lay in the street.  
The landlord took his rent from the house where all were dead.  
The physician asked a [[cur:823|myiu]] at the door and would not come in for less.  
**We confess it. We have not been moderate. We have not been just.**  

*Now ask, O people, what you would have of the gods.*  
**We ask it.**  

That the ague which has departed from our gates may not return to them.  
**Hear us.**  
That the dead who lie without rites may be received in their places.  
**Hear us.**  
That the orphans may find a door, and the widows bread, and the lords remember their obligation to both.  
**Hear us.**  
That our lord Ie Shimin may be kept in health, and that his house may stand.  
**Hear us.**  
That the young men who have gone out in the [[evt:38408|Ölie–Tsey War]] may find the enemy and not the fever.  
**Hear us.**  
That we may keep the order of the priesthood, and pay the gods their due, and our lords their due, and the poor their due.  
**Hear us.**  

*The priest pours water on the threshold. The people kneel.*

O gods, each in your place: we have counted our dead, and they are too many to count.  
Do not count our sins as we have counted our dead.  
Shut the door of the sickness, and give us moderation, that we may keep it shut.  
**So may it be, now and while the realm stands.**
