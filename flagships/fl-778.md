*Taken down at [[set:326|Izhityi]], on the coast of [[reg:23|Puvu]], from the singing of the fisher-women on the strand, the 19th of Shimin, in the year 4191 of the Myitiyishiu, some weeks after the [[evt:45594|Founding of the Colony of Izhithuyi]]; rendered as near to the sense as their tongue permits.*

Out of the morning came the ships,  
three ships, four ships, white as a gull's breast,  
with eyes painted on their bows and their bellies full of men.  
We put down the nets. We stood on the strand.  
The children ran to the dunes and lay in the grass like hares.

They came up the strand in boots.  
They set a pole in the sand and a cloth upon the pole,  
and a man in a black coat read from a paper,  
and we did not know the words, but we knew the words.

*Ships, ships, go back to the morning,*  
*go back to the place where the sun gets up.*  
*The sea was ours when it was empty.*

They say their king is called [[per:44961|Oi Tluie]];  
he sits in [[set:7|Ane]] across the water and has never seen our sand.  
They say this strand is his now, and the headland, and the spring,  
as far as a man can walk in a day, and further when they have walked it.

They cut the pines on the headland for a wall.  
They dug a well beside our spring and said the spring was theirs.  
They buy our fish for little white coins, [[cur:1038|Withe]] they call them,  
thin as a herring's scale: one withe for a basket.  
Then they sell us their grain at three withe the measure,  
and the coins fly home to them like pigeons to the cote.

*Ships, ships, go back to the morning,*  
*go back to the place where the sun gets up.*  
*The sea was ours when it was empty.*

Their priests sing at dawn to many gods, the gods of [[mov:25|Myitiyishiu]],  
and preach to us of justice in dealings and moderation in all things.  
They say the wise withdraw from wealth and appetite,  
and they have crossed the whole sea to find it.

Not all of them are hard. I will sing that too.  
One of them, a boy with a cough, carried my mother's water up the dune  
and would take nothing, not a fish, not a thank-you.  
One of them gave my son a knife with a horn handle.  
One of them cries at night in the long hut, we hear him through the wall.  
He is far from his mother. We know what that is.

They say more ships were to come in the summer,  
but their king has a war elsewhere, the [[evt:45590|Second Ane–Fiw War]],  
and the ships and the men are wanted there.  
So the men of [[pol:1656|Ane]] who are here look out to sea every morning,  
and we look out to sea every morning,  
and we are all of us waiting for the same sails,  
and praying for different things.

*Ships, ships, go back to the morning.*  
*The sea was ours when it was empty.*  
*Now it has roads on it.*
